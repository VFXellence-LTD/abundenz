## What it does

Generates product landing pages from product links. Input a product URL; output a conversion-optimized landing page or sales copy block. Automates the copywriting and layout step for affiliate or product pages.

## Where it fits in polymath

- **Conduit**: Affiliate landing pages are a key Conduit asset — Pinterest pins link somewhere, and that somewhere needs to convert. PagePilot could generate those pages at volume, one per product or niche.
- **Atelier**: Product listing optimization. Generate sales copy for digital product stores when Atelier activates.

Neither ecosystem is active yet. Conduit is in early build; Atelier is parked.

## Pricing

Unknown. Verify before adopting — landing page tools often charge per page or by subscription tier.

## Decision rule

Adopt when: Conduit is producing content manually and landing page creation is the identified bottleneck, OR Atelier activates and listing copy is the bottleneck.
