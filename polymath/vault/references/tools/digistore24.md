## What it does

Affiliate network and payment platform focused on digital products — courses, ebooks, software, memberships. Strong European market penetration. Sellers list products; affiliates promote them for commissions typically ranging 30-75%. Handles tracking, payments, and refunds automatically.

## Where it fits in polymath

- **Conduit**: Affiliate product source. Conduit promotes digital products via Pinterest; Digistore24 provides the products to promote. Complements (doesn't replace) Amazon Associates or other networks — different product catalog, different commission rates, European angle.

Key advantage over Amazon: digital product commissions are substantially higher (30-75% vs Amazon's 2-3% on physical goods). Aligns with the high-ticket digital thesis from [[2026-05-08_pinterest-affiliate-model]].

## Pricing

Free to join as affiliate. Commission rates set by sellers. No cost to promote.
