# Signal — Elevator Pitch

## The Problem

Small and mid-size VFX studios don't have pipeline departments. They have one overwhelmed TD juggling broken naming conventions, manual renders, and duct-tape integrations — or worse, no one at all. The knowledge they need is locked inside large studios like ILM and Weta, buried in $50K vendor contracts, or scattered across outdated forum posts. The few YouTube creators in this space are either artists without engineering depth or engineers without production experience.

## The Solution

**Signal** is a daily, voice-driven content engine covering practical VFX pipeline engineering — the stuff that actually ships frames. One recorded pillar per day gets atomized by AI into 12-18 derivatives across YouTube, newsletters, blogs, shorts, and social. The voice is real. The production experience is real. The AI handles everything except taste and delivery.

## Why Me

I'm a working pipeline TD at a VFX studio. I build tools artists use every day. I've shipped Ayon plugins, Maya toolkits, cross-DCC integrations, and automation systems in active production. I'm not teaching theory — I'm sharing what worked on the last show.

## The Business

Seven stacked revenue streams: YouTube ads, affiliate (Perforce, ShotGrid, cloud render), sponsorships, paid newsletter, digital products (pipeline starter kits, audit checklists, configs), community, and a consulting funnel at $150-250/hr. Consulting pays first; products compound over time. Target: $5-15K/mo at Month 12.

## The Edge

- **No one else is doing this.** The intersection of production-tested pipeline engineering + polished content creation is nearly empty.
- **20-30 min/day owner time at steady state.** AI does production; I do the hard part only I can do — knowing what to say.
- **Niche is affluent.** Pipeline TDs and studio supervisors have budget. Studios pay for tools.
- **Moat deepens over time.** Every video is a consulting referral. Every product compounds the catalog. Every subscriber is harder for a competitor to take.
