# Niche Selection

How to identify and evaluate affiliate niches for Conduit. Apply this framework when activated before committing to any niche.

---

## Selection Criteria

A viable Conduit niche scores well across five dimensions:

| Criterion | What to Measure | Why It Matters |
|-----------|----------------|----------------|
| **Search volume** | Monthly searches for core buying queries | No searches = no traffic ceiling |
| **Commission rates** | Affiliate rate × average order value | Revenue per click |
| **Competition level** | Number of established affiliate sites, domain authority of top results | Determines time to rank |
| **Affiliate availability** | Amazon, Clickbank, or direct programs covering the niche | No program = no income |
| **Content automability** | Can AI generate credible, accurate content without domain expertise? | Determines operating cost |

---

## Evaluation Matrix

Score each criterion 1–5 per niche. Minimum viable score: **18/25**. Prioritise niches scoring 22+.

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|-----------|---|---|---|
| Search volume | <1K/mo core queries | 10K–100K/mo | 100K+/mo |
| Commission ($/click est.) | <$0.02 | $0.05–$0.15 | $0.20+ |
| Competition | Major brands dominate all results | Mix of brands and independent sites | Mostly independent sites, thin content |
| Affiliate availability | No programmes | 1–2 programmes | 3+ programmes, multiple price points |
| Content automability | Requires expert knowledge/testing | Basic research sufficient | Bestseller lists + review mining fully sufficient |

## Example Niches to Evaluate at Activation

These passed initial screening but require full matrix scoring before committing:

### Physical Product Niches (Amazon-primary)
- **Home office setup** — desk accessories, monitors, chairs, webcams, lighting. High search volume. Pinterest-friendly visuals. Amazon 4–8% commissions.
- **Pet products** — cat trees, dog beds, pet cameras, feeders. Passionate buyer segment. Repeat purchases. Amazon 3–5%.
- **Kitchen gadgets** — air fryers, instant pots, coffee makers. Gift-driven purchases spike seasonally. Amazon 4–8%.
- **Fitness gear** — resistance bands, dumbbells, yoga mats. Perennial demand. January and September spikes. Amazon 3–5%.
- **Baby products** — monitors, travel gear, sleep aids. High urgency buyers. Amazon 3–5%.
- **Tech accessories** — phone cases, charging gear, cable management. Very high volume, competitive. Amazon 2–4%.

### Digital / SaaS Niches (Clickbank/direct-primary)
- **AI tools and software** — writing, image generation, productivity. Clickbank/direct 20–40% recurring. Higher per-click value.
- **Online course platforms** — Teachable, Kajabi, etc. Recurring SaaS commissions.
- **Finance tools** — budgeting apps, investment platforms. Regulated — requires extra compliance review.

## Anti-Niches

Do not enter these niches:

| Anti-Niche | Reason |
|-----------|--------|
| VFX software, 3D tools, pipeline tools | Overlaps with Signal's domain — identity bleed risk |
| Kids entertainment, bedtime content, toddler products | Overlaps with Lullaby's domain |
| AI art tools positioned for artists | Overlaps with Atelier's potential domain |
| Supplements, weight loss products | High FTC scrutiny, claim restrictions, deplatforming risk |
| Financial advice, investment products | Regulatory exposure, requires disclaimers that increase friction |
| Adult products | Blanket disqualification — payment processor and platform exposure |
| Political / news content | Toxic brand association, no consistent affiliate angle |
| Anything requiring personal testing or experience claims | Conduit cannot honestly claim "I tested this" |

## Multi-Niche Strategy

Run 3–5 niches simultaneously. Each niche maps to:
- One Pinterest board (or board cluster)
- One blog category
- One tracked affiliate link group

**Operations per niche per week** (when steady-state):
- 15–25 new pins
- 1–2 new blog posts
- Weekly performance review (automated report, ~5 min human review)

**Quarterly kill rule**: Any niche with negative 90-day ROI and no improvement trend gets killed. Resources (posting slots, Agent time) reallocated to winners.

**Expansion rule**: When a niche consistently generates $100+/month net, investigate adding one adjacent sub-niche.

## Niche Validation Protocol

Before full commitment, run a minimum viable test. This can be done in parallel with Phase 0 setup using manually created content.

**MVP Test Parameters:**
- 20 pins per niche
- 30-day run
- Track: impressions, outbound clicks, CTR

**Pass criteria:**
- CTR ≥ 0.5% (clicks / impressions)
- Outbound link clicks ≥ 10 in 30 days
- No account flag / policy warning from Pinterest

**Fail action:**
- Below 0.5% CTR at 30 days → adjust pin design and copy, extend 15 days
- Still below at 45 days → kill niche, do not invest further
- Account flag → review [[safeguards/compliance]], adjust all content in that niche before re-posting

**Data to collect during validation:**
```
Niche: [name]
Test period: [start date] – [end date]
Pins published: [count]
Total impressions: [count]
Outbound clicks: [count]
CTR: [clicks/impressions as %]
Affiliate clicks: [count from affiliate dashboard]
Conversions: [count]
Revenue: [$]
Decision: Pass / Fail / Extend
```

## Seasonal Weighting

Some niches have strong seasonal patterns that affect when to ramp content:

| Niche | Peak Months | Build-Up Lead Time |
|-------|------------|------------------|
| Home office | January (new year), September (back to work) | 6 weeks prior |
| Kitchen gadgets | October–December (gift season) | 8 weeks prior |
| Fitness gear | January, late August | 6 weeks prior |
| Baby products | Year-round, slight spring spike | Evergreen |
| Pet products | December (gifts), April (spring) | 6 weeks prior |
| Tech accessories | October–December, Black Friday | 8 weeks prior |
