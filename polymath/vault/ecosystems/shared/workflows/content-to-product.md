# Shared Workflow — Content → Product → Affiliate Flywheel

## Purpose

The three ecosystems are independent brands but share an economic flywheel. Content builds authority → authority sells products → products generate affiliate-compatible review content → affiliate traffic validates product-market fit. This workflow maps the feedback loops.

## The flywheel

```
Content (authority)
    ↓ mentions tools/workflows
    ↓ audience trusts recommendations
Products (sales)
    ↓ product reviews generate SEO content
    ↓ customer questions become content ideas
Affiliate (validation)
    ↓ click/conversion data reveals demand
    ↓ high-converting niches inform new products
Content (informed by data)
    ↓ ...cycle continues
```

## Cross-ecosystem data flows (allowed)

These are PRIVATE data flows through the controller. No public cross-promotion.

| From | To | Data | Example |
|------|-----|------|---------|
| Affiliate → Products | Conversion data | "Home office gear converts at 4% on Pinterest" → create home office template products |
| Content → Products | Audience questions | "Viewers keep asking for a naming convention tool" → build one |
| Products → Content | Customer feedback | "Buyers of Pipeline Starter Kit want video walkthrough" → make that video |
| Affiliate → Content | Trending topics | "AI writing tools trending in affiliate clicks" → cover in content |

## What is NOT allowed

- Content linking to Products store (different brands)
- Affiliate pins promoting Products (different brands)
- Any public connection between ecosystems
- Sharing customer lists between ecosystems

## Implementation

The controller dashboard shows these data flows. Boss reviews weekly and manually routes insights between ecosystems. No automated cross-ecosystem content routing — that risks brand bleed.

### Monthly cross-pollination review

1. Review Affiliate conversion data — any niches Products should enter?
2. Review Content audience questions — any products to build?
3. Review Products customer feedback — any content to create?
4. Log decisions in controller/escalation-log.md
