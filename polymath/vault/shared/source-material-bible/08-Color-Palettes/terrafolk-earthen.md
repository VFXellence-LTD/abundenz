# Terrafolk Earthen

> Terracotta, ochre, olive, and clay — the warm, grounded palette of natural pigment and folk craft.

## Swatches & roles

| Role | Hex | Name | Notes |
|------|-----|------|-------|
| Primary | `#A8492A` | Terracotta | Warm red-clay; the dominant earth tone |
| Secondary | `#C8893B` | Burnt Ochre | Orange-gold; sun, harvest, fills |
| Accent (warm) | `#D9B26A` | Wheat Gold | Light warm; highlights and field |
| Accent (cool) | `#6E7A4A` | Olive Leaf | Muted green; the only cool note, balances the warmth |
| Deep | `#7A5C3E` | Clay Brown | Earthy dark; line-work and shadow |
| Background | `#EFE3CC` | Raw Linen | Natural undyed ground |

## Structure

**Thematic / earth-toned** — built from the colors of natural pigments (iron oxides give the terracotta and ochre, earth greens give the olive). Mostly warm and analogous, with olive as the single cool counterweight so the palette doesn't feel monotonous. Low-to-mid saturation keeps it natural, not cartoonish.

## Mood & emotional read

Grounded, warm, honest, handmade. Reads as folk craft, harvest, pottery, woven textiles, and rural tradition. Cozy and trustworthy. Strong for "made by hand", "from the earth", and heritage/sustainability brand voices.

## Usage notes

Raw linen is the ground; terracotta and ochre carry the bulk of the color; olive is a deliberate accent (a little goes far); clay brown does line-work and type (warmer than black, fits the natural feel). This palette loves **block-print and woodcut textures** and folk-pattern repeats. Proportion ~ground + 40% warm earths + 10% olive + line-work in clay brown.

## POD / print considerations

- **CMYK**: Excellent print palette — earth tones are built from the colors CMYK reproduces most faithfully (warm reds, oranges, browns). Olive can shift slightly toward grey-green on press; add a little yellow to keep it lively.
- **Apparel**: Versatile — works on **natural, sand, tan, and olive garments** (tonal, sophisticated) and on cream/white. On black it reads warm and rich but loses the "natural linen" softness; reserve raw linen role for the design fill, not the garment, on dark fabric.
- **Accessibility**: Clay brown on raw linen passes AA for text. Wheat gold on raw linen is decorative only (too low contrast for words).

## Remix hooks

- Folk-textile: add `#2E4A6B` indigo for an embroidery-on-cloth feel.
- Mediterranean: push terracotta-dominant, add a single bright `#1E6FA8` ceramic blue.
- Faded homestead: desaturate everything ~15% for a sun-bleached look (overlaps with [[sepiaz-vintage]]).

## Legal / risk

Earth tones and folk color conventions are generic and unownable — legally safe everywhere. The consideration is **cultural respect**, not IP: if a design closely echoes a specific living culture's folk-art tradition (a particular indigenous pattern or palette), present it as inspired-by and avoid marketing it as that community's authentic/endorsed work. The palette itself is neutral.
