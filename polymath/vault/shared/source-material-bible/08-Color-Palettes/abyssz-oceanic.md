# Abyssz Oceanic

> From sunlit shallows to the dark deep — a cool analogous gradient of teals and blues for everything maritime and calming.

## Swatches & roles

| Role | Hex | Name | Notes |
|------|-----|------|-------|
| Deep | `#03252E` | Abyss | Near-black teal; the deep, shadow, drama |
| Primary | `#0A4D5C` | Deep Tide | Dominant ocean blue-green |
| Secondary | `#1E869B` | Lagoon | Mid teal; the "sea" color |
| Accent | `#5FC2C9` | Shallows | Bright aqua; highlights, foam edges |
| Highlight | `#BFE9E6` | Sea Glass | Pale aqua; light, mist, tints |
| Background | `#F2FBFA` | Foam White | Cool off-white ground |

## Structure

A cool **analogous gradient** running through the blue-green family from abyss-dark to foam-white. Because it's a single-direction value/hue ramp, it naturally suggests *depth* — perfect for layering shallow-to-deep scenes. Calm and cohesive by construction.

## Mood & emotional read

Calm, cool, refreshing, contemplative, slightly mysterious at the dark end. Reads as ocean, water, freshness, and serenity. Versatile: the light end is breezy and clean, the dark end is moody and deep. Strong for wellness, travel, marine, and calm-content brands.

## Usage notes

Treat it as a **depth map**: foam white and sea glass for the top/light, lagoon for the body, deep tide and abyss for the bottom/shadow. Use the value range to create dimensional, layered scenes (waves, water columns). For type, use abyss/deep tide on light grounds or foam white on dark. Proportion follows the scene's depth. Pairs naturally with flowing, curved shapes.

## POD / print considerations

- **CMYK**: `partial` — the brightest teals (lagoon, shallows) sit near the cyan-green edge of CMYK and can shift slightly muddy or lose vibrancy on press. Build them with strong cyan and minimal magenta to hold the clean teal; expect the *brightest* aqua to dull a touch in print. Darks and the pale end reproduce well.
- **Apparel**: Beautiful on white/light garments (the gradient reads clean) and excellent on navy/dark teal garments (the lighter aquas pop). On black, the deep end disappears — lead with the bright aquas as the visible design.
- **Accessibility**: Deep tide / abyss on foam white passes AA for text. Shallows or sea glass on white is decorative only — too low contrast for words.

## Remix hooks

- Complementary pop: add `#FF7A4D` coral/orange for a sunset-over-sea contrast.
- Tropical shallows: drop the abyss-dark, lighten everything for a Caribbean look.
- Deep-abyss horror: push to near-black with a single bioluminescent `#39FF88` accent.

## Legal / risk

Blues and teals are generic and unownable — safe across every application here. The only narrow trademark note in this color family is **Tiffany & Co.'s robin's-egg blue, which is protected only as packaging for jewelry/boxes** — irrelevant to oceanic design unless you specifically mimic that exact pale-blue box for jewelry products, which we never do. This palette's teals are deliberately deeper and greener than that hue.
