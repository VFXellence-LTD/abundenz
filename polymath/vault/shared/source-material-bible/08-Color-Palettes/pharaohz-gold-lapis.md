# Pharaohz Gold & Lapis

> The color signature of Ancient Egypt — pharaonic gold against deep lapis-lazuli blue, the look of tomb treasure and divine kingship.

## Swatches & roles

| Role | Hex | Name | Notes |
|------|-----|------|-------|
| Primary | `#1A3A6B` | Lapis Deep | The ground/field color; reads as night sky and the divine |
| Accent | `#D4AF37` | Pharaoh Gold | Metallic-warm; carries borders, symbols, type |
| Secondary | `#0E7C7B` | Faience Teal | Egyptian-blue/turquoise glaze; softens the gold-blue tension |
| Background (dark) | `#1C1C1C` | Tomb Black | Near-black ground for high-drama variants |
| Background (light) | `#F2E6C9` | Papyrus Cream | Warm parchment for light layouts and text-heavy pieces |

## Structure

Thematic, leaning on a near-**complementary** relationship: gold (warm yellow-orange) sits opposite deep blue on the wheel, so the two vibrate against each other and feel rich. Faience teal bridges them as a cooler mid-tone. Black and cream are the two ground options.

## Mood & emotional read

Luxury, antiquity, the sacred and the eternal. Signals "treasure", "wisdom", "ancient power". Strong fit for premium feel without being cold — the gold keeps it warm and human.

## Usage notes

Run roughly **60% lapis / 30% black-or-cream ground / 10% gold**. Gold is precious — use it sparingly for symbols, fine line-work, and type, never as a large fill (it cheapens fast and prints poorly as a flat tint). Faience teal is your "release valve" when gold-on-blue gets too intense. Keep type either gold-on-blue or cream-on-blue for legibility.

## POD / print considerations

- **CMYK**: Gold is a *color illusion*, not a real ink — `#D4AF37` prints as a flat mustard, not metallic. For true gold use a **metallic spot ink / gold foil** if the product supports it; otherwise accept the matte mustard and don't promise "shiny". Faience teal (`#0E7C7B`) is near the cyan-green edge and can shift slightly muddy; nudge toward cyan if it dulls.
- **Apparel**: Excellent on black and navy garments — gold and teal pop on dark fabric. On light/white shirts the lapis ground must be printed as a large fill (cost/feel consideration); consider an outline-only version for light tees.
- **Accessibility**: Gold-on-lapis (`#D4AF37` on `#1A3A6B`) passes large-text contrast but is borderline for small body text — use cream for small text instead.

## Remix hooks

- Aged/weathered tomb: replace pharaoh gold with `#A67C2E` antique bronze.
- Cosmos variant: lapis-dominant, add tiny gold stars on tomb black.
- Add `#7BC4C4` bright turquoise as a youthful 4th accent for kid-facing Egyptian content.

## Legal / risk

Color is not copyrightable, and ancient Egyptian palettes are public-domain heritage. The only caution: gold-on-blue is widely used, so it gives no brand distinctiveness on its own — pair it with original motifs. Do not reproduce a specific copyrighted modern artwork or film's Egyptian color treatment as "the reference"; define your own hex (done here).
