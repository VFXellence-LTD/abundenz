# Sepiaz Vintage Muted

> Aged paper, faded ink, and warm dust — a low-saturation analogous system for nostalgic, literary, and heritage work.

## Swatches & roles

| Role | Hex | Name | Notes |
|------|-----|------|-------|
| Primary / Text | `#3B2F2A` | Walnut Ink | Dark warm brown; type and line-work (softer than black) |
| Secondary | `#7A6450` | Antique Bark | Mid-brown; secondary shapes, borders |
| Accent | `#B79B77` | Faded Tan | Warm tan; fills and aged tone |
| Highlight | `#D9C7A8` | Old Parchment | Light warm; panels and tints |
| Background | `#EDE3D0` | Aged Cream | The paper; default ground |

## Structure

**Analogous and warm** — all five colors live in the yellow-orange-brown family, stepped from dark walnut to cream. Low saturation throughout gives the "faded with age" feel. Because nothing is fully saturated, it's calm and easy on the eye.

## Mood & emotional read

Nostalgia, warmth, quiet authority, literariness. Reads as old books, letterpress, vintage maps, and family photographs. Comforting and timeless rather than loud. Strong for "wisdom", "heritage", and "slow living" brand voices.

## Usage notes

Use aged cream as the ground, walnut ink for text and engraving-style line-work (avoid pure black — it breaks the vintage spell). Antique bark and faded tan build mid-tones and borders; old parchment is for subtle panels. This palette *wants* texture — add a subtle paper grain or halftone for authenticity. Pairs beautifully with serif/old-style typography.

## POD / print considerations

- **CMYK**: One of the safest palettes for print — muted warm tones reproduce faithfully and forgive press variation. Walnut ink reads richer than black-substitute K and avoids the harsh look of true black on cream.
- **Apparel**: Designed for **light/natural garments** (natural, sand, cream, oatmeal tees) where the warm tones feel intentional. On dark shirts the palette mostly vanishes — for dark apparel, invert to a cream-on-walnut "negative" treatment.
- **Accessibility**: Walnut ink on aged cream passes AA for body text. Faded tan on cream is decorative only — never put text in faded tan on a light ground.

## Remix hooks

- Cooler "old newspaper": shift all colors ~8% toward grey.
- Add one period accent: faded teal `#5E7E7A` or oxblood `#6E2E2A` for a single highlight color.
- Storybook variant: lighten and warm for a gentle children's-book ground (overlaps with [[dreamz-pastel]]).
