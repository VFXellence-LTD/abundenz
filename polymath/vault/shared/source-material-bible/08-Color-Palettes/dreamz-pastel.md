# Dreamz Pastel Nursery

> Soft sky, blush, mint, butter, and lavender on warm white — the gentle, soothing palette for children's books and nursery decor.

## Swatches & roles

| Role | Hex | Name | Notes |
|------|-----|------|-------|
| Primary | `#C9D8F2` | Cloud Blue | Calm sky; default ground for scenes |
| Accent 1 | `#F7C5D9` | Blush Pink | Warm focal accent; cheeks, hearts, blossoms |
| Accent 2 | `#CFEBD8` | Mint Soft | Cool green; grass, leaves, soft fields |
| Accent 3 | `#FBE7C2` | Butter Cream | Warm light; sun, sand, gentle glow |
| Accent 4 | `#E7D6F2` | Lilac Mist | Dreamy purple; twilight, magic, shadow |
| Background | `#FFFDF8` | Warm White | Off-white page; never pure white |

## Structure

A soft **analogous-to-spread** set: gentle blue, green, and lavender sit close together (calm) while blush and butter add warm balance. Every color is high-value, low-saturation, so nothing competes — the look is uniformly soft, exactly what young eyes and bedtime content want.

## Mood & emotional read

Safe, soothing, sweet, dreamy. Reads as nursery, bedtime, gentleness, and innocence. Lowers energy rather than raising it — perfect for sleep stories and calm-down content. Warm and unisex (not the old pink-for-girls cliché — the full spread keeps it neutral).

## Usage notes

Use warm white as the page, cloud blue as the primary scene ground, and rotate the four accents for characters and props. Keep saturation low and edges soft (the palette pairs with rounded, hand-drawn shapes). For text, don't use a pastel on a pastel — use a soft dark like `#4A4458` (slate-plum) for legible storybook type. Proportion: lots of white and one ground, accents in small joyful doses.

## POD / print considerations

- **CMYK**: Pastels print well but can **drift** — light tints are sensitive to press calibration and may look patchy or slightly off-hue at low ink coverage. Specify a touch more saturation than the screen value so they don't wash out on paper. Avoid relying on very pale tints for fine detail.
- **Apparel**: Designed for **white/light garments** (the soft look depends on a light ground). On dark fabric pastels look chalky and need a white underbase — usually not worth it. For baby/kids apparel, confirm the print method uses child-safe, certified inks.
- **Accessibility**: Pastel-on-pastel has poor contrast — never use it for text. Pair with a soft dark for any words. For picture books, ensure illustration-vs-text contrast meets readability for early readers.

## Remix hooks

- Bedtime/night variant: deepen blue and lilac, dim the warm white to a soft dusk.
- Focal pop: add one saturated color (e.g. `#FF8FA3` coral) for the story's main character so they stand out from the soft world.
- Cozy autumn nursery: warm the whole set toward peach and sage.

## Legal / risk

Pastels are generic and unownable — completely safe across all uses and categories. The only real-world consideration is **product safety**, not IP: physical items intended for babies/toddlers must use certified non-toxic inks per local regulations. That's a manufacturing/compliance note, not a copyright or trademark issue.
