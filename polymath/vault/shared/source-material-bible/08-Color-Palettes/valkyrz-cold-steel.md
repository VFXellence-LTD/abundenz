# Valkyrz Cold Steel

> The cold of the north rendered in tempered iron and overcast sky — a near-monochrome steel-grey system for Viking, Norse, and dark-fantasy work.

## Swatches & roles

| Role | Hex | Name | Notes |
|------|-----|------|-------|
| Primary | `#2E3338` | Forged Iron | Dark blue-grey; the dominant body color |
| Secondary | `#5A656E` | Gunmetal | Mid steel; secondary shapes and shadows |
| Accent | `#8A949C` | Hewn Stone | Lighter grey; runestone and texture |
| Highlight | `#C7CED3` | Frost Silver | Cool near-white; edge light, type, snow |
| Ground (dark) | `#0F1418` | Void Black | Near-black for deep shadow and night |

## Structure

**Monochrome** built on a single cool blue-grey hue stepped through five values from void black to frost silver. Because it's value-driven rather than hue-driven, it's extremely flexible and never clashes — ideal for adding one bold accent.

## Mood & emotional read

Cold, stoic, severe, rugged. Reads as iron, weather, endurance, and restraint. Masculine-leaning but not exclusively. Excellent base for "earned strength" and "harsh beauty" messaging.

## Usage notes

This is a **base**, not a finished look — a true monochrome can feel flat, so it's designed to host ONE saturated accent (see remix hooks). Use forged iron and void black as grounds, gunmetal for structure, hewn stone for texture/detail, frost silver for the lightest highlights and crisp type. Proportion roughly 50/25/15/10 dark-to-light.

## POD / print considerations

- **CMYK**: All greys print cleanly and predictably — this is one of the most print-safe palettes in the library. Watch the blue-grey shifting too warm on uncalibrated presses; keep a touch of cyan in the build (e.g. greys built as `K + slight C` rather than pure K) to hold the cold cast.
- **Apparel**: Reads strongly on black, charcoal, and heather-grey garments. On white tees, frost silver disappears — use forged iron / gunmetal as the lightest visible value and reserve frost silver only when there's a dark fill behind it. Embroidery in gunmetal thread on dark fabric is a premium-feeling option.
- **Accessibility**: Frost silver on void black / forged iron passes AA comfortably. Gunmetal on forged iron is too low-contrast for text — decorative only.

## Remix hooks

- Forge variant: add a single `#E2691A` ember orange for sparks/heat.
- Battle variant: add `#7A1E1E` blood crimson.
- Pure ice / frost-giant: shift everything 10% toward blue and lighten — overlaps with [[frostz-winter]].

## Legal / risk

Color is not copyrightable. The risk here is cultural reception, not IP: a subset of Norse/runic symbolism has been appropriated by extremist groups. Keep the palette's *symbol* pairings unambiguous (heritage, mythology, fantasy) and avoid any motif with hate-group associations. The steel palette itself is neutral and safe.
