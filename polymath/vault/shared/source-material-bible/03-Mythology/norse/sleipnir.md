# Sleipnir

> Odin's eight-legged horse: the swiftest steed in all the worlds, able to gallop across sky and sea and down into the land of the dead — born, strangely, of the trickster Loki.

## Who / What
Sleipnir is Odin's horse, the greatest of all steeds — grey, powerful, and uniquely possessed of **eight legs**. It can run across the sky and over the sea and is the only horse that can carry a rider down into Hel, the land of the dead, and back. Its origin is one of myth's strangest tales: when Loki needed to sabotage a giant's wager, he transformed into a mare to lure away the giant's stallion, and later gave birth to Sleipnir — making the trickster Loki the horse's mother. Sleipnir appears on real Viking-age runestones, giving it an authentic, distinctive visual pedigree.

## Archetype
The Divine Steed / World Traveler / Swiftest / Impossible Mount. The supreme mount that crosses every boundary — including the boundary of death. A clean, charming, instantly recognizable motif.

## Visual Motifs (safe to draw)
A powerful grey or white horse with eight legs (the defining feature); galloping across sky, sea, or the threshold of the underworld; ridden by the one-eyed Odin; rendered in runestone / Viking-age art style (which is itself public domain and authentically mythological).
