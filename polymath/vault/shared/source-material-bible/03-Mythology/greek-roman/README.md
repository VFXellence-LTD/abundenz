# Greek & Roman Mythology

The Olympian tradition is the single most productive mythology for Western content: instantly recognizable, deeply documented, and entirely public domain at the source. Greek myths were recorded by Homer, Hesiod, Ovid, and many others more than two thousand years ago; the texts and the figures are free. The Romans adopted and renamed the same gods (Zeus → Jupiter, Aphrodite → Venus, and so on), so each entry notes both names.

## The big trap

Greek myth is the most *adapted* mythology on earth, which means it is surrounded by protected modern versions. Before building anything, separate the free myth from the restricted product:

- **Disney's Hercules (1997)** — the specific character designs, songs, and the blue-flame Hades are Disney's. The myth of Heracles is free.
- **God of War (Sony)** — Kratos, and that franchise's specific takes on Zeus, Ares, Kronos, etc., are Sony copyrights. The mythological figures are free.
- **Percy Jackson / Rick Riordan** — the modern-teen reinterpretations, character names like Percy, and the specific Camp Half-Blood designs are Riordan's. The gods themselves are free.
- **Marvel's Hercules / Ares / Zeus**, **DC's Wonder Woman pantheon**, **Smite**, **Hades (Supergiant Games)**, **Assassin's Creed Odyssey**, **Clash of the Titans (the film designs)** — all carry their own protected designs.

Rule of thumb: draw from the ancient iconography (red-figure vase aesthetics, classical statuary, the attributes described by Homer and Ovid), never from the movie or game you remember.
