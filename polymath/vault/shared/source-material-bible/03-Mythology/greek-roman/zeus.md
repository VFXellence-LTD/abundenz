# Zeus (Jupiter)

> The king of the gods: a thunderbolt in one hand, an eagle at his side, ruling sky and storm from the peak of Olympus.

## Who / What
Zeus is the supreme ruler of the Greek pantheon, god of the sky, thunder, lightning, law, and order. He overthrew his father Kronos, drew lots with his brothers Poseidon and Hades to divide the cosmos, and won the heavens. The Romans called him Jupiter (Jove). He is famous both for upholding cosmic order and for an endless string of affairs that produced many heroes and gods.

## Archetype
The King / Sky-Father / Ruler. Also a recurring The Philanderer figure. Zeus is the prototype of the powerful patriarch whose authority is absolute but whose appetites cause chaos. Cross-link: [[the-king-archetype]] style figures across pantheons (Odin, Ra, Indra).

## Visual Motifs (safe to draw)
Mature bearded man, powerfully built, often half-draped or nude in classical style; arm raised hurling a jagged thunderbolt; an eagle perched nearby or in flight; seated on a cloud-throne; oak-leaf crown; scepter. Storm clouds, lightning forks, and Mount Olympus as setting.

## Symbols
- **Thunderbolt** — his signature weapon, a clean standalone icon.
- **Eagle** — his sacred bird and messenger.
- **Oak tree / oak leaves** — his sacred tree.
- **Bull** — a form he takes (the abduction of Europa).
- **Scepter and throne** — kingship.

## Themes
Authority and order, the burden and abuse of power, justice and oath-keeping, fatherhood, the volatility of the powerful, sky and storm.

## Reusable Concepts
The ruler who keeps the cosmos in balance but cannot keep his own desires in check. The lottery that divides the world (sky/sea/underworld). The god who disguises himself to walk among mortals. Divine judgment and the weighing of fates.

## Story / Narrative Hooks
The war against the Titans (the Titanomachy). The swallowing of Metis and the birth of Athena from his head. The countless transformations to seduce mortals (swan, bull, golden rain). The punishment of Prometheus. Settling disputes among the gods.
