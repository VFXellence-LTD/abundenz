# Mesopotamian Mythology (Sumerian, Akkadian, Babylonian)

The world's oldest recorded mythology, from the lands between the Tigris and Euphrates — Sumer, Akkad, Babylon, and Assyria. This is the source of the *Epic of Gilgamesh* (humanity's oldest surviving great story), the original flood myth, and primordial figures like Tiamat and Marduk. All of it is thousands of years old and public domain. The aesthetic — ziggurats, cuneiform, winged bulls, lapis and gold, lush beards and stylized eyes — is striking and underused, which makes it a strong differentiator.

## The traps

This is "dead" religion (no living faith claims it), so cultural-respect concerns are light, but note:

1. **Modern depictions are restricted.** Tiamat is a famous Dungeons & Dragons creature (a specific five-headed dragon — Wizards of the Coast IP); the *Final Fantasy*, *Smite*, and various game versions of these gods are protected; the *Annunaki* feature heavily in (copyrighted) ancient-astronaut / Zecharia Sitchin writings and conspiracy media. Use the ancient figures, not the game/conspiracy versions.
2. **Translations of Gilgamesh** can be copyrighted — use a public-domain translation (e.g. early-20th-century) or your own rendering.
3. **The "Lilith" figure** has heavy modern occult, feminist, and pop-culture freight — the ancient demoness is free, but be aware of the dense modern associations.
