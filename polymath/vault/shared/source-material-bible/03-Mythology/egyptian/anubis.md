# Anubis (Anpu)

> The black-jackal-headed god who guards the dead, oversees embalming, and weighs each soul's heart against the feather of truth — the solemn guide between this world and the next.

## Who / What
Anubis is the ancient Egyptian god of embalming, mummification, and the dead — the guardian of tombs and the guide of souls into the afterlife (a psychopomp). He invented embalming when he prepared the body of Osiris. In the judgment of the dead, it is Anubis who leads the deceased into the Hall of Truth and oversees the weighing of their heart against the feather of Ma'at (truth); if the heart is lighter, the soul passes on, if heavier, it is devoured. He is depicted as a man with the head of a black jackal (jackals haunted Egyptian cemeteries) or as a full reclining black jackal. Black, here, is the color of fertile rebirth and of the embalmed body — not evil. He is one of the most instantly recognizable and beloved figures in all of Egyptian myth.

## Archetype
The Psychopomp / Guardian of the Dead / Guide / Judge's Assistant. The solemn, dutiful guide who escorts souls across the ultimate threshold — dignified, not sinister.

## Visual Motifs (safe to draw)
A man's body with the head of a sleek black jackal (tall pointed ears, long muzzle); or a full reclining black jackal atop a shrine; tending a mummy on an embalming table; holding or operating the scales that weigh the heart; a *was* scepter and *ankh*; leading a soul by the hand. Black, gold, and profile-art style.

## Symbols
- **The jackal head** — his defining, iconic feature.
- **The scales** — the weighing of the heart.
- **Black color** — rebirth and the embalmed (not evil).
- **The embalming table / the mummy** — his domain.
- **The was scepter and ankh** — power and life.

## Themes
Death and the threshold, embalming and the preservation of the dead, the guide of souls, the weighing and judgment of the heart, protection of the dead, duty, the passage between worlds.

## Reusable Concepts
The guide through the darkness (the psychopomp — a comforting rather than scary death figure). The weighing of the heart (judgment, truth, "is your heart light?" — a profound moral concept). The loyal guardian of the dead. The threshold-keeper. Dignity in the face of death. The protector who prepares the way.

## Story / Narrative Hooks
His invention of embalming for the body of Osiris. His leading of souls into the Hall of Truth. His operation of the scales in the weighing of the heart (with Thoth recording and Ammit waiting). His guardianship of tombs and cemeteries.
