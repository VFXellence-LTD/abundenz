# Ra (Re)

> The sun god who sails his golden barque across the sky each day and battles through the underworld each night to be reborn at dawn — the supreme creator and king of the Egyptian gods.

## Who / What
Ra is the ancient Egyptian sun god, creator deity, and king of the gods, often considered the supreme power of the pantheon. Each day he sails across the sky in his solar barque as the sun; each night he travels through the underworld (the Duat), where he battles the chaos-serpent Apophis, to be reborn at dawn. He is frequently merged with other gods (Amun-Ra, Ra-Horakhty). He is depicted as a falcon-headed man crowned with a sun disk encircled by a cobra (the uraeus). Ra embodies light, creation, kingship, and the eternal, dependable cycle of day and night.

## Archetype
The Sun-King / Creator / Supreme God / Cyclical Traveler. The radiant ruler whose daily journey is the very rhythm of the world — and a perfect "darkness to dawn" cycle.

## Visual Motifs (safe to draw)
A falcon-headed man crowned with a golden sun disk encircled by a rearing cobra (uraeus); sailing a ceremonial barque across the heavens or through the underworld; radiant solar rays; sometimes shown as a scarab pushing the sun (the dawn aspect, Khepri) or as a great cat slaying the serpent. Gold, lapis blue, and Egyptian profile-art style.

## Symbols
- **The sun disk** — his defining emblem; light and creation.
- **The falcon head** — his sky aspect.
- **The solar barque** — his daily/nightly voyage.
- **The uraeus cobra** — royal protection around the disk.
- **The scarab** — the dawn / rebirth aspect (see [[scarab]]).

## Themes
The sun and light, creation and kingship, the daily and nightly cycle, order battling chaos, renewal at dawn, the dependable rhythm of existence, supremacy.

## Reusable Concepts
"The journey through darkness back to dawn" (a powerful renewal/resilience narrative — every night he must fight to rise again). The sun-disk as a clean, supreme emblem (energy, solar, light brands). The dependable cycle. The supreme creator-king. Order vs. chaos (Ra vs. Apophis).

## Story / Narrative Hooks
His nightly battle with the serpent Apophis in the underworld. His daily voyage across the sky. The myth of his secret name (which Isis tricks out of him to gain power). His merging with other gods. His role as the first king of Egypt who later withdrew to the sky.
