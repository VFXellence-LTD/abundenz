# Brand Generation — Z-Named Brand Identity from Archetype + Palette + Motif

## Purpose

Generates a complete Z-named brand identity — name candidates, archetype-rooted voice, color palette, primary motif, logo concept, and tagline — for a product line, series, or content brand within the VFXellence / Abundenz family. Every output follows the Z-naming convention (name incorporates a Z for distinctiveness and trademark-clearability) and is grounded in a specific [[10-Archetypes]] archetype, a [[08-Color-Palettes]] palette, and a [[13-Visual-Motifs]] or [[09-Symbols]] motif, ensuring the brand has both creative coherence and a cleared visual language. Resolve all slots per [[shared/slot-resolution-protocol]] before committing to any name or mark.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[ARCHETYPE]` | [[10-Archetypes]] | yes | The brand's personality and promise pattern | `archetype-ruler` → "commands, stabilizes, delivers order and prosperity; voice: authoritative, decisive, premium" |
| `[PALETTE]` | [[08-Color-Palettes]] | yes | The brand's color system | `palette-regaliz-jewel` → "jewel-tone tri-tone: plum #4B1248, forest emerald #0F5C4A, cobalt #1B3A8C, accent gold #C9A227" |
| `[MOTIF]` | [[13-Visual-Motifs]] or [[09-Symbols]] | yes | The brand's primary visual symbol | `motif-laurels-and-wreaths` → "twin laurel arcs enclosing a central mark; signals victory, honor, prestige" |
| `[CATEGORY]` | [[14-Trending-Categories]] or inline | yes | Product category / market this brand operates in | "philosophy/wisdom merch / mythology print line / detective fiction adjacent stationery" |

| `[TAGLINE_FORMAT]` | inline | optional | Length/form for the tagline | "3 words / imperative verb / question / under 8 words" |

## Z-Naming Rules

Every brand name generated by this template must:
1. Incorporate a letter Z (hard rule — it is the family signature for distinctiveness and TM clearability).
2. Be a coined or unusual word — not a dictionary word in common commercial use.
3. Sound good when spoken aloud and be easily spelled after hearing it once.
4. Be generated in a batch of 5–8 candidates for the operator to choose from.
5. Be cleared: before committing to a name, perform a basic trademark spot-check (USPTO.gov / EUIPO / national registry) and a domain availability check. The template generates candidates; clearing is the operator's responsibility.

## Base Prompt (model-agnostic)

```
You are a brand-naming and identity specialist for the VFXellence / Abundenz product family.

Brand parameters:
- Archetype: [ARCHETYPE] — core personality, promise, voice register, shadow to avoid.
- Color system: [PALETTE] — list colors and their roles.
- Primary motif: [MOTIF] — the visual anchor.
- Category: [CATEGORY].
- Thematic source: [THEME_SOURCE] (public domain; use themes and symbols, not the copyrighted name as a brand mark).
- Concept words to evoke in the name: [Z_SEED].
- Tone: [TONE].

Deliverables:
1. NAME CANDIDATES (generate 6–8 options): Each name must contain a Z. Coin new words — do not use existing trademarks or common dictionary phrases. After each candidate, write one sentence explaining the Z placement and meaning.
2. LOGO CONCEPT BRIEF (text): Describe the mark in 3–4 sentences. Reference [MOTIF] as the primary visual, [PALETTE] for color, and [ARCHETYPE] for character. Do NOT describe a design that imitates any existing trademark or protected logo.
3. VOICE AND TONE GUIDE (5 bullet points): What this brand says, how it says it, what it never says.
4. TAGLINE CANDIDATES (4 options in [TAGLINE_FORMAT] format).
5. ONE-LINE BRAND PROMISE: Complete the sentence — "We exist to ___."

Do not: use a protected brand name as inspiration for the Z name; suggest a logo that imitates any existing mark; invoke a copyrighted franchise as a design reference; use any real person's name or likeness in the identity.
```

## Worked Examples

### Example 1 — Wisdom/Sage Mythology Brand (Athena substrate)

**Slots:**
- `[ARCHETYPE]` = `archetype-sage`
- `[PALETTE]` = `palette-pharaohz-gold-lapis` → lapis deep blue, pharaoh gold, faience teal, papyrus cream
- `[MOTIF]` = `motif-laurels-and-wreaths` → twin laurel arcs, victory and wisdom framing
- `[CATEGORY]` = philosophy/mythology art prints and merch
- `[THEME_SOURCE]` = `myth-greek-athena` → wisdom, strategy, the owl
- `[Z_SEED]` = "wisdom, clarity, Athena, owl, knowing"
- `[TONE]` = "scholarly and accessible, premium but not cold"
- `[TAGLINE_FORMAT]` = "imperative verb, under 6 words"

**Full prompt output:**

**Name candidates:**
1. **Aelyza** — ae (ancient/aeon) + lyz (clarity/light) + a; sounds classical, feminine-neutral, Z at the heart.
2. **Zenoath** — zen (wisdom, serenity) + oath; a name that sounds like a vow of knowledge.
3. **Vylenz** — vel (to see, from Latin *velox*) + enz; sharp, modern, the Z closes with authority.
4. **Ozinara** — oz (a coined core) + inara (starlight); mythic and distinctive, Z prominent.
5. **Clarivaz** — clariv (clarity) + az (suffix with classical weight); readable, speaks its meaning.
6. **Nozenth** — noz (coined) + enth (enthusiasm/knowledge suffix); scholarly resonance.
7. **Zelorath** — zel (zeal, clarity) + orath (coined suffix with an ancient ring); Z-first, strong.

**Logo concept brief:**
A centered owl head in minimal flat-line style, rendered in lapis deep blue (#1A3A6B), enclosed by a pair of slim laurel branches that arc up from the base and meet at the top. Gold (#D4AF37) accents the laurel details and owl eye. The brand name sits in a classical serif typeface in papyrus cream below the mark on a lapis ground. No gradients; the mark reads at stamp size and on merchandise.

**Voice and tone guide:**
- The brand speaks from a position of earned authority — it has done the reading.
- It never talks down; it invites the audience in.
- It references mythology and philosophy as living ideas, not museum pieces.
- It never uses hustle-culture language, urgency manipulation, or empty superlatives.
- Sentence structure: declarative, clean, occasionally aphoristic. Paragraphs under 3 sentences.

**Tagline candidates:**
1. *Know the oldest things.*
2. *See clearly. Act wisely.*
3. *The owl was here first.*
4. *Clarity as a practice.*

**Brand promise:** "We exist to put the oldest ideas about wisdom in front of people who are ready to think."

**Legal note:** `myth-greek-athena` risk_level: **safe** (ancient myth). `palette-pharaohz-gold-lapis` risk_level: **safe**. `motif-laurels-and-wreaths` risk_level: **caution** — do not replicate the UN emblem arrangement. `archetype-sage` risk_level: **safe**. None of the Z-names use trademarked terms; operator must run TM spot-check before filing or using in commerce.

### Example 2 — Art Deco Luxury Line (Ruler archetype, Gatzby Deco palette)

**Slots:**
- `[ARCHETYPE]` = `archetype-ruler`
- `[PALETTE]` = `palette-gatzby-deco` → near-black #0B0B0B, deco gold #C9A227, teal #0E6E6E, ivory #F4EFE6
- `[MOTIF]` = `motif-retro-sunbursts` → centered radiating sunburst, geometric rays
- `[CATEGORY]` = luxury poster and stationery line
- `[THEME_SOURCE]` = Art Deco aesthetic (from [[06-Historical-Art/art-deco]] and [[17-Design-Recipes/deco-sunburst-poster]] `recipe-deco-sunburst-poster`)
- `[Z_SEED]` = "gold, deco, radiance, command, glamour"
- `[TONE]` = "bold, sophisticated, minimal in language but maximal in visual impact"
- `[TAGLINE_FORMAT]` = "3 words or fewer"

**Name candidates:**
1. **Glamz** — already in use as internal Polymath codename for this aesthetic; suitable for internal series naming (confirm TM status before external use).
2. **Goldvanz** — gold + van (movement, forward) + z; the feeling of golden momentum.
3. **Raziant** — raz (coined) + iant (radiant suffix); sounds immediately like it glows.
4. **Luxenz** — lux (light/luxury) + enz; clear, premium, Z carries the closer.
5. **Zenholm** — coined; Z-first, holm (Old Norse: island) suggests an island of refinement.
6. **Vorzeld** — vor (before, primary) + zeld (coined, golden-adjacent); commanding and unusual.
7. **Gilzdor** — gild + z + dor (golden gateway); rich register, fantasy-adjacent premium.

**Logo concept brief:**
A centered sunburst mark: 12 equal rays radiating from a solid circle, in deco gold (#C9A227) on near-black (#0B0B0B). The brand name in a geometric Art Deco typeface — all-caps, equal stroke widths, no serifs — sits centered below the sunburst. A fine single-weight border rule frames the lockup. The whole mark is geometric, bilateral, and resolves cleanly at small sizes on gold foil or embossed card stock.

**Tagline candidates:**
1. *Command the room.*
2. *Pure gold.*
3. *Geometry is power.*
4. *The age of Glamz.* (internal use only until TM cleared)

**Legal note:** `palette-gatzby-deco` risk_level: **caution** — Art Deco style is PD, but do NOT brand as "Gatsby" (trademark risk in some classes). The Z-name "Glamz" is internal shorthand; do not release under that name without TM clearance. `motif-retro-sunbursts` risk_level: **caution** — avoid the 16-ray imperial Japanese rising sun arrangement and do not clone any specific brand's sunburst logo. `archetype-ruler` risk_level: **safe**.

### Example 3 — Detective/Mystery Content Brand (Sage + Holmes iconography, no name use)

**Slots:**
- `[ARCHETYPE]` = `archetype-sage` (detective variant)
- `[PALETTE]` = `palette-monocrome-ink` → ink black, off-white, mid-grey, amber highlight
- `[MOTIF]` = `motif-vintage-engraving-anatomical` → fine engraving cross-hatch texture, Victorian detail
- `[CATEGORY]` = mystery/detective podcast and merch brand
- `[THEME_SOURCE]` = `pd-char-sherlock-holmes` iconography — deerstalker, pipe, magnifier — do NOT use the name as brand
- `[Z_SEED]` = "deduction, logic, mystery, case, fog"
- `[TONE]` = "cerebral, dry wit, authoritative"
- `[TAGLINE_FORMAT]` = "under 8 words"

**Name candidates:**
1. **Deducz** — deduc (deduction) + z; already used in [[17-Design-Recipes/victorian-detective-tee]] as the Z-named stand-in; strong candidate.
2. **Cluevanz** — clue + van + z; mobile, investigative energy.
3. **Inferenz** — inference + z; the core cognitive act of detection.
4. **Logizcal** — logic + z + cal; slightly playful, Z embedded.
5. **Fogveil** — fogveil without Z (invalid by rule); revise → **Fzogveyl** (reject — unpronounceable).
6. **Murkalyz** — murky + lyz; suggests fog and clarity simultaneously.
7. **Razonal** — raz (coined) + onal (rational suffix); deliberate Z placement in a word that echoes "rational."

**Logo concept brief:**
A silhouetted figure in a deerstalker and inverness cape, three-quarter profile, rendered as a Victorian wood-engraving in black line on off-white. A magnifying glass overlaps the figure's right side; the glass circle frames the brand name in an engraved Victorian serif. Fine cross-hatch shading throughout. The whole mark feels like it came from an 1890s illustrated periodical — no gradients, no fills, pure line-art logic.

**Tagline candidates:**
1. *When you eliminate the impossible.*
2. *Logic is the only light.*
3. *See what others overlook.*
4. *The case is always open.*

**Legal note:** `pd-char-sherlock-holmes` risk_level: **caution**. Use the iconography and Z-named brand character only; do NOT use "Sherlock Holmes," "221B," or "Watson" in the brand name, as these carry trademark risk from the Conan Doyle Estate. Do not copy BBC Sherlock or RDJ film design elements. The deerstalker + pipe + inverness look comes from Sidney Paget's PD illustrations — that is safe. See [[11-characters/sherlock-holmes]] `risk_notes`.

## Legal Pre-Flight

Sections this template touches: 08-Color-Palettes, 09-Symbols, 10-Archetypes, 13-Visual-Motifs, 14-Trending-Categories. Before committing to a brand name or mark: run a basic TM spot-check on all name candidates (USPTO.gov for US, EUIPO for EU); verify no logo element imitates a protected trademark; confirm all PD thematic sources are tagged `pd_status: public-domain`; check `risk_notes` on all `caution`-level slots; run [[shared/legal-preflight]]. The Z-name itself carries no inherent legal safety — it is a distinctiveness strategy, not a legal shield.
