# Botanical Illustration × Sacred Geometry

> Nature never improvises — every petal sits on a hidden grid. This remix peels back the antique botanical plate to reveal the spiral, the hexagon, and the golden ratio that built the flower.

## The collision
- **Source A:** Antique botanical illustration — Redoute roses, Curtis's *Botanical Magazine*, Besler's florilegium; meticulous engraved/painted plates of plants. Public domain; pre-1930.
- **Category B:** Sacred and hard geometry — the golden spiral, Fibonacci phyllotaxis, hexagonal grids, mandalas, Platonic solids, fractals. Uncopyrightable mathematics.
- **Tension (the productive clash):** Botanical illustration is soft, organic, irregular, painterly; geometry is rigid, abstract, exact, machinelike. Watercolor petal meets steel compass line.
- **Harmony (the unexpected rhyme):** *Plants are literally built on geometry.* Phyllotaxis follows Fibonacci; sunflower seeds spiral by the golden angle; ferns are fractals; honeycomb and seed-heads are hexagonal. The geometry was always there inside the botany — this remix just draws it back in.

## Concept generation rules
Overlay or fuse a real plant with the exact geometry that governs its growth. Rose→golden spiral. Sunflower→Fibonacci seed spirals. Fern→fractal. Leaf arrangement→hexagonal grid. Flower cross-section→radial mandala / Platonic solid. Use antique plate accuracy + clean construction lines.

## Visual generation rules
- **Palette:** "Botanic Grid" — herbarium cream #F1EAD8, ink line #2B2B2B, sage green #7C8C5A, dusty rose #C98B8B, ochre #C8923A, faint gold-line #BFA15A.
- **Primary motif:** botanical accuracy + visible geometric armature (spirals, hex grids, radial guides) in fine gold/ink line.
- **Composition / layout rules:** centered radial symmetry for mandalas; specimen-plate framing for charts; faint construction lines beneath painterly subjects; generous negative space.
- **Render style cues for AI image prompts:** "antique botanical plate (Redoute/Besler) accuracy overlaid with fine gold sacred-geometry construction lines, golden spiral, hexagonal grid, herbarium cream background, calm and precise, original construction (not a copied existing mandala)."
- **What to avoid visually:** a specific living artist's sacred-geometry artwork; any wellness/yoga brand mandala mark. Generate fresh from the math.
