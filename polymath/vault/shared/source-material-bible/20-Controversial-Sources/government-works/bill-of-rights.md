# Bill of Rights

The first ten amendments to the US Constitution, ratified December 15, 1791. The Bill of Rights is the constitutional foundation of American civil liberties and generates some of the most commercially durable patriotic content in the POD market.

---

## Rights status

**Fully public domain.** All ten amendments are works of the US Federal Government under 17 U.S.C. § 105 and are entirely free to reproduce commercially.

---

## Per-amendment commercial notes

### First Amendment — highest broad-market appeal
"Congress shall make no law respecting an establishment of religion, or prohibiting the free exercise thereof; or abridging the freedom of speech, or of the press; or the right of the people peaceably to assemble, and to petition the Government for a redress of grievances."

- **Buyer communities:** Journalists, academics, civil libertarians, free-speech advocates, across the political spectrum.
- **Design angle:** Full text, "Freedom of Speech" pull-quote, "Freedom of the Press" standalone.
- **Platform risk:** LOW. First Amendment content is universally accepted on major platforms.

### Second Amendment — highest single-niche intensity
"A well regulated Militia, being necessary to the security of a free State, the right of the people to keep and bear Arms, shall not be infringed."

- **Buyer communities:** Gun owners, Second Amendment advocates. This community is large, vocal, and purchase-active.
- **Design angle:** Full text, "Shall Not Be Infringed" standalone, "Right to Keep and Bear Arms" typography.
- **Platform risk:** MEDIUM. Amazon Merch and some other platforms have policies restricting firearms-related content. Even purely typographic Second Amendment products (no images of firearms, no functional weapons-related content) can be caught by automated filters. Review [[political-content/platform-policy-risk|platform-policy-risk]] and check current platform policies before launching a 2nd Amendment product line.
- **Revenue note:** Despite platform complexity, this niche has exceptional conversion rates for operators who successfully navigate it.

### Fourth Amendment — growing niche
"The right of the people to be secure in their persons, houses, papers, and effects, against unreasonable searches and seizures, shall not be violated..."

- **Buyer communities:** Civil libertarians, privacy advocates, technologists, lawyers.
- **Design angle:** "Unreasonable Searches and Seizures" typography; privacy-rights framing.

### Fifth Amendment — legal/rights niche
Includes the right against self-incrimination ("plead the fifth"), due process, and eminent domain protections.

- **Design angle:** "Plead the Fifth" is an extremely well-known phrase with standalone cultural meaning. Verify exact Amendment text for the full passage.

### Other amendments
The remaining amendments (III, VI, VII, VIII, IX, X) have smaller but dedicated buyer communities in legal, civic, and political spheres. A "Complete Bill of Rights" poster is a reliable product — all ten amendments presented as a reference piece.
