# Constellation Maps

## Description
A **constellation map** plots stars as dots connected by thin lines into figures, often over a celestial grid, with optional concentric ecliptic rings, moon phases, and mythic figure overlays. It evokes the cosmos, destiny, and wonder. The motif ranges from a single zodiac constellation to a full antique-style star chart of an entire sky.

## Visual vocabulary
- **Dots and lines:** stars as filled dots (sized by magnitude) joined by fine connecting lines into figures.
- **Celestial grid:** thin radial/concentric coordinate lines (ecliptic, equator) behind the stars.
- **Constellation figures:** the classic line-figures (a dipper, a cross, a "W") or mythic figure overlays (a hunter, a lion) drawn faintly behind the stars.
- **Ring border:** an outer circle with degree/coordinate ticks (antique-chart look).
- **Moon phases:** a row or arc of moon-phase discs.
- **Background:** deep navy/black with scattered fine star-dots and faint nebula wash.

## Where it shines
- **Posters & wall art (best):** "the night you were born" custom star maps — an enormous personalized-gift market.
- **Apparel:** a single zodiac constellation on the chest; whole-sky charts on the back.
- **Children's books:** dreamy bedtime/space themes, endpapers.
- **Merch:** mugs, enamel pins (zodiac sets), phone cases, jewelry cards.
- **Brand:** astrology, wellness, "cosmic" lifestyle identities.
- **Struggles:** very small scale (fine star-grid disappears); bright/high-key contexts (the motif wants a dark ground).

## Pairs with
- **Categories / subjects:** astrology/zodiac, space/astronomy, romance/anniversary, mythology, dreams/bedtime.
- **Palettes:** navy + gold, deep space (black + violet + white), midnight + foil. (link section 11)

## Remix hooks
- **Custom-date star map:** generate the sky for a specific date/location framed in a coordinate ring — flagship personalized POD product (data is free).
- **Zodiac line-figure series:** twelve clean constellation graphics, one per sign — a complete merch collection.
- **Constellation inside a silhouette:** a deer/wolf/face outline filled with a connected star map.
- **Glow-on-navy** for premium foil prints and apparel.
- **Mythic-figure overlay:** the faint engraved figure (lion, archer) behind the connected stars for an antique-chart romance.

## Legal note
Star positions are facts and constellations are ancient — both free; pre-1930 star atlases are PD. Do not trace a *specific* modern star-map product's artistic rendering or template, and never imply a real "star naming registry." Generate the chart from generic celestial data and your own styling.
