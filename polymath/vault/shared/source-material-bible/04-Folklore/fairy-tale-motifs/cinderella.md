# Cinderella

> A mistreated girl, magical help, a transformation that lasts only until midnight, and a lost slipper that proves her identity — the most widely-retold fairy tale on Earth.

## Origin & Tradition
"Cinderella" is not one story but a **tale type** (ATU 510A, "the persecuted heroine"), found in hundreds of cultures. The oldest written form is the Chinese *Ye Xian* (c. 860 CE), where a magic fish's bones grant wishes and a lost golden shoe ends in marriage to a king. Giambattista Basile's Italian *La Gatta Cenerentola* (1634), Charles Perrault's French *Cendrillon* (1697, source of the glass slipper and fairy godmother), and the Brothers Grimm's German *Aschenputtel* (1812, with the hazel tree and helpful birds) are the influential European fixations. All of these source texts are long in the public domain.

## The Story / The Figure
A kind girl is reduced to servitude by a stepmother and stepsisters and made to sleep among the cinders. Magical aid (a fairy godmother, a wishing tree, a fish's bones) gives her finery to attend a royal ball or festival, on the condition that the magic ends at midnight. She enchants the prince but must flee, leaving a single shoe. The prince searches the kingdom; the shoe fits only her; her hidden worth is publicly revealed and rewarded.

## Visual Identity
Free, traditional motifs: a soot-smudged girl by a hearth; a transformation into a gown; a pumpkin turned to a coach; a single lost slipper (glass in the Perrault line, gold in older forms); the stroke of midnight on a clock. **These motifs are public domain.** What is NOT free is any specific modern rendering — above all the Disney 1950 design language.

## Symbolism & Themes
Hidden virtue rewarded; patience under injustice; transformation as the outward sign of inner worth; the fragile, time-limited nature of fortune (midnight). A near-universal "the deserving will rise" fantasy.

## Legal & Cultural Status
Tale type and motifs: **public domain worldwide.** Perrault (1697) and Grimm (1812) source texts: public domain. **Restricted:** Disney's 1950 *Cinderella* (character designs, songs, named mice, castle logo) and any other specific copyrighted film/illustration.
