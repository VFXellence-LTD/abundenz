# Guilloché

> Hypnotically precise interlaced bands and spirograph rosettes produced by engine-turning — the visual language of banknotes, fine watch dials, and Fabergé-era luxury.

## Defining Features
Extremely fine, mathematically-generated curves that interlace, weave, and spiral with machine precision. Two flavors: **band guilloché** (parallel wavy lines plaiting over and under each other in a ribbon) and **rosette guilloché** (spirograph-like radial bursts of overlapping curves). The hallmark is exactness — hairline-thin, perfectly even lines that no hand could draw, producing a shimmering moiré.

## Visual Vocabulary
- **Plaited band** — interweaving sine-wave lines forming a ribbon.
- **Rosette** — radial spirograph burst, often centered in a panel.
- **Basket / wicker lattice** — over-under woven appearance.
- **Security lathe-work** — the dense fine-line field behind text on official documents.
- **Engine-turned ground** — a textured guilloché backing for a watch dial or locket.

## Shapes
Sinusoidal curves, epicycloid/hypocycloid spirograph loops, perfect radial symmetry, interlacing over-under weaves. Everything is thin-line and mathematically regular.

## Textures
Engraved metal (the original engine-turning), intaglio security print, enamel-over-guilloché (guilloché enamel), engraved watch dial. Always hairline crisp; never sketchy.

## Palette(s)
Single fine-line color on a ground: black on cream (banknote), gold on enamel blue/green, silver on black. Two-color intaglio (black + one accent) is classic for "official document" looks.

## Modern Remix
- **Brand / packaging:** instant "premium, secure, official" feel for finance, crypto, luxury, and gift-card aesthetics.
- **POD:** spirograph-rosette art prints, watch-dial graphic tees, "certificate of [x]" novelty posters.
- **AI prompt:** *"guilloché rosette, fine interlacing spirograph lines, gold on deep blue enamel, perfectly symmetric, security-print style."*

## Legal & Risk
The pattern is free. The serious caution is **counterfeiting law**: guilloché is the actual basis of currency and document security printing. Never make something that could pass as real money, a real passport, or a genuine official certificate. Keep novelty/parody use unmistakably fake (wrong denominations, joke text, watermark "NOT LEGAL TENDER").
