# Paisley (Boteh / Buta)

> The curving, hook-tipped teardrop — the Persian *boteh* — densely filled with florals and packed into swirling all-over fields; the soul of the bandana, the Kashmir shawl, and 1960s psychedelia.

## Defining Features
A droplet/comma/mango shape with a curved, tapering tail that hooks at the tip. The boteh is rarely empty: it is filled with flowers, vines, dots, and smaller botehs, then tiled densely so individual shapes interlock into a writhing all-over rhythm. Scale varies from tiny "pine" botehs in a bandana to monumental single botehs. The overall read is dense, organic, and slightly hypnotic.

## Visual Vocabulary
- **Boteh / buta** — the core teardrop unit.
- **Floral fill** — interior packed with stylized blossoms, leaves, seeds.
- **Nested botehs** — small botehs inside or around large ones.
- **Connecting vine** — sinuous stems linking units into a field.
- **Bandana grid** — botehs arranged around a bordered central medallion (the classic neckerchief layout).

## Shapes
Asymmetric teardrop with a hooked tail; densely curvilinear interior. Tessellating, edge-matched for seamless repeat. Strong directional flow — the hooks all curve the same way, creating motion.

## Textures
Woven and printed cloth (the original), block-printed cotton, fine woolen Kashmir shawl, screen-printed cotton bandana. Implies dense, busy linework and layered fills; works in flat color blocks or detailed line.

## Palette(s)
Two distinct families: (1) traditional rich jewel tones — deep red, indigo, ochre, cream, forest green; (2) psychedelic 1960s — hot pink, orange, lime, purple. Also the iconic two-color bandana (red/black-and-white or navy/white).

## Modern Remix
- **POD:** all-over paisley leggings, bandanas, durags, scarves; line-art paisley tees.
- **Mashup:** "paisley made of [skulls / circuitry / sea creatures]" is a strong novelty hook.
- **Poster:** one giant ornate boteh as a centerpiece.
- **AI prompt:** *"seamless paisley boteh pattern, dense floral fill, jewel tones on cream, symmetric repeat, no brand logos."*

## Legal & Risk
The boteh is ancient and free. Two cautions: (1) **cultural respect** — paisley carries deep Persian/Kashmiri heritage; treat it as celebration, not generic "exotica"; (2) **brand** — do not clone a specific living fashion or homeware brand's exact paisley artwork and colorway. Freshly-generated paisley is safe.
