# Peter Pan

## Snapshot

The boy who never grows up, from J. M. Barrie's 1904 play *Peter Pan, or The Boy Who Wouldn't Grow Up* and his 1911 novel *Peter and Wendy*. Peter leads the Lost Boys in Neverland, battles Captain Hook, and whisks the Darling children away on flying adventures.

## Copyright status (the text/expression)

In the **US**, public domain: the 1904 play and the 1911 novel are both pre-1930 works. Peter, Wendy, the Lost Boys, Captain Hook, Tinker Bell, the crocodile, Neverland — all free to use in the US.

In the **UK and life+70 countries**, ordinary copyright expired at the end of **2007** (Barrie died 1937).

## What is RESTRICTED (the modern design / trademark / publicity)

- **The Great Ormond Street Hospital (GOSH) perpetual right — UK only.** This is the famous caveat. Barrie gifted his Peter Pan rights to GOSH, and the UK Parliament (Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, Schedule 6) granted GOSH a **perpetual right to royalties** on UK stage performances, broadcasts, and publications of the work — *in the UK only*, even after copyright otherwise expired. It is a special statutory royalty, not a normal copyright, and does **not** apply in the US or most other countries. A commercial product **distributed or performed in the UK** could owe GOSH royalties; products outside the UK are unaffected.
- **Disney's 1953 animated design.** The Disney Peter Pan — the specific green felt cap with red feather, the green tunic and tights, the elfin Disney face — is a Disney copyright and trademark. Do not copy it. (Disney's Tinker Bell is even more aggressively protected; see [[tinker-bell]].)
- **Barrie's text describes Peter loosely** (a boy clad in "skeleton leaves and the juices that ooze out of trees"), leaving a lot of design freedom — use that, not the Disney look.

## Risk level and why

**Caution**, driven by two non-copyright factors: the UK GOSH royalty (a genuine, enforceable UK obligation) and the ever-present temptation to default to the restricted Disney design. For a primarily US/global digital POD business, Peter Pan is usable; for UK stage/broadcast/publishing, check the GOSH obligation.

## Safe-use guidance

- **Do:** Use Barrie's text and characters freely (US/most countries). Design your own Peter from the "skeleton leaves" description. Use Neverland, the Lost Boys, Captain Hook, the crocodile.
- **Don't:** Copy Disney's 1953 design or the Disney Tinker Bell. For **UK** commercial performances/broadcasts/publications, don't ignore the GOSH royalty right. Don't reproduce live-action actor likenesses.

### Design / POD
"Second star to the right" map/constellation posters, leafy-costume original Peter line art, "To die would be an awfully big adventure" quote products (PD), pirate-ship and crocodile-clock motifs.

### Story / writing
Grown-up-Peter reframes, a Captain Hook origin/villain spinoff, darker Neverland fiction, an audio-drama. All characters and the full story are remixable (mind UK GOSH for UK distribution).

### Other applications
Children's-book retellings with original art, nursery/adventure aesthetics, AI-writing "lost boys" prompt packs, a Z-named original lost-child character for a UK-safe branded line.
