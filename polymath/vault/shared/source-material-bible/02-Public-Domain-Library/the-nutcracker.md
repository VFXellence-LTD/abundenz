# The Nutcracker

> A rare PD entry that gives you both a **story** and a **melody** — perfect for video and holiday merch — provided you grasp the "composition is free, this *recording* is not" rule.

## Overview
*The Nutcracker* is Tchaikovsky's 1892 ballet, based on E.T.A. Hoffmann's 1816 tale *The Nutcracker and the Mouse King* (via Dumas's 1844 French adaptation). On Christmas Eve, young Clara/Marie receives a nutcracker that comes to life, battles the Mouse King, and leads her through a snow kingdom to the Land of Sweets ruled by the Sugar Plum Fairy. The 1892 *score* and the *story* are public domain. **The crucial nuance for music**: the *composition* is free, but any *specific recorded performance* is its own copyright. Use a PD/CC0 recording, license one, or record fresh.

## Themes
- **Childhood wonder and the magic of Christmas** — the festive dream.
- **The dream world** — the line between toy and living prince.
- **Good vs. evil** — nutcracker soldiers vs. the Mouse King.
- **Transformation** — the toy becomes a prince; the ordinary becomes magical.
- **The journey to an enchanted land** — the Land of Sweets.

- **The Sugar Plum Fairy** — sweetness and enchantment.
- **The Mouse King** — the rodent villain.
- **The Land of Sweets / snow kingdom** — confectionery wonder.

## Risks
- **Copyright (composition & story):** none — score (1892) and Hoffmann/Dumas story PD.
- **Recording trap (primary):** a SPECIFIC recorded performance is a separate copyright — use a PD/CC0 recording, license, or record fresh. Do NOT rip a commercial recording.
- **Choreography/design trap:** a particular company's staging, choreography, costumes, and sets are copyrighted.
- **Adaptation trap:** Disney's Fantasia Nutcracker segment and the 2018 Disney film designs are RESTRICTED.
