# The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

> America's first great fairy tale — gloriously public domain in the *book*, and a textbook example of the "the movie is NOT free" trap (the slippers are **silver** in the book).

## Overview
L. Frank Baum's 1900 children's fantasy, illustrated by W.W. Denslow. A Kansas cyclone carries Dorothy and her dog Toto to the land of Oz, where she joins a Scarecrow, a Tin Woodman, and a Cowardly Lion on a journey down the Yellow Brick Road to find a wizard who can send her home. The 1900 book and Denslow's original illustrations are public domain. **The 1939 MGM film is the canonical adaptation trap of the whole Bible**: its ruby slippers, songs, and character designs are all separately and heavily protected.

## Themes
- **The journey home / "there's no place like home"** — displacement and belonging.
- **Found family** — the four companions complete each other.
- **Courage, brains, heart already within** — the wizard grants what they already possess.
- **Appearance vs. reality / the humbug** — the great and powerful Oz is a man behind a curtain.
- **Innocence and wonder** — a pure fairy-tale lens.

## Symbols
- **The Yellow Brick Road** — the path / the quest; a near-universal metaphor.
- **The Emerald City** — the dazzling promised destination (and the deception within).
- **The slippers** — magical agency. **Silver in the book**; ruby only in the film.
- **The cyclone** — sudden transformation, the threshold to the other world.
- **The curtain** — hidden truth behind authority.
- **The poppy field** — seductive sleep / danger disguised as beauty.

## Character archetypes
- **Dorothy** — the Innocent Wanderer (book character PD; *Garland design* restricted).
- **Scarecrow** — the Brainless Seeker (who is in fact clever).
- **Tin Woodman** — the Heartless Seeker (who is in fact tender).
- **Cowardly Lion** — the Cowardly Seeker (who is in fact brave).
- **The Wizard** — the False Authority / humbug.
- **The Wicked Witch of the West** — the Antagonist (book: one-eyed old woman; *green MGM makeup* restricted).

## Visual motifs
- Yellow Brick Road spiraling to a green-spired Emerald City.
- Kansas farmhouse caught in a cyclone; sepia prairie vs. vivid Oz.
- Scarecrow on a pole, jointed tin figure, poppy field, hot-air balloon.

## Reusable concepts
- The **companion quest** — a band of misfits each seeking one missing quality.
- "The thing you seek is already inside you" moral engine.
- Portal fantasy / the ordinary-world-to-magic-world structure.

## Inspirational quotes
> "There is no place like home." — Dorothy, *The Wonderful Wizard of Oz* (1900) — (the BOOK phrasing; the film's "There's no place like home" is fine too as it derives from this PD text, but the SONG is not).

> "Hearts will never be practical until they can be made unbreakable." — the Wizard, *The Wonderful Wizard of Oz* (1900)

(PD 1900 text.)

## Design opportunities
- Yellow Brick Road spiral graphic; Emerald City Art-Nouveau skyline (Denslow's style is PD).
- **Silver** slippers iconography (the safe, distinctive choice).
- Sepia-to-color "Kansas vs. Oz" split posters.

## Story opportunities
- Companion-quest retelling with original Polymath characters (Z-named).
- "The humbug behind the curtain" — satire of hollow authority.
- A child's imaginary-friend journey echoing the four-companion structure.

## POD opportunities
- "There's no place like home" typography (text-derived, not the song).
- Silver-slipper and Yellow-Brick-Road motif prints.
- Denslow-style PD illustration reprints (verify the specific scan is PD).

## Risks
- **Copyright:** book + Denslow illustrations PD. ALL 14 of L. Frank Baum's own Oz books (1900–1920) are now US PD. CAUTION: Ruth Plumly Thompson's continuations are mixed — several (e.g. her 1930–1934 titles) remained under copyright and only entered/are entering PD on a rolling basis through 2030; do NOT assume a post-Baum Oz book is free. Check per title.
- **Adaptation trap (major):** 1939 MGM ruby slippers, songs ("Over the Rainbow"), Garland/Hamilton designs and likenesses, specific Munchkin/Emerald City designs — all RESTRICTED. Render slippers **silver**; witch per the book.
- **Trademark:** "OZ" / "WIZARD OF OZ" have live Warner/Turner trademarks in merchandise classes — avoid implying affiliation.
