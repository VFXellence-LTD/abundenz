# Norse Mythology

> Gods, giants, runes, and the doom of Ragnarök — ancient and free — with one giant flashing sign: **the mythological Thor is yours; Marvel's Thor is absolutely not.**

## Overview
The pre-Christian mythology of Scandinavia, preserved chiefly in the 13th-century *Prose Edda* (Snorri Sturluson) and *Poetic Edda*, plus the Icelandic sagas. It tells of Odin, Thor, Loki, the nine worlds bound by the world-tree Yggdrasil, and the prophesied end, Ragnarök. The underlying myths are ancient folk material with no copyright; the medieval Eddas are public domain. **Two cautions dominate**: modern translations/retellings are copyrighted, and — most importantly — **Marvel's Thor/Loki/Asgard are protected character designs and trademarks**, entirely separate from the free mythology.

## Themes
- **Fate and doom (wyrd)** — even the gods are bound for destruction at Ragnarök.
- **Courage in the face of the end** — heroism precisely *because* doom is certain.
- **Honor, glory, and the warrior's death** — Valhalla, the chosen slain.
- **The cycle of worlds** — destruction and rebirth.
- **Wisdom through sacrifice** — Odin's eye, Odin on the tree to win the runes.
- **The trickster** — Loki as catalyst of both help and ruin.

## Symbols
- **Yggdrasil** — the world-tree binding the nine realms.
- **Mjölnir** — Thor's hammer (mythological symbol = free; Marvel's design = not).
- **The runes** — written magic and wisdom (handle with cultural sensitivity).
- **The Bifröst** — the rainbow bridge to Asgard.
- **Fenrir, Jörmungandr** — the wolf and world-serpent; prophesied destroyers.
- **Odin's ravens (Huginn & Muninn)** — thought and memory.

## Character archetypes
- **Thor** — the Thunder-God / protector (mythological version is PD).
- **Odin** — the Allfather / wisdom-seeker who sacrifices for knowledge.
- **Loki** — the Trickster-God / chaos-bringer.
- **The Valkyries / shieldmaidens** — choosers of the slain.
- **The Jötnar (giants)** — primordial adversaries.

## Visual motifs
- The world-tree; longships; runestones; ravens in flight.
- Frost-and-fire (the realms of Niflheim and Muspelheim); the rainbow bridge.
- Viking warriors; the wolf and the world-serpent.

## Reusable concepts
- The **"heroism in the face of certain doom"** ethos (distinct from Greek hubris).
- The Ragnarök apocalypse-and-rebirth structure.
- The trickster-god as a character engine.

## Inspirational quotes

## Risks
- **Copyright:** myths ancient/free; Eddas PD. **Modern translations/retellings copyrighted** — use PD sources or paraphrase.
- **Trademark/copyright trap (severe):** **Marvel's Thor, Loki, Asgard character designs and names-as-used are protected.** Mythological versions are free; Marvel's are not. Avoid the Marvel helmet/costume designs entirely.
- **Cultural sensitivity:** some Norse runes/symbols have been co-opted by hate groups — use thoughtfully and avoid associations.
