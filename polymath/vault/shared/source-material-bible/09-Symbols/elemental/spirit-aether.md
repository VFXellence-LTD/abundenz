# Spirit / Aether (Fifth Element)

> The quintessence — the divine, immaterial fifth that binds the four.

## Meaning
Spirit (also aether or quintessence — "the fifth essence") is the transcendent, immaterial element beyond the four physical ones. It represents the divine, life-force, unity, the soul, and the binding principle of the cosmos. In the upright [[../sacred-geometry/pentagram]], spirit is the topmost point ruling over fire, water, air, and earth. It is wholeness, source, and the connection between matter and the divine.

## Origin
Aristotle proposed aether as the celestial fifth element. The concept recurs in alchemy (quintessence) and Western esotericism. Glyphs include a plain circle, a circle with a central dot, or an eight-spoked wheel. Ancient and PD.

## Visual description
Most commonly a simple circle, or a circle with a dot at center, or an eight-spoked wheel. As the crown of a pentagram it is the top point. Illustrative versions render it as a glowing aura, a radiant orb, or a swirling vortex of light. The circle form is intentionally minimal.
