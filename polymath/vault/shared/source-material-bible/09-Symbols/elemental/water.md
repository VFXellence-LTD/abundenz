# Water (Classical Element)

> The downward triangle of emotion, flow, and the deep unconscious.

## Meaning
Water is the element of emotion, intuition, the unconscious, flow, adaptability, and cleansing. Cold and wet, feminine and receptive, it nourishes life and dissolves boundaries. It carries connotations of depth, mystery, healing, and sometimes grief or being "overwhelmed." Astrologically it rules the water signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces).

## Origin
Part of the Greek four-element cosmology (Empedocles, Aristotle) and standardized in alchemy as a downward triangle. Water imagery — waves, ripples, drops — is universal and ancient. PD.

## Visual description
A downward-pointing equilateral triangle (▽), the inverse of [[fire]]. Illustrative versions use stacked horizontal wave lines, a single rolling wave (Hokusai-style), ripples, or a triangle filled with water texture. Minimal/tattoo styles favor the clean inverted triangle.

## Emotional tags
Calm, depth, emotion, fluidity, mystery, sorrow, healing, adaptability.
