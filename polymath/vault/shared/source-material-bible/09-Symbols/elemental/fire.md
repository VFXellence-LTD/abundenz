# Fire (Classical Element)

> The upward triangle of passion, energy, and transformation.

## Meaning
Fire is the element of energy, passion, will, drive, destruction, and transformation/purification. In the four-element system (fire, water, air, earth — plus a fifth, aether/spirit) it is hot and dry, masculine and active. It can warm and create or burn and destroy; it is the agent of change that turns raw into refined. Astrologically it rules the fire signs (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius).

## Origin
The four-element cosmology was systematized by the pre-Socratic Greeks (Empedocles) and Aristotle, though similar element schemes appear across cultures. The elemental triangle glyphs — fire as an upward triangle — were standardized in alchemy and persist in occult and design use. Ancient and PD.

## Visual description
An upward-pointing equilateral triangle (△). Alchemical fire is the plain upward triangle. Often paired in sets with the other elements. Illustrative versions replace the bare triangle with a stylized flame or a triangle filled with flame textures. Minimal line-art and tattoo styles favor the clean geometric triangle.
