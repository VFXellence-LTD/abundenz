# Sulfur (Alchemical)

> The fiery soul — one of alchemy's three primes.

## Meaning
Sulfur is one of the **tria prima** (three primes) of alchemy — alongside [[alchemy-mercury]] and [[alchemy-salt]] — representing the soul, the combustible/active masculine principle, expansiveness, and the force that animates and transforms. It is associated with fire, the sun, and the volatile energy that drives the Great Work. Symbolically it is passion, will, and the "burning" aspect of spirit.

## Origin
Alchemical symbolism developed across Hellenistic Egypt, the Islamic Golden Age (Jabir ibn Hayyan), and medieval/Renaissance Europe. The standardized glyphs date to this tradition and are ancient/PD. Specific modern grimoire artworks are their own works.

## Visual description
A triangle (point up) sitting atop an equal-armed cross — like the [[../elemental/fire]] triangle balanced on a plus sign. A common variant places the cross above a doubled-loop. Drawn as a clean occult line glyph; works in gold-foil-on-black "grimoire" styling.
