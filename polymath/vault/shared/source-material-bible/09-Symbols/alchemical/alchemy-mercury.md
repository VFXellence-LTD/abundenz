# Mercury (Alchemical / Planetary)

> Quicksilver — the fluid spirit and mind, second of the three primes.

## Meaning
Mercury is one of the **tria prima** (with [[alchemy-sulfur]] and [[alchemy-salt]]), representing the spirit/mind, fluidity, volatility, communication, and the mediating principle that connects body and soul. As quicksilver (the metal) it embodies that which is liquid yet metallic — the "spirit made visible." It is also the planetary glyph for Mercury and the god/planet of messengers, intellect, commerce, and trickery. Astrologically it rules Gemini and Virgo.

## Origin
Greco-Roman planetary symbolism merged with alchemy; the glyph is ancient and PD. The same symbol is used in astronomy/astrology for the planet Mercury and is closely related to the modern "female" biological sign (Venus's glyph). Specific modern artworks are their own.

## Visual description
A circle with a small upturned crescent (horns) on top and an equal-armed cross beneath — read top to bottom as crescent / circle / cross. The horned-crescent distinguishes it from Venus (♀, which lacks the crescent). Clean occult/planetary line glyph.
