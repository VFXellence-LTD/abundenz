# Peace Sign (CND Symbol)

> The free-for-all emblem of peace — deliberately never copyrighted.

## Meaning
The peace sign signifies peace, anti-war sentiment, nuclear disarmament, unity, and idealism. Designed in 1958 for the British Campaign for Nuclear Disarmament (CND), it became the global emblem of the 1960s–70s peace and counterculture movements and remains an instantly recognizable symbol of hope and solidarity.

## Origin
Created by Gerald Holtom in 1958 for the CND. Its lines combine the semaphore signals for "N" and "D" (Nuclear Disarmament) within a circle (also read as a stylized figure with arms downturned, or the world). Crucially, **Holtom and the CND deliberately chose never to copyright or trademark it**, so that anyone, anywhere could use it freely — making it one of the clearest, most intentionally public-domain modern symbols.

## Visual description
A circle enclosing a vertical line that branches into two lines angling downward and outward from the center (like an upside-down "Y" with a vertical stem, inside a circle). Clean and simple. Render in 60s/70s retro, floral fill, tie-dye, or bold minimalist styles.
