# Ichthys (Jesus Fish)

> The early Christians' secret sign — a simple fish of faith and fellowship. Treat with respect.

## Meaning
The Ichthys (Greek for "fish") is an early Christian symbol — a simple fish outline. "ΙΧΘΥΣ" was read as an acronym for "Jesus Christ, God's Son, Savior" (Iesous Christos Theou Yios Soter). It signifies Christian faith, discipleship (Jesus called his followers "fishers of men"), and fellowship. Tradition holds it was a covert sign used by persecuted early Christians to identify one another. Today it is a popular emblem of Christian identity.

## Origin
Dates to the 1st–2nd centuries CE among early Christians in the Roman Empire, found in catacomb art and inscriptions. Fully PD. It remains a **living Christian symbol** in active use.

## Visual description
Two intersecting arcs that cross at the left and extend past each other on the right to form a simple fish profile (a pointed head and a tail of two crossing lines). Minimal and instantly recognizable. Render as a clean single-line fish. Note: the parody "Darwin fish" (with legs) is a distinct anti-creationist symbol — do not mix it onto a faith product.
