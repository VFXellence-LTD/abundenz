# Recycling Symbol (Chasing Arrows)

> The Mobius loop of three chasing arrows — the free, universal emblem of sustainability.

## Meaning
The universal recycling symbol signifies recycling, sustainability, renewal, reuse, environmental responsibility, and the circular flow of materials. The three arrows folded into a Möbius loop represent the cycle: collection, processing, and reuse — an endless loop with no waste. It has become a global shorthand for eco-consciousness.

## Origin
Designed in 1970 by Gary Anderson, then a college student, for a contest sponsored by the Container Corporation of America during the first Earth Day era. The company **did not retain exclusive rights**, effectively placing the generic Möbius design in the public domain and free for universal use. (Note: certain *specific* eco-certification marks built around recycling are separately trademarked — see risk.)

## Visual description
Three broad arrows, each bent at two points, chasing one another around a triangle and twisted into a Möbius loop (one arrow appears to fold over). Render filled (solid arrows) or outlined, in green/earth tones, or with leaf-textured arrows.

## Emotional tags
Responsibility, optimism, freshness, eco-consciousness, renewal, the cyclical.

- **Posters**: environmental/educational art, eco-messaging.
- **Children's books**: environmental education, "where does it go?" themes.
- **Story / AI writing**: environmentalism, circular economy, renewal.
- **Branding**: sustainability, eco-products (use the generic loop, not a protected eco-certification mark).

## Remix hooks
Eco-branding, earth-tone styling, leaf-fill arrows, sustainability messaging, upcycle merch. Conceptual crossover with [[../mathematical/infinity]] (the endless loop).

## Legal / risk
Copyright: the generic Möbius design is **PD** (not held as exclusive property). Trademark: **specific certified eco-labels (e.g. the Green Dot / Der Grüne Punkt) ARE trademarked** — do not use those; use only the plain generic loop. Bottom line: **safe for the generic chasing-arrows; avoid protected eco-certification marks.**
