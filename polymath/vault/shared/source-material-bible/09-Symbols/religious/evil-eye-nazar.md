# Evil Eye / Nazar

> The blue eye-bead that reflects malevolence back — an ancient charm of protection.

## Meaning
The nazar (commonly "evil eye" amulet) is an apotropaic charm believed to protect the wearer from the "evil eye" — a malevolent glare, often born of envy, thought to bring misfortune. By displaying an eye, the amulet reflects the harmful gaze back, neutralizing it. It signifies protection, watchfulness, and good fortune. It is one of the most widespread folk-protection symbols in the world.

## Origin
Ancient, with roots across the Mediterranean, Middle East, and Aegean (Greek/Turkish *nazar boncuğu*), dating back thousands of years. Fully PD. Because it is a **folk belief** rather than a doctrinal symbol of any single religion, it is among the more freely usable "spiritual" symbols — but it remains rooted in living cultures, so frame respectfully.

## Visual description
Concentric circles forming a stylized eye: an outer dark-blue ring, then light/turquoise blue, then white, with a small black pupil at center. The classic glass "evil eye bead" is round and domed. Render as a gradient-blue bead, a minimalist eye icon, or boho jewelry.
