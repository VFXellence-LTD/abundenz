# Scorpio (The Scorpion)

> The fixed water sign — intense, transformative, master of the hidden.

## Meaning
Scorpio (October 23 – November 21) is a fixed water sign ruled by Mars (and modern Pluto), symbolized by the Scorpion (with the Eagle and Phoenix as higher totems). It signifies intensity, transformation, mystery, passion, power, secrecy, and death-rebirth. Keywords: magnetic, probing, passionate, secretive, resilient.

## Origin
Babylonian/Greek zodiac; tied to the scorpion that killed Orion in myth. The glyph (an "M" with an arrow-tipped tail) is ancient and PD. Modern illustration sets are their own.

## Visual description
The glyph resembles an "M" whose final stroke turns into an arrow pointing up-and-out (the scorpion's stinger) — distinguishing it from Virgo's inward loop. Illustrative versions: a scorpion with raised stinger, or the "higher" eagle/phoenix totem, or the constellation (with the red star Antares).
