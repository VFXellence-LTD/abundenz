# Taurus (The Bull)

> The fixed earth sign — steadfast, sensual, and unshakeable.

## Meaning
Taurus (April 20 – May 20) is a fixed earth sign ruled by Venus, symbolized by the Bull. It signifies stability, persistence, sensuality, loyalty, material comfort, and patience. Keywords: steadfast, indulgent, reliable, stubborn, grounded.

## Origin
Babylonian/Greek zodiac. The glyph (a circle topped by upturned horns, a stylized bull's head) is ancient and PD. Modern illustration sets are their own works.

## Visual description
A circle with two upward-curving horns on top (bull's head). Illustrative versions: a bull's head in profile, a standing/charging bull, or the constellation (with the Pleiades and the bright star Aldebaran). Minimal-line and blackwork styles common.

## Emotional tags
Calm, steadiness, indulgence, stubbornness, security, sensual comfort.
