# Sri Yantra

> The cosmic diagram of nine interlocking triangles — a living Hindu sacred geometry. Treat with respect.

## Meaning
The Sri Yantra (Sri Chakra) is a sacred Hindu/Tantric diagram of nine interlocking triangles radiating from a central point (the bindu), representing the union of the divine masculine (Shiva, four upward triangles) and divine feminine (Shakti, five downward triangles), the cosmos, manifestation, abundance, and the path to spiritual realization. It is an object of worship and meditation, not merely decoration.

## Origin
An ancient Tantric and Sri Vidya tradition diagram from India, used for centuries in Hindu worship and meditation. The geometry is ancient and PD, but it is a **living sacred symbol** in active religious use.

## Visual description
Nine interlocking triangles (four pointing up, five down) overlapping to form 43 smaller triangles around a central bindu (dot), enclosed by two rings of [[../botanical/lotus]] petals (8 and 16), framed by a square "bhupura" with four T-shaped gates at the cardinal directions. Traditionally drawn with great precision; rendered in gold line for devotional contexts.
