# Flower of Life

> Overlapping circles in perfect hexagonal symmetry — the geometric blueprint of creation.

## Meaning
The Flower of Life is a figure of multiple evenly spaced, overlapping circles forming a flower-like hexagonal pattern. In sacred-geometry traditions it represents creation, the interconnection of all life, cosmic order, unity, and the underlying blueprint from which forms emerge (it contains the Seed of Life, the Tree of Life, and can generate Metatron's Cube). It is widely used as a meditative and decorative emblem of harmony.

## Origin
Overlapping-circle grids appear at ancient sites including the Temple of Osiris at Abydos, Egypt, and across Mesopotamian, Phoenician, Assyrian, Indian, and Asian decorative art. The pattern is ancient and PD. Its popular "sacred geometry" branding is a modern interpretive framework, not an intellectual-property claim — the geometry itself is free.

## Visual description
A central circle surrounded by six circles of equal radius (each centered on the first's circumference), expanded outward in rings, all overlapping, bounded by a larger circle. The result is a lattice of vesica-piscis lens shapes arranged with six-fold symmetry. Render as fine gold line on black, laser-cut geometry, embossed, or as a faint background lattice.
