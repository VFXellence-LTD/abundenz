# Seed of Life

> Seven circles in hexagonal bloom — the geometric seed of creation.

## Meaning
The Seed of Life is the seven-circle core of the [[flower-of-life]]: one central circle ringed by six. It symbolizes genesis, creation, fertility, beginnings, and pure potential — often mapped to the "seven days of creation" and the foundational stages of growth. It is the seed from which the larger Flower of Life pattern unfolds.

## Origin
A subset of the ancient overlapping-circles grid; the seven-circle rosette appears in ancient and medieval decorative art worldwide. Ancient and PD. The "sacred geometry" framing is a modern interpretive layer, not an IP claim.

## Visual description
One central circle with six circles of equal radius arranged around it, each centered on the central circle's circumference, producing a six-petaled rosette inside a hexagonal envelope. Often drawn as fine line work, gold-on-black, or embossed.
