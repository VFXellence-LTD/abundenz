# Vesica Piscis

> The almond of overlapping circles — union, creation, and the sacred portal.

## Meaning
The Vesica Piscis ("bladder of the fish") is the almond/lens shape formed where two equal circles overlap, each passing through the other's center. It symbolizes union, the meeting of two worlds (heaven and earth, divine and human), creation, fertility (the womb/yoni), and the threshold or portal between states. In Christian art the enlarged form (the mandorla) frames Christ and the Virgin in glory; the [[../religious/ichthys]] derives from it.

## Origin
A foundational figure of classical and Pythagorean geometry, and a building block of the [[flower-of-life]]. Used in Gothic architecture, Christian iconography (the mandorla), and esoteric design. Ancient and PD.

## Visual description
Two circles of equal radius overlapping so each passes through the other's center; the shared overlap is a pointed almond (lens/vesica). The mandorla is the same shape enlarged as a frame. Render as a clean two-circle construction, a glowing portal, or a stained-glass mandorla frame.
