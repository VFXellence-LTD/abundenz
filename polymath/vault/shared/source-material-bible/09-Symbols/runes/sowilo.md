# Sowilo (Elder Futhark Rune)

> The PD sun-rune of victory — but its DOUBLED form is the banned SS symbol. Handle with extreme care.

## Meaning
Sowilo (ᛋ, "s") means "sun." It signifies the sun, success, victory, energy, life-force, guidance, and wholeness — a wholly positive rune in its original Norse context, akin to the [[../celestial/sun]]. **However**, the Nazi SS adopted a stylized doubled Sowilo (the "Sig" / SS bolts ⚡⚡) as their insignia, making the doubled lightning-bolt form a recognized and (in Germany/Austria) illegal hate symbol.

## Origin
A core Elder Futhark rune (c. 150–800 CE), ancient and PD in its single original form. The Nazi appropriation of the doubled form is a 20th-century corruption documented in the ADL Hate Symbols Database (see [[../_restricted/ss-runes-warning]]). The original single rune is not itself a hate symbol, but proximity and doubling create serious risk.

## Visual description
A single angular zigzag/lightning-bolt stroke (like a stylized "S" made of straight segments). **Never draw two side by side** — that produces the SS bolts. Even a single bolt in heavy militaristic-Germanic styling can read wrong.

## Emotional tags
Triumph, vitality, clarity, success, energy — undermined entirely by the hate-symbol risk.

## Design & story applications
- **Use sparingly and only as a single rune** within a full, clearly cultural-historical Futhark context.
- **Never doubled, never in a militaristic, Germanic-nationalist, or quasi-military-insignia context.**
- For "sun" and "victory" themes, prefer the unambiguous [[../celestial/sun]] or other runes instead.

## Remix hooks
Single sun-rune only, in a neutral folk/Norse context. Otherwise prefer the [[../celestial/sun]]. There is no safe "doubled" or militaristic remix.

## Legal / risk
Copyright: single rune PD. Cultural/legal: **the doubled form = SS bolts = banned hate symbol** (illegal in some countries; offensive everywhere). Bottom line: **avoid the doubled/SS form entirely; use the single rune only with great care, never militaristic.**
