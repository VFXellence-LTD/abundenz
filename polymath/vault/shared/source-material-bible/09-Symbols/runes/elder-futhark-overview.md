# Elder Futhark — Overview & Full Alphabet

> The 24 ancient Norse/Germanic runes — beautiful, PD, and the most-appropriated symbol set by hate groups. Screen everything.

## Meaning
The Elder Futhark is the oldest runic alphabet (named for its first six runes: F-U-Þ-A-R-K), used by Germanic-speaking peoples. Beyond writing, the runes carried magical and divinatory meaning, each tied to a concept of fate, nature, or the gods. The 24 runes are traditionally divided into three groups of eight called *aettir* (Freyr's, Heimdall's/Hagal's, and Tyr's aett).

## The 24 runes (name — sound — core meaning)
**Freyr's aett:** Fehu (f, wealth) · Uruz (u, wild ox/strength) · Thurisaz (th, thorn/giant) · Ansuz (a, god/wisdom) · Raidho (r, journey) · Kenaz (k, torch/knowledge) · Gebo (g, gift) · Wunjo (w, joy).
**Heimdall's aett:** Hagalaz (h, hail/disruption) · Nauthiz (n, need) · Isa (i, ice/stillness) · Jera (j, harvest/year) · Eihwaz (ï, yew/endurance) · Perthro (p, lot-cup/mystery) · Algiz (z, protection/elk) · Sowilo (s, sun/victory).
**Tyr's aett:** Tiwaz (t, the god Tyr/justice) · Berkano (b, birch/growth) · Ehwaz (e, horse/partnership) · Mannaz (m, humankind) · Laguz (l, water/flow) · Ingwaz (ng, the god Ing/potential) · Dagaz (d, day/breakthrough) · Othala (o, heritage/homeland).

## Origin
Inscriptions date c. 150–800 CE on stones, weapons, jewelry, and amulets across Scandinavia and Germanic Europe. Entirely ancient and PD.

## Visual description
Angular, straight-line glyphs (no curves) designed to be carved into wood and stone. They make a striking, ancient-looking display typeface and tattoo style. The full row of 24 reads as a powerful decorative band.

## CRITICAL hate-appropriation screening
The Elder Futhark is **the single most appropriated symbol system by white-nationalist and neo-Nazi movements.** Before using runic imagery commercially, screen against the [ADL Hate Symbols Database](https://www.adl.org/resources/hate-symbols/search). High-risk runes:
- **Sowilo doubled** = the SS "Sig" bolts — **banned hate symbol, never use** (see [[../_restricted/ss-runes-warning]]).
- **Algiz** = Nazi "Lebensrune/Todesrune" — **avoid standalone/inverted** (see [[algiz]]).
- **Tiwaz/Tyr** and **Othala** — used by extremist groups; avoid standalone, especially in militaristic styling.
- The **Wolfsangel** and **Odal with serifs** are also flagged.
Frame all Norse imagery as cultural-historical (Viking heritage, Norse mythology, fantasy worldbuilding), never as coded extremism, and avoid militaristic/Germanic-nationalist aesthetics.
