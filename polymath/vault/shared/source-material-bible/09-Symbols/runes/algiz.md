# Algiz (Elder Futhark Rune)

> The PD protection/elk rune — but appropriated as the Nazi "life rune." Handle with extreme care.

## Meaning
Algiz (ᛉ, "z") means "elk" (or "protection"). It signifies protection, defense, the warding shield, connection to the divine/higher self, and sanctuary. In rune-lore it is one of the strongest protective runes. **However**, the Nazis and later white-nationalist groups adopted it as the "Lebensrune" ("life rune," upright) and "Todesrune" ("death rune," inverted), and it remains a flagged hate-adjacent symbol in the ADL database.

## Origin
A core Elder Futhark rune (c. 150–800 CE), ancient and PD. The 20th-century völkisch/Nazi appropriation (life-rune/death-rune) is the source of the risk (see [[../_restricted/runes-hate-appropriation-warning]]). The original rune is not inherently a hate symbol, but the appropriation is widely recognized.

## Visual description
A vertical stave with two arms branching upward and outward from the top, like elk antlers or a splayed hand (Y-on-a-stick). The inverted form (arms pointing down) is the "death rune" — **never use the inverted form.** Render only within a clearly cultural-historical Futhark row.

## Emotional tags
Protection, safety, aspiration, resilience, the sacred — heavily compromised by the hate association.

## Design & story applications
- **Use only within a full, clearly cultural-historical Elder Futhark set**, never standalone.
- **Never inverted. Never in Germanic-nationalist, völkisch, or militaristic styling.**
- For "protection" themes, prefer an unambiguous symbol (e.g. a [[../heraldic/shield]], [[../nautical/anchor]], or [[../religious/hamsa]] with appropriate respect) instead of Algiz.

## Remix hooks
Only as part of a full Futhark alphabet set in a neutral, scholarly/folk context. Prefer other protection symbols for standalone use.

## Legal / risk
