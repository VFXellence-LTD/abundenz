# Fehu (Elder Futhark Rune)

> The rune of cattle, wealth, and mobile prosperity — first letter of the Futhark.

## Meaning
Fehu (ᚠ, "f") is the first rune of the Elder Futhark. Its name means "cattle" — and by extension wealth, livestock, movable property, abundance, and prosperity. In rune-lore it speaks of earned wealth, ambition, new financial beginnings, and the energy that must keep circulating to stay alive (like a herd). It is broadly an auspicious, abundance-oriented rune.

## Origin
The Elder Futhark is the oldest runic alphabet (c. 150–800 CE), used by Germanic peoples and recorded on stones, weapons, and artifacts. It is ancient and fully PD. **Important caution**: Norse/runic imagery has been appropriated by white-nationalist and neo-Nazi groups, and certain specific runes are flagged hate symbols (see [[../_restricted/runes-hate-appropriation-warning]] and the ADL database). Fehu is low-risk individually, but the overall aesthetic must be framed historically/culturally and screened against hate associations.

A vertical stave with two short arms angling upward to the right from its upper portion (like an "F" with slanted arms). Render as carved stone, woodburn, clean runic typography, or part of a full Futhark row.
