# Raidho (Elder Futhark Rune)

> The rune of the journey — travel, rhythm, and right movement along the path.

## Meaning
Raidho (ᚱ, "r") means "ride" / "journey" / "wagon." It signifies travel, movement, rhythm and order, the right path, progress, and taking action in alignment with cosmic order (sometimes "right action," doing the right thing at the right time). It is the rune of the traveler and of momentum with purpose.

## Origin
A core Elder Futhark rune (c. 150–800 CE), ancient and PD. Screen broader Norse projects against extremist appropriation (see [[../_restricted/runes-hate-appropriation-warning]]); Raidho individually is low-risk.

## Visual description
A vertical stave with an upper loop and a lower diagonal leg — closely resembling the Latin letter "R." Render as carved stone, woodburn, runic typography, or part of a full Futhark row.
