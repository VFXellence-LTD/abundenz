# Crescent Moon

> The emblem of cycles, intuition, and the quiet mystery of night.

## Meaning
The moon symbolizes cycles, change, intuition, the subconscious, the feminine and receptive principle (counterpart to the [[sun]]), time, tides, and transformation. Its waxing and waning map onto birth-death-rebirth, growth and release. The crescent specifically evokes new beginnings (waxing) or letting go (waning). It is a near-universal night and dream symbol.

## Origin
Lunar imagery is ancient and unauthored, found in Mesopotamian, Egyptian (Khonsu, Thoth), Greco-Roman (Selene, Luna, Diana), and countless other traditions. The crescent became a powerful religious and political emblem in the Islamic world and across the Ottoman sphere; the **star-and-crescent** is now strongly associated with Islam and appears on many national flags (see [[../religious/star-and-crescent]]). The plain crescent is PD and unowned.

## Visual description
A thin sliver (waxing or waning), a fat gibbous, or a full disc. The iconic "logo crescent" is a bold C-shape with pointed horns. Common variants: crescent cradling a star between its horns; a row of phases (new → full → new); a crescent with a sleeping face; a crescent with closed eyes and stars. Horns point left (waning) or right (waxing) in the northern hemisphere.

## Emotional tags
Calm, mystery, dreaminess, intuition, serenity, melancholy, romance, the uncanny. A blood moon flips to ominous and supernatural.
