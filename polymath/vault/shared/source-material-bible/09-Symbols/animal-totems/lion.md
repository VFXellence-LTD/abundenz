# Lion (Totem)

> The king of beasts — courage, royalty, and radiant strength.

## Meaning
The lion symbolizes courage, royalty, strength, pride, and leadership — "the king of beasts" and a near-universal emblem of solar power and majesty. Associated with the sun, kingship, and divine authority across cultures (the Lion of Judah, the Sphinx, Sekhmet, Narasimha), it conveys fearless nobility and protective might. The lioness adds connotations of fierce maternal protection and the true hunter of the pride.

## Origin
A universal symbol across Egyptian, Mesopotamian, African, biblical, and European traditions. Fully PD. **Caution**: avoid copying a specific live lion logo (sports teams, film studios, brands) or a nation's heraldic arms. For the heraldic posture, see [[../heraldic/lion-rampant]]. Design your own lion.

## Visual description
Common forms: a maned head facing forward, a roaring profile, a regal seated pose, or a geometric/low-poly mane. The lioness is maneless and sleek. Render in blackwork, geometric, watercolor, regal-crested, or realistic styles.

Courage, power, majesty, pride, confidence, nobility.
