# Ram (Totem)

> The charging horned force of drive, leadership, and bold new beginnings.

## Meaning
The ram symbolizes drive, determination, leadership, virility, and forceful new beginnings — it charges headfirst at obstacles. Its great curling horns make it an emblem of power and dominance. In myth it carries the Golden Fleece and is the zodiac's first sign ([[../astrological/aries]]); in religious tradition the ram is also a sacrificial animal (Abraham's ram). It pairs raw force with pioneering leadership.

## Origin
A universal symbol across Egyptian (Khnum, Amun), Greek (the Golden Fleece, Aries), and Abrahamic traditions. Fully PD. Avoid copying a live brand/sports ram or a franchise design. Design your own.

## Visual description
A ram's head with large, spiraling curled horns, shown frontal or in profile, or a charging full body. The horned skull is a popular rugged/occult motif. Render in geometric, blackwork, rugged-line, or heraldic styles.
