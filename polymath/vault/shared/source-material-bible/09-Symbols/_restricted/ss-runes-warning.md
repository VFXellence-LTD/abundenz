# SS Bolts (Doubled Sowilo) — DO NOT USE

> One Sowilo is the ancient sun-rune. Two side by side are the banned Nazi SS bolts.

## What it is

The SS symbol derives from the **Sowilo ("sun") rune** of the pre-Roman runic alphabet. Because Sowilo resembles a lightning bolt, the doubled form (⚡⚡, the "Sig runes") became the insignia of the Nazi *Schutzstaffel* (SS). After WWII it was adopted by neo-Nazis and white supremacists worldwide (ADL Hate Symbols Database). It is **illegal to display in Germany and Austria** and is offensive everywhere.

## The line

- **Single Sowilo** — ancient, PD; use only with care, never doubled, never militaristic (see [[../runes/sowilo]]).
- **Doubled Sowilo / SS bolts** — **banned hate symbol. Never use, in any styling, ever.**

## Doctrine

Never pair two Sowilo runes or two lightning bolts. Recognize and reject the doubled-bolt form. When designing any Norse/runic project, ensure no layout accidentally produces the SS bolts, and screen against the ADL database.

## Bottom line

**Recognize and reject. The doubled bolt is a banned hate symbol — never deploy.**
