# Little Wild Ones

**Resulting concept:** A warm cover scene: a smiling **fox cub** sits in soft grass with an **owl**, a **bunny**, and a little **bear**, a gentle **sun** rising behind a rounded hill. Everything in soft pastel blocks with simple line detail. The title — "Little Wild Ones" — sits big across the top in a friendly rounded face, with a tiny original rhyme beneath. Calm, cheerful, and entirely original (no character lookalikes).

## Target product

- **Surface:** **board-book / picture-book cover**; matching nursery poster.
- **Print method / color space:** CMYK offset/digital at 300dpi; pastels reproduce reliably.
- **Layout & technical notes:** title safe-zone top third, author/subtitle bottom; leave spine allowance; keep faces simple and friendly; high contrast between title and background for legibility.

## Legal guardrails

**Never** let an animal resemble a known children's character or imitate a specific illustrator's signature style; use original or verified-PD text and non-trademarked fonts.

- [[line-art-animals]] — generic friendly real species; no character lookalikes (Peppa, Disney-Pooh, Carle, Boynton, Pokémon).
- [[dreamz-pastel]] — colors unownable.
- Text — original rhyme or verified PD verse, exact attribution; no copyrighted modern children's text.
