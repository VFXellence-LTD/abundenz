**Resulting concept:** A symmetrical **luna-style moth** sits wings-open at center, a thin **crescent moon** behind it. Hand-drawn **ferns and wildflowers** arc up either side into a loose frame. Everything is fine green linework — forest `#1E3D2F` down to sage `#A9C99B` — on a cream/natural ground. A short verified nature quote curls below in warm hand-script. Calm, organic, gift-shop-ready.

## Target product

- **Surface:** natural cotton **tote bag** (canvas); also sticker sheet and folded greeting card.
- **Print method / color space:** screen-print (1–2 greens) on canvas; CMYK for cards/stickers. Greens are CMYK-safe here.
- **Layout & technical notes:** tote print ~10" wide, centered high on the bag; keep linework crisp (≥2px). Card: emblem top, quote bottom, blank interior. Sticker: die-cut to the moth-and-foliage silhouette.
