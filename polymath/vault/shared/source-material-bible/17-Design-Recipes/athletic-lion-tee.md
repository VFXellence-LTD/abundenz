**Resulting concept:** A bold, faceted **lion head** faces forward, mane radiating, set against a high-contrast **sunburst**. Beneath, a verified courage line ("Cowards die many times…" — Shakespeare, PD) in heavy condensed caps. Maximum contrast so it punches across a gym. Equally strong as a portrait poster.

## Target product

- **Surface:** performance/athletic tee (black or bold color); poster.
- **Print method / color space:** screen or DTF; high-contrast handles any garment. CMYK for poster.
- **Layout & technical notes:** centered, large (~12" on tee); keep the silhouette bold and readable from 3 metres; quote in a tight band beneath.

## Target category

- **Niche:** [[14-Trending-Categories/sports]] (fitness/motivation sub-niche).

## Legal guardrails

**Never** render the lion as a team/studio mascot, and **never** ship an unverified or misattributed quote.

- [[line-art-animals]] — generic real lion; no team/Simba/MGM lion.
- [[courage]] — verified PD lines only; avoid the Mandela/Twain misattributions; attribute exactly.
- [[streetwize-contrast]] — colors unownable; marks original.
