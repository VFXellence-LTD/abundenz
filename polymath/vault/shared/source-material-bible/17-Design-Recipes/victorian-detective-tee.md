**Resulting concept:** A clean emblem stacks a **deerstalker cap**, a crossed **calabash pipe** and **magnifying glass**, over a faint **gaslit-fog London skyline** silhouette. Ink-black on cream (or warm sepia). Beneath, a public-domain detective line in tight Victorian slab serif, attributed to the source story — not used as a brand. The product is sold under the original Z-name **"Deducz"**, keeping the trademarked character name out of the branding.

## Target product

- **Surface:** tee (heather grey / cream garment); also poster.
- **Print method / color space:** CMYK DTG or 1-color screen (it's essentially monochrome).
- **Layout & technical notes:** centered emblem ~10" on tee; fog skyline kept light so the icons pop; attribution line small but legible.

## Target category

- **Niche:** mystery/detective fandom (via [[14-Trending-Categories/mythology]] literary-genre adjacency).
- **Adjacent niches:** [[14-Trending-Categories/retro-technology]] (steampunk crossover), bookish/literary apparel.

## Legal guardrails

**Never** use "Sherlock Holmes" as a product brand/trademark, imply Conan Doyle Estate endorsement, copy a modern BBC/film design, or render a specific actor's likeness. Use period iconography + PD text + an original Z-name.
