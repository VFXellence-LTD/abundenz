# skibidi / skibidi toilet

**Meaning/usage:** "Skibidi Toilet" is a copyrighted YouTube animated series by **DaFuq!?Boom!** (Alexey Gerasimov), featuring toilet-headed characters battling "Cameramen." Massively popular with Gen Alpha. "Skibidi" alone became a nonsense intensifier.

**Three-force assessment:**
- **Trend:** Declining from its 2023-2024 peak, but still recognized.
- **Legal:** **GATE STOPS HERE.** This is the textbook **copyrighted-baggage trap.** The series, its characters, and its art are copyrighted. There is **active IP-ownership litigation** (Invisible Narratives v. Next Level) over trademark/DMCA control of the franchise, and DMCA takedowns have been fired in connection with it. Any design using skibidi-toilet characters/art is infringement; even the word is entangled in disputed trademark filings. → `trademark_status: disputed`, `legal_verdict: avoid`.
- **Ethical:** The kid-meme itself isn't hateful/sexual; passes the 12-yo test. But the legal force already kills it.

**POD guidance:** **Do not produce.** Worked example of the copyrighted-baggage trap: the bare word "skibidi" might survive in isolation, but the value comes entirely from the protected franchise, and the IP is in active litigation. Hard `NO`. Defer to [[../../01-Legal-Guidelines/README]].
