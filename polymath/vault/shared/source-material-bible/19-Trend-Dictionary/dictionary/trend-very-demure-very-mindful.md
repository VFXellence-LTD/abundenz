# very demure, very mindful

**Meaning/usage:** Jools Lebron's August 2024 viral catchphrase ("see how I do my makeup for work? Very demure, very mindful"). Exploded, then faded fast — a textbook short-half-life trend.

**Three-force assessment:**
- **Trend:** **Dead.** Peaked and crashed within weeks in late 2024. Fails the freshness side of the 12-yo test now (`twelve_yo_test: fail`).
- **Legal:** **Disputed trademark + publicity risk.** Multiple third parties (Jefferson Bates, others) filed competing trademark applications on the phrase; Lebron herself had not pre-filed and had to fight for it. It's her **signature catchphrase** (publicity risk) *and* a contested-ownership trademark mess. → `trademark_status: disputed`, `legal_verdict: avoid`.
- **Ethical:** Not hateful/sexual, but the dead-trend status fails the 12-yo test. → `caution` ethically, but moot.
