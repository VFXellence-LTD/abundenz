## Skeptical lens (read this before joining)

1. **Is "AI art seller" a real niche or a meta-niche?** Most successful "sell AI art" communities make their money from teaching people to sell AI art, not from selling AI art. Check whether the founder's actual storefront revenue is verifiable.
2. **Does it conflict with Operation Signal's core thesis?** The plan in §4.3 is explicit: real voice is the moat. Selling commodity AI art is the opposite trade — pure volume, zero differentiation, race-to-the-bottom pricing. These are incompatible business models running in parallel; pick one, don't split focus.
3. **Etsy / POD platform risk is severe and current.** Etsy has been actively delisting AI-generated work in 2024-2025; Amazon KDP has tightened rules on AI books. Any business model dependent on these platforms can be invalidated overnight. Verify the founder's current (not historical) listing status.
4. **Paid ad → free Skool → paid course is a known pattern.** The community itself may be valuable, but be aware of the funnel: free tier teaches enough to make you want the paid tier; paid tier teaches enough to make you want the cohort/coaching. Budget accordingly or stop at the free tier.
