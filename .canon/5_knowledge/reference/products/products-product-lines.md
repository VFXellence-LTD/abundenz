# Atelier Product Lines — POD Design Catalog

Print-on-demand designs for Etsy (Ateliez store), Printify, Redbubble. We supply designs; fulfillment partners handle production and shipping.

---

## Line 1: Alphabet Initials

Single letter designs where each letter is filled/composed of themed objects.

**Scale**: 26 letters × 10 themes × 8 palettes = 2,080 unique designs.

### 10 Themes

| # | Theme | Fill Logic |
|---|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Musical | Instruments, notes, speakers |
| 2 | Botanical | Plants, flowers, leaves |
| 3 | Animals | Creatures starting with the letter |
| 4 | Ocean / Marine | Sea life + nautical elements |
| 5 | Space | Cosmic objects (universal, not letter-specific) |
| 6 | Culinary | Foods + kitchen items starting with the letter |
| 7 | Steampunk | Gears, pipes, brass (universal aesthetic) |
| 8 | Woodland | Forest creatures + flora |
| 9 | Sports | Equipment + actions |
| 10 | Whimsical / Fantasy | Mythical + fantastical |

**Best products**: Nursery wall art, personalized gifts, tote bags, mugs, phone cases, t-shirts.

---

## Line 2: Themed Skulls

Skull silhouettes composed of or integrated with themed elements.

**Themes**: Playground, Musical, Botanical, Mechanical, Ocean, Gaming, Culinary, Celestial.

**Scale**: 10+ themes × color variants = 100+ designs.

**Best products**: T-shirts (huge market), hoodies, posters, stickers, phone cases.

---

## Line 3: Vibrant Symbol Art

Bold, colorful designs on dark backgrounds — psychedelic/tie-dye aesthetic.

**Core symbols**: Astronaut, Teddy bear, Headphones, Boombox, Mushrooms, Butterflies, Roses, Sneakers, Cameras, Cats.

**Style variants**: Neon on black, Muted earth tones, Pastel on cream, Black & white, Watercolor, Retro 80s, Minimalist line art, Grunge.

---

## Line 4: Quote Portraits

Famous quote paired with stylized portrait of the speaker.

**Safe categories** (100+ years dead, no active estate enforcement):
- Stoic philosophers: Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Epictetus
- Ancient philosophers: Socrates, Plato, Aristotle, Confucius, Lao Tzu
- Literary: Shakespeare, Poe, Wilde, Twain, Emerson

**⚠ See `products-licensing-guide.md` for full legal risk assessment.**

---

## Lines 5-6: High Legal Risk

- **Line 5: Animalized Icons** — requires historical figures 100+ years dead only. See licensing guide.
- **Line 6: Famous Photos Reimagined** — must use original composition inspired by pose, not derivative of specific photo. See licensing guide.

---

## Production Pipeline

1. Concept brief (theme + symbol + style + color palette)
2. AI generation (Stable Diffusion local / Meta AI / Ideogram for text)
3. Upscale (Real-ESRGAN, 300 DPI minimum, 4500px+)
4. Quality check (human review)
5. Mockup generation (Placeit / Canva)
6. Batch upload to Etsy / Printify / Redbubble
