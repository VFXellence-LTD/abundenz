# TikTok Best Practices Playbook

Platform-specific tactics, FTC compliance, and Creator Affiliate setup for the Conduit / Zrodinger ecosystem.

---

## First 3 Seconds = Everything

The first 3 seconds determine whether viewers watch to 10 seconds. Hit 10 seconds → likely watch to end.

### Hook Types

| Hook Type | Success Rate |
|-----------|---|
| Question ("Can this AI write essays?") | High (60%+) |
| Bold claim ("This saved me 5 hours") | High (60%+) |
| Pattern interrupt (unexpected sound/visual) | Very High (70%+) |
| Trend riding (trending sound's iconic moment) | Very High (70%+) |
| Curiosity gap | Medium (50%+) |
| Relatability (familiar pain point) | High (60%+) |

Test 3-5 hooks per week per product. Track watch completion %.

---

## Text Overlays (50%+ views are silent)

- **Top 1/3**: Headline (3-5 words max)
- **Middle**: Key benefit or question
- **Bottom**: CTA or product name
- **Font**: Bold sans-serif (Montserrat, Impact), 48-72pt minimum
- **Color**: High contrast to video background

---

## CTA Best Practices

| CTA | CTR | Conversion |
|-----|-----|-----------|
| "Link in pinned comment" | 3-5% | 1-3% |
| "Check pinned comment" | 3-5% | 1-3% |
| "Link in bio" | 2-4% | 0.5-2% |

Show CTA text in last 3-5 seconds. Mention verbally 15-20 seconds in.

---

## Trending Sounds

TikTok algorithm heavily favors trending sounds, especially in first 7 days.

1. Check Discover tab daily (2-3 minutes)
2. Identify top 3 trending sounds
3. Match to product vibe
4. Use within first 24h of trend lifecycle
5. Apply to 3-5 videos/week

---

## Hashtag Strategy (Refresh Weekly)

10-15 total per post:
- 2-3 trending (this week)
- 1-2 sound-specific
- 3-5 niche-specific (#AITools, #ProductReview)
- 3-5 long-tail niche
- 1-2 branded (#Zrodinger)

---

## FTC Compliance (Legal Requirement)

Every product post must disclose:
- `#ad` in caption, OR
- Text overlay "Paid partnership", OR
- Verbal disclosure "This is an ad"

Risk of non-compliance: account suspension, FTC fine. Non-negotiable.

---

## TikTok Creator Affiliate Setup

Prerequisites: 1,000+ followers, Business account, identity verification (required since July 2025), 30+ days active, no violations.

**Steps**:
1. Settings → Account Type → Switch to Business → "Creator"
2. Creator tools → TikTok Shop → Affiliate → Apply
3. Complete identity verification
4. Approval: 1-3 days
5. New accounts (<5K followers) enter 30-day pilot: limited products, 3 posts/day cap

**Commissions**: Open Collaboration 10-15%, Targeted/Invite 18-25%+. Beauty/wellness 20%+, electronics 5-10%.

---

## Video Specs

- Dimensions: 1080x1920 (vertical, required)
- Format: MP4, H.264
- File size: <287 MB
- Frame rate: 24-60 fps (30fps ideal)
- Bitrate: 3-6 Mbps

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Weak hook (>3s) | Bold statement first 3 sec |
| No trending sound | Use trending sound weekly |
| No text overlays | Add overlays, 48-72pt font |
| Generic CTA | Pin comment with link + mention verbally |
| No FTC disclosure | Add #ad every product caption |
| Same product 2x/day | Rotate — max 1x/day per product |
| Inconsistent posting | Schedule 3-5x daily, same times |
| Ignore underperformers | Kill <100 views/24h products |
