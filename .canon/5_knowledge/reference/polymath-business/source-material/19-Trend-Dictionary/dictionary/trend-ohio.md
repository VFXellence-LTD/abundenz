# only in Ohio / Ohio

**Meaning/usage:** "Ohio" / "only in Ohio" labels anything weird, chaotic, surreal, or cringe ("bro thinks he's in Ohio"). Gen Alpha/Z meme; the state is used purely as a hyperbolic punchline, not a serious insult.

**Three-force assessment:**
- **Trend:** Peaked; durable kid/teen humor.
- **Legal:** Generic word/state name, no copyright. **Caution: "Ohio" is also a place name with state/university marks** — a TESS search will be noisy; ensure the meme phrasing doesn't collide with an official "Ohio" branded mark for apparel. → `caution`.
- **Ethical:** Clean. Punching at a *state-as-bit*, not a person or protected group; widely understood as affectionate absurdism. Passes 12-yo test. (If a design were to genuinely demean Ohioans, that'd flip to caution — keep it surreal/absurd, not contemptuous.) → `clean`.
