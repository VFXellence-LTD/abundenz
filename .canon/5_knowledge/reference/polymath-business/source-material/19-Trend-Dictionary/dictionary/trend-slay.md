# slay

**Meaning/usage:** "Slay" = to excel, look amazing, or do something impressively ("you slayed"). AAVE/ballroom origin; long mainstream. Possibly *over*-used — borderline on freshness.

**Three-force assessment:**
- **Trend:** Evergreen-meme, though some audiences find it slightly tired (still passes the 12-yo test as a recognized empowerment word).
- **Legal:** Extremely common word, no copyright. **Heavily used in apparel — TESS Class 25 will likely show many marks for longer slogans containing "slay."** Bare word probably weak as a mark but `unknown`; spot-check carefully. → `caution`.
- **Ethical:** Clean; honor origin. → `clean`.

**POD guidance:** Empowerment merch; the literal "slay" dragon/sword pun (links to [[../../03-Mythology/README]]) differentiates from the saturated plain-text market. `HOLD` — and the saturation/freshness risk is higher here than other entries.
