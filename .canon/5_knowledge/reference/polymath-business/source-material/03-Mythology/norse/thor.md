# Thor

> The red-bearded thunder-god: a working man's protector who rides a goat-drawn chariot, swings a short-handled hammer, and stands between mankind and the giants.

## Who / What
Thor is the Norse god of thunder, lightning, storms, strength, and the protection of mankind — the most beloved god of the common people in the Viking age (Thursday is "Thor's day"). In the myths he is a burly, **red-bearded** figure (not the blond movie version), hot-tempered but good-natured, who rides a chariot pulled by two magical goats and wields the hammer Mjölnir, which always returns to his hand and can level mountains. He is the eternal enemy of the giants and of the world-serpent Jörmungandr, whom he is fated to slay at Ragnarök — at the cost of his own life.

## Archetype
The Protector / Strongman / Storm-God / People's Champion. The reliable, mighty defender — not a brooding king but a hearty guardian of ordinary folk against chaos. Easy to love, easy to mis-copy.

## Visual Motifs (safe to draw — and how to differentiate from Marvel)
A burly man with a **full red beard** (Marvel's is blond and beardless/short); a **short-handled** hammer (Marvel's Mjolnir has a longer, distinct design); a chariot drawn by **two goats** (Tanngrisnir and Tanngnjóstr — a wonderfully unique motif Marvel doesn't use); iron gloves and a belt of strength; rugged Viking-age wool and iron, **no cape, no metallic Asgardian armor**; lightning and storm. Leaning into the red beard and goat chariot is the cleanest way to stay clearly in the free myth.

## Symbols
- **Mjölnir (the hammer)** — protection and thunder; an ancient amulet (see [[mjolnir]]). Use a runestone/archaeological hammer style, not the Marvel prop.
- **Lightning** — his thunder.
- **The goat chariot** — uniquely mythological; great for differentiation.
- **Iron gloves & belt of strength** — his gear.
- **The red beard** — the single clearest signal of "myth, not Marvel."

## Themes
Strength and protection, thunder and storm, the defense of ordinary people, courage against overwhelming odds, the rugged everyman-hero, duty unto death (Ragnarök).

## Reusable Concepts
The protector of the common folk (not a royal but a guardian). The boomerang weapon that returns. The strongman who is hearty and likeable rather than grim. The goat-drawn chariot (charming, unique, kid-friendly). The hero who knows he'll die fighting the serpent and fights anyway.

## Story / Narrative Hooks
The theft of Mjölnir and Thor dressing as a bride to recover it (a genuinely funny myth, great for comedy/kids). His fishing expedition to catch the world-serpent. His goats that he eats and resurrects each night. His foretold mutual death with Jörmungandr at Ragnarök.

## Design / POD Opportunities

- Goat-chariot whimsical art for children's content.
- Mjölnir-amulet jewelry/merch in archaeological/runestone style.
- "Protector" thunder/strength fitness branding (own hammer design).

## Remix Hooks
- A people's-protector strongman for any setting.
- A thunder / storm fitness brand (original design).
- A red-bearded rugged hero.
- A Z-named original thunder god (e.g. "Zorthor" / "Zund").

## Legal & Safety
- **Free to use:** the mythological red-bearded Thor, the goat chariot, the Eddas.
- **AVOID (critical):** the blond, caped Marvel/Disney Thor and Chris Hemsworth's likeness; Sony's God of War Ragnarök Thor; Smite; Assassin's Creed Valhalla. The Marvel look is the default association — differentiate hard (red beard, short hammer, goat chariot).
