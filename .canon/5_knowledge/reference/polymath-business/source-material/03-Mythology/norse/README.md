# Norse / Germanic Mythology

The Norse myths — recorded chiefly in the Icelandic *Poetic Edda* and *Prose Edda* (13th century) and entirely public domain — give us some of the most evocative and marketable material in all of mythology: Odin and his ravens, Thor and his hammer, the World Tree Yggdrasil, the trickster Loki, and the apocalyptic battle of Ragnarök. The aesthetic (runes, longships, frost and fire, wolves and ravens) is perennially popular across metal, fantasy, fitness, and tattoo cultures.

## The big trap (this is the most dangerous pantheon for IP)

Norse myth is the single most aggressively monetized mythology in modern media, which makes it the **most likely place to accidentally copy a protected design**:

- **Marvel's Thor, Loki, Odin, Asgard, Mjolnir, the Bifrost** — Marvel/Disney's comic and film versions are among the most valuable and aggressively enforced IP on earth. A blond, clean-shaven, caped Thor with a short-handled hammer reads as *Marvel* to virtually everyone. The mythological Thor (red-bearded, rugged, a working farmer-god) is free; the Marvel look is not. This is the central caution of the entire folder.
- **God of War (2018) and Ragnarök (Sony)** — its specific Kratos-era designs of Thor, Odin, Baldur, Freya, etc. are Sony copyrights.
- **Assassin's Creed Valhalla, Smite, Hellblade, "Vikings" / "Vikings: Valhalla" (the TV shows), the comic and game "Loki" variants** — all carry their own protected designs.

**Rule of thumb:** go back to the Eddas and the rugged, archaic look (red-bearded Thor, one-eyed wandering Odin in a broad hat, raw iron and wool, runestone art), and never to the cinematic-universe version.

## A note on modern revival

A small modern revival (Ásatrú / Heathenry) treats these as living beliefs, and some Norse-pagan symbolism (notably certain runes and the Othala/Sun-wheel) has been co-opted by hate groups. This folder flags those specific symbols (`risk_level: caution` or `avoid`) so they are never used naively. The mythology itself remains free to build on respectfully.
