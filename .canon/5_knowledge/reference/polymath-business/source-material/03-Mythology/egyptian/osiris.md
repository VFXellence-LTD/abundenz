# Osiris (Wesir)

> The murdered king who became lord of the dead: green-skinned with new life, crook and flail crossed over his chest, judge of every soul that enters the afterlife.

## Who / What
Osiris is the ancient Egyptian god of the afterlife, death, resurrection, fertility, and just kingship. Once the good king of Egypt, he was murdered and dismembered by his jealous brother Set, then restored by the magic of his wife Isis — long enough to conceive Horus — before descending to rule the underworld as king and judge of the dead. He is the great "dying-and-rising god," his death and resurrection mirroring the annual flooding of the Nile and the regrowth of crops (hence his green skin, the color of vegetation and rebirth). In the afterlife, the heart of each dead soul is weighed against the feather of truth before Osiris's throne. He is solemn, just, and eternal.

## Archetype
The Dying-and-Rising God / Judge of the Dead / Underworld King / Resurrected. The figure who conquers death and presides over the just afterlife — a profound symbol of renewal and justice.

## Visual Motifs (safe to draw)
A mummiform king (wrapped like a mummy) with **green or black skin** (vegetation and fertile silt — both signify rebirth); the crossed **crook and flail** held over his chest; a tall white *atef* crown with plumes; enthroned in the underworld; presiding over the weighing of the heart against a feather. Gold, green, and Egyptian profile-art style.

## Symbols
- **The crook and flail** — kingship, authority, and care (shepherd's crook + grain flail).
- **Green / black skin** — rebirth, vegetation, fertile Nile silt.
- **The atef crown** — his royal afterlife crown.
- **Mummy wrappings** — death conquered.
- **The scales of judgment** — the weighing of the heart.

## Themes
Death and resurrection, the afterlife and judgment, fertility and the green rebirth, just kingship, justice and truth, eternity, renewal mirrored in nature (the Nile flood, the harvest).

## Reusable Concepts
Death-and-rebirth / "green renewal" (a deep, positive renewal metaphor tied to nature). The weighing of the heart / scales of justice (judgment, truth, accountability — a powerful, reusable concept). The resurrected king. The shepherd-king (crook and flail = guidance and provision). The good ruler betrayed and vindicated.

## Story / Narrative Hooks
His murder and dismemberment by Set. His restoration by Isis and the conception of Horus. His descent to rule the underworld. The weighing of the heart against Ma'at's feather of truth (with Anubis and Thoth presiding). His role as the prototype every dead pharaoh hoped to become.
