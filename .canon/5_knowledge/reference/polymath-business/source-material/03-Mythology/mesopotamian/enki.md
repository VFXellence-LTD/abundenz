# Enki (Ea)

> The wise and cunning god of fresh water and creation, from whose shoulders the life-giving rivers flow — the clever friend of humanity who outwits even the other gods to save us.

## Who / What
Enki (Akkadian: Ea) is the Mesopotamian god of fresh water, wisdom, crafts, creation, and cunning intelligence. He dwells in the *Abzu*, the freshwater deep beneath the earth, and is the source of the rivers and springs that make life possible. He is the great problem-solver and friend of humanity: in the flood myth, when the gods decide to destroy mankind, it is Enki who cleverly warns the human survivor (Utnapishtim/Ziusudra) to build a boat, saving the human race. He is endlessly resourceful, often solving the other gods' problems through trickery and ingenuity, and is credited with giving humanity the arts of civilization. He is depicted as a bearded god with two streams of water — full of leaping fish — flowing from his shoulders, and is associated with the goat-fish (the origin of the Capricorn symbol).

## Archetype
The Wise Trickster / Creator / Friend of Humanity / Water Lord. The clever, benevolent intelligence that solves problems and protects mankind — wisdom paired with mischief.

## Visual Motifs (safe to draw)
A bearded god seated or standing with two streams of water flowing from his shoulders or a vase, the water full of swimming fish; the goat-fish creature (a goat's front, a fish's tail — the Capricorn motif); reed marshes and rivers; ancient Mesopotamian relief style. Lapis and gold.

## Symbols
- **Water streaming from the shoulders** — the source of the life-giving rivers.
- **The goat-fish** — his hybrid creature (Capricorn's origin).
- **Fish** — abundance and fresh water.
- **The reed** — the marshes and his cleverness (he whispers warnings to a reed wall).
- **The twin rivers** — Tigris and Euphrates.

## Themes
Wisdom and cunning, fresh water and life, creation and craft, the friend and savior of humanity, ingenuity and problem-solving, mischief, generosity.

## Reusable Concepts
The wise, clever helper (the fixer, the problem-solver, the resourceful friend). Fresh water as life (sustainability, hydration, water brands). The cunning solution (cleverness over force). The friend of humanity (a benevolent, likeable god). The goat-fish / Capricorn zodiac link. Civilization's gifts.

## Story / Narrative Hooks
His clever saving of humanity from the flood (warning through a reed wall to keep his oath technically unbroken). His ordering of the world and gift of crafts to humanity. His resolving of conflicts among the gods. His creation of humans (in some myths) to do the gods' labor.
