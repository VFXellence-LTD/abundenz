# Pegasus

> The pure-white winged horse, born from a monster's blood: a steed for heroes, a spring-maker for poets, and the very emblem of soaring freedom and inspiration.

## Who / What
Pegasus is the magnificent winged horse of Greek myth, sprung from the blood (or the severed neck) of Medusa when Perseus beheaded her. He was tamed by the hero Bellerophon (with the help of a golden bridle from Athena) to defeat the Chimera, and later ascended to Olympus to carry Zeus's thunderbolts. Where his hooves struck the ground, the spring Hippocrene burst forth on Mount Helicon — sacred to the Muses, making Pegasus a symbol of poetic inspiration. He is one of the most beloved and universal mythic creatures.

## Archetype
The Noble Steed / Spirit of Freedom / Bearer of Heroes / Muse-Creature. The pure, uplifting creature of flight and inspiration — aspiration made flesh.

## Visual Motifs (safe to draw)
A pure-white horse with great feathered wings; wings spread mid-gallop or soaring through clouds; springing dramatically from Medusa's blood; striking the ground to open a spring; carried aloft by Bellerophon. Sky, clouds, and light.

## Symbols
- **Winged horse** — flight, freedom, inspiration.
- **White stallion** — purity and nobility.
- **The Hippocrene spring** — poetic inspiration (struck open by his hoof).
- **Clouds and sky** — the ascent.

## Themes
Freedom and flight, inspiration and creativity (the Muses' spring), purity and nobility, the ascent to the divine, partnership between hero and steed, soaring aspiration.

## Reusable Concepts
Aspiration and freedom made visible (the soaring winged horse). Inspiration springing from a single touch (the Hippocrene). Beauty born from horror (Medusa's blood). The faithful steed that lifts the hero beyond reach. The ascent from earth to sky.

## Story / Narrative Hooks
His birth from Medusa's blood. The taming by Bellerophon with Athena's golden bridle. The defeat of the Chimera from the air. Bellerophon's prideful attempt to fly to Olympus (and his fall) while Pegasus continues up alone. His final place carrying Zeus's thunderbolts.
