# Sphinx (Greek)

> The winged riddler who guarded the road to Thebes: answer her question and pass; fail, and be devoured. The deadly gatekeeper of wisdom.

## Who / What
The Greek Sphinx is a monster with the body of a lion, the face and bust of a woman, and the wings of an eagle. She sat on a rock outside Thebes and posed a riddle to every traveler — "What walks on four legs in the morning, two at noon, and three in the evening?" — devouring all who failed to answer. The hero Oedipus solved it ("Man — who crawls as a baby, walks upright as an adult, and uses a cane in old age"), whereupon the Sphinx threw herself from the rock in defeat. (The Egyptian sphinx is a related but distinct figure: male, wingless, a benevolent guardian — see the Egyptian folder.) The Greek Sphinx is the archetypal riddling guardian.

## Archetype
The Riddler / Guardian / Tester / Keeper of Secrets. The gatekeeper who tests the worthiness of those who would pass — wisdom guarded by a deadly question. A powerful symbol for puzzles, mysteries, and tests of intellect.

## Visual Motifs (safe to draw)
A creature combining a lion's reclining body, a woman's face and upper body, and eagle's wings; perched on a rocky outcrop or city gate; an enigmatic, knowing expression; travelers below; the riddle itself as a graphic element. Note: the Egyptian sphinx (male, wingless, headdress) is a separate visual.

## Symbols
- **The riddle** — the deadly test of wits; her defining trait.
- **Lion body** — strength.
- **Woman's face** — wisdom and mystery.
- **Eagle wings** — the Greek (vs. Egyptian) version.
- **The crossroads / gate** — the threshold she guards.

## Themes
Riddles and wisdom, the test of worthiness, mystery and the enigmatic, the deadly question, knowledge as a gate, the guardian who must be outwitted.

## Reusable Concepts
"Answer the riddle to pass" — a perfect framework for puzzles, quizzes, and gated content. The guardian who tests rather than fights. Wisdom as the key, not strength. The enigma personified. The famous riddle of the three ages of man (a ready-made content piece).

## Story / Narrative Hooks
The riddle posed to all who approach Thebes. Oedipus solving it. The Sphinx's self-destruction in defeat. The contrast with the benevolent Egyptian sphinx. Inventing new riddles for new heroes.

## Legal & Safety
- **Free to use:** the Greek Sphinx, the famous riddle, and classical art (and the ancient Egyptian sphinx).
- **Avoid:** specific modern video-game and film sphinx designs. "Sphinx" is a common brand name — check trademark.
