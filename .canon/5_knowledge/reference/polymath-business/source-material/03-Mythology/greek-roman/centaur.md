# Centaur

> Half man, half horse — and half wild, half wise. The centaur embodies the eternal struggle between our reasoning mind and our animal instincts.

## Who / What
Centaurs are creatures with the upper body of a human joined to the body and legs of a horse. In most myths they are wild, drunken, and prone to violence (the famous brawl at the wedding of Pirithous), embodying untamed animal instinct. But the centaur Chiron is the great exception: noble, wise, and immortal, he is the tutor of heroes (Achilles, Jason, Asclepius) and a master of medicine, music, and hunting. This split — savage herd versus wise teacher — makes the centaur a perfect symbol of the dual nature within humanity. The constellation and zodiac sign Sagittarius is a centaur archer.

## Archetype
The Hybrid / Wild Man / Wise Mentor / Dual-Natured Being. Both the brute that gives in to instinct and the sage that masters it — the human condition split in two.

## Visual Motifs (safe to draw)
A human upper body seamlessly joined to a horse's body; drawing a bow (the Sagittarius archer); either wild, bearded, and brandishing a club or wine-cup, or noble, dignified, and bearing a lyre or healer's herbs (for Chiron); set in forests or mountains.

## Symbols
- **Man-horse hybrid body** — the dual nature.
- **Bow and arrow** — the centaur archer; Sagittarius.
- **Wine cup and club** — the wild, drunken centaurs.
- **Lyre and herbs** — the wise Chiron (music and medicine).

## Themes
The dual nature (reason vs. instinct), civilization vs. wildness, mentorship and wisdom, savagery and self-control, the hybrid that belongs to two worlds, the noble exception within a wild kind.

## Reusable Concepts
The "two natures within one being" — instinct vs. reason (a deep, universal theme). The wild kind and the wise exception (Chiron among the brutes). The mentor who trains heroes. The hybrid that must reconcile its halves. The archer (Sagittarius) and the seeker.

## Story / Narrative Hooks
The drunken brawl at the centaurs' / Lapiths' wedding feast. Chiron the wise tutor and his pupils (Achilles, Jason, Heracles). Chiron's accidental poisoning by a Hydra-venom arrow and his choice to give up immortality. Nessus the centaur and the poisoned shirt that kills Heracles.
