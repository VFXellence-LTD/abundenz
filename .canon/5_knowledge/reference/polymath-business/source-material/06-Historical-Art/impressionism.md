# Impressionism

> Capturing light and the fleeting moment in loose dabs of broken color: shimmering water, dappled gardens, and sunlight rendered as sensation rather than line.

## Overview
Impressionism abandoned crisp outlines and studio finish to paint the optical *sensation* of light outdoors — short, visible strokes of pure broken color that the eye blends. Its luminous, airy, joyful aesthetic (gardens, water, leisure) is one of the most beloved and reproduced in the world. The founding works are public domain, and the style is endlessly applicable to soft, warm, lifestyle-leaning products.

## Defining Features
- Broken color: small visible dabs of pure color blended by the eye.
- Painting *en plein air* (outdoors) to capture real light.
- Emphasis on the fleeting moment, weather, and time of day.
- Soft, dissolved edges; no black outlines.
- Everyday modern life and leisure as subject.

## Visual Vocabulary
Loose, energetic brushwork; shimmering reflective water; dappled light through foliage; soft luminous color; informal "snapshot" compositions; figures dissolving into atmosphere; bright, high-key palettes.

## Shapes
Soft dissolved forms, the loose dab/stroke as building block, informal cropped compositions, the shimmering horizontal of water, blurred organic masses.

## Textures
Visible impasto brushstrokes, broken color, soft feathered edges, shimmering surface, light-filled haze.

## Palette
The "luminous garden" family: high-key blues, greens, lilac, rose, sunlit yellow, and white, with violet shadows (never black). Bright, soft, airy. See [[08-Color-Palettes/README]].

## Key Motifs
Water lilies and ponds, gardens, dappled sunlight, riverside leisure, haystacks/series studies, parasols and boating, urban boulevards, shimmering reflections.

## Rights Note
The STYLE and the founding 19th-century works are public domain — the founders died well over 70 years ago. One nuance: a handful of *very late* works by long-lived Impressionists sit near the term boundary in some countries, so for **direct image use** confirm open-access status (Wikimedia/Met Open Access). The broken-color, plein-air, dappled-light *style* is entirely free to emulate. See [[01-Legal-Guidelines/us-public-domain-cutoff]].
