# Badges & Crests

## Description
A **badge** is a self-contained emblem with a clear outer boundary (circle, shield, oval, or hexagon) enclosing a central icon, framed by curved text and supporting flourishes. A **crest** is its heraldic cousin — a shield divided into fields, often topped by a banner and flanked by supporting elements. Both motifs declare "this is an official mark of *something*," lending instant credibility and a sense of belonging.

## Visual vocabulary
- **Outer container:** circle/roundel, escutcheon (shield), oval, rounded-corner rectangle, or hexagon.
- **Concentric structure:** inner ring + outer ring, the gap filled with arched text ("EST. 1923 · MOUNTAIN CO.").
- **Center hero:** one dominant icon (mountain, anchor, eagle, gear, star) sitting on a clean ground.
- **Anchored text curves:** top text arcs up-and-over, bottom text arcs down-and-under, sometimes flipped so it reads outward.
- **Dividers:** small stars, dots, diamonds, or laurel sprigs separating top and bottom text runs.
- **Symmetry:** strong vertical-axis mirror symmetry; the eye reads it as "stamped" and authoritative.
- **Line treatment:** monoline (modern), engraved-thin (vintage), or chunky-bold (athletic/varsity).

## Where it shines
- **Apparel (best):** left-chest pocket print, full-back crest, sleeve patch. Reads at small scale because the silhouette is recognizable even when text is illegible.
- **Brand marks:** secondary/"seal" logos, "established" stamps, loyalty/club identities.
- **Merch:** enamel pins, embroidered patches, stickers, mugs, tote stamps.
- **Struggles:** large posters where it floats alone (needs a surrounding system); very small favicons (concentric text turns to mush below ~64px).

## Pairs with
- **Categories / subjects:** outdoors/adventure, coffee/craft-beverage, motorcycle/garage, maritime, academia/varsity, esoteric societies.
- **Palettes:** muted heritage (cream + forest + rust), monochrome ink, navy + gold, athletic two-tone. (link section 11)
- **Art movements:** Arts & Crafts, mid-century, varsity/collegiate, [[vintage-engraving-anatomical]] for an antique seal.
- **Other motifs:** nest [[laurels-and-wreaths]] inside, hang a [[banners-and-ribbons]] beneath, distress with [[halftone-and-risograph-textures]].

## Remix hooks
- Replace the heroic central icon with an unexpected niche object (a USB stick, a ramen bowl, a houseplant) — the gravitas of the badge plus a mundane subject = comedic apparel gold.
- Invent a fictional founding year and motto in dog-Latin for instant fake-heritage.
- Strip to **monoline single-weight** for a clean modern-brand feel; or crush with grit for thrift-store vintage.
- Double-ring with counter-rotated text (top reads clockwise, bottom reads counter-clockwise) for a coin/seal feel — links to [[stamp-and-postmark]].

- **Modifiers:** `letterpress`, `distressed worn print`, `two-color spot print`, `gold foil on navy`, `athletic varsity style`, `est. 19XX`
- **Negative hints:** suppress `photo, gradient mesh, 3d render, drop shadow, realistic metal` when you want flat print-ready output; suppress `real brand logos, trademark, copyrighted emblem`.

## Legal note
The emblem geometry is an unprotectable idea — generate freely. Never instruct the model to reproduce a *real* crest (team, school, military, corporate, national). Keep the central icon and motto invented or drawn from public-domain symbols. If a generated badge accidentally resembles a known registered mark, regenerate — do not ship it.
