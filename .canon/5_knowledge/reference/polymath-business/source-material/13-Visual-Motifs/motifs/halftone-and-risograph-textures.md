# Halftone & Risograph Textures

## Description
**Halftone** simulates continuous tone with patterns of dots that vary in size — the classic comic-book and newspaper-print look (Ben-Day dots at the extreme). **Risograph** (riso) mimics the soft, grainy, slightly-misregistered, limited-spot-color output of the Riso duplicator beloved by zine-makers. Both are *surface treatments* — applied over shapes, type, or imagery to add analog warmth, tactility, and retro-print authenticity.

## Visual vocabulary
- **Halftone dots:** regular grids of dots scaling from tiny (light) to large (dark); angled screens (15°/45°/75°).
- **Ben-Day dots:** evenly sized, evenly spaced dots in flat areas (pop-art/comic look).
- **Misregistration:** color layers shifted slightly off-alignment for the charming "imperfect print" effect.
- **Grain & noise:** soft speckled texture over flat color (riso signature).
- **Overprint:** where two spot colors overlap a third blended color appears (multiply behavior).
- **Limited palette:** riso typically 1–3 bold spot inks (fluoro pink, blue, yellow, green).
- **Paper texture:** subtle fibrous ground showing through.

## Where it shines
- **Posters (best):** gig posters, indie/zine aesthetics, art prints — texture does the heavy lifting.
- **Apparel:** halftone gradients and Ben-Day fills give screen-print authenticity; great for retro/comic tees.
- **Social & blog:** texture overlays unify a feed's look; warms up flat graphics.
- **Video/motion:** animated grain and dot-screen overlays for lo-fi/retro motion design.
- **Merch:** stickers, zines, risograph-printed art cards.
- **Struggles:** ultra-clean corporate/luxury contexts; very fine detail (dots can clog at small print sizes).

## Remix hooks
- **Duotone misregister:** two spot colors deliberately offset = instant indie-print cool.
- **Ben-Day pop fill:** flat areas filled with even dots for a comic/pop feel (the *technique*, never a specific Lichtenstein composition).
- **Gradient-to-dots:** a smooth gradient broken into a halftone ramp for retro shading.
- **Grain overlay** as a finishing pass on any flat graphic to add warmth.

- **Negative hints:** suppress `smooth gradient, glossy, hd photo, clean vector` when you specifically want the textured analog look.

## Legal note
Halftone and risograph are printing processes — never protectable, never infringing on their own. Applying them to imagery does *not* clear that image's rights: halftoning a copyrighted photo, film still, or celebrity image still infringes copyright/likeness. Apply textures only to your own or genuinely public-domain source imagery.
