# Frames & Borders

## Description
A **frame/border** is the ornamental edge that contains and dignifies whatever sits within. It ranges from a single hairline rule to an elaborate filigree cartouche. The motif's whole job is to create a *threshold* — separating the artwork from the world and signaling "look here, this is finished and intentional."

## Visual vocabulary
- **Edge types:** single/double rule lines, dotted/dashed rules, rope/twist borders, Greek-key (meander) repeats, Art-Deco stepped corners, organic filigree scrolls, beaded edges.
- **Corner treatment:** ornamental corner flourishes (used alone = "corners-only" minimal frame), mitered geometric corners, fan/sunburst corners.
- **Cartouche:** an ornamental panel (oval or shaped) that frames text within a larger composition.
- **Guilloche:** fine interwoven line patterns (currency/certificate look).
- **Inner margin:** the breathing gap between border and content — critical to legibility.
- **Symmetry:** four-fold (all corners match) or top/bottom mirror.

## Where it shines
- **Posters & prints:** instantly elevates a typographic quote to "framed art."
- **Book covers & title pages:** classic and children's books lean on ornamental frames for chapter heads and cover panels.
- **Social:** quote-card templates, announcement graphics, certificate-style posts.
- **Brand/merch:** certificate/voucher design, premium packaging edges, label borders.
- **Struggles:** very busy interior content (frame + busy art = visual noise); responsive/cropping contexts where edges get clipped.

## Pairs with
- **Categories / subjects:** quotes/typography, vintage advertising, certificates, classical literature, ornamental monograms.
- **Palettes:** gold + ivory, monochrome ink, deco black + gold, soft pastel (nursery). (link section 11)
- **Art movements:** Art Deco, Art Nouveau, Victorian, neoclassical, illuminated-manuscript.
- **Other motifs:** the outer ring of [[mandalas]], the casing of [[tarot-card-framing]], complements [[laurels-and-wreaths]] inside it, sits over [[geometric-backdrops]].

## Remix hooks
- **Corners-only:** drop the connecting rules, keep four ornamental corners — modern, airy, lets the content breathe.
- **Art-Deco stepped frame** in black + gold = luxury/1920s instantly.
- **Dot-grid or thin double-rule** = clean editorial/modern.
- Nest two borders (ornate outer + hairline inner) for a museum-mat effect.
- Turn the border into a **repeating word/phrase** running the perimeter.

## AI prompt phrasing
- **Core phrase:** `ornamental rectangular border frame, filigree corner flourishes, thin double rule line, symmetrical, flat vector, empty center for content`
- **Deco variant:** `art deco stepped border frame, gold geometric corners, black background, 1920s`
- **Modifiers:** `corners only`, `greek key meander border`, `guilloche fine-line frame`, `rope twist border`, `victorian filigree cartouche`
- **Negative hints:** suppress `busy center, photo, 3d bevel frame, picture-frame moulding` when you want a flat graphic border.

## Legal note
Ornamental borders are generic, unprotectable design vocabulary — generate freely. Two cautions: do not copy a *specific* modern stock-frame asset wholesale, and never use guilloche/certificate framing to forge real currency, official documents, or genuine credentials. Stylistic homage is fine; forgery is not.
