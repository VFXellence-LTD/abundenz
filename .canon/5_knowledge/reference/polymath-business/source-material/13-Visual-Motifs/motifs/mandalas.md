# Mandalas

## Description
A **mandala** is a radially symmetric pattern built from concentric rings of repeating motifs around a central point. The eye is pulled inward to the core and outward through expanding bands of ornament. The structure communicates wholeness, balance, and meditative focus — equally at home as sacred geometry, coloring-book line art, or decorative surface pattern.

## Visual vocabulary
- **Radial symmetry:** typically 6-, 8-, 12-, or 16-fold rotational symmetry around a center point.
- **Concentric bands:** rings of repeating units (petals, teardrops, dots, arcs) growing outward.
- **Center anchor:** a focal bloom, dot, or small motif that seeds the whole pattern.
- **Petal/lotus forms:** stylized lotus petals are the most common building block.
- **Line vs fill:** pure outline (coloring-book), filled silhouette (bold print), or detailed engraving.
- **Edge:** can fade to white, sit in a circle, or dissolve into surrounding negative space.

## Where it shines
- **Coloring books (best):** the canonical adult-coloring product — pure line, high detail, satisfying to fill.
- **Apparel:** center-chest or back placement; reads as boho/festival/spiritual.
- **Posters & wall art:** symmetrical centerpiece, great for meditation/yoga decor.
- **Social/brand:** wellness, yoga, meditation, astrology niches.
- **Struggles:** tiny scale (detail vanishes — use a bold simplified version); asymmetric layouts (a mandala wants to be centered).

## Pairs with
- **Categories / subjects:** wellness/yoga/meditation, astrology/celestial, boho/festival, floral, henna/mehndi aesthetics.
- **Palettes:** jewel tones, monochrome ink (coloring), sunset gradient, earthy boho. (link section 11)

## Remix hooks
- **Half-mandala** rising over a horizon line = sun/moon scene; very popular boho apparel layout.
- Build the mandala out of **niche objects** instead of petals (tools, coffee beans, bones, leaves) for a themed product.
- **Line-only** versions feed directly into coloring-book products (Atelier Coloring Books vertical).
- **Neon-glow** mandala on black = festival/rave merch.
- **Deconstructed/broken** mandala (a quarter dissolving into particles) = modern edgy twist.

- **Negative hints:** suppress `asymmetric, off-center, photo, 3d, gradient` when you want clean print/coloring output.

## Legal note
The radial mandala form is ancient and free of copyright. Treat *specific consecrated religious diagrams* (Sri Yantra, named deity mandalas) with cultural care — prefer abstract decorative mandalas for commercial product to avoid appropriation concerns. This is a respect/brand-safety judgment, not a copyright one.
