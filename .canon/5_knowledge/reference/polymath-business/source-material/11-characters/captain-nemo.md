# Captain Nemo

## Snapshot

The brilliant, brooding, anti-imperialist commander of the submarine *Nautilus* from Jules Verne's *Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea* (1870) and *The Mysterious Island* (1875). A scientific genius who has renounced the surface world, roaming the oceans in vengeance against the empires that destroyed his family.

## Copyright status (the text/expression)

Fully public domain worldwide. *Twenty Thousand Leagues* appeared in 1870 (English 1872); Verne died in 1905 (life+70 long expired). Captain Nemo, the *Nautilus*, Professor Aronnax, Conseil, Ned Land, and both novels are free to use. The original Alphonse de Neuville and Édouard Riou engravings (1870s) are also PD and may be reproduced/remixed directly.

## What is RESTRICTED (the modern design / trademark / publicity)

Only specific modern adaptations:

- **Disney's 1954 *20,000 Leagues Under the Sea*** created an iconic Art-Nouveau riveted-iron *Nautilus* design and James Mason's bearded Nemo. That submarine design is a Disney copyright; Mason's likeness is a publicity-rights concern. Don't copy them.
- **The *League of Extraordinary Gentlemen*** version of Nemo and the Nautilus (Alan Moore / Kevin O'Neill, and the 2003 film) is the creators'/studio's — don't copy that design.
- Other film/TV Nautilus designs are likewise individually copyrighted.
- **Verne's own text leaves the Nautilus and Nemo open to interpretation** (and his *Mysterious Island* reveals Nemo as an Indian prince, Dakkar, fighting British colonialism) — use the PD text and original engravings, not the film designs.

## Risk level and why

**Safe.** PD text and PD original art, a full cast, and no single locked-in design you're forced to use. Just avoid copying a specific modern film's Nautilus or Nemo and you're clear. This is a strong, under-exploited steampunk property.

## Safe-use guidance

- **Do:** Use Verne's text and characters; design your own Nautilus (or remix the 1870s PD engravings); use the deep-sea adventure, the organ-playing captain, the giant squid, the diving suits, the anti-colonial backstory.
- **Don't:** Copy the Disney 1954 Nautilus/Nemo design or James Mason's likeness; don't copy the *League of Extraordinary Gentlemen* designs.

## Creative opportunities

### Design / POD
Ornate submarine blueprint/cutaway posters, deep-sea creature plates (PD engraving style), "the sea is everything" quote products (PD text), giant-squid battle art, steampunk diving-helmet designs, Nautilus silhouette branding.

### Story / writing
Nemo origin stories (the anti-colonial prince angle is rich), steampunk reframes, the Nautilus itself as a character, audio-dramas, an original Z-named deep-sea captain. Both novels are remixable.

### Other applications
Children's adventure books (PD engravings or original art), steampunk/maritime brand identities, AI-writing undersea-exploration prompt packs, full-range merch.
