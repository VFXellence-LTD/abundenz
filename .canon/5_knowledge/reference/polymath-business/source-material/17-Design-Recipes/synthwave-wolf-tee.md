# Neon Wolf

**Resulting concept:** A faceted, low-poly **wolf head** in profile howls upward, outlined in glowing cyan and hot-pink. Behind it: a receding **perspective grid** floor and a striped hot-pink **sunset** with a thin **crescent moon** above. The whole thing glows against midnight violet `#1A0B2E`. A short chrome-italic tagline ("NIGHT RUNNER") arcs beneath. Pure 1980s outrun energy on a real animal.

## Target product

- **Surface:** tee (black or midnight-violet garment makes the neon glow).
- **Print method / color space:** **RGB workflow only** — DTF transfer or dye-sublimation; for screen-print use fluorescent pink/cyan inks. A flat CMYK DTG run will look muddy.
- **Layout & technical notes:** centered, ~11" wide; let the glow halo sit on the dark garment (no white box). Sticker variant: die-cut to the wolf+sunset silhouette, glossy vinyl, 1/8" bleed.

## Target category

- **Niche:** [[14-Trending-Categories/retro-technology]] (synthwave/outrun sub-niche) — note `saturation: high`, so lean on the animal twist for differentiation.
- **Adjacent niches:** [[14-Trending-Categories/space-exploration]], gaming/arcade décor.

## Legal guardrails

**Never** copy a specific synthwave game/film/album artwork or logo, and **never** print the neon palette in flat CMYK and expect fidelity.

- [[voltz-retro-neon]] — RGB-only; don't copy a specific cover/game/film artwork.
- [[line-art-animals]] — generic real wolf, not an IP character.
- [[crescent-moon]] — plain crescent only; not the star-and-crescent.
