**Resulting concept:** A horizontal **moon-phase row** (new → full → new) arcs gently, a small symmetrical **moth** centered beneath the full moon, an **eight-pointed star** and tiny dots scattered around. Fine ink linework on cream, optionally with gold for the moon fills. A short mystic line in thin serif underneath. Reads as a tarot-adjacent celestial emblem — perfect die-cut sticker, mug band, or card.

## Target product

- **Surface:** glossy/matte vinyl **sticker** (die-cut to the phase-row); also mug wrap and folded card.
- **Print method / color space:** CMYK for stickers/cards; sublimation for mugs (gold becomes ochre — preview it). Ink-only version is screen/riso friendly.
- **Layout & technical notes:** sticker = horizontal phase row with 1/8" bleed; mug = wrap the phase row around the full circumference, moth at the front-center.
