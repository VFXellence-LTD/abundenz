# Wild Crest

> A noble stag's head inside a laurel-and-banner badge, with an outdoor motto in vintage slab type. Earthy, rugged, retro-adventure. The outdoors/wildlife apparel workhorse — tees, embroidered patches, slap stickers.

**Resulting concept:** A **stag head** with broad antlers sits front-and-center inside an open **laurel wreath**, a small **mountain-and-sun** scene behind it, all bounded by a circular badge outline. A **ribbon banner** below carries a short outdoor motto in vintage slab caps. Rendered in terracotta `#A8492A`, ochre `#C8893B`, and olive `#6E7A4A` on cream. Reads instantly as a retro national-park/outfitter crest — without copying any real one.

## Target product

- **Surface:** tee (heather or earth-tone garment); embroidered **patch**; vinyl sticker.
- **Print method / color space:** screen-print (2–3 earthy inks) on tee; embroidery for patch (limit to ~4 thread colors, merrowed border); CMYK for sticker. Earth tones are CMYK-safe.
- **Layout & technical notes:** circular badge ~9–10" on tee, centered chest; for patch simplify detail and thicken lines for stitch; sticker die-cut to the circle.

## Legal guardrails

**Never** mimic a specific existing outdoor-brand badge, beer crest, or institutional seal; keep the crest layout and wordmark original and the stag a real species.

- [[badges-and-crests]] — layout is free; don't clone a specific brand's mark.
- [[line-art-animals]] — generic real animal, not an IP mascot.
- [[terrafolk-earthen]] — earth tones unownable; cultural-sensitivity note if echoing a living folk tradition.
