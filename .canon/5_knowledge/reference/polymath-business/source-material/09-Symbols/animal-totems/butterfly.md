# Butterfly (Totem)

> The fragile wings of transformation, the soul, and hopeful change.

## Meaning
The butterfly symbolizes transformation and metamorphosis (caterpillar → chrysalis → winged beauty), the soul (Greek *psyche* means both soul and butterfly), rebirth, and hope. Its fragility and brief life add poignancy — beauty and transience together. It is a leading emblem of personal change, recovery, mental-health awareness, and the freed spirit.

## Origin
A universal natural symbol with soul/transformation meanings across Greek, Mesoamerican, Japanese, and Christian traditions. Fully PD. Avoid copying a specific copyrighted butterfly artwork or live brand. Design your own.

## Visual description
A symmetrical butterfly with two pairs of patterned wings, antennae, and a slender body — the monarch (orange-black) being iconic. Render in watercolor, blackwork, geometric, line-art, or as a metamorphosis sequence (caterpillar → chrysalis → butterfly).

## Emotional tags
Hope, transformation, delicacy, freedom, lightness, the soul.

## Design & story applications
- **POD / apparel**: a top motif — transformation, mental-health/recovery, "embrace change," delicate-feminine and floral lines.
- **Posters**: inspirational/transformation wall art, nursery and botanical prints.
- **Children's books**: the metamorphosis story (very popular), garden settings.
- **Story / AI writing**: transformation arcs, the soul, hope, fragility.
- **Branding**: transformation, wellness, beauty, mental health (design original).
