# Serpent / Snake (Totem)

> The shedding-skinned emblem of transformation, healing, and primal danger.

## Meaning
The serpent is one of the most dual-natured symbols. Shedding its skin, it signifies transformation, rebirth, renewal, and immortality. Coiled around a staff (Rod of Asclepius) it means healing and medicine; biting its tail ([[../alchemical/ouroboros]]) it means eternity. Yet it also carries temptation and evil (the Eden serpent), danger, and the primal/chthonic. Across cultures it is wisdom, fertility, and the life-force — and the venomous threat.

## Origin
A universal symbol across virtually every mythology. Fully PD. Context-match the healing/wisdom vs. temptation/evil readings. Avoid copying a specific live snake logo or franchise. Design your own.

## Visual description
A coiled or sinuous snake, sometimes rearing, sometimes wrapped around a staff, branch, or biting its tail. Render in blackwork tattoo, fine line, geometric, or ornate scales. Often paired with roses, daggers, or skulls.
