# Dove (Totem)

> The white bird of peace, love, and the gentle spirit.

## Meaning
The dove symbolizes peace, love, hope, innocence, and the soul. It is the classic peace emblem (carrying an [[../botanical/olive-branch]]), the bird of Aphrodite/Venus (love), and in Christianity the form of the Holy Spirit. Released doves signify freedom, new beginnings, and reconciliation; a pair signifies devoted love (weddings).

## Origin
A universal symbol across Mesopotamian, Greco-Roman, Jewish, and Christian traditions (the dove of Noah's ark; the Holy Spirit). Fully PD. **Note**: strong Christian association — respectful in faith contexts. Avoid copying a specific live dove logo (beauty/charity brands). Design your own.

## Visual description
A white bird in flight with spread or upswept wings, often carrying an olive branch in its beak. A pair facing each other for love/weddings. Render in clean line, Picasso-style continuous line, watercolor, or paper-cut silhouette.
