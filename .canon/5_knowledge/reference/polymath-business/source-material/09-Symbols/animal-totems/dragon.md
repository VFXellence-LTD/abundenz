# Dragon (Totem)

> The mythic embodiment of power, wisdom, and primal force.

## Meaning
The dragon is one of the most powerful mythological symbols, but its meaning splits by tradition. In **East Asian** culture the dragon is benevolent — a serpentine bringer of rain, fortune, imperial power, wisdom, and cosmic balance. In **Western** tradition the dragon is often a fearsome, winged, fire-breathing guardian of treasure, an embodiment of chaos and greed to be slain by heroes. Both convey awesome power, the primal, and command over the elements.

## Origin
Ancient and near-universal (Chinese, European, Mesopotamian, Norse/wyrm, Mesoamerican). Fully PD. **Caution**: avoid copying a specific franchise dragon (film/game/book) or a live brand dragon — those designs are protected. Design your own.

## Visual description
**Eastern**: a long, sinuous, legged serpent with antlers/whiskers, no wings, often clutching a pearl. **Western**: a stout, winged, reptilian beast with horns, claws, and fire. Render in tribal blackwork, ornate scales, geometric, ink-wash (Eastern), or fantasy illustration.
