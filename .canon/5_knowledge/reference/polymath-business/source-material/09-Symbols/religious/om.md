# Om / Aum (ॐ)

> The primordial sound — Hinduism's most sacred symbol. Treat with deep respect.

## Meaning
Om (Aum, ॐ) is the most sacred syllable and symbol in Hinduism, also revered in Buddhism, Jainism, and Sikhism. It represents the primordial sound of creation, the Absolute (Brahman), the union of body-mind-spirit, and the essence of ultimate reality. Its three curves are often said to represent the waking, dreaming, and deep-sleep states, with the dot and crescent representing the absolute beyond. It is chanted at the start and end of prayers and meditation.

## Origin
Ancient, rooted in the Vedas and Upanishads of India (over 3,000 years old). The written form is in Devanagari script. Fully PD as a form, but it is a **living, deeply sacred symbol** in active worship and meditation worldwide.

## Visual description
A stylized Devanagari character: a large lower curve (like a "3"), an upper smaller curve, a tail/hook extending to the right from the middle, and a crescent with a dot (bindu) floating above. Render in gold line for devotional contexts, or simply and respectfully for yoga/meditation art.
