# Lotus

> The flower that rises pure from the mud — enlightenment, rebirth, and spiritual awakening.

## Meaning
The lotus rises from muddy water to bloom immaculate above the surface, making it a universal symbol of purity, spiritual awakening, enlightenment, rebirth, and rising above adversity. In Hinduism and Buddhism it is profoundly sacred — the seat of deities (Vishnu, Lakshmi, the Buddha), the unfolding of the soul, and the path to enlightenment ("Om mani padme hum" — the jewel in the lotus). In Egypt the blue lotus symbolized the sun and rebirth.

## Origin
Ancient and sacred across India, Egypt, and Asia, central to Hindu, Buddhist, and Egyptian symbolism for millennia. Fully PD. **Note**: it is a **living sacred symbol** in Hinduism and Buddhism — frame respectfully, though it is broadly used in wellness/yoga decor with low friction. Avoid copying a specific artwork or wellness-brand logo.

## Visual description
A radially symmetric water flower with layered pointed petals opening from a central seed-pod, rising on a tall stem from water, often with broad floating leaves. Bud, opening, and full-bloom stages. Render as watercolor bloom, geometric line, mandala-center, or minimalist outline.
