# Olive Branch

> The ancient offering of peace, reconciliation, and goodwill.

## Meaning
The olive branch is the classic symbol of peace, reconciliation, victory (the olive crown), abundance, and goodwill — "to extend an olive branch" means to offer peace. Sacred to Athena (who gave the olive to Athens), it has signified peace and prosperity since antiquity, and in the Genesis flood narrative the dove returns with an olive leaf as a sign of restored peace between God and humanity.

## Origin
Ancient Mediterranean (Greek, Roman, Hebrew) peace symbolism. Fully PD as a generic plant. **Caution**: the olive branch is central to the **United Nations emblem** and various national/official seals — design a generic branch; never imitate the UN emblem or a national seal (these are protected).

## Visual description
A slender branch with narrow, pointed leaves arranged along the stem, often bearing small olives. May be a single branch, a pair (peace offering), or a wreath/crown. Render as Mediterranean line art, botanical minimalism, or paired with a [[../animal-totems/dove]].
