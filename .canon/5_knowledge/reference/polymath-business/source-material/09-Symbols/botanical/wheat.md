# Wheat Sheaf

> The golden harvest — abundance, nourishment, and the fruit of the earth.

## Meaning
Wheat symbolizes harvest, abundance, fertility, prosperity, nourishment, and the cyclical bounty of the earth. The sheaf (a bundle of cut stalks) signifies the gathered harvest, plenty, and community provision. Associated with grain goddesses (Demeter/Ceres) and with the zodiac sign [[../astrological/virgo]] (the maiden with the wheat), it carries warm, rustic, abundant connotations.

## Origin
A universal agricultural symbol across cultures since the dawn of farming. Fully PD. **Note**: wheat appears on some national emblems and currency — design a generic sheaf; avoid imitating an official seal. Generic wheat is free.

## Visual description
A bundle of upright stalks tied at the base, each topped by a grain head with fine arching awns (bristles). Single ears, a tied sheaf, or a wreath of wheat. Render in golden-field, rustic-farmhouse, vintage-engraving, or clean line.
