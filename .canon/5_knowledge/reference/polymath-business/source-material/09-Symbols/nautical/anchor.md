# Anchor

> The classic mark of stability, hope, and steadfastness against the storm.

## Meaning
The anchor symbolizes stability, steadfastness, safety, grounding, and — especially — hope. It holds a ship firm against wind and current, making it an emblem of staying grounded amid turmoil. Early Christians used it as a covert symbol of hope and faith (the anchor cross). In tattoo tradition it signifies a sailor's stability, a safe return home, and loyalty ("anchored" to loved ones).

## Origin
A maritime symbol as old as seafaring itself, with the anchor-as-hope meaning rooted in antiquity (the Letter to the Hebrews calls hope "an anchor for the soul"). Fully PD. Iconic in nautical and traditional-tattoo design. Avoid imitating a specific apparel or naval anchor logo.

## Visual description
A vertical shank ending in two curved flukes (arms) at the bottom, a horizontal stock (crossbar) near the top, and a ring at the very top for the chain/rope. Often wrapped with rope. Render in traditional-tattoo style (with a banner), navy stripes, or clean line.
