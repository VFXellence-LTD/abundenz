# Compass Rose

> The mariner's star of directions — guidance, navigation, and the call to explore.

## Meaning
The compass rose displays the cardinal (N, E, S, W) and intermediate directions, symbolizing navigation, guidance, exploration, finding one's way, and purpose. It is the emblem of the traveler and adventurer — "knowing your direction" both literally and metaphorically ("find your true north"). Hugely popular as a wanderlust and tattoo motif.

## Origin
Developed on medieval portolan charts and compasses (c. 1300s) in the Mediterranean, evolving from the older "wind rose." Traditionally the North point is marked with a [[../heraldic/fleur-de-lis]] and East with a cross. Centuries old and fully PD. Avoid copying a specific ornate artwork.

## Visual description
A radiating star with points for the cardinal and ordinal (and often half-ordinal) directions — 4, 8, 16, or 32 points — alternating long and short rays, centered on a hub, frequently labeled N/E/S/W. North often carries a fleur-de-lis. Render in vintage-map, nautical-tattoo, or clean modern styles.
