# Eye of Horus (Wedjat)

> The healed eye of the falcon god — protection, healing, and royal power.

## Meaning
The Eye of Horus (Wedjat or Udjat, "the whole/sound one") is the stylized eye of the falcon god Horus, lost and magically restored — making it a symbol of protection, healing, restoration, wholeness, and royal power. It was worn as a protective amulet and used to ward off evil. Its parts were also used to represent fractional measures. The closely related "Eye of Ra" emphasizes the sun god's fierce, protective power.

## Origin
Ancient Egyptian, dating from the Old Kingdom onward, ubiquitous on amulets, jewelry, and tomb art. Fully PD. **Important distinction**: it is NOT the same as the "Eye of Providence" (eye in a triangle), which carries Masonic and conspiracy-theory connotations (see [[../religious/eye-of-providence]]). The Eye of Horus is a distinct, low-risk ancient symbol.

## Visual description
A stylized human/falcon eye: an almond eye with a pupil, an eyebrow above, a vertical teardrop line below the inner corner, and a curling spiral "tail" extending from the outer-lower corner (the falcon's cheek marking). Render in gold-on-black, hieroglyphic line, or blackwork tattoo. The Eye of Ra variant may add flames or solar rays.
