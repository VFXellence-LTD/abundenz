# Was Scepter

> The staff of power and dominion carried by Egypt's gods and kings.

## Meaning
The was scepter is the ancient Egyptian symbol of power, dominion, and divine authority — and also connotes well-being and control over the forces of chaos. Carried by gods, pharaohs, and priests, it represented command over the cosmos and the maintenance of order (ma'at). Together with the [[ankh]] (life) and [[djed]] (stability), it forms a classic triad of Egyptian power concepts.

## Origin
Ancient Egyptian, appearing throughout dynastic art in the hands of deities and rulers. Fully PD. A "deeper cut" than the ankh or eye, useful when you want a less-overexposed Egyptian motif. Respect Kemetic use.

## Visual description
A long straight staff with a forked (two-pronged) base and a stylized canine/animal head (often interpreted as a Set-animal or jackal) curving at the top. Render in hieroglyphic line, gold regalia styling, or as inspiration for a fantasy staff design.
