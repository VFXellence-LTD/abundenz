# Star of David (Hexagram)

> The Magen David — a living symbol of Jewish faith and identity. Treat with respect.

## Meaning
The Star of David (Magen David, "Shield of David") is a six-pointed star formed by two interlocking equilateral triangles. As pure geometry the hexagram is ancient and appears in many cultures. **In practice, however, the six-pointed star is overwhelmingly read as the primary symbol of Judaism and of the State of Israel** — a living religious and ethnic identity marker. The interlocking triangles are also read as the union of opposites (the upward [[../elemental/fire]] and downward [[../elemental/water]] triangles; "as above, so below").

## Origin
The hexagram as geometry is ancient and cross-cultural. Its specific identification with the Jewish people developed from the medieval period and became the dominant Jewish symbol from the 17th–19th centuries, later placed on the flag of Israel. It also carries the painful history of the Nazi-imposed yellow badge — another reason to handle with the utmost care.

## Visual description
Two overlapping equilateral triangles (one pointing up, one down) forming a six-pointed star with a hexagon at the center. Often drawn as a clean interlaced "woven" star.

## Emotional tags

## Design & story applications
- **Editorial/story/video/blog**: appropriate when discussing Judaism, Jewish history, or as authentic representation in respectful storytelling.
- **POD / merch / decoration**: **avoid** unless producing a genuine, respectfully-made Jewish-faith product with appropriate authenticity and sensitivity. Never use it as a generic decorative "star," never on gag or political products.
