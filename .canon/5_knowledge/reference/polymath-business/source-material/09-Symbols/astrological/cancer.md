# Cancer (The Crab)

> The cardinal water sign — nurturing, protective, ruled by the moon.

## Meaning
Cancer (June 21 – July 22) is a cardinal water sign ruled by the Moon, symbolized by the Crab. It signifies home, nurturing, emotion, protection, intuition, and memory. Keywords: caring, sensitive, protective, nostalgic, home-loving.

## Origin
Babylonian/Greek zodiac. The glyph (two curled spirals resembling a sideways "69" — the crab's claws or a yin-yang of two interlocking forms) is ancient and PD. Modern illustration sets are their own.

## Visual description
The glyph is two curled spirals, often described as a sideways 69, suggesting two crab claws or two interlocking circles. Illustrative versions: a crab, a crab beneath the moon, or the constellation. Pairs naturally with the [[../celestial/crescent-moon]].
