# Capricorn (The Sea-Goat)

> The cardinal earth sign — disciplined ambition climbing toward mastery.

## Meaning
Capricorn (December 22 – January 19) is a cardinal earth sign ruled by Saturn, symbolized by the Sea-Goat (a goat with a fish's tail). It signifies ambition, discipline, structure, perseverance, authority, and long-term mastery. Keywords: determined, responsible, patient, ambitious, pragmatic.

## Origin
Babylonian/Greek zodiac; the sea-goat derives from the Mesopotamian god Enki/Ea and the myth of Pan diving into a river. The glyph (a stylized "V" flowing into a looping fish-tail) is ancient and PD. Modern illustration sets are their own.

## Visual description
The glyph blends an angular "V" (goat horns/head) into a curling loop (fish tail). Illustrative versions: a goat with a fish tail, a mountain goat climbing a peak, or the constellation. Mountain-goat styling emphasizes the "climb to the summit" ambition theme.
