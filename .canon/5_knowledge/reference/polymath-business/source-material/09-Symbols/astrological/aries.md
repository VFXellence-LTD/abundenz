# Aries (The Ram)

> The cardinal fire sign — first of the zodiac, the pioneer's spark.

## Meaning
Aries (March 21 – April 19) is the first zodiac sign, a cardinal fire sign ruled by Mars and symbolized by the Ram. It signifies initiative, courage, leadership, impulse, raw energy, and new beginnings — the assertion of the self. Keywords: bold, pioneering, competitive, headstrong.

## Origin
The Babylonian zodiac, transmitted through Greek astronomy, assigned the Ram to this constellation. The glyph (stylized ram horns) is ancient and PD. Specific modern zodiac illustration sets are their own copyrighted works.

## Visual description
The glyph is a "V" or "Y" shape with the two arms curling outward at the top — stylized ram horns. Illustrative versions show a ram's head in profile, a charging ram, or the Aries constellation as connected stars. Clean minimal-line and blackwork-tattoo styles are popular.
