# Dracula

> The template for every vampire story since — and a reminder that the cape-and-fangs "classic vampire" you picture is a *movie*, not the book.

## Overview
Bram Stoker's 1897 epistolary novel assembled from journals, letters, telegrams, and news clippings. Solicitor Jonathan Harker travels to Count Dracula's Carpathian castle; the Count relocates to England, preying on Lucy and Mina, and is hunted by a band led by Professor Van Helsing. The text is fully public domain. The crucial caveat: **the iconic "Dracula look" (slick hair, opera cape, medallion, suave accent) is the 1931 Lugosi film, not Stoker.** Stoker's Count is an elderly, repellent figure — tall, thin, white-moustached, with hairy palms and pointed ears.

## Themes
- **The undead / immortality's curse** — life prolonged at others' expense.
- **Seduction and danger** — the erotic charge beneath the horror; repression and desire.
- **The foreign threat / invasion** — the outsider corrupting the homeland (a rich, complicated allegory to handle thoughtfully).
- **Science vs. superstition** — Van Helsing bridges both; blood transfusion meets folk wards.
- **Blood and contagion** — vampirism as a transmissible condition (very modern reading).

## Symbols
- **Blood** — life, contagion, kinship, transgression.
- **Bats and wolves** — the Count's animal forms and heralds.
- **The crucifix, garlic, the host** — wards; faith as a weapon.
- **Mirrors without reflection** — the vampire's lost soul.
- **The castle and the coffin** — the threshold between living and dead.

## Character archetypes
- **Count Dracula** — the Ancient Predator / corrupting aristocrat. Name PD; Lugosi *design* restricted.
- **Van Helsing** — the Wise Elder / monster-hunting polymath.
- **Mina Harker** — the Resourceful Heroine / nearly-lost victim.
- **The band of hunters** — the Loyal Fellowship.

## Visual motifs
- Carpathian castle on a crag, bat silhouettes against the moon.
- Foggy English moors, crumbling Whitby abbey, a derelict ship run aground.
- Victorian bedrooms, candelabra, garlic flowers strung at windows.

## Reusable concepts
- The **epistolary / found-document horror format** — diaries, letters, recordings; directly portable to podcasts and "found footage."
- The "band of ordinary people hunt an ancient evil" structure.
- Contagion-as-horror — the modern pandemic reframe.

## Risks
- **Copyright (text):** none — PD.
- **Adaptation trap:** Lugosi 1931 cape/medallion design RESTRICTED; Hammer (Christopher Lee) designs RESTRICTED; specific 1992 Coppola designs RESTRICTED. The 1922 *Nosferatu* film is PD but copy carefully.
- **Trademark / publicity:** Lugosi's likeness is protected (Lugosi v. Universal led to publicity-rights law). Do not depict him. "DRACULA" has live entertainment trademarks.
- **Sensitivity:** the "foreign invader" subtext warrants careful, modern handling.
