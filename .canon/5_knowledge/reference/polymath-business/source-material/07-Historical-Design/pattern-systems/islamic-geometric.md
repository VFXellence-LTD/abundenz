# Islamic Geometric Pattern (Girih)

> Endlessly tessellating star-and-polygon patterns built from compass-and-straightedge construction — the mathematical glory of Islamic art, expressing infinity and divine unity through pure geometry.

## Defining Features
Patterns generated from an underlying grid of regular polygons, from whose intersections radiate **stars** (6-, 8-, 10-, 12-, 16-pointed and beyond) connected by interlacing bands (**girih**, "knot"). The construction is rigorous — every line is justified by an invisible polygonal scaffold — yet the result reads as an effortless, seamless, infinitely-extendable field. Often built in two interlocking layers (a "background" tessellation and a "foreground" strapwork). Pattern implies continuation beyond the frame: a window onto the infinite.

## Visual Vocabulary
- **Star rosette** — the radiating n-pointed star, the focal node.
- **Girih strapwork** — the interlacing over-under bands linking stars.
- **Polygonal tessellation** — the tiling of regular polygons underneath.
- **Tile families** — recurring shapes (e.g., the classic five girih tiles: decagon, pentagon, bowtie, rhombus, hexagon).
- **Seamless field** — edge-matched for infinite repeat.

## Shapes
Regular polygons, n-pointed stars, rhombi, hexagons, interlacing bands; strict rotational and reflective symmetry; radial bursts; perfect tessellation. All straight-line geometry, no freehand.

## Textures
Glazed ceramic tile (zellij), carved stucco, pierced stone screens (jali/mashrabiya), inlaid wood and mother-of-pearl, brick patterning. Implies crisp flat color fields and clean line; the pierced-screen version implies light shining through negative space.

## Palette(s)
Zellij palette — cobalt blue, turquoise, white, green, ochre, terracotta, black; or monochrome stone; or single-color gold line on dark. High clarity favored so the geometry reads.

## Modern Remix
- **POD / apparel:** seamless star-tessellation all-over prints; girih-line tees.
- **Brand / motion:** an unfolding/rotating star pattern is a strong animated logo bumper.
- **Decor / merch:** "sacred geometry" poster series, laser-cut screen graphics, phone cases.
- **AI prompt:** *"seamless Islamic geometric girih pattern, interlacing 12-pointed star strapwork, cobalt and gold on white, perfect tessellation, no text."*

## Legal & Risk
Geometry is ancient and free. The real concerns are **cultural/religious respect** (these patterns adorn mosques; treat with dignity, never pair with anti-Islamic content) and **not cloning a specific living designer's artwork**. Respectful, freshly-constructed patterns are safe.
