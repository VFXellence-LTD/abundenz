## What it does

Product research platform showing real-time revenue data for e-commerce products. Surfaces which products are actually generating revenue (not just traffic), estimated sales volume, pricing trends, and market saturation signals. Cuts through vanity metrics to show what's actually converting.

## Where it fits in polymath

- **Conduit**: Product selection for affiliate promotion. Conduit's thesis requires promoting high-converting, high-commission products — Callodata tells you which products actually sell before you build content around them.
- **Atelier**: Research layer for KDP or digital product niches when Atelier activates. Validate demand before producing.

Better product research = higher conversion rate = higher affiliate revenue per pin. This is upstream of everything Conduit does.
