## What it does

Pinterest's business tier provides analytics, Rich Pins, ads manager, and audience targeting tools. Free business account unlocks analytics and pin scheduling. Paid ads layer adds promoted pins with CPC/CPM targeting — extremely granular by interest, keyword, and visual search.

Pinterest is a search engine as much as a social network. Pins have long half-lives (months to years) compared to Instagram or TikTok content (hours to days). Evergreen content performs disproportionately well here.

## Where it fits in polymath

- **Conduit**: Primary traffic driver. The core Conduit model (Pinterest → affiliate product → commission) lives here. Pinterest business account is non-optional infrastructure for Conduit.
- **Signal**: Secondary distribution platform. If Signal produces evergreen infographic or visual content, Pinterest can drive long-tail discovery without algorithm dependency.
- **Atelier**: Product discovery. Digital product pins can drive Etsy or Gumroad traffic when Atelier activates.

Pinterest Business account is free. The ads layer is pay-per-click — relevant for Conduit Phase 2+ when testing which niches convert.

## Pricing

- Business account: **free**
- Ads: CPC/CPM, budget-controlled. No minimum. Verify current minimums before adopting.

## Decision rule

- **Free business account**: Adopt immediately when Conduit begins. Zero cost, required infrastructure.
- **Pinterest Ads**: Adopt in Conduit Phase 2 when organic Pinterest strategy is validated and paid amplification makes sense.
