# Atelier: Marketplace AI Content Policies

**Last verified:** 2025-05-08

**IMPORTANT:** These policies change quarterly or faster. Before activating Atelier, re-verify every platform below. Do not rely on this document past 90 days from last-verified date.

---

## How to Re-Verify

1. Check platform's official seller/creator help center for "AI" or "artificial intelligence" in their content policies
2. Search the platform's seller community forums for recent threads on AI content removal
3. Check r/Etsy, r/selfpublishing, r/PrintOnDemand for recent policy enforcement reports
4. Google: `site:<platform>.com "AI generated" policy` filtered to past 3 months

---

## Etsy

**Current stance:** AI-generated products are allowed with mandatory disclosure.

**Disclosure requirements:**
- Sellers must disclose AI use in listing description and item specifics
- "Created with AI" or "AI-assisted" language required
- Etsy has a "Made with AI tools" category tag (use it)

**Restricted categories:**
- Custom portraits where a human's likeness is generated without their consent
- Products claiming to be handmade when fully AI-generated (Etsy's core handmade policy applies; digital products are exempt from the handmade requirement)
- Content that violates existing prohibited items policy (hate speech, deceptive claims, etc.)

**AI saturation risk:**
- Wall art, print categories are heavily saturated with AI content
- Etsy's algorithm reportedly deprioritizes shops with low review velocity — new shops need early sales and reviews
- Etsy periodically runs "quality review" sweeps that can de-list low-quality AI listings

**Practical notes:**
- Use mockup images that show product in realistic context (not just white-background file preview)
- Listing quality (photos, copy, tags) matters more than AI origin for discoverability
- Etsy Star Seller program not achievable until 95+ ratings and <24h message response — plan accordingly

## Amazon KDP

**Current stance:** AI content allowed with disclosure; human authorship still required on account.

**Disclosure requirements (as of 2025):**
- During KDP upload, there is a content disclosure form with checkboxes:
  - "AI-generated text" — check if text is primarily AI-written
  - "AI-generated images" — check if cover or interior images are AI-generated
  - "AI-assisted content" — check if AI tools assisted but human authored primary content
- Failure to disclose is grounds for account termination

**Content quality standards:**
- KDP actively reviews low-content books for quality; thin content can be de-listed
- Journal and planner interiors must have functional layouts, not just blank lines on repeat
- Coloring book pages must be distinct — not the same image tiled

**Duplicate content risk:**
- High-risk: generating near-identical interiors across multiple KDP listings (same page layouts, same prompts)
- KDP has flagged accounts for publishing hundreds of near-identical low-content books
- Strategy: vary interior designs meaningfully; don't run same Midjourney prompt 200 times

**Category restrictions:**
- Children's books with AI illustrations: requires disclosure; some reviewers flag inconsistencies
- Medical/health content: heightened scrutiny regardless of AI use
- Public domain content: AI-illustrated public domain works are generally fine

**Account risk:**
- KDP can terminate accounts for policy violations; this terminates ALL books, not just one
- Use a dedicated KDP account for Atelier; do not co-mingle with any personal author account
- Keep records of AI disclosures made at upload time

## Redbubble

**Current stance:** AI-generated art allowed; disclosure required via tags.

**Disclosure requirements:**
- Add "ai-art" and "ai-generated" tags to all AI-generated work
- Do not claim AI art as original hand-drawn artwork in listing copy

**Content restrictions:**
- No copyrighted characters, logos, or brand marks (standard across all POD)
- Redbubble has a quality review process; low-quality upscaled images get rejected
- Minimum resolution requirements enforced: images must meet print resolution at upload

**AI saturation:**
- Redbubble is heavily saturated with AI content
- Discoverability is difficult without external traffic (Pinterest, etc.)
- Best use case for Atelier: volume play with niche-specific designs that aren't already oversaturated

**Account risk:**
- Multiple DMCA strikes → account termination
- Redbubble is IP-enforcement aggressive; design anything that could be confused with a brand

---

## Society6

**Current stance:** AI art allowed; following similar rules to Redbubble.

**Disclosure requirements:**
- No formal tag requirement as of 2025, but do not misrepresent AI art as hand-created
- Society6 reviews uploads for quality; blurry or obviously low-effort AI output is rejected

**Notes:**
- Society6 has wider product range than Redbubble (furniture, rugs, shower curtains)
- Lower traffic than Redbubble; treat as secondary channel
- Artist commission is ~10% of sale price on most products — lower margin than Redbubble (~20%)

---

## Creative Market

**Current stance:** Nuanced; AI-assisted content may be allowed; AI-generated content scrutinized.

**Disclosure requirements:**
- Sellers must disclose AI use in product description
- Creative Market distinguishes "AI-assisted" (human designs enhanced by AI) from "AI-generated" (primarily AI-produced)

**Restrictions:**
- Pure AI-generated assets (no meaningful human creative input) have faced removal
- Creative Market's reviewer community has advocated for stricter AI limits
- Texture packs and pattern packs are gray area — verify at activation

**Practical assessment:**
- Higher risk platform for Atelier as of 2025
- Better to use Gumroad for digital asset sales where AI policy is permissive
- Creative Market is worth monitoring but not a primary channel at activation

---

## Canva Creator (Canva Elements Marketplace)

**Current stance:** Accepting submissions from creators; AI-generated elements have specific requirements.

**Requirements:**
- Elements must be submitted in vector or high-resolution format
- AI-generated images: Canva accepts with disclosure; must not include recognizable people or IP
- Creator program is invite-based (application required) — approval not guaranteed

**Revenue model:**
- Canva pays per use of element in Canva Pro accounts
- Revenue is typically low per use ($0.02-0.10 range) — requires high volume of good elements
- Better for Canva template sales (Etsy links to Canva templates) than the Creator program itself

**Practical assessment:**
- Low-priority channel for Atelier; high approval friction and low per-use revenue
- Canva templates sold on Etsy as "editable Canva templates" are better use of Canva in the Atelier workflow

## Adobe Stock

**Current stance:** RESTRICTED — AI-generated content banned from contributor program.

**Status as of 2025:**
- Adobe Stock suspended acceptance of AI-generated images mid-2024
- Contributor accounts with AI-generated content have been flagged and suspended
- This applies to any image "substantially generated" by AI tools (Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, Flux, etc.)

**Action:** Do NOT use Adobe Stock as a channel for Atelier. Remove from consideration entirely unless policy reversal is confirmed.

---

## Shutterstock

**Current stance:** RESTRICTED — AI-generated content banned from contributor program.

**Status as of 2025:**
- Shutterstock stopped accepting AI-generated content for contributor upload
- Existing AI-uploaded content has been removed from many accounts
- Shutterstock has its own AI image generation tool (partnered with Getty and OpenAI's DALL-E) — they want to control AI content in their marketplace, not have contributors submit external AI work

**Action:** Do NOT use Shutterstock as a channel for Atelier.

---

## Getty Images / iStock

**Current stance:** BANNED — no AI-generated content accepted.

**Action:** Do NOT use Getty/iStock as a channel for Atelier.

---

## Gumroad

**Current stance:** No AI content restrictions. Permissive platform.

**Notes:**
- Gumroad takes 10% of sales (flat fee, no tiered pricing as of 2025)
- Good for digital downloads, template packs, asset bundles
- No built-in discovery; requires external traffic (Pinterest, own site, email)
- Lower friction for selling than marketplace platforms — direct link to product page

---

## Printful / Printify (POD Providers, not Marketplaces)

**Current stance:** No AI content restrictions. They print what you upload.

**Notes:**
- Printful and Printify are fulfillment providers, not marketplaces
- They do not police AI-generated artwork — that's the marketplace's job (Etsy, Shopify, etc.)
- Responsibility for policy compliance falls on the seller using the marketplace
