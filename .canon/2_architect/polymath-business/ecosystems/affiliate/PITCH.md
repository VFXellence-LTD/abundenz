# Conduit — Elevator Pitch

## The Problem

Millions of people search "best [product] for [need]" every day. They want a recommendation, not a catalog. Most affiliate sites are either SEO-gamed garbage or require a human to research and write every article. Both approaches cap out: garbage gets penalized, humans get tired.

## The Solution

**Conduit** is a fully autonomous affiliate income machine. AI scouts trending products on Amazon and digital marketplaces, mines real buyer reviews for marketing angles, generates Pinterest pins and blog posts recommending those products, posts them on schedule, and tracks what converts. Commissions flow from clicks. The operator reviews a weekly batch for 30 minutes and adjusts. That's it.

## Why This Works

Pinterest is the last major platform where affiliate links still drive organic purchase traffic. Buyers search with intent — they're already looking to buy. Conduit meets them with recommendations at exactly the right moment. No audience required. No followers needed. No brand to build. Search → find → click → buy → commission.

## The Business

Start with Amazon Associates (1-10% commission on physical products) to learn traffic patterns. Pivot to Clickbank and Digistore24 (30-75% commission on digital products) once conversion data validates niche selection. Scale by adding niches — each niche is a duplicated pipeline, not more work.

## The Edge

- **Near-zero startup cost.** Pinterest business account: free. Affiliate programs: free. Blog hosting: $9/mo. AI tools: existing Claude API.
- **95% autonomous.** Human input = 30 minutes/week reviewing a batch.
- **Uncorrelated risk.** No personal brand means no reputation exposure. No inventory means no capital at risk. Fails cleanly.
- **Horizontal scaling.** Adding a niche = duplicating the pipeline. Kill losers, compound winners.

## Startup Cost

$0-9/month. Everything is free tier at launch. Make.com ($9/mo) when automation volume warrants it. Revenue potential: first affiliate click within days of posting.

## Why Not Now

Conduit is parked until Signal reaches Phase 2. Note: Viral/Surge is now active as the second ecosystem, so Conduit activation depends on operator bandwidth after both Signal and Surge are stable. The operator's attention is the scarcest resource. Building Conduit before Signal is stable would starve both.

## One Line

> Fully automated affiliate income — AI finds the products, writes the recommendations, posts to Pinterest, and deposits commissions. The operator just watches the dashboard.
