# Verdanz Botanical

> Forest, leaf, sage, and new growth — a fresh analogous green system for botanical illustration, garden, and wellness work.

## Swatches & roles

| Role | Hex | Name | Notes |
|------|-----|------|-------|
| Deep | `#1E3D2F` | Forest Pine | Dark green; shadow, line-work, type |
| Primary | `#3E6B4A` | Leaf | Dominant healthy green |
| Secondary | `#6FA177` | Sage | Mid green; foliage body |
| Accent | `#A9C99B` | New Growth | Light green; young leaves, highlights |
| Tint | `#D8E6C5` | Soft Moss | Pale green; panels, backgrounds |
| Background | `#FBFDF7` | Paper Leaf | Warm-cool white ground |

## Structure

A cool-to-warm **analogous green ramp** from deep forest pine to soft moss, on a near-white ground. The single-family hue range keeps it harmonious and natural; the value spread gives enough range to render full botanical scenes (shadowed undergrowth to sunlit new leaves).

## Mood & emotional read

Fresh, natural, calming, healthy, organic. Reads as plants, gardens, wellness, sustainability, and renewal. Wholesome and trustworthy. Strong for plant-shop, wellness, eco, and gentle-nature brand voices, and for botanical wall art.

## Usage notes

Paper leaf is the ground; leaf and sage carry the foliage; forest pine handles line-work and type (botanical-illustration style loves fine dark-green lines on cream). New growth and soft moss are for highlights and soft panels. This palette is *almost monochromatic*, so it benefits from either (a) a single complementary floral accent, or (b) confident botanical line-art to provide visual interest. Proportion: greens dominant, white ground generous.

## Pairs with (sections / categories)

Botanical and plant-themed merch, garden/wellness brands, [[13-Visual-Motifs/botanical-plate]], pressed-flower and herbarium aesthetics. Pairs with [[terrafolk-earthen]] (nature), [[dreamz-pastel]] and [[bloomz-spring-pastel]] (gentle/seasonal), and feeds children's-book nature scenes.

## POD / print considerations

- **CMYK**: `partial` — bright/vivid greens are a classic **CMYK weak spot**; the most saturated leaf greens can print slightly duller or shift toward olive. Mid and dark greens (leaf, forest pine) reproduce well. For the lightest tints, add a hair of saturation so they don't vanish. Botanical *line-art* in forest pine prints crisply and is the safest expression of this palette.
- **Apparel**: Excellent on white/natural and on sage/olive garments (tonal, premium). On black, forest pine disappears — lead with sage and new growth as the visible greens. Single-color forest-pine line-art on a natural tee is a clean, cheap-to-print, premium-looking option.
- **Accessibility**: Forest pine on paper leaf passes AA for text. Sage/new growth on white is decorative only.

## Remix hooks

- Floral pop: add `#E8889B` blush or `#FF7A4D` coral for flowers against the green.
- Moody jungle: deepen toward forest, add a near-black `#0F2018` for dense shadow.
- Luxe botanical print: add `#C9A227` gold line-art for an upscale herbarium look.

## Legal / risk

Greens are generic and unownable — safe across all uses here. Two narrow trademark notes that **do not apply** to our use cases: John Deere's specific green-and-yellow is protected only for agricultural machinery, and Starbucks' green is tied to its coffee-retail branding. Neither restricts botanical art, apparel, or wellness design. Use freely; just keep any logo/wordmark original.
