# Gatzby Deco

> The Jazz-Age look: lacquer black, brass gold, and peacock teal — Art Deco glamour distilled into a tri-tone.

## Swatches & roles

| Role | Hex | Name | Notes |
|------|-----|------|-------|
| Primary | `#0B0B0B` | Lacquer Black | Dominant ground; the deco "frame" |
| Accent 1 | `#C9A227` | Brass Gold | Lines, frames, type, sunbursts |
| Accent 2 | `#0E6E6E` | Peacock Teal | The "color" — fills and feature shapes |
| Background (light) | `#F4EFE6` | Ivory Card | Cream alternative ground for invitations/posters |

## Structure

A **tri-tone** (three working colors plus an optional cream ground). Black + gold is the classic high-contrast luxury pairing; teal is the saturated jewel accent that makes it specifically *Deco* rather than generic. Gold and teal are roughly complementary, giving the palette its lively tension.

## Mood & emotional read

Glamour, prosperity, celebration, sophistication. Reads instantly as "1920s / luxury / nightlife / premium". Confident and decorative without being gaudy if gold is used as line, not fill.

## Usage notes

Black is the field, gold is the **line and type** (thin geometric rules, sunburst rays, frames), teal is the **fill** for feature shapes. Classic ratio: ~70% black, ~20% teal, ~10% gold. Keep everything geometric and symmetrical — Deco is about precise line-work. For light layouts, swap the black field for ivory card and use black as the line color.

## POD / print considerations

- **CMYK**: Gold prints as flat ochre — use **gold foil** for true metallic on premium products (foil + black is the signature Deco print effect). Peacock teal sits near the cyan-green boundary and can shift; build it with strong cyan + a little black to keep it deep, not turquoise.
- **Apparel**: Outstanding on black garments (gold + teal pop). On light shirts, print the black as the design itself (line-art deco motifs) rather than a full black field. Foil-print or metallic vinyl gold is a strong premium tee option.
- **Accessibility**: Gold-on-black large type only; for body text use ivory `#F4EFE6` on black.

## Remix hooks

- Jewel-deco: swap teal for `#0F6B3C` emerald.
- Streamline Moderne: replace gold with `#C0C6CC` silver for a cooler, machine-age feel.
- Foil/stamp reduction: drop teal, keep black + gold only for two-color foil work.

## Legal / risk

**Style is free; the name is the trap.** Art Deco is a public-domain *movement* — no one owns it, so the colors and geometry are safe to use commercially. However: do not market this palette (or products) under the name "Gatsby" or with imagery from any specific copyrighted film/poster. The novel *The Great Gatsby* entered US public domain in 2021 (text only), but the title can still operate as a trademark for certain goods, and the famous Art Deco *movie posters* are separately copyrighted. "Gatzby Deco" is an internal codename; sell it as "Jazz-Age black, gold & teal".
