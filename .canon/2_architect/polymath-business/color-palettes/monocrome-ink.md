# Monocrome Ink

> Pure ink to pure paper through four greys — the most flexible, most print-safe, most timeless system in the library.

## Swatches & roles

| Role | Hex | Name | Notes |
|------|-----|------|-------|
| Primary / Ink | `#000000` | Ink Black | Type, line-art, the design itself |
| Dark | `#3A3A3A` | Charcoal | Deep shadow, heavy fills |
| Mid | `#6E6E6E` | Graphite | Secondary tone, mid shadow |
| Light | `#A6A6A6` | Ash | Soft tone, subtle structure |
| Tint | `#D6D6D6` | Mist | Pale panels, halftone, light shading |
| Paper | `#FFFFFF` | Paper White | The ground |

## Structure

A neutral **monochrome / grayscale ramp** in six even steps. No hue at all, so it never clashes with anything, reproduces perfectly, and works as either a complete look (classic ink illustration) or a foundation to drop one accent color onto.

## Mood & emotional read

Classic, clean, serious, timeless, editorial. Reads as ink drawing, woodcut, fine print, and considered minimalism. Reads "premium" through restraint. Endlessly versatile — the safe choice when in doubt, and the strongest base for a single bold accent.

## Usage notes

Two main modes: **(1) full grayscale** — render the whole piece in ink and greys (line-art, halftone, silhouette, ink-wash); **(2) base + accent** — keep everything grey/black and add exactly one spot color for the focal point ("minimal with a pop"). Ink black does type and outlines; greys build tone and depth; mist is for subtle shading. Embrace negative space. Proportion driven by the artwork.

## POD / print considerations

- **CMYK**: The single safest palette here. **Pure black single-color print is the cheapest, most reliable, most consistent POD option** — no registration issues, no color drift, works on every product and process. Greys print as black halftones/tints and reproduce predictably.
- **Apparel**: Works on **everything**. On light garments: black ink design. On dark garments: invert to white/light-grey ink (white ink prints opaque on black). The grey midtones are the only watch-out on dark fabric — very light greys can disappear into a black shirt, so keep enough value separation. Single-color (black on light / white on dark) is the lowest-cost, highest-reliability apparel print.
- **Accessibility**: Maximum flexibility — ink black on white is AAA; choose grey values with enough contrast for any text role.

## Remix hooks

- Minimal + pop: add ONE spot color (e.g. `#E03A3A` red, `#F2B705` yellow, `#1E6FA8` blue) for the focal element.
- Warm ink: warm all greys toward brown for an ink-on-cream vintage feel (see [[sepiaz-vintage]]).
- Night mode: invert to white-on-black.

## Legal / risk

Grayscale is the most generic and unownable palette possible — completely safe in every category and application. There is no trademark or copyright concern whatsoever. Recommended as the **default starting palette** when legal caution is the priority and color isn't essential to the concept.
