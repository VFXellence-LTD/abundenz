# Frostz Winter

> Deep night-blue to icy white through frost-blues — a cool, crisp, slightly magical winter system.

## Swatches & roles

| Role | Hex | Name | Notes |
|------|-----|------|-------|
| Deep | `#0E2A47` | Winter Night | Dark navy; the cold night sky / ground |
| Primary | `#2E5E8C` | Glacier | Dominant cool blue |
| Secondary | `#6FA3C7` | Frost Blue | Mid icy blue; structure |
| Accent | `#A9CCE3` | Ice | Light blue; highlights, snow shadow |
| Tint | `#DCEBF5` | Snow Tint | Pale blue-white; soft snow |
| Highlight | `#FBFEFF` | Snow White | Cool white; sparkle, type, deepest snow |

## Structure

A cool **monochrome ramp** in the blue family (with the lightest steps reading as snow-white), stepped from winter night to snow white. Like a cool sibling of [[valkyrz-cold-steel]] but hue-blue rather than grey, and brighter at the top. Cohesive and calm, with enough range for full snowy scenes.

## Mood & emotional read

Cold, crisp, serene, clean, quietly magical. Reads as winter, snow, ice, stillness, and the holidays (in its festive variant). Peaceful and pure at the light end, deep and wintry at the dark end. Strong for seasonal, holiday, and "calm/clean" content.

## Usage notes

Winter night is the ground for night scenes; snow white/snow tint dominate for bright snowy scenes. Glacier and frost blue build the cold mid-tones; ice for the soft shadows on white snow. For type, use winter night on light grounds or snow white on dark. Sparse white sparkle/snowflakes against the dark end create the "magical winter" effect. Proportion follows day-vs-night scene.

## Pairs with (sections / categories)

Seasonal and holiday merch, winter/Christmas printables and cards, snow-globe and ski aesthetics, [[13-Visual-Motifs/snowflakes]] and [[13-Visual-Motifs/frost-fractals]]. Relatives: [[valkyrz-cold-steel]] (cold steel), [[abyssz-oceanic]] (cool blues), [[monocrome-ink]] (neutral base).

## POD / print considerations

- **CMYK**: Cool blues reproduce well and predictably — a print-friendly palette. The very pale tints (snow tint, ice) are sensitive at low coverage and can look patchy; specify a touch more value so snow doesn't disappear into white paper. Snow white on a white product is *invisible* — only meaningful against the darker blues.
- **Apparel**: Beautiful on **navy/blue and white garments**. On white tees, lead with glacier/frost blue as the visible color and use winter night for line/type; snow white needs a dark fill behind it to register. On black, the blues pop coolly and white sparkle reads as snow. For premium holiday merch, **silver foil** on navy is a strong upgrade.
- **Accessibility**: Winter night on snow tint/white passes AA. Frost blue / ice on white is decorative only — too low contrast for words.

## Remix hooks

- Holiday variant: add deep evergreen `#1E3D2F` and a single `#C8102E` red for Christmas.
- Premium: add silver/foil (`#C7CED3` → highlight) for an icy-luxe look.
- Cozy cabin: warm slightly and add a single amber `#E2A23B` window-glow against the cold blue.

## Legal / risk

Icy blues and whites are generic and unownable — safe across all uses and categories. No brand owns "winter blue". The only practical note: for holiday/seasonal products, keep characters, logos, and artwork original (don't reproduce a brand's mascot or a specific film's snow scene). The palette itself is free to use.
