# Pinterest Growth Strategy

Long-form content strategy for organic traffic. Pins are indexed by Google, drive long-tail SEO traffic with compounding reach.

---

## Cold Start (Month 1-3): Build Presence

### Board Strategy
Create 5-8 boards: Top Products, Unboxings, Comparison Guides, Tips & Tricks, Gift Ideas.

- 20-30 pins on top board in Month 1
- Pin dimensions: 1000x1500px optimal

### Pin Creation
- Design: Text overlay + product image + branding
- Tools: Canva Pro, Adobe Express, or Figma
- Volume: 30-50 pins/month (Month 1-3)

### Descriptions (SEO)
- 300-500 chars, keyword-rich
- Include affiliate link in description
- Hook + benefit + CTA structure
- 2-3 target keywords naturally embedded

---

## Growth Phase (Month 3-6)

- 3-5 pins daily (mix new + repins from relevant creators)
- Same time each day (6-9am, 12-2pm, 6-8pm)
- Batch: create 30 pins on Sunday, schedule throughout month
- Repin top-performing content from 30-50 followed creators (3-5/day)

### Analytics
- Track outbound clicks per pin
- Pins with 1k+ impressions: repin and boost
- Pins with <100 impressions in 30 days: archive

---

## Monetization Phase (Month 6+)

**High-value pin types**:
- Comparison pins: "[Product A] vs [Product B]" — high intent
- Best-of lists: "5 Best [category] under $100"
- Tutorial pins: "How to [use product]"
- Seasonal: "Gift ideas for...", "Black Friday [category]"

**Revenue targets**:
- Month 3: 1-5k views, 0-5 clicks/month
- Month 6: 10-50k views, 50-200 clicks/month
- Month 9: 50-200k views, 200-1k clicks/month
- Month 12: 100k+ views, 1k+ clicks/month

---

## Design Best Practices

- Contrast: text readable on mobile (80% of views)
- Font: bold, large (minimum 40pt)
- Image: high quality, no blur
- Branding: logo in corner
- First 100 chars: compelling before click

---

## Content Strategy

- 90% evergreen (not "2025 trend")
- Specific beats generic
- Appeal to pain point or aspiration
