# Niche Selection Framework

Decision framework for choosing product categories and content angles. Minimize risk, maximize revenue potential.

---

## Scoring Framework

Score each candidate niche on 6 dimensions (1-10), apply weights, sum. Target: 65+ total (6.5/10).

| Dimension | Weight | Definition |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Traffic Potential | 25% | Can this niche get 5k+ monthly views in 6 months? |
| Commission Rate | 20% | Affiliate commission on typical product |
| Competition | 20% | How many established creators are in this niche? |
| Product Availability | 15% | Are there 5+ affiliate products available? |
| Evergreen Demand | 10% | Does interest stay constant (not seasonal/fad)? |
| Passion / Authenticity | 10% | Could you promote this for 6 months without losing interest? |

## Validation Steps

1. **TikTok**: search `#[niche keyword]` — do top videos have 1k+ views? Creators posting daily?
2. **Google Trends**: Is interest growing, flat, or declining?
3. **Pinterest**: Are there pins with 1k+ saves?
4. **Commission**: Find 3-5 products, check Amazon Associates, TikTok Shop, brand portals. Target 10%+ avg.
5. **Competition**: Count creators with 5k+ followers. <10 = underserved, 10-50 = moderate, >50 = saturated.
6. **Differentiation**: What angle can you take that established creators don't? Budget vs premium, beginner vs pro, humor, etc.

## Decision Checklist

```
□ Traffic potential score: 7+ (from scoring framework)
□ Commission average: 10%+
□ Competition: <50 established creators
□ 5+ affiliate products available
□ Evergreen or known seasonal timeline
□ I'm genuinely interested in this topic
□ Differentiation angle identified
□ TikTok hashtag has 1k+ avg views on top posts
□ Pinterest equivalent shows niche demand

If all ✓: MOVE FORWARD
If any ✗: Reconsider or adjust niche angle
```

---

## When to Pivot

Revenue flat 2+ months, top products all <500 avg views, commission rates cut 50%+, algorithm shift, burnout. Transition gradually: 20% new niche, 80% old, test for 2-4 weeks.
