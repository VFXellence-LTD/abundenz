# Affiliate Pipeline

End-to-end monetization setup for Conduit / Zrodinger ecosystem. Flow: platform traffic → affiliate links → commissions.

---

## Affiliate Programs

### Amazon Associates (Day 1 — start here)
- Eligibility: instant approval, no follower minimum
- Commission: 1-10% depending on category (physical: 1-4.5%)
- Payout: monthly via bank transfer (min $10)
- Bridge monetization from day 1 while building to TikTok Shop

### TikTok Shop Affiliate (at 1K followers)
- Eligibility: 1,000+ followers, identity verification, US accounts
- Commission: 10-15% open collab, 18-25%+ targeted
- Payout: settles 15-31 days after delivery, ~$20 minimum, weekly/bi-weekly
- Steps: TikTok → Creator tools → TikTok Shop → Affiliate → identity verify → apply

### Brand Affiliate Programs
- Eligibility varies (sometimes 500 followers, sometimes none)
- Commission: 5-20%
- Apply via brand portal (ShareASale, Impact, or custom)

**High-commission categories**: SaaS/subscriptions 20-50%, Fashion 10-30%, Beauty/skincare 10-25%, Electronics 5-15%

---

## Link Strategy

**TikTok**: Product tags (native, shows preview) > pinned comment affiliate link > bio link
**Pinterest**: Include affiliate link in pin description. SEO-friendly.

---

## Revenue Projections

### TikTok (post 1K followers)
- 500-2k avg views/video → 2-5% CTR → 0.1-3 sales/video
- $20-50 commission/sale
- Early stage (3-5 posts/day): $3-20/day
- Month 3 (20k+ avg views): $50-300/day

### Pinterest (100k monthly views)
- 2-5% CTR = 2k-5k clicks/month
- 1-3% conversion = 20-150 sales/month
- Monthly: $400-7,500 (niche-dependent)

| Program | Payout Day | Min Amount |
|---------|-----------|------------|

---

## Compliance

**FTC**: All affiliate content must disclose `#ad` or `#affiliate`. Required by FTC Endorsement Guides.
**TikTok**: Allowed. Disclose in caption.
**Pinterest**: Allowed. Disclose in pin description.
**Amazon Associates**: Disclose "As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases."
