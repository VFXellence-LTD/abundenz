# Atelier Licensing & Legal Guide

What you can and cannot use for POD designs. Err on the side of caution — one DMCA takedown or trademark complaint can shut down the entire store.

---

## The Three Legal Risks

### 1. Trademark

=== NEVER USE TRADEMARKED CHARACTERS, LOGOS, OR TEAM MARKS ===

No Disney, Marvel, Warner Bros, NFL/NBA/MLB teams, band logos, brand logos. Fan art is not a legal defense.

### 2. Right of Publicity (Celebrity Likenesses)

=== LIVING CELEBRITIES ARE HIGH RISK — AVOID UNLESS CLEARLY TRANSFORMATIVE ===

- **Safe list** (100+ years dead, no active enforcement): Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Socrates, Plato, Shakespeare, Poe, Wilde, Twain, Lincoln, Ben Franklin, Da Vinci.
- **Gray zone** (50-100 years dead, some risk): Einstein (Hebrew University enforces), Gandhi, Churchill.
- **Hard no** (active estate enforcement): Bob Marley, Tupac, MLK, Marilyn Monroe, Jimi Hendrix, Steve Jobs, any living person.

### 3. Copyright (Photos, Artwork, Text)

- Photos are copyrighted by photographer's estate. Do not reproduce or directly derive.
- Song lyrics (even one line) = infringement.
- Movie/TV quotes = copyrighted screenplay.

**Safe**: Bible verses (public domain text), ancient philosopher quotes (public domain), original text, quotes from authors dead 70+ years (verify jurisdiction: life + 70 years).

| Design Element | Safe? | Notes |
|----------------|-------|-------|
| Original abstract art | ✅ | Always safe |
| Letter filled with generic objects | ✅ | No IP issues |

| Generic neon astronaut | ✅ | Generic symbols fine |
| Bible verse + cross | ✅ | Public domain text |
| Stoic quote + ancient portrait | ✅ | 2000+ years dead |

| Einstein quote + portrait | ⚠ RISKY | Hebrew University enforces likeness |
| MLK quote + portrait | ❌ NO | Estate extremely protective |
| Bob Marley as lion | ❌ NO | Estate enforces aggressively |
| NFL team skull design | ❌ NO | Trademark infringement |
| Batman skull | ❌ NO | Trademark + copyright |
| Generic skeleton kiss (original composition) | ✅ | Not derived from specific photo |

---

## Safe Strategy

1. Default to historical figures 100+ years dead
2. Use generic symbols (skulls, astronauts, animals, flowers, geometric shapes)
3. Write original quotes or use truly ancient/PD text
4. Create original compositions inspired by poses — not derivatives of specific photos
5. Never use trademarked characters, logos, or team marks
6. When in doubt, don't

---

## Platform Policies

**Etsy**: Repeat violations = permanent store ban. DMCA responses in 24-48h. No appeals for clear infringement.
**Printify/Printful**: Will remove flagged designs, may suspend account for patterns. Liability is on you.
**Redbubble**: Automated trademark filters for known marks. Most major IP holders NOT in fan art program.
