# Atelier Color Palettes

8 standard palettes applied across ALL product lines. Every design gets rendered in at least 3 palettes — multiply catalog size without new concepts.

---

## 1. Neon Vibrant
Bright fluorescent on black. Eye-catching, high contrast. Classic POD aesthetic.

Background: `#000000` | Primary: `#FF00FF` | Secondary: `#00FFFF` | Accent: `#FFFF00` | Highlight: `#FF4500` | Detail: `#39FF14`

**Best for**: Skulls, vibrant symbols, space themes. **Products**: T-shirts (dark), hoodies, posters, stickers.

---

## 2. Muted Earth
Warm, desaturated natural tones. Sophisticated, gender-neutral, premium feel.

Background: `#F5F0E8` | Primary: `#8B6F47` | Secondary: `#6B7B3A` | Accent: `#C4846C` | Highlight: `#D4A574` | Detail: `#4A5D4A`

**Best for**: Botanical, woodland, alphabet initials, quote portraits. **Products**: Wall art, nursery prints, tote bags, mugs.

---

## 3. Black & White
Pure monochrome. High contrast ink illustration.

Background: `#FFFFFF` | Primary: `#000000` | Range: `#333333` → `#CCCCCC`

**Best for**: Quote portraits, steampunk, skulls.

---

## 4. Monochrome Tinted
Single color family — Navy, Burgundy, Forest, Plum, Rust, or Slate. 5-6 shades from near-black to near-white.

**Best for**: Sophisticated single-color prints, alphabet initials, quote art.

---

## 5. Tri-Tone
Exactly three colors. Bold, graphic, poster-like.

Combos: Sunset (orange/cream/dark navy) | Ocean (blues) | Retro (red/cream/dark) | Jungle (greens) | Royal (purples) | Fire (red/gold/black)

**Best for**: Skulls, vibrant symbols, bold statement pieces.

---

## 6. Pastel
Soft, light, approachable, nursery-safe.

Background: `#FEFEFE` | Soft pink, green, blue, yellow, purple.

**Best for**: Alphabet initials (nursery market), botanical, whimsical/fantasy.

---

## 7. Vintage / Distressed
Aged, weathered. Yellowed paper tones, faded ink. Apply grain/noise texture overlay.

Background: `#F2E8D5` (aged paper) | Primary: `#3C2415` (dark sepia) | Secondary: `#8B6914` (faded gold)

**Best for**: Quote portraits, steampunk, skulls.

---

## 8. Watercolor
Soft bleeds, wet edges, translucent washes on white paper.

Background: `#FFFFFF` | Watercolor blue `#4A90D9`, rose `#E8737A`, green `#7BC67E`, gold `#F5C242`, violet `#9B7ED8`

**Best for**: Botanical, alphabet initials, animals, ocean themes.

---

## AI Generation Prompt Suffix

Append to every generation prompt:

```
Color palette: [palette name]. Use ONLY these colors: [hex list].
Background: [bg hex]. No other colors. High contrast. Print-ready,
clean edges, no watermark.
```
