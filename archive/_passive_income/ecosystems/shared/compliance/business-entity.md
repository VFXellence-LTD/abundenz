# Business Entity — VFXellence

## Overview

All Polymath ecosystems operate under **VFXellence**, a registered UK business. VFXellence is the holding entity — individual ecosystems use separate public brand names but share one legal/financial entity.

### What's shared (VFXellence level)
- Bank account
- Tax return (Self Assessment)
- Accountant relationship
- Domain registrar account
- Software subscriptions
- Legal counsel

### What's separate (ecosystem level)
- Brand names
- Domains
- Social media accounts
- Email addresses (public-facing)
- Payment processors (Stripe accounts or sub-accounts)
- Audience — no cross-promotion

## Financial routing

**All money flows through VFXellence business account:**
- Platform payouts (YouTube, Etsy, Amazon, etc.) → VFXellence bank
- Tool subscriptions → paid from VFXellence bank
- Freelancer payments → paid from VFXellence bank
- Tax payments → paid from VFXellence bank

**Dashboard tracks by ecosystem** for internal reporting. HMRC sees one business, one tax return. The ecosystem breakdown is for Boss's management, not for tax filing.

## Reactivation checklist

- [ ] Confirm business registration status with Companies House / HMRC
- [ ] Verify business bank account is active and accessible
- [ ] Update HMRC Self Assessment registration if needed (notify of trading resumption)
- [ ] File W-8BEN with US platforms (YouTube, Amazon, Etsy, Clickbank) for treaty benefits
- [ ] Set up accounting software or spreadsheet (FreeAgent, Xero, or manual)
- [ ] Book 30-min accountant call to confirm structure (sole trader vs Ltd)
- [ ] Register for VAT only when approaching £90k threshold

## When to create separate entities

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Single ecosystem > £25k/yr revenue | Consider separate Ltd for liability isolation |
| Total turnover > £90k/yr | Mandatory VAT registration |
| Lullaby Format B generates revenue | Open custodial account for daughter's share |
| Consulting clients require Ltd | Convert VFXellence to Ltd if not already |
| Any ecosystem faces legal action | Isolate that ecosystem into its own Ltd immediately |

## Account setup per ecosystem (when activating)

Each ecosystem needs at activation time:
1. Domain registered (paid by VFXellence)
2. Email address on that domain (Zoho free or Google Workspace)
3. Social media accounts under ecosystem brand name
4. Platform accounts (YouTube, Etsy, etc.) registered to ecosystem email
5. Payment processor (Stripe) connected to VFXellence bank but under ecosystem brand
6. W-8BEN filed with any US platform used

All costs deducted as VFXellence business expenses.
