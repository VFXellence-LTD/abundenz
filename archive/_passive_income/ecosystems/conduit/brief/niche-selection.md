# Niche Selection Framework

Decision framework for choosing product categories and content angles. Minimize risk, maximize revenue potential.

## Scoring Framework

### Evaluation Matrix

For each potential niche, score on these dimensions (1-10):

| Dimension | Weight | Definition | Scoring |
|-----------|--------|-----------|---------|
| **Traffic Potential** | 25% | Can this niche get 5k+ monthly views in 6 months? | Low (<2k views): 1-3; Medium (2-5k): 4-6; High (5k+): 7-10 |
| **Commission Rate** | 20% | Affiliate commission on typical product | <5%: 1-3; 5-10%: 4-6; 10%+: 7-10 |
| **Competition** | 20% | How many established creators are in this niche? | Saturated (100+): 1-3; Moderate (20-100): 4-6; Underserved (<20): 7-10 |
| **Product Availability** | 15% | Are there 5+ affiliate products available? | <3 products: 1-3; 3-7 products: 4-6; 7+ products: 7-10 |
| **Evergreen Demand** | 10% | Does interest stay constant (not seasonal/fad)? | Fad (interest dies): 1-3; Seasonal (spikes): 4-6; Evergreen: 7-10 |
| **Passion / Authenticity** | 10% | Could you promote this for 6 months without losing interest? | Low (forced): 1-3; Medium (okay): 4-6; High (excited): 7-10 |

**Scoring**: Multiply dimension score × weight. Sum all weighted scores. **Target: 65+ total**

### Example Scoring

**Niche A: AI Writing Tools**
- Traffic potential: 8/10 × 25% = 2.0
- Commission: 7/10 × 20% = 1.4
- Competition: 6/10 × 20% = 1.2
- Product availability: 9/10 × 15% = 1.35
- Evergreen demand: 9/10 × 10% = 0.9
- Passion: 9/10 × 10% = 0.9
- **Total: 7.65/10 = 76.5** ← GOOD

**Niche B: NFT Gaming**
- Traffic potential: 4/10 × 25% = 1.0
- Commission: 5/10 × 20% = 1.0
- Competition: 9/10 × 20% = 1.8
- Product availability: 3/10 × 15% = 0.45
- Evergreen demand: 2/10 × 10% = 0.2
- Passion: 6/10 × 10% = 0.6
- **Total: 5.05/10 = 50.5** ← RISKY (avoid)

## Niche Research Workflow

### Step 1: Identify Candidate Niches (Brainstorm)
List 10-15 product categories you could authentically promote:
- What do you use daily?
- What have you recommended to friends?
- What gaps do you see in market?

**Examples**: AI tools, home gadgets, fitness trackers, productivity apps, kitchen gear, pet supplies

### Step 2: Validate Traffic Potential
For each candidate, check:

**TikTok Traffic**:
1. Open TikTok, search "#[niche keyword]" (e.g., "#AIWritingTools")
2. Check top videos: Do they have 1k+ views? 5k+ views?
3. Check posting frequency: Are creators posting daily?
4. If yes to both → traffic potential exists

**Google Trends**:
1. Go to `trends.google.com`
2. Search "[niche keyword]" (e.g., "AI writing tools")
3. Check trend line: Is interest growing, flat, or declining?
4. Interest graph shows search volume over time

**Pinterest**: 
1. Search niche keyword
2. Note pins with 1k+ saves
3. If multiple pins with high saves → audience exists

### Step 3: Validate Commission Structure
For each top niche, identify 3-5 products and check commission:

| Platform | Commission Lookup |
|----------|---|
| Amazon Associates | associates.amazon.com → choose product → check commission % |
| TikTok Shop | Affiliate panel → browse products → commission shown |
| Brand programs | Search "[brand name] affiliate program" |
| ShareASale | affiliate.com → search brand → commission listed |

Create spreadsheet:
| Niche | Product A | Product B | Product C | Avg Commission |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| AI Writing | Grammarly (10%) | Jasper (20%) | Copy.ai (15%) | 15% avg |

**Goal**: Find niche with avg commission >10% (preferably 15%+)

### Step 4: Analyze Competition
For each shortlisted niche:

1. **Search "[niche] TikTok creators"**
   - Count creators with 5k+ followers
   - If <10 creators = underserved
   - If 10-50 creators = moderate competition
   - If >50 creators = saturated

2. **Check top creators' strategy**
   - What products do they promote?
   - How often do they post?
   - What's their engagement rate?

3. **Assess your differentiation**
   - Can you offer different angle? (e.g., budget version, professional angle, humor)
   - Unique hook? (e.g., "tested 10, ranked them")
   - Different audience? (e.g., beginners vs professionals)

### Step 5: Evaluate Evergreen Demand
Check if demand is stable year-round or seasonal:

| Niche | Demand Pattern | Timeline Impact |
|-------|---|---|
| AI Tools | Evergreen (growing) | Can start anytime |
| Gaming Gadgets | Holiday spike (Nov-Dec), summer | Start Sep or June for seasonality |
| Fitness Gadgets | Jan peak (resolutions), September | Start Nov or August to capture peaks |
| Holiday gifts | Dec peak | Start Aug for prep, capitalize |

**Rule**: Evergreen niches = safer, faster to $100/day. Seasonal = higher revenue spikes, but inconsistent.

## Final Decision Checklist

Before committing to niche:

```
□ Traffic potential score: 7+ (from scoring framework)
□ Commission average: 10%+ (from research)
□ Competition: <50 established creators
□ 5+ affiliate products available
□ Evergreen or known seasonal timeline
□ I'm genuinely interested in this topic
□ Differentiation angle identified (how I'll stand out)
□ TikTok hashtag has 1k+ avg views on top posts
□ Pinterest equivalent (if applicable) shows niche demand

If all ✓: MOVE FORWARD
If any ✗: Reconsider or adjust niche angle
```

## Niche Pivots & Multi-Niche Strategy

### When to Pivot (Red Flags)
- Revenue flat 2+ months
- Top 3 products all <500 avg views
- Commission rates cut 50%+ (brand program change)
- Algorithm heavily shifts away from niche
- Personal burnout (lost passion)

### Pivoting Process
1. Don't delete existing channel (followers stay)
2. Transition content gradually (20% new niche, 80% old)
3. Test new niche for 2-4 weeks (5-10 posts)
4. If new niche performs better (2x views): Full pivot
5. If performs worse: Resume old niche

### Multi-Niche Approach (Advanced)
Once primary niche reaches 10k followers:
1. **Leverage audience**: Launch second niche channel (same followers follow new account)
2. **Cross-promote**: Mention second channel in videos, bios
3. **Shared infrastructure**: Agent 02/03 handles both niches
4. **Time to 1K followers**: ~4 weeks (vs 4-8 weeks for first niche)
5. **Scale to 3-5 niches**: Each generates $1-5k/month independently

**Multi-niche math**:
- Niche 1: $2k/month
- Niche 2 (launched month 4): +$1k/month (month 6 onward)
- Niche 3 (launched month 8): +$1.5k/month (month 10 onward)
- **Total month 12**: $4.5k/month from Conduit ecosystem
