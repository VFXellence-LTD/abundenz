# Pinterest Growth Strategy

Long-form content strategy for organic traffic. Pins are indexed by Google, drive long-tail SEO traffic with compounding reach.

## Cold Start (Month 1-3): Build Presence

### Channel Setup
- **Profile type**: Creator account
- **Profile name**: Clear, keyword-rich (e.g., "AI Gadget Reviews")
- **Bio**: 160 chars, keyword-focused (e.g., "Latest AI tools, gadget reviews & unboxings. Tested & recommended.")
- **Profile image**: High contrast, recognizable
- **Website link**: Direct to [[workflows/affiliate-pipeline|affiliate landing page]] or niche hub

### Board Strategy
Create 5-8 boards focused on core topics within niche:

| Board | Keywords | Pins (Month 1) |
|-------|----------|---|
| Top Products | "best [category]", "top-rated" | 20-30 |
| Unboxings | "unboxing [product]", reviews | 15-20 |
| Comparison Guides | "[product] vs [product]", reviews | 10-15 |
| Tips & Tricks | "how to use", tutorials | 10-15 |
| Gift Ideas | "gifts for", holiday | 10-15 |

### Pin Creation
- **Dimensions**: 1000x1500px (optimal), or 1000x1200px (quick)
- **Tools**: Canva Pro (templates), Adobe Express, or Figma
- **Design**: Text overlay + product image + branding
- **Text**: Hook (first line) + benefit (second line) + CTA (implicit)

**Monthly volume**:
- **Month 1-3**: 30-50 pins/month (mix new + repins of top content)
- **Goal**: Establish presence, seed boards, get baseline traction

### Descriptions (SEO)
- **Format**: 300-500 chars, keyword-rich
- **Structure**: Hook + benefit + CTA
- **Example**: "Best AI writing tools to 10x productivity. Free & paid options tested. Save this pin for product links & review comparisons."
- **Keywords**: Include 2-3 target keywords naturally (e.g., "AI writing tools", "productivity apps", "best AI tools")
- **Link**: Include affiliate link in description (direct to Amazon or brand affiliate page)

## Growth Phase (Month 3-6): Scale Audience

### Publishing Schedule
- **Daily**: 3-5 pins (mix new + repins from relevant creators)
- **Consistency**: Same time each day (optimal: 6-9am, 12-2pm, 6-8pm your timezone)
- **Batch creation**: Create 30 pins on Sunday, schedule throughout month

### Repinning Strategy
- **Follow** relevant creators (30-50) in your niche
- **Repin** their top-performing content (3-5 pins/day)
- **Don't just repin passively** — optimize descriptions with your affiliate links
- **Track**: Which creators' content gets most traction when you repin (work with them if >100 monthly saves)

### Engagement
- **Comments**: Reply to comments on your pins (10-15/week)
- **Follows**: Follow relevant creators, niche accounts, competitor profiles
- **DMs**: Don't spam, but engage with engaged followers

### Analytics Tracking
- **Outbound clicks**: Which pins drive traffic? Track in Pinterest Analytics
- **Top performers**: Pins with 1k+ impressions warrant repinning and boosting
- **Underperformers**: If a pin gets <100 impressions in 30 days, archive it

## Monetization Phase (Month 6+): Affiliate Revenue

### High-Value Pin Tactics
- **Comparison pins**: "[Product A] vs [Product B]" — trigger high intent (buyer mindset)
- **Best of lists**: "5 Best [category] under $100" — aggregation drives traffic
- **Tutorial pins**: "How to [use product]" — drives niche long-tail search
- **Holiday/seasonal**: "Gift ideas for [person]", "Black Friday [category]" — seasonal spikes

### Link Optimization
- **Amazon Associates**: Bulk of affiliate revenue (1-10% commission)
- **Brand partnerships**: If pin gets 5k+ impressions, reach out to brand for partnership (higher commission)
- **CTA copy**: "Save this for product links" (drives saves, boosts algorithm)

### Audience Refinement
- **Analytics review** (monthly): Which audiences engage most?
  - Age, gender, interests (if visible)
  - Geography (if relevant)
- **Double down**: Create more pins for high-performing demographics
- **Pivot away**: Underperforming demographics = delete those pins

## Scaling (Month 6+)

### Expansion Niches
Once one niche hits 10k monthly views:
- **Create second niche board** (same channel)
- **Leverage audience**: Cross-promote between boards
- **Separate channel** (optional): If niches are unrelated, consider separate Pinterest account

### Team/Automation
- **Content calendar**: Plan 60-90 days ahead in spreadsheet
- **Batch creation**: Spend 4 hours/week creating pins
- **Scheduler**: Use Later or Pinterest native scheduling for consistency

### Revenue Targets
- **Month 3**: 1-5k monthly views, 0-5 clicks/month
- **Month 6**: 10-50k monthly views, 50-200 clicks/month
- **Month 9**: 50-200k monthly views, 200-1k clicks/month
- **Month 12**: 100k+ monthly views, 1k+ clicks/month

## Best Practices

### Pin Design
- **Contrast**: Text readable on mobile (phones = 80% of views)
- **Font**: Bold, large (minimum 40pt for key text)
- **Image**: High quality product photos, not blurry
- **Branding**: Logo in corner (builds brand recognition)

### Descriptions
- **First 100 chars**: Visible before click, make compelling
- **Keywords**: Natural inclusion, not forced
- **Link**: Always include affiliate link (drives revenue)

### Content Strategy
- **Evergreen**: 90% of pins should be timeless (not "2025 trend")
- **Specific**: "Best AI writing tools" beats "AI tools"
- **Emotional**: Appeal to pain point or aspiration

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
|-------|-------|-----|
| Pins get <100 impressions | Bad description/design/hook | Rewrite description, check design clarity |
| High impressions, low clicks | Bad CTA or link placement | Move link to first 100 chars of description |
| Clicks but no conversions | Wrong product for audience | Swap to different product in same category |
| Audience isn't growing | Not repinning enough | Increase repin volume to 5/day |
| Engagement flatlined | Posting inconsistently | Lock in daily schedule, use scheduler |
