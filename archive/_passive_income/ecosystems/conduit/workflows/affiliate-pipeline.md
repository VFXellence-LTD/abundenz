# Affiliate Pipeline

End-to-end monetization setup for Conduit ecosystem. Flow: platform traffic → affiliate links → commissions.

## Affiliate Programs Setup

### Amazon Associates (Day 1 — start here)
**Eligibility**: Instant approval, no follower minimum
**Commission**: 1-10% depending on product category (physical products typically 1-4.5%)
**Payout**: Monthly via bank transfer (min $10)

This is your **bridge monetization** — earning from day 1 via bio links while building to TikTok Shop.

### TikTok Shop Affiliate (at 1K followers)
**Eligibility**: 1,000+ followers, identity verification, US accounts
**Commission**: 10-15% open collab, 18-25%+ targeted/invite-only — 5-10x Amazon rates
**Payout**: Settles 15-31 days after delivery, ~$20 minimum, weekly/bi-weekly

**Steps**:
1. Reach 1,000 TikTok followers (use Amazon bio links in the meantime)
2. Open TikTok → Creator tools → TikTok Shop → Affiliate
3. Complete identity verification (required since July 2025)
4. Apply as affiliate creator — approval takes 1-3 days
5. New accounts (<5K) enter 30-day pilot: limited product access, 3 posts/day max
6. Browse marketplace, sort by commission rate, add products to showcase

**Best practices**:
- Focus on TikTok Shop products (in-app checkout = highest conversion)
- Sort by commission rate — beauty/wellness 20%+, electronics 5-10%
- Test 3-5 products per week, track click-through and conversion
- Rotate products based on performance
- Product tags > bio links (native tags show product preview, higher CTR)

### Amazon Associates (continued after TikTok Shop unlock)
Keep Amazon as secondary channel — bio links still earn on every video. Amazon becomes the fallback for products not in TikTok Shop.

**Setup**:
1. Sign up at `https://associates.amazon.com`
2. Add website/content channel (TikTok profile URL + description)
3. Create shareable links via Associates dashboard
4. Use short URLs (bit.ly or TikTok's built-in link shortener) to fit captions

**Best practices**:
- Link directly to product pages, not category pages
- Include Amazon Associate ID in all links (`tag=yourname-20`)
- Disclose: "As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases"

### Brand Affiliate Programs
**Eligibility**: Varies by brand (sometimes 500 followers, sometimes none)
**Commission**: 5-20% depending on brand and product category
**Payout**: Monthly, biweekly, or per-sale depending on program

**Process**:
1. For each product category you promote, search "[brand] affiliate program"
2. Apply via brand's affiliate portal (often ShareASale, Impact, or custom dashboard)
3. Wait for approval (1-7 days typically)
4. Get unique referral links
5. Add to TikTok captions and pin descriptions

**High-commission categories**:
- Electronics/gadgets: 5-15%
- Home/kitchen: 8-20%
- Fashion: 10-30%
- SaaS / subscriptions: 20-50%
- Beauty/skincare: 10-25%

## Link Strategy

### TikTok
- **Video caption**: Primary link placement. "Link in bio" no longer optimal; use TikTok link features
- **Product tag**: If TikTok Shop product, use native product tag (shows product preview)
- **Link in bio**: Secondary fallback; use Later, Linktree, or TikTok Shop direct link

### Pinterest
- **Pin description**: Include affiliate link in description text (SEO-friendly)
- **Pin URL**: If using custom domain, redirect to product page with affiliate ID
- **Board description**: Social proof — mention bestselling products

## Tracking & Analytics

### TikTok Creator Affiliate
- Native dashboard shows: clicks, conversions, revenue
- View in: Creator Fund panel → Affiliate tab

### Amazon Associates
- Dashboard shows: clicks, conversions, earnings
- Export reports monthly for analysis

### Custom tracking
- UTM parameters: `?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=video&utm_campaign=product-x`
- Bit.ly shortened links: track clicks and referrers
- Spreadsheet: log product → platform → clicks → conversions → revenue

## Revenue Projections

### TikTok (after 1K followers — TikTok Shop unlocked)
- **500-2k avg views per video**
- **2-5% click-through rate** = 10-100 clicks per video
- **1-3% conversion rate** = 0.1-3 sales per video
- **$20-50 commission per sale** (varies by product)
- **Daily revenue potential**: $3-20 (3-5 posts/day, early stage)
- **3 months in (20k+ avg views)**: $50-300/day

### Pinterest (after 100k monthly views)
- **100k views/month**
- **2-5% click-through** = 2k-5k clicks/month
- **1-3% conversion** = 20-150 sales/month
- **$20-50 commission per sale**
- **Monthly revenue**: $400-7,500 (heavily dependent on niche)

## Optimization Loop

1. **Weekly review**: Check CTR and conversion rates per product
2. **Kill underperformers**: If a product gets <1% CTR after 10 posts, remove it
3. **Double down winners**: If a product gets >5% CTR, post it 2x more frequently
4. **A/B test**: Try different hooks, product angles, CTA wording on similar products
5. **Expand winners**: Once a product hits $50/week, promote it across both TikTok and Pinterest

## Compliance

### FTC Disclosure
- All affiliate content must disclose: `#ad` or `#affiliate` in caption or video overlay
- Required by FTC Endorsement Guides and similar laws globally
- TikTok: auto-tags some content, but manually adding ensures compliance

### Platform Terms
- **TikTok**: Allowed. Disclose in caption.
- **Pinterest**: Allowed. Disclose in pin description.
- **Amazon Associates**: Disclose "As an Amazon Associate..."
