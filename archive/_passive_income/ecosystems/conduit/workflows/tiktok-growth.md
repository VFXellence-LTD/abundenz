# TikTok Growth Strategy

Short-form video strategy for rapid audience building. Algorithm-driven platform favors trending content and consistent daily posting.

## Phase 1: Cold Start (Week 1-4): Build to 1K Followers

### Bridge Monetization (Day 1 — no followers needed)
- **Amazon Associates**: Sign up instantly, get tracking links same day
- **Linktree/Beacons**: Set up bio link page with Amazon affiliate links
- **Every video**: Reference "link in bio" — Amazon pays 1-4.5% per sale via bio clicks
- **This is revenue from day 1** while building toward TikTok Shop (1K followers)

### Channel Setup
- **Account type**: Switch to Business Account (Settings → Account type → Business)
- **Profile name**: Clear, keyword-rich (e.g., "@ai_gadget_reviews")
- **Bio**: 150 chars, keyword-focused + emoji (e.g., "AI tools & gadget reviews. Tested & recommended. 🚀")
- **Profile image**: High contrast, recognizable face or logo
- **Link in bio**: Linktree with Amazon affiliate links (monetizing from day 1)

### Posting Schedule
- **Volume**: 3-5 posts per day (non-negotiable for cold start)
- **Timing**: Post at peak hours — 7-9am, 12-2pm, 7-11pm (adjust for your timezone)
- **Consistency**: Same times daily (algorithm rewards patterns)
- **Duration**: 15-45 seconds optimal (longer = lower completion rate)

### Content Types for Cold Start
| Type | Purpose | Weekly Volume |
|------|---------|---|
| Product unboxing | First-look, novelty | 3-5 |
| Quick demo/test | "Does it work?" | 3-5 |
| Before/after | Tangible transformation | 2-3 |
| Trending sound + product | Hook into algorithm | 3-5 |
| Comparison clip | "[Product A] vs [Product B]" | 2-3 |

### Video Specs
- **Dimensions**: 9:16 (vertical), 1080x1920px
- **Format**: MP4, H.264 codec
- **Max file size**: 287.6 MB
- **Recommended bitrate**: 3-6 Mbps (higher = better quality on TikTok)
- **Frame rate**: 24-60 fps

### Scripting (Agent 02 owns this)
**Hook (1-3 seconds, critical)**:
- Ask question: "Guess how much this AI tool costs?"
- Bold statement: "This gadget just changed my life"
- Pattern interrupt: Unusual visual, sudden sound

**Demo (8-20 seconds)**:
- Show product in action
- Highlight key feature
- Keep visually interesting (cuts, zoom, B-roll)

**CTA (2-3 seconds)**:
- "Link in bio" or "Check pinned comment"
- Soft sell: "I tested 5 of these, this one's best"
- Don't hard sell

**Captions**:
- On-screen text for critical moments (product name, price, link)
- Use captions for silent viewers

### Hashtag Strategy
| Type | Count | Examples |
|------|-------|----------|
| Trending (this week) | 2-3 | #FYP, #ForYouPage, #Viral (check Discover tab) |
| Niche-specific | 3-5 | #AITools, #GadgetReview, #ProductUnboxing |
| Niche long-tail | 3-5 | #BestAIWritingTools, #ProductReviewUnboxing |
| Branded | 1-2 | #YourChannelName, #GadgetTestLab |

**Total**: 10-15 hashtags per post

### Sound Strategy
- **Use trending sounds** (first 7 days of sound's trend lifecycle get algorithm boost)
- **Check Discover tab** for trending sounds daily
- **Apply sound to product**: Does sound match product vibe?
- **Don't force it**: If sound doesn't fit, use a different one
- **Original audio acceptable** if you have 3-5 posts with trending sounds first

### Example Cold Start Week

**Monday**:
- 8am: Product unboxing (trending sound)
- 1pm: Quick demo clip (different product)
- 8pm: Before/after transformation

**Tuesday-Friday**: Repeat pattern (different products)

**Saturday-Sunday**: Double volume (10 posts total)

**Target**: 30-35 posts by end of Week 1

## Phase 2: Growth (Week 5-8): Push to 1K Followers

### Optimization Based on Analytics
- **Weekly review**: Which videos got >500 views? >1k views?
- **Study winners**: What hook worked? What sound? What topic?
- **Iterate**: Double down on successful content formula
- **Kill losers**: If video gets <100 views after 48 hours, delete it (affects channel health)

### Engagement Tactics
- **Reply to comments** (post video, refresh every 2 hours for first 12 hours)
- **Engage with similar creators** (3-5 follows/likes of competitor videos daily)
- **Duets/stitches** (respond to trends, challenge other creators)
- **Go live** (once you have 100+ followers, go live 2x/week to boost engagement)

### Content Expansion
| Week | Content | Objective |
|------|---------|-----------|
| 5 | Comparison (vs competitor) | Higher intent |
| 6 | Tutorial / "how to use" | Depth + niche SEO |
| 7 | Trending + product | Viral potential |
| 8 | Engagement bait (polls, "guess") | Comment ratio boost |

### Posting Continues
- **3-5 posts/day** through Week 8 (consistency critical for algorithm)
- **Refresh sounds weekly** (don't reuse sounds, trends change)
- **Test captions** (try different CTAs, emojis, hooks)

### Mid-Phase Checkpoint (Week 6)
- **Expected followers**: 150-300
- **Expected avg views/video**: 200-800
- **If behind**: Increase posting to 6-7/day, revert to pure unboxing/demo format
- **If ahead**: Experiment with narrative/storytelling format (longer scripts)

## Phase 3: Unlock TikTok Shop Affiliate (at 1K followers)

### 1K Follower Achievement
Once 1,000+ followers:
1. Open TikTok → Creator tools → TikTok Shop → Affiliate
2. Complete identity verification (required since July 2025)
3. Apply as affiliate creator — approval takes 1-3 days
4. New accounts (<5K followers) enter 30-day pilot: limited product access, 3 posts/day max

### Monetization Upgrade (Amazon → TikTok Shop)
- **TikTok Shop commission**: 10-15% open collab, 18-25%+ targeted/invite — 5-10x Amazon rates
- **Product tags**: Add product links directly to videos (native in-app tag, not external link)
- **In-app checkout**: Viewer buys without leaving TikTok — much higher conversion than bio links
- **Keep Amazon as secondary**: Bio links still earn, but TikTok Shop becomes primary revenue

### Post-1K Content Strategy
- **Shift to high-commission TikTok Shop products**: Sort marketplace by commission rate
- **Product tag every video** (if relevant product exists in TikTok Shop)
- **Focus on demo/test content** (shows product in action = highest CTR)
- **Rotation strategy**: Promote 3-5 products at a time, rotate weekly based on performance

### Posting Schedule (Optimized)
- **3 posts/day** (30-day pilot cap for <5K accounts)
- **Keep consistency**: Same times daily
- **Focus on high-performing formats** (unboxing + demo = highest CTR)

## Phase 4: Scale (Month 3+)

### Analytics Review (Weekly)
| Metric | Target | Action |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Avg views/video | 500-2k | Maintain or improve |
| Avg views/video | 2k-5k | Increase posting frequency slightly |
| Avg views/video | 5k-20k | Expand into adjacent niches |
| Engagement rate | >3% | Good, maintain |
| Engagement rate | <1% | Review hook/caption strategy |
| CTR (to product) | 2-5% | Good |
| CTR (to product) | <1% | Product not resonating, swap |

### Revenue Optimization
- **Product rotation**: If product gets <2% CTR, stop promoting (swap for another)
- **Price testing**: Higher-priced products = higher commission, but lower conversion
- **Bundle approach**: Promote 2-3 related products in one video
- **Seasonal pivots**: Holiday content gets engagement spikes

### Audience Scaling
- **Multi-niche strategy**: Once niche hits 10k followers, add second niche board
- **Cross-promotion**: Mention second account in videos (drives audience transfer)
- **Leverage followers**: Use existing audience to bootstrap new niche (easier to reach 1K)

## Compliance & FTC

### Disclosure Requirements
- Every affiliate product must disclose in caption: `#ad` or `#affiliate`
- Required by FTC Endorsement Guides (US) + similar laws globally
- Non-compliance risks account suspension

### TikTok Creator Affiliate Terms
- Products must be from TikTok Shop marketplace (not arbitrary links)
- Don't promote competing platforms or off-platform links
- Don't disclose commission rates (against TikTok ToS)

## Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Algorithm changes | Views plummet | Diversify content types, stay current with trends |
| Product shortage | Can't fulfill demo videos | Pre-select 5-10 evergreen products, buy in bulk |
| Platform moderation | Videos removed, account banned | Follow community guidelines, disclose #ad |
| Seasonal drops | Revenue volatility | Maintain 2-3 niches, promote seasonal products |
| Competitor saturation | Lower views | Find underserved product angle, go deeper |
