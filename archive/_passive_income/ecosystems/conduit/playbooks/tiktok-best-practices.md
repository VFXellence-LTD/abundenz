# TikTok Best Practices Playbook

Platform-specific tactics, FTC compliance, and Creator Affiliate setup.

## First 3 Seconds = Everything

The first 3 seconds determine whether viewers watch to 10 seconds. If they watch 10 seconds, they're likely to watch the whole video.

### Hook Types (Pick One)

| Hook Type | Visual | Script | When | Success Rate |
|-----------|--------|--------|------|---|
| **Question** | Text overlay: "Can this AI write essays?" | "Wait for the answer..." | General | High (60%+) |
| **Bold claim** | Text overlay: "This saved me 5 hours" | "Here's how..." | Emotional | High (60%+) |
| **Pattern interrupt** | Unexpected sound/visual shift | Sudden music change | Attention | Very High (70%+) |
| **Trend riding** | Trending sound's iconic moment | Use trending sound's natural hook | Viral | Very High (70%+) |
| **Curiosity gap** | Mysterious text/visual | "You won't believe..." | Engagement | Medium (50%+) |
| **Relatability** | Familiar scenario (pain point) | "Ever felt like this?" | Niche | High (60%+) |

### Testing Protocol

Test 3-5 different hooks per week per product. Track watch completion % for each:

```
Product: AI Writing Assistant

Hook A: "I tested 5 AI tools..." — 65% completion ← WINNER
Hook B: "This changed everything..." — 48% completion
Hook C: "Can AI write better than you?" — 52% completion
Hook D: "The #1 AI writing tool" — 38% completion

Action: Repeat Hook A 5x next week, test 3 new hooks
```

## Text Overlays (Silent Viewers Matter)

50%+ of TikTok views are **with sound off**. Text overlays are required.

### Overlay Placement
- **Top 1/3**: Headline (3-5 words max)
- **Middle**: Key benefit or question
- **Bottom**: CTA or product name
- **Avoid**: Don't cover product (viewers need to see it clearly)

### Overlay Text
| Placement | Text | Examples |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Top | Hook headline | "I tested 5 AI tools", "This is insane", "Watch this" |
| Middle | Key benefit or question | "$100 in 1 minute", "Would YOU buy this?", "See the difference?" |
| Bottom | CTA | "Link in bio", "Pinned comment", "Product name" |

### Design Tips
- **Font**: Bold, sans-serif (Montserrat, Impact)
- **Size**: 48-72pt minimum (readable on phone)
- **Color**: High contrast to video background (white text on dark, black on light)
- **Animation**: Fade in/out (not spinning, bouncing, or distracting)
- **Duration**: Each overlay on screen 3-5 seconds minimum

## Call-to-Action Wording

### Most Effective CTAs (A/B Tested)

| CTA | CTR | Conversion | Best Used |
|-----|-----|-----------|-----------|
| "Link in pinned comment" | 3-5% | 1-3% | Most reliable |
| "Check pinned comment" | 3-5% | 1-3% | Same as above |
| "I'll link below" | 2-4% | 1-2% | Casual, conversational |
| "Check the link in bio" | 2-4% | 0.5-2% | Weaker (link requires bio navigation) |
| "First link in comments" | 2-4% | 1-2% | Works if video short |
| Direct product mention | 1-3% | 0.5-1% | Soft sell, lower CTR |

**Best practice**: Pinned comment with affiliate link + "Tap the first comment for the link"

### CTA Timing
- **Show CTA text**: Last 3-5 seconds of video
- **Mention CTA verbally**: 15-20 seconds into video (gives viewers time to find it)
- **Reinforce**: End screen or last frame (text + verbal)

## Trending Sounds

TikTok's algorithm heavily favors videos using trending sounds, especially in first 7 days of a sound's trend.

### Sound Strategy

1. **Check Discover tab daily** (2-3 minutes)
2. **Identify top 3 trending sounds** (high usage, high engagement)
3. **Match to product**: Does sound's vibe match product?
4. **Use in first 24h of trend** (algorithm boost)
5. **Apply aggressively**: Use trending sound on 3-5 videos/week

### Sound Types

| Sound Type | Algorithm Boost | Best For | Examples |
|-----------|---|---|---|
| Trending 0-2 days old | Very High | Unboxing, reaction | Check Discover now |
| Trending 3-7 days old | High | Demo, tutorial | Still strong reach |
| Niche-specific sound | Medium | Product-specific content | [Product name] trending |
| Original audio | Low | Voiceovers, talking heads | Use only if no trending sound fits |

### Applying Sound
- Drag sound into CapCut or native TikTok editor
- If sound doesn't match visually, add B-roll that does
- Keep sound volume high (not background music)
- Trim to 15-45 seconds (longer = lower completion)

## Hashtag Strategy Refresh Weekly

Hashtags drive discoverability. Mix trending + niche + branded.

### Hashtag Composition (10-15 total)

| Category | Count | Purpose | Examples |
|----------|-------|---------|----------|
| Trending (this week) | 2-3 | Algorithm boost | #FYP, #ForYouPage, #Viral (check Discover tab) |
| Trending sound-specific | 1-2 | Sound-associated content | [Sound name] hashtag |
| Niche-specific | 3-5 | Niche audience | #AITools, #ProductivityApps, #GadgetReview |
| Niche long-tail | 3-5 | Specific searches | #BestAIWritingTools, #ProductReviewUnboxing |
| Branded | 1-2 | Channel branding | #YourChannelName, #ProductTestLab |

### Hashtag Research
```
Weekly checklist (Sunday evening):
1. Open TikTok Discover tab
2. Note top 5 trending hashtags
3. Note top 5 trending sounds
4. Update hashtag list in Agent 02 config
5. Start creating videos using new hashtags
```

## Engagement Tactics

### Comment Replies (Critical)
- **Timing**: Reply to comments in first 12 hours (boosts engagement signal)
- **Frequency**: Reply to 10-15 comments per video
- **Style**: Personal, conversational, genuine
- **Don't**: Generic "Thanks!" — add value or personality

**Examples**:
- ❌ "Thanks for watching!"
- ✓ "Yes! This one's my favorite too — the AI rewriting is insane"

### Leverage Comments for Product Links
- Pin your own comment with affiliate link + "Here's the product link"
- Keep it in top 2-3 comments (algorithm favors pinned)
- Update pinned comment if higher-CTR link becomes available

### Duets & Stitches
- **Duets** (side-by-side): React to competitor videos, show your product as alternative
- **Stitches** (clip + response): Respond to relevant creator content, plug your product
- **Frequency**: 1-2 per week (don't overdo it)

## Content Moderation & Compliance

### FTC Disclosure (Legal Requirement)
**Every product must disclose**:
- `#ad` in caption OR
- Text overlay "Paid partnership" OR
- Verbal disclosure "This is an ad"

**Risk of non-compliance**: Account suspension, FTC fine

### TikTok Community Guidelines
**Don't**:
- Promote illegal products/services
- Misinformation (false claims about product)
- Hate speech, harassment
- Self-harm, violence
- Sexual content

**Do**:
- Keep it authentic (no fake testimonials)
- Disclose limitations of product
- Show real product (not stock footage)
- Be honest about affiliate relationship

### Product Claims (Avoid Legal Trouble)
❌ "This AI will make you rich"
✓ "This AI helped me save 5 hours/week writing"

❌ "Cures headaches"
✓ "Users report feeling more relaxed"

❌ "100% guaranteed results"
✓ "Here's what happened when I tested it"

## TikTok Creator Affiliate Setup (Step-by-Step)

### Prerequisites
- 1,000+ followers on TikTok
- Business account (not personal)
- Identity verification (required since July 2025)
- No account violations (last 3 months clean)
- Account active for 30+ days

### Steps to Unlock

**Step 1**: Upgrade to Business Account
```
Settings → Account Type → Switch to Business Account
→ Select "Creator" as account category
```

**Step 2**: Join Creator Fund (Optional, but required for Affiliate access)
```
Creator Fund → Join (if available)
→ Wait for approval (24-48 hours)
```

**Step 3**: Apply for TikTok Shop Affiliate
```
Creator tools → TikTok Shop → Affiliate
→ Complete identity verification
→ Accept terms
→ Click "Apply"
```

Approval takes 1-3 days. New accounts (<5K followers) enter 30-day pilot with limited product access and 3 posts/day cap.

**Step 4**: Browse TikTok Shop Products
```
Affiliate → Browse Products
→ Search product category (e.g., "AI Tools", "Electronics")
→ Select products to promote
```

**Step 5**: Add Product Links to Videos

**Option A: Native Product Tag**
```
Create video → Post
→ Edit video → Add product tag
→ Select product from TikTok Shop
→ Save
```

**Option B: Caption Link (for non-TikTok Shop products)**
```
Caption text: "Link in pinned comment"
→ Pinned comment: Your affiliate URL
→ Use bit.ly shortener if too long
```

### Commission Structure
- **Open Collaboration** (public marketplace): 10-15% commission
- **Targeted Collaboration** (invite-only): 18-25%+ commission
- **Top categories**: Beauty/wellness 20%+, electronics 5-10%
- **Payout**: Settles 15-31 days after delivery, ~$20 min, weekly/bi-weekly
- Sellers set commission rates, not TikTok

### Tracking Revenue
```
Affiliate Dashboard → Analytics
→ Views: Total impressions of videos with product links
→ Clicks: Total clicks on affiliate links
→ Conversions: Purchases attributed to your links
→ Revenue: Total commission earned
```

Review daily first week, then weekly.

## Video Quality Standards

### Resolution & Format
- **Dimensions**: 1080x1920 (required for vertical)
- **Format**: MP4 preferred (H.264 codec)
- **File size**: <287 MB (TikTok max)
- **Framerate**: 24-60 fps (30fps ideal)
- **Bitrate**: 3-6 Mbps (higher = better quality, slower to render)

### Audio Quality
- **Trending sound**: High volume (not background)
- **Voiceover**: Clear, no background noise
- **Music**: License-free (or use TikTok's sound library)

### Lighting & Clarity
- **Avoid**: Dark videos, blurry product images
- **Prefer**: Natural light or ring light
- **Product**: Always visible and clear on camera

## Common Mistakes (Avoid These)

| Mistake | Why It Fails | Fix |
|---------|---|---|
| Weak hook (>3s) | Viewers skip before hook appears | Use bold statement first 3 sec |
| No trending sound | Algorithm deprioritizes | Use trending sound (check Discover) |
| No text overlays | Silent viewers see nothing | Add overlays, 48-72pt font |
| Generic CTA | Viewers don't know where to find product | Pin comment with link + mention in video |
| No FTC disclosure | Legal risk | Add #ad in every product caption |
| Old trending sounds | Trend expired, no boost | Update sounds weekly |
| Same product 2x/day | Audience saturation | Rotate products, same product max 1x/day |
| Hard sell | Inauthentic, lower engagement | Soften CTA, show genuine use |
| Inconsistent posting | Algorithm deprioritizes | Post 3-5x daily, same times |
| Ignore underperformers | Wasted effort on flopping products | Kill <100 views/24h products |

## Scaling to Multiple Niches

Once primary niche hits 10k followers:
1. **Create second niche channel** (separate Business account, same email)
2. **Repurpose content**: First-week videos from Niche 1 can become Niche 2's (resonate differently)
3. **Cross-promote**: Mention Niche 2 in Niche 1's videos, vice versa
4. **Target same audience**: Leverage existing followers to bootstrap second niche
5. **Scale to 3-5 niches**: Each niche → separate account, shared Agent 02/03 infrastructure

**Timeline**: Niche 1 → 1K followers (4-8 weeks) → Launch Niche 2 → 1K followers (3-4 weeks, shared audience) → Repeat
