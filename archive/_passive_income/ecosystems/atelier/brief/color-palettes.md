# Color Palettes

8 standard palettes applied across ALL product lines. Every design gets rendered in at least 3 palettes — multiply catalog size without new concepts.

---

## 1. Neon Vibrant
Bright fluorescent colors on pure black background. Eye-catching, high contrast. The classic POD aesthetic.

| Role | Hex | Color |
|------|-----|-------|
| Background | `#000000` | Black |
| Primary | `#FF00FF` | Magenta |
| Secondary | `#00FFFF` | Cyan |
| Accent | `#FFFF00` | Yellow |
| Highlight | `#FF4500` | Orange-Red |
| Detail | `#39FF14` | Neon Green |

**Best for**: Skulls, vibrant symbols, space themes
**Products**: T-shirts (dark), hoodies, posters, stickers

---

| Role | Hex | Color |
|------|-----|-------|

## 3. Black & White
Pure monochrome. High contrast ink illustration style.

| Role | Hex | Color |
|------|-----|-------|
| Background | `#FFFFFF` | White |
| Primary | `#000000` | Black |
| Secondary | `#333333` | Dark gray |
| Accent | `#666666` | Medium gray |
| Highlight | `#999999` | Light gray |
| Detail | `#CCCCCC` | Pale gray |

**Best for**: Quote portraits, steampunk, detailed illustrations, skulls
**Products**: Prints, t-shirts (white), minimalist wall art

---

## 4. Monochrome Tinted
Single color family in multiple shades — like B&W but in any hue. Each design picks ONE base color.

**Base color options** (generate separate variant for each):

| Variant | Base Hex | Name |
|---------|----------|------|
| Navy | `#1B2A4A` | Deep navy mono |
| Burgundy | `#5C1A1B` | Wine mono |
| Forest | `#1A3C2A` | Deep green mono |
| Plum | `#3D1F4E` | Purple mono |
| Rust | `#6B3410` | Rust mono |
| Slate | `#2F3640` | Cool gray mono |

Each variant uses 5-6 shades of the base from near-black to near-white. Background = lightest or darkest shade depending on design.

**Best for**: Sophisticated single-color prints, alphabet initials, quote art
**Products**: Wall art, premium prints, apparel

| Combo | Color 1 | Color 2 | Color 3 | Vibe |

## 7. Vintage / Distressed
Aged, weathered look. Yellowed paper tones, faded ink, grunge textures.

| Role | Hex | Color |
|------|-----|-------|
| Background | `#F2E8D5` | Aged paper |
| Primary | `#3C2415` | Dark sepia |
| Secondary | `#8B6914` | Faded gold |
| Accent | `#6B3A2A` | Burnt sienna |
| Highlight | `#C4956A` | Faded tan |
| Detail | `#4A4A3A` | Weathered dark |

Apply grain/noise texture overlay. Slightly uneven ink distribution.

**Best for**: Quote portraits, steampunk, skulls, famous photo reimaginings
**Products**: Posters (aged look), t-shirts (distressed print), canvas

---

## 8. Watercolor
Soft bleeds, wet edges, translucent washes. Organic, handmade feel.

| Role | Hex | Color |
|------|-----|-------|
| Background | `#FFFFFF` | White (paper) |
| Primary | `#4A90D9` | Watercolor blue |
| Secondary | `#E8737A` | Watercolor rose |
| Accent | `#7BC67E` | Watercolor green |
| Highlight | `#F5C242` | Watercolor gold |
| Detail | `#9B7ED8` | Watercolor violet |

Colors should bleed and overlap. No hard edges. Paper texture visible through washes.

**Best for**: Botanical, alphabet initials, animals, ocean themes
**Products**: Wall art (premium look), greeting cards, tote bags, mugs

## AI Generation Prompt Suffix

Append to every generation prompt to enforce palette:

```
Color palette: [palette name]. Use ONLY these colors: [hex list]. 
Background: [bg hex]. No other colors. High contrast. Print-ready, 
clean edges, no watermark.
```

For monochrome, specify: `Monochrome [base color name]. Use only shades of [base hex] from near-black to near-white.`

For watercolor, specify: `Watercolor style with soft bleeds, wet edges, translucent washes on white paper background. Colors: [hex list].`
