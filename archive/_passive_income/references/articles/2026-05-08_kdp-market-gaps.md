## The approach

Data-driven method for finding underserved KDP (Kindle Direct Publishing) niches instead of entering crowded markets by intuition. Most KDP publishers fail because they enter niches that look appealing from the outside but are either:

1. **Too crowded**: established sellers with hundreds of reviews, impossible to rank against
2. **Too thin**: low search volume, not enough buyers even if you rank
3. **Too seasonal**: demand spikes then collapses

The Market Gaps approach uses search data, competitor analysis, and sales rank research to identify niches with:
- Consistent demand (search volume is real and year-round)
- Underserved supply (top sellers have weak listings, low review counts, or poor quality)
- Viable price points (enough margin to justify production cost)

## The method (summary)

1. Use KDP-specific research tools (Publisher Rocket, KDSPY, or similar) to find keywords with volume
2. Check top 10 results for: average BSR (Best Seller Rank), review count, listing quality
3. Target niches where top sellers have <50 reviews and BSR <150,000 (still selling, not dominant)
4. Cross-reference with Google Trends to confirm sustained demand
5. Validate with a thin prototype (cover + sample interior) before full production
