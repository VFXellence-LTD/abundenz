## What it does
AI creative agent. Describe what you want in plain English → agent plans the project, picks the right model per shot, writes prompts, generates, and iterates until result matches intent. All in one workspace.

**Models available:**
- Video: Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0 Pro, Seedance 2.0, Hailuo 2.3, Kling o3 Edit/Ref
- Image: Seedream 4.5, Nano Banana Pro
- Audio: ElevenLabs 3.0 (voice), Suno 5.5 (music), Mirelo 1.5 (sound)
- Agent: Claude Opus 4.7, Claude Sonnet 4.6, Gemini 3.1 Pro

## Where it fits in polymath
- **Conduit**: Replaces OBS + ElevenLabs + CapCut manual workflow for short-form product review videos. Single tool for script → video → voiceover → music.
- **Signal**: Potential for thumbnail generation, short-form derivative clips from pillar content.
- **Atelier**: Product mockup videos, animated previews for listings.
