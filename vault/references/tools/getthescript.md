## What it does

Transcribes Instagram Reels to text. Paste a Reel URL, get back the full spoken transcript. Removes the manual step of watching and writing out competitor or reference content.

## Where it fits in polymath

- **References workflow**: Primary use — transcribe competitor Reels, inspiration content, or research material directly into `references/reels/`. Dramatically speeds up the capture-and-process workflow.
- **Signal**: Competitor/niche research. Transcribe reels from VFX accounts, pipeline TDs, or adjacent creators. Feed into Signal's niche brief and trend analysis.
- **Shared skill**: Could become the backbone of a `reference-synthesizer` agent — automated transcription of inbox Reels before human synthesis.

This is a reference-processing tool, not a publishing tool. Low risk, low cost, directly useful now.
