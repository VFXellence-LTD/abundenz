# Polymath Taxonomy

Standard vocabulary for referring to every layer of the business. Scales to hundreds of listings across all ecosystems.

---

## The Hierarchy

```
EMPIRE          Polymath (the whole operation)
 └─ ECOSYSTEM   Business model (Surge, Signal, Atelier, Conduit)
     └─ VERTICAL   Topic niche within an ecosystem (Tech, Kitchen, Finance)
         └─ BAND       Price/commitment tier (Impulse, Recurring, Premium)
             └─ LISTING    Individual product, program, or offer
                 └─ STREAM     Revenue type generated by that listing
```

### Example — fully qualified path:

```
Polymath > Surge > Tech > Recurring > Notion > Affiliate Commission
Empire     Eco     Vert   Band        Listing   Stream
```

---

## Layer Definitions

### ECOSYSTEM — "What business model?"

The top-level operating model. Each has its own brand, accounts, audience, and revenue logic.

| Ecosystem | Codename | Model | Core mechanic |
|-----------|----------|-------|---------------|
| Viral | **Surge** | Anonymous short-form video | Volume × RPM + affiliate |
| Content | **Signal** | Voice-driven pillar content | Audience × trust × monetization |
| Products | **Atelier** | AI-generated products sold on marketplaces | Design × listings × margin |
| Affiliate | **Conduit** | SEO content driving affiliate traffic | Search traffic × conversion × commission |

### VERTICAL — "What niche?"

The topic domain within an ecosystem. Each vertical gets its own brand identity, content strategy, and product catalog.

Examples by ecosystem:

| Ecosystem | Verticals |
|-----------|-----------|
| Surge | Tech/AI Tools, Kitchen Gadgets, Finance, Scary Stories, Quiz/Trivia |
| Signal | VFX Pipeline, Lullaby |
| Atelier | Wall Art, Coloring Books, Templates, Digital Downloads |
| Conduit | Software Reviews, Tool Comparisons, Buyer Guides |

A vertical can span ecosystems (Tech content in both Surge AND Conduit) but each ecosystem's instance operates independently under its own brand.

### BAND — "What price/commitment tier?"

The monetization tier within a vertical. Three standard bands apply across ALL ecosystems:

| Band | Alias | Price range | Buyer psychology | Revenue shape |
|------|-------|------------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Impulse** | Low | $1-30 | See it, want it, buy it. No deliberation. | High volume, low margin. Spikes on virality. |
| **Recurring** | Mid | $5-50/mo | Subscribes once, pays monthly. Sticky. | Compounds over time. Passive after conversion. |
| **Premium** | High | $100-500+ | Researches first. Needs trust. Long sales cycle. | Low volume, high payout. Windfall events. |

#### Band mapping across ecosystems

| Band | Surge (Viral) | Signal (Content) | Atelier (Products) | Conduit (Affiliate) |
|------|--------------|------------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| **Impulse** | TikTok Shop accessories, Amazon gadgets | Merch, $5 digital downloads | $1-15 Etsy digital downloads, stickers | Amazon Associates (physical goods) |
| **Recurring** | SaaS affiliate (Notion, Canva, NordVPN) | Newsletter subscription, Patreon, membership | Subscription boxes, template packs | SaaS referrals (HubSpot, Shopify) |
| **Premium** | High-ticket SaaS referrals (ClickFunnels, Webflow) | Courses, consulting, premium community | $50-200 wall art, book bundles, custom designs | High-ticket referrals (Cloudways, hosting) |

### LISTING — "What specific product/offer?"

An individual monetizable item. This is what you track at scale — hundreds of these across the operation.

A listing has:
- **Name** — the product or program (e.g., "Notion", "MagSafe Phone Mount", "VFX Pipeline Course")
- **Band** — which tier it belongs to (Impulse/Recurring/Premium)
- **Platform** — where it's sold/linked (TikTok Shop, Amazon, Gumroad, direct affiliate link)
- **Commission/margin** — what you earn per unit
- **Cookie/window** — how long after click you still earn (affiliate-specific)
- **Status** — active, testing, retired

### STREAM — "What type of revenue?"

The mechanism by which money flows in. A single listing can generate multiple streams.

| Stream | Code | Description | Example |
|--------|------|-------------|---------|
| **Commission** | `COM` | One-time affiliate payout per sale | Amazon Associates, TikTok Shop affiliate |
| **Recurring** | `REC` | Monthly/annual affiliate payout that repeats | Notion 50% for 12mo, Systeme.io 60% lifetime |
| **RPM** | `RPM` | Platform ad revenue per 1000 views | TikTok Creativity Program, YouTube Shorts |
| **Margin** | `MAR` | Profit on products you sell (price - COGS) | Etsy digital download, Gumroad course |
| **Sponsorship** | `SPO` | Flat fee for featuring a brand | Sponsored video integration |
| **Subscription** | `SUB` | Recurring payment from YOUR audience to YOU | Patreon, newsletter premium tier, membership |
| **Licensing** | `LIC` | Ongoing royalty from created content/products | KDP royalties, stock footage licensing |

## Applying the Taxonomy

### How to refer to things

| Instead of... | Say... |
|--------------|--------|
| "the cheap products" | "Impulse band" |
| "the subscription stuff" | "Recurring band" or "REC stream" |
| "the expensive affiliate programs" | "Premium band" |
| "the different ways we make money" | "Streams" |
| "the tech niche" | "Tech vertical" |
| "all the products we list" | "Listings" |
| "our TikTok business" | "Surge ecosystem" |
| "our blog affiliate thing" | "Conduit ecosystem" |

### Tracking at scale

When you have hundreds of listings, track them as:

```
LISTING: Notion
  Ecosystem: Surge, Conduit
  Vertical:  Tech
  Band:      Recurring
  Platform:  Bio link → notion.so/affiliate
  Stream:    REC (50% × 12 months)
  Cookie:    180 days
  Status:    Active
  Est. LTV:  $60-120/referral
```

```
LISTING: MagSafe Phone Mount (Generic)
  Ecosystem: Surge
  Vertical:  Tech
  Band:      Impulse
  Platform:  TikTok Shop
  Stream:    COM (15%)
  Cookie:    In-app (session)
  Status:    Testing
  Est. Rev:  $3-5/sale
```

## Cross-Ecosystem Scaling

The same vertical CAN appear in multiple ecosystems with different treatments:

### Example: "Tech/AI Tools" across all ecosystems

| Ecosystem | What it looks like | Band focus |
|-----------|--------------------|-----------|
| **Surge** | Faceless TikTok demos of AI tools | Impulse (TikTok Shop gadgets) + Recurring (SaaS links in bio) |
| **Signal** | Deep VFX Pipeline tutorials featuring tools | Premium (courses) + Recurring (Patreon) |
| **Atelier** | AI-generated templates/presets for sale | Margin (Gumroad/Etsy digital products) |
| **Conduit** | SEO blog: "Best AI Video Generators 2026" | Recurring (SaaS referrals) + Premium (high-ticket hosting) |

Same topic. Four different businesses. Four different brands. Four different audiences. Shared knowledge, isolated execution.

### Example: "Kitchen" across ecosystems

| Ecosystem | Treatment |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Surge** | Faceless TikTok gadget demos → TikTok Shop affiliate |
| **Atelier** | AI-generated recipe cards, kitchen printables → Etsy |
| **Conduit** | SEO blog: "Best Kitchen Gadgets" → Amazon Associates |

## Scaling Path

```
Month 1:    1 ecosystem (Surge), 1 vertical (Tech), 3 bands, ~10 listings
Month 3:    1 ecosystem, 1 vertical, 3 bands, ~30 listings
Month 6:    1 ecosystem, 2 verticals, 3 bands, ~80 listings
Month 9:    2 ecosystems, 3 verticals, 3 bands, ~150 listings
Month 12:   3 ecosystems, 5+ verticals, 3 bands, ~300+ listings
Year 2:     4 ecosystems, 10+ verticals, 3 bands, ~500+ listings
```

Each listing is a potential revenue node. The operation scales by adding listings (easy), verticals (medium), and ecosystems (hard). Bands stay constant.
