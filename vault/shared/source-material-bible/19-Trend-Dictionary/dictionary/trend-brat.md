# brat

**Meaning/usage:** "Brat" as a 2024 cultural identity = messy, confident, party-girl energy, tied to **Charli XCX's album "Brat"** and its instantly recognizable **lime-green ("brat green") branding**.

**Three-force assessment:**
- **Trend:** Declining from the 2024 "brat summer" peak.
- **Legal:** **GATE STOPS HERE.** While the bare dictionary word "brat" is generic, in *this trend context* it carries **Charli XCX's common-law trademark / brand identity**, and the distinctive lime-green look functions as trade dress. Commercially exploiting "brat" (especially with the green) trades on her brand → infringement/dilution exposure + publicity risk. Legal commentary widely flags POD use as risky. → `trademark_status: disputed`, `legal_verdict: avoid`.
- **Ethical:** Word itself is clean; passes 12-yo test. Legal force already kills it.

**POD guidance:** **Do not produce** "brat"-branded merch riding the album, and **never** use the lime-green trade dress. Worked example of common-law TM + trade-dress rejecting a phrase that *looks* like a generic word. Hard `NO`. See sibling [[trend-brat-summer]].
