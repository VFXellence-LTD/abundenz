# Line-Art Animals

## Description
**Line-art animals** depict a creature using clean, often continuous, outline strokes with minimal or no fill — the elegance comes from economy. The style spans a delicate single unbroken line (continuous-line drawing), faceted geometric/low-poly forms, and crisp monoline silhouettes. The animal becomes a graceful, modern emblem rather than a realistic portrait.

## Visual vocabulary
- **Continuous line:** one unbroken stroke describing the whole animal — the signature minimalist look.
- **Monoline:** uniform stroke weight throughout; clean and print-friendly.
- **Geometric/low-poly:** the form built from triangular facets or intersecting straight lines.
- **Open silhouette:** outline only, interior left empty (great for placing patterns or quotes inside).
- **Selective detail:** eye, antlers, or feathers detailed while the body stays simple.
- **Negative space:** the empty interior is part of the design — sometimes filled with a landscape, galaxy, or floral.

## Where it shines
- **Apparel (best):** clean single-color print, scales beautifully, gender-neutral, evergreen. A staple POD category.
- **Brand marks:** modern minimal logos (a monoline fox, a geometric deer).
- **Posters & nursery art:** elegant animal series, alphabet-animal sets for children's rooms.
- **Merch:** enamel pins, tote bags, phone cases, stickers.
- **Struggles:** highly detailed/realistic contexts; very small sizes for the most delicate continuous-line versions (strokes merge).

## Pairs with

- **Palettes:** monochrome ink, single accent on cream, duotone, soft pastel (nursery). (link section 11)

## Remix hooks
- **Continuous single-line** version = premium-feeling, gallery-ready apparel.
- **Double-exposure fill:** the animal silhouette filled with a forest, mountain range, or starfield.
- **Animal built from other objects** (a wolf made of pine trees, a whale made of waves) — clever conceptual POD.
- **Duotone split:** half clean line / half watercolor-bleed for a modern-craft look.
- **Geometric low-poly** for a tech/modern brand feel.

## AI prompt phrasing
- **Core phrase:** `minimalist continuous single-line drawing of a [ANIMAL], one unbroken stroke, clean monoline, black on white, elegant`
- **Geometric variant:** `geometric low-poly [ANIMAL], faceted triangular form, line art, modern`
- **Fill variant:** `[ANIMAL] silhouette outline filled with a starry night sky / pine forest, double exposure, flat`
- **Modifiers:** `monoline`, `negative space`, `nursery pastel`, `gold foil line`, `mid-century minimal`
- **Negative hints:** suppress `realistic fur, photo, 3d, cartoon mascot, brand logo, copyrighted character`.

## Legal note
Line art as a technique is free; a generic real-species animal drawn in it is safe. Never render a *specific* IP character animal or a brand mascot — even stylized or geometric versions of Pikachu, Hobbes, the Lacoste crocodile, or the WWF panda infringe. Keep animals generic and species-accurate.
