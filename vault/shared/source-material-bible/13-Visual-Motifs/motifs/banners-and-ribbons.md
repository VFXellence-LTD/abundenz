# Banners & Ribbons

## Description
A **banner/ribbon** is a strip of "fabric" or "parchment" drawn with folds, curls, or swallowtails, carrying a short line of text — a name, motto, date, or proclamation. It is the design world's caption-holder: wherever a piece of art needs to *say something* declaratively, a ribbon unfurls beneath, above, or across it.

## Visual vocabulary
- **Ribbon types:** the classic three-section tattoo banner (center panel + two angled tails), the curled scroll/banderole, the flat modern "tab," the swallowtail pennant, and **bunting** (a string of triangular flags).
- **Folds & shadow:** end tails fold back on themselves; a darker tone on the underside of folds creates depth.
- **Notched tails:** ends cut in a "fishtail"/swallowtail V.
- **Text fitting:** lettering bends slightly to follow the ribbon's curve; tight, bold, condensed faces fit best.
- **Anchor behavior:** ribbons wrap *around* a central object (encircling a heart, anchor, skull) or sit flat beneath it.

## Where it shines
- **Apparel:** the single most reliable way to add a word/name to a graphic — "MOM," a band name, a city, a date. Core of American traditional tattoo style.
- **Posters/social:** ties a headline to a hero image; bunting strings festive event graphics together.
- **Brand/merch:** "since 19XX" tabs, name banners on enamel pins and stickers.
- **Struggles:** holding long text (folds eat legibility); ultra-minimal brand systems (can read as kitsch unless flattened).

## Pairs with
- **Categories / subjects:** tattoo-flash subjects (hearts, daggers, anchors, swallows, roses), sports/team names, family/sentiment, events/festivals.
- **Palettes:** classic-tattoo (red + black + cream), heritage muted, festive primaries. (link section 11)
- **Art movements:** American traditional tattoo, sign-painting/gold-leaf, vintage advertising.
- **Other motifs:** wraps around the center of [[badges-and-crests]], hangs beneath [[laurels-and-wreaths]], radiates from [[retro-sunbursts]], anchors [[vintage-label-layouts]].

## Remix hooks
- **Classic tattoo name banner:** a heart or dagger with a ribbon reading any name — endlessly personalizable POD product (print-on-demand winner).
- **Bunting** of tiny flags spelling a word, one letter per flag — great for nursery/childrens-book and party merch.
- Flatten to a **modern minimal tab** for clean brand "EST." marks.
- Stack three ribbons (top motto / middle name / bottom date) for a full heraldic statement.

## AI prompt phrasing
- **Core phrase:** `classic banner ribbon scroll with folded ends and swallowtail tails, bold text on the center panel, flat vector, traditional tattoo style`
- **Bunting variant:** `string of triangular bunting flags, one letter per flag, festive, flat illustration`
- **Modifiers:** `american traditional tattoo`, `sign painter gold leaf`, `engraved banderole scroll`, `subtle fold shadows`, `condensed bold lettering`
- **Negative hints:** suppress `long paragraph text, photo fabric, 3d ribbon, gradient` for clean flat output.

## Legal note
The ribbon/scroll form is entirely generic — zero IP in the shape. Only the *text* you letter onto it can create risk: never set a trademarked tagline, a protected brand slogan, or a real person's name presented as endorsement. Invented mottos, generic names, and dates are all safe.
