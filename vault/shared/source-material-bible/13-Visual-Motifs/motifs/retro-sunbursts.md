# Retro Sunbursts

## Description
A **retro sunburst** radiates lines or wedges outward from a central or horizon-anchored sun, evoking dawn, optimism, and 1960s–70s warmth. Variants include the **starburst** (sharp pointed rays), the **wedge sunburst** (alternating triangular rays), and the **stacked-stripe sunset** (horizontal bands of warm color). It floods a composition with energy and instantly time-stamps it as groovy, hopeful, and analog.

## Visual vocabulary
- **Radiating rays:** straight lines, thin triangles, or alternating wide/narrow wedges fanning from a point.
- **Anchor point:** centered (full sunburst), bottom-center (rising/horizon sun), or off-corner.
- **Stacked-stripe sunset:** concentric horizontal bands in a warm gradient (orange→pink→cream).
- **Wedge alternation:** two-color alternating rays create a spinning/pinwheel energy.
- **Halo behavior:** rays often radiate from *behind* text or a central icon, haloing it.
- **Curved/groovy rays:** wavy rather than straight for a psychedelic '70s feel.

## Where it shines
- **Apparel (best):** behind a slogan or icon — the rays add instant retro energy and fill the print area.
- **Posters & packaging:** event/festival posters, retro product packaging, surf/skate graphics.
- **Brand:** retro-revival logos, "good vibes" lifestyle marks.
- **Social:** announcement and promo graphics ("NEW DROP" haloed by rays).
- **Struggles:** corporate-serious contexts; very small scale (thin rays merge — use bold wedges).

## Pairs with
- **Categories / subjects:** summer/beach/surf, optimism/affirmation quotes, '70s nostalgia, music/festival, camping/national-park revival.
- **Palettes:** '70s warm (mustard + rust + cream), sunset gradient (orange→pink), two-tone pop. (link section 11)
- **Art movements:** 1970s/psychedelic, mid-century, Art Deco (geometric ray fans), retro-Americana.
- **Other motifs:** halos a [[banners-and-ribbons]] motto, fills inside [[badges-and-crests]] and [[vintage-label-layouts]], layers over [[geometric-backdrops]].

## Remix hooks
- **'70s stacked sunset** behind a black silhouette (mountains, palms, a figure) = the quintessential retro tee.
- **Behind-text halo:** rays radiating from behind a single bold word.
- **Half-sunburst on a horizon** = sunrise/sunset scene; pairs with [[line-art-animals]] silhouettes.
- **Wavy groovy rays** in two tones for psychedelic energy.
- **Wedge sunburst** in deco gold + black for a 1920s-meets-retro look.

- **Negative hints:** suppress `imperial rising sun flag, red 16 rays on white, photo, 3d, realistic sun` (avoid the offensive military-flag arrangement and keep it flat).

## Legal note
Radiating sun-rays are an ancient free convention. Avoid the specific Imperial Japanese Rising Sun arrangement (16 red rays on white) — historically offensive across East Asia — and do not clone a particular brand's sunburst logo. Generic retro sunbursts are safe.
