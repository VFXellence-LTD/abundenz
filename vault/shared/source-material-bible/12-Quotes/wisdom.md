# Wisdom Quotes — Verified Public-Domain Collection

> Quiet, grounded lines about knowing, learning, and the examined life. The backbone of "sage"-toned posters, journals, and podcast cold opens.

This collection leans on the classical philosophers, the great essayists (Emerson, Thoreau), Shakespeare, and the wisdom literature of antiquity. Where a work was originally in another language, only **public-domain translations** are quoted and the translator is named.

## How to use this collection
- Filter by the per-quote `applications` line.
- Render the **Attribution** line exactly.
- Ship only quotes marked `pd_verified: true`.

### Q22 — common sense

> "Common sense is not so common."

- **Attribution:** commonly attributed to Voltaire, *Dictionnaire philosophique portatif* (1764) — **caution on attribution**
- **Author:** Voltaire (1694–1778) (attribution contested)
- **pd_verified:** false
- **pd_basis:** "Voltaire (d. 1778) is firmly PD, so copyright is not the issue. The ATTRIBUTION is contested: in the article 'Sens commun' Voltaire prefaced the remark with words to the effect of 'people say sometimes,' i.e. he was repeating a proverbial saying rather than coining it, and no exact source for the pithy 'Le sens commun n'est pas si commun' has ever been pinned. The same line is also popularly miscredited to Mark Twain and Will Rogers."
- **theme_tags:** [wisdom, sense, society]
- **emotional_tags:** [wry, dry]
- **applications:** [design, social, podcast]
- **risk_level:** caution
- **risk_notes:** "Copyright is fine (Voltaire is PD). But do not present as a precise, verbatim Voltaire quotation — the attribution is contested and the line is proverbial. If used on products, present unattributed as a saying, or attribute as 'attributed to Voltaire.' See [[misattributed-avoid-list]]."
