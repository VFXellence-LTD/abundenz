# Perseverance Quotes — Verified Public-Domain Collection

> Determined, steady lines for gym merch, founder hustle posters, and comeback stories. A deliberately PD-safe alternative to the swarm of fake "motivational" quotes online.

The motivational-quote space is the most polluted corner of the internet for misattribution. This collection gives the pipeline **real, sourced** persistence lines so it never has to reach for a fabricated one. The famous fakes are catalogued on [[misattributed-avoid-list]].

## How to use this collection
- Filter by the per-quote `applications` line.
- Render the **Attribution** line exactly.
