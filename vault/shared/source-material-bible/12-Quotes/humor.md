# Humor Quotes — Verified Public-Domain Collection

> Dry, witty, irreverent one-liners for snarky merch, greeting cards, and comedy intros. Mark Twain (pre-1930 works only), Oscar Wilde, Jane Austen, and Ambrose Bierce dominate.

**Twain warning:** Mark Twain (d. 1910) wrote a great deal, and *all* of his genuine work is public domain — but he is also the single most-faked author in quote culture. Use only the sourced lines below; never trust a "Mark Twain said" line you cannot tie to a specific work. The famous fakes are on [[misattributed-avoid-list]].

## How to use this collection
- Filter by the per-quote `applications` line.
- Render the **Attribution** line exactly.
- Ship only quotes marked `pd_verified: true`.
