# Stoicism Quotes — Verified Public-Domain Collection

> Calm, disciplined, sober lines for stoic-minimalist posters, self-mastery merch, and philosophy podcasts. The single best-selling quote niche on POD — and the single most translation-sensitive.

## The translation trap (read before using any quote here)

Marcus Aurelius wrote in Greek; Seneca and (the recorder of) Epictetus in Greek/Latin. **The ancient text is public domain. A specific modern English translation is its own copyrighted work.** The hugely popular phrasings circulating online are very often from **Gregory Hays' 2002 translation** of *Meditations*, which is **in copyright**. Reproducing Hays' wording on a product is infringement even though Marcus Aurelius died in 180 CE.

**This collection quotes ONLY from public-domain translations:**

- **Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations*** — George Long, 1862 (PD); secondary: Meric Casaubon, 1634 (PD).
- **Seneca** (*Letters*, *On the Shortness of Life*, etc.) — Aubrey Stewart (1880s–1900s) and other pre-1930 PD translations; the early PD Loeb-era renderings.
- **Epictetus** (*Enchiridion*, *Discourses*) — Elizabeth Carter, 1758 (PD); P. E. Matheson, 1916 (PD).

Each quote below states its PD translator. If you cannot match a wording to one of these PD translations, do not ship it — it may be a copyrighted modern translation.

## How to use this collection
- Filter by the per-quote `applications` line.
- Render the **Attribution** line exactly, **including the PD translator**.
- Ship only quotes marked `pd_verified: true`.
