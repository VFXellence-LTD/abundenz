# Leadership Quotes — Verified Public-Domain Collection

> Dignified, authoritative lines for office posters, civic prints, and business podcasts. Lincoln's speeches anchor this collection; classical statesmen and Shakespeare's kings supply the rest.

**Key legal note:** Speeches and writings produced by US federal officials **in the course of their official duties** are public domain under 17 U.S.C. §105, regardless of date. That is why Lincoln's and FDR's official addresses are usable. But **20th-century non-government figures** (Churchill, JFK as estate-managed, MLK) are *not* free — see [[misattributed-avoid-list]].

## How to use this collection
- Filter by the per-quote `applications` line.
- Render the **Attribution** line exactly.
- Ship only quotes marked `pd_verified: true`.
