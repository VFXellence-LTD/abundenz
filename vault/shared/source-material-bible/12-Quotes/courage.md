# Courage Quotes — Verified Public-Domain Collection

> Bold, defiant lines for athletic prints, hero-themed merch, and motivational social posts. Heavy on Shakespeare, the Stoics, and the great speeches.

Several of the most-shared "courage" quotes online are modern or misattributed (the famous Mandela and Twain courage lines). Those are deliberately excluded from this collection and recorded on [[misattributed-avoid-list]].

## How to use this collection
- Filter by the per-quote `applications` line.
- Render the **Attribution** line exactly.
- Ship only quotes marked `pd_verified: true`.
