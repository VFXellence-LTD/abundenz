# Solaraz Sunset

> Violet dusk melting through magenta, coral, and orange into gold — the golden-hour gradient for warm, optimistic, dreamy work.

## Swatches & roles

| Role | Hex | Name | Notes |
|------|-----|------|-------|
| Top / Deep | `#3B1E5E` | Dusk Violet | The upper sky; cool anchor for the warm ramp |
| Transition | `#C13584` | Magenta Glow | Where day meets night; rich mid-stop |
| Mid | `#F5567B` | Coral Pink | Warm heart of the sunset |
| Primary | `#FF8E53` | Sunset Orange | The dominant glow; fills and focus |
| Highlight | `#FFC15E` | Golden Hour | Warm gold; the sun, brightest light |
| Background | `#FFF1D6` | Warm Light | Pale warm ground / sky-near-horizon |

## Structure

A warm **analogous gradient** spanning violet → magenta → coral → orange → gold, designed to be read as a literal sunset sky from top to horizon. The single cool anchor (dusk violet) gives the warm sweep somewhere to resolve and prevents it from feeling flat. Built for smooth blends.

## Mood & emotional read

Warm, optimistic, uplifting, dreamy, nostalgic. Reads as sunsets, summer, golden hour, good feelings, and possibility. Universally appealing and emotionally positive. Strong for travel, wellness, music, motivational, and feel-good content.

## Usage notes

This palette *is* a gradient — its best expression is a smooth blend (top violet to bottom gold), not flat blocks, though it also works as discrete stripes (retro sunset bars). Place type in dusk violet or a near-black over the lighter warm areas, or in warm-light/gold over the darker top. Use golden hour as the single brightest accent (the sun). Excellent for AI **video** (animated sky glow).

## POD / print considerations

- **CMYK**: `partial` — the brightest coral, magenta, and orange are vivid and can lose some punch in CMYK (the gamut struggles with the most saturated warm pinks/oranges). Gradients also risk **banding** in print — use enough resolution and a little noise/dither to keep blends smooth. The palette is at its best on **screen** (social, video); for print, accept slightly muted brights and proof gradients.
- **Apparel**: Strong as a printed gradient graphic on **white/light garments** (the warm sky reads clean). On black, the dark violet top blends into the shirt — lead with the coral/orange/gold band as the visible design. Gradient prints need DTG or high-quality screen separations; cheap printing may band.
- **Accessibility**: Dusk violet or near-black on the gold/warm-light end passes AA. Avoid coral/orange text on the warm-light ground (too low contrast). Keep text on the highest-contrast stop available.

## Remix hooks

- Sunset over night-sea: pair the warm ramp above a deep navy/violet ocean.
- Poster duotone: reduce to two stops (orange + dusk violet) for a bold flat poster.
- Magic hour: cool the top further toward purple-blue for a moodier twilight.

## Legal / risk

Warm gradients and sunset color sweeps are generic and unownable — safe across all uses and categories. No brand owns "a sunset". The only practical caveat is print fidelity (bright warm hues and gradient banding in CMYK), not legality. Keep any added logos/type original.
