# Bloomz Spring Pastel

> Blossom pink against fresh green, with peach, sky, and lilac — a soft complementary spring palette, brighter and more playful than the nursery set.

## Swatches & roles

| Role | Hex | Name | Notes |
|------|-----|------|-------|
| Primary | `#BFE3C0` | Spring Leaf | Fresh soft green; the "growth" ground |
| Accent (complement) | `#F6B6C8` | Blossom Pink | The complementary pop; flowers, focus |
| Warm | `#F7D9A0` | Peach Cream | Soft warm; sun, light, balance |
| Cool | `#A9D4E5` | Spring Sky | Light blue; sky, air, freshness |
| Soft | `#E3C6E8` | Lilac Bloom | Gentle purple; secondary florals |
| Background | `#FFFCF6` | Cloud White | Warm-clean white ground |

## Structure

A soft **complementary** core (spring leaf green vs. blossom pink sit opposite on the wheel) rounded out with light analogous support (peach, sky, lilac). Higher in chroma than [[dreamz-pastel]] — playful and fresh rather than purely soothing — but still pastel and gentle. The green-vs-pink complement gives it more energy and pop than a single-family pastel set.

## Mood & emotional read

Fresh, joyful, light, optimistic, sweet. Reads as spring, blossom, gardens, renewal, and gentle celebration (Easter, Mother's Day, spring launches). Cheerful and inviting without being loud. Broadly appealing and unisex-leaning.

## Usage notes

Use cloud white as the ground and spring leaf as the primary color field; blossom pink is the complementary focal accent (flowers, hearts, the "hero" element) — its pop against the green is what makes the palette sing. Peach, sky, and lilac are supporting florals. For text, use a soft dark (e.g. `#4A4458` slate-plum or spring leaf darkened) — never pastel-on-pastel. Keep shapes soft and floral; watercolor texture suits it well. Proportion: white + green ground, pink as the deliberate accent, others in small floral doses.

## Pairs with (sections / categories)

Relatives: [[dreamz-pastel]] (softer/calmer sibling), [[verdanz-botanical]] (the green nature counterpart), [[solaraz-sunset]] (warm seasonal).

## POD / print considerations

- **CMYK**: Soft pastels generally print well, but like all light tints they can **drift or look patchy at low ink coverage** and the complementary green/pink may shift slightly — specify a hair more saturation than the screen value so they don't wash out on paper. The green is a gentle tint (not a vivid green), so it avoids the worst of the CMYK-green problem.
- **Apparel**: Designed for **white/light garments** — the soft fresh look depends on a light ground. On dark fabric pastels go chalky and need a white underbase (rarely worth it). For kids' spring apparel, confirm child-safe certified inks.
- **Accessibility**: Pastel-on-pastel has poor contrast — never use it for text. Pair words with a soft dark. For greeting cards/books, ensure illustration-vs-text contrast stays readable.

## Remix hooks

- Focal flower: deepen blossom pink to `#EE7FA0` for the hero bloom so it stands out from the soft field.
- Spring luxe: add soft `#D9BA6A` muted gold line-art for an elevated seasonal feel.
- Earthy spring: shift toward peach + sage for a calmer, more grown-up palette (toward [[verdanz-botanical]]).

## Legal / risk

Soft complementary pastels are generic and unownable — safe across all uses and categories. No brand owns "spring pastels". The only standing caution is the universal one: don't reproduce a *specific* seasonal artwork, character, or mascot (e.g. a brand's Easter character). The palette and its color relationships are completely free to use.
