# Snow White

> The fairest in the land, a jealous queen, a poisoned apple, and a glass coffin in the forest — a tale of envy, false death, and awakening.

## Origin & Tradition
A widespread European tale type (ATU 709) fixed most influentially by the Brothers Grimm in 1812 (*Schneewittchen*). The Grimm version supplies the talking mirror, the heart demanded as proof, the comb and lace and apple poisonings, and the glass coffin. The Grimm source text is public domain. The 1937 Disney film, by contrast, is a 20th-century work still under copyright.

## The Story / The Figure
A queen, obsessed with being "the fairest of them all," is told by her magic mirror that her stepdaughter Snow White has surpassed her. She orders the girl killed; the huntsman spares her; Snow White hides with seven dwarfs in the forest. The disguised queen poisons her with an apple; she falls into a death-like sleep and is laid in a glass coffin; she is revived (by a jolt that dislodges the apple, or by a prince). The queen is punished.

## Visual Identity
Free, traditional motifs: skin "white as snow," lips "red as blood," hair "black as ebony"; a poisoned red apple; an ornate magic mirror; a glass coffin in the woods; a cottage of seven dwarfs (generic, un-named). **The Disney 1937 designs are restricted** — the specific dwarf personalities/names, the queen's costume, and the film's look are all protected.

## Symbolism & Themes
Envy and the destructiveness of vanity; beauty as both gift and target; the false-death-and-awakening motif (a near-resurrection); the contrast of innocence (snow) and corruption (the queen).

## Legal & Cultural Status
Tale type and Grimm 1812 text: **public domain.** Restricted: Disney's 1937 film and all its specific designs and named characters. Defers to [[../../01-Legal-Guidelines/pd-source-vs-modern-adaptation|PD source vs modern adaptation]].

## Remix Hooks
- Retell from the Queen's perspective as a tragedy of aging and envy.
- Lean into horror: the glass coffin, the poisoned apple, the false death.
- Reimagine the magic mirror as an AI oracle.
- Iconic single-motif merch: a poisoned apple, an ornate empty mirror frame.
- Generic "seven of them" guild of helpers (never the Disney names).
