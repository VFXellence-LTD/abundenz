# Sleeping Beauty

> A christening curse, a pricked finger, a hundred-year sleep behind a wall of thorns, and an awakening — the tale of fate, enchantment, and time held still.

## Origin & Tradition
A European tale type (ATU 410). Giambattista Basile's *Sole, Luna e Talia* (1634) is an early, darker form; Charles Perrault's *La Belle au bois dormant* (1697) and the Brothers Grimm's *Dornröschen* ("Little Briar-Rose," 1812) are the canonical fixations, all public domain. Disney's 1959 animated film and its later *Maleficent* spin-offs are 20th/21st-century copyrighted works.

## The Story / The Figure
At a princess's christening, an uninvited or slighted fairy curses her to die (softened by another fairy to a long sleep) on pricking her finger on a spindle. Despite precautions, the curse falls; the princess and her whole court sleep, and a hedge of thorns grows around the castle. After a hundred years a prince breaks through and wakes her, ending the enchantment.

## Visual Identity
Free, traditional motifs: a spinning wheel and spindle; a pricked finger; a vast overgrown thorn/rose-briar hedge; an entire court frozen in sleep mid-action; a dark uninvited fairy. **Restricted:** Disney's 1959 designs and the named, horned **Maleficent** design (her specific look and the *Maleficent* films are protected).

## Symbolism & Themes
Fate and prophecy that cannot be fully outrun; the suspension of time; dormancy and renewal; the danger lurking inside celebration. The thorn hedge is a potent symbol of an inaccessible, frozen world.

## Legal & Cultural Status
Tale type and Basile/Perrault/Grimm source texts: **public domain.** Restricted: Disney's 1959 film, its designs, and the *Maleficent* character design. Defers to [[../../01-Legal-Guidelines/pd-source-vs-modern-adaptation|PD source vs modern adaptation]].

## Remix Hooks
- Tell the dark fairy's side; make the curse a justified grievance (new design, never Disney's Maleficent).
- Treat the sleeping court as eerie, liminal horror.
- A botanical, art-nouveau rose-briar poster.
- The sleeper wakes into a wholly changed future (time-travel re-skin).
