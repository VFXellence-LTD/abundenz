# Greek Mythology

> The deepest archetype-mine in Western culture — gods, heroes, and monsters all free — but **Percy Jackson, Disney's Hercules, and Edith Hamilton's book are not the mythology; they're copyrighted takes on it.**

## Overview
The body of myths of ancient Greece — the Olympian gods (Zeus, Hera, Athena, Poseidon, Hades), heroes (Heracles, Perseus, Theseus, Jason), monsters (Medusa, the Minotaur, the Hydra, Cerberus), and stories of creation, love, and transformation. Recorded in ancient PD texts (Hesiod's *Theogony*, Ovid's *Metamorphoses*, Apollodorus). The myths and their figures are ancient folk material with no copyright. **The trap is the modern layer**: contemporary translations and retellings, plus studio/game character designs, are all separately copyrighted.

## Themes
- **Hubris and nemesis** — overreaching pride and its divine punishment.
- **Fate vs. free will** — oracles, prophecies, and the struggle against them.
- **Love and transformation** — Ovid's metamorphoses; gods and mortals entangled.
- **The hero quest** — labors, monsters, and trials.
- **Gods among mortals** — the divine meddling in human affairs.
- **Punishment and justice** — Prometheus, Sisyphus, Tantalus.

## Symbols
- **Mount Olympus / the throne** — divine power.
- **The labyrinth and the Minotaur** — the maze, the monster within.
- **Medusa's gaze** — the petrifying look (rich for feminist reframing).
- **The laurel wreath** — victory, poetry (Daphne and Apollo).
- **Pandora's box** — released evils, and hope remaining.
- **Winged sandals / Pegasus / the lightning bolt / the trident** — divine attributes.

## Character archetypes
- **Zeus** — the King of the Gods.
- **Heracles / Perseus / Theseus / Jason** — the Heroes on Quests (mythological versions free; **Disney's Hercules and game designs are not**).
- **Medusa / the Minotaur / the Hydra** — the Monstrous Foes.
- **Athena / Hera / the Furies** — wisdom, jealousy, vengeance.
- **Icarus / Sisyphus / Prometheus** — the Tragic Overreachers.

## Visual motifs
- Greek temples, columns, laurel wreaths, black-figure/red-figure pottery.
- Medusa's head, the Minotaur in the labyrinth, Pegasus, winged sandals, Olympian thrones.

## Reusable concepts
- A near-infinite **archetype and monster library** for character design.
- "Hubris and nemesis" and "the labyrinth" as portable structures.
- "Pandora's box," "Achilles' heel," "Midas touch," "Sisyphean" as everyday metaphors.

- **Attribution:** distinguish ancient myth from a modern author's invention layered on top.
