# Sherlock Holmes (Early Canon)

> The archetypal detective — now *fully* US public domain (since Jan 1 2023), with the famous deerstalker silhouette coming from PD Paget illustrations, not the modern films.

## Overview
Arthur Conan Doyle's consulting detective debuted in *A Study in Scarlet* (1887) and ran across four novels and 56 short stories through 1927. Holmes solves crimes by minute observation and deductive reasoning, narrated by his loyal friend Dr. Watson, from 221B Baker Street in foggy Victorian/Edwardian London. **As of January 1, 2023, the entire original canon is in the US public domain**, ending years of Conan Doyle Estate litigation. We still mark this **caution** because (a) live entertainment trademarks exist on "Sherlock Holmes," and (b) jurisdictions outside the US/UK vary.

## Themes
- **Deduction and logic** — the triumph of reason; reading the world like a text.
- **Order from chaos** — the detective restores sense to a baffling crime.
- **The eccentric genius** — brilliant, aloof, addicted, magnetic.
- **Friendship and loyalty** — the Holmes/Watson bond is the emotional core.
- **Crime and justice** — Victorian anxieties about the modern city.

## Symbols
- **The deerstalker, Inverness cape, and curved pipe** — the iconic silhouette (Paget illustrations + stage; **Paget art is PD**).
- **The magnifying glass** — scrutiny, evidence.
- **221B Baker Street** — the sanctum of reason.
- **The violin** — Holmes's restless, artistic mind.
- **Fog-bound gaslit London** — the atmospheric stage.

## Character archetypes
- **Sherlock Holmes** — the Eccentric Genius Detective (canon PD; modern film designs not).
- **Dr. Watson** — the Loyal Chronicler / everyman narrator.
- **Professor Moriarty** — the Criminal Mastermind / "Napoleon of crime."
- **Inspector Lestrade** — the Bumbling Official.

## Visual motifs
- Foggy Victorian London, gas-lit cobbled streets, hansom cabs.
- Cluttered study, magnifying glass over a clue, pipe-smoking silhouette in a window.

## Reusable concepts
- The **detective format** — observe, deduce, reveal (a content engine for puzzles, podcasts, video).
- The brilliant-detective + grounded-sidekick **duo dynamic**.
- "The game is afoot" investigation framing.

## Inspirational quotes
> "When you have eliminated the impossible, whatever remains, however improbable, must be the truth." — Sherlock Holmes, *The Sign of the Four* (1890)

> "You see, but you do not observe." — Sherlock Holmes, *A Scandal in Bohemia* (1891)

> "The game is afoot." — Sherlock Holmes, *The Adventure of the Abbey Grange* (1904) (echoing Shakespeare's PD line)

(All from PD canon stories.)

## Risks
- **Copyright (text):** all 60 original stories US PD since Jan 1 2023; fully PD in UK. **Check other jurisdictions** (some had differing terms).
- **Adaptation trap:** BBC "Sherlock" (Cumberbatch) and Warner films (Downey Jr.) designs and likenesses RESTRICTED. Paget illustrations are PD and safe.
- **Trademark:** "SHERLOCK HOLMES" has live entertainment-class registrations — do not imply affiliation with a current franchise.
