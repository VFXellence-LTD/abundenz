# Peter Pan

> The boy who wouldn't grow up — US public domain, but carrying the Bible's most unusual legal footnote: a *perpetual UK royalty* owed to a children's hospital.

## Overview
J.M. Barrie's Peter Pan first appeared in the 1904 stage play and the 1911 novel *Peter and Wendy*. Peter, an eternal child, takes the Darling children to Neverland — a world of fairies, pirates, mermaids, and Lost Boys — where he battles Captain Hook. In the **US** the 1911 novel is public domain. The famous quirk: in the **UK**, Barrie gifted the rights to **Great Ormond Street Hospital (GOSH)**, and UK law grants GOSH a **perpetual royalty** on commercial UK stage performance, publication, and broadcast of the work — even though ordinary copyright has lapsed. We mark this **caution** specifically so anyone publishing/performing commercially **in the UK** accounts for that royalty.

## Themes
- **Eternal childhood / refusal to grow up** — Peter's gift and his tragedy.
- **Imagination and play** — Neverland as the landscape of a child's mind.
- **Lost innocence and the passage of time** — the bittersweet undertow (the ticking crocodile = mortality).
- **Motherhood and belonging** — Wendy as mother to the Lost Boys; the longing for home.
- **Adventure** — pirates, mermaids, flight.

## Symbols
- **Neverland** — imagination, the eternal present, escape.
- **The shadow** — the self that can be detached and sewn back; identity.
- **Fairy dust / flight** — belief and joy as the power to rise ("think happy thoughts").
- **The ticking crocodile** — time and death pursuing Hook.
- **The hook** — fixation, vengeance, the wounded adult.
- **The second star to the right** — wayfinding to wonder.

## Character archetypes
- **Peter Pan** — the Eternal Child (text PD; *Disney design* restricted).
- **Tinker Bell** — the Fairy Companion (Barrie's fairy is PD as a character; **Disney's specific Tinker Bell design is a trademarked mascot** — avoid).
- **Captain Hook** — the Theatrical Pirate Villain.
- **Wendy** — the Mother-Figure / "Wendy bird."
- **The Lost Boys** — the band of orphan-children.

## Visual motifs
- Flying over moonlit London rooftops; the second star to the right.
- Neverland island, pirate ship, mermaid lagoon, ticking crocodile.
- A fairy with a light-trail (render generically, NOT as Disney's Tink).

## Reusable concepts
- **Neverland as "imagination space"** — a portable setting for any childlike-wonder narrative.
- "The cost of never growing up" — a mature thematic engine.
- Pirate-adventure aesthetic.

## Risks
- **Copyright:** US PD (1911 novel). **UK: ordinary copyright lapsed but a PERPETUAL GOSH ROYALTY applies to commercial UK performance/publication/broadcast** — budget for it or restrict UK commercial use.
- **Adaptation trap:** Disney 1953 designs (Peter's look, Captain Hook) RESTRICTED; **Tinker Bell's Disney design is a trademarked mascot** — do not use.
- **Jurisdiction:** terms differ internationally — verify per market.
