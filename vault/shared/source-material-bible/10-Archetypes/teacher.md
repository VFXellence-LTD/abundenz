# The Teacher

> One-line essence: the one who grows others by passing on what they know.

## Description
The Teacher is the *transmitter* of wisdom — the bridge between knowledge and the next person who needs it. Where the [[sage]] seeks and holds truth, the Teacher is defined by the act of giving it away, patiently, in a form the learner can absorb. The mentor who trains the [[hero]], the master who takes an apprentice, the elder who hands down the craft, the coach who builds confidence — all are the Teacher. The pattern's drama lives in the relationship: the bond between mentor and student, the moment the student surpasses the master, the legacy carried forward.

The Teacher overlaps with both the Sage (the source of the knowledge) and the [[caregiver]] (the nurturing of a person), but is its own pattern because its defining motivation is *empowerment through transmission*. A Teacher succeeds only when the student no longer needs them.

In branding, the Teacher is the empowerment-and-growth voice: coaching, courses, how-to and skill content, "I'll show you how," mentorship and onboarding. It promises that you can learn this, with the right guide.

## Core values
- Generosity with knowledge.
- Patience and meeting the learner where they are.
- Growth of others as the measure of success.
- Legacy — passing the torch.
- Belief in the student's potential.

## Motivations
- **Deep want:** to see others grow and to leave knowledge that outlives them.
- **Deep fear:** that what they know will die with them, or that they will fail the student.

## Shadow
The shadow Teacher is the **condescending pedant** or the **controlling mentor** — someone who teaches to feel superior, who keeps the student dependent rather than freeing them, who cannot let the student surpass them. The bond becomes a leash.

## Visual motifs
Elder and student side by side; a hand pointing toward the horizon or guiding another hand; the passing of a torch or staff; the classroom, the dojo, the workshop. See [[13-Visual-Motifs]] for "passing the torch" and "the lesson" compositions.

## Colors
Warm, trustworthy, encouraging — chalkboard green, warm oak brown, honeyed gold, soft sky blue. See [[08-Color-Palettes]] — chalkboard-and-oak and mentor-warmth palettes.

## Symbols
The open book and the passed torch (transmission), the guiding hand, the key (unlocking the student), the bridge and ladder (helping the learner cross/climb), the pointing staff. Cross-link [[09-Symbols]].

## Story uses
The mentor of the [[hero]] or [[explorer]]; the master-apprentice arc; the teacher whose lesson lands only after they're gone; the mentor revealed as flawed or as the antagonist. Pairs with nearly every protagonist archetype.

## Design uses
Course and coaching brands, educational children's books, "learn / grow / teach" slogan merch, classroom and graduation posters, mentorship and onboarding identities, how-to podcast/blog branding.
