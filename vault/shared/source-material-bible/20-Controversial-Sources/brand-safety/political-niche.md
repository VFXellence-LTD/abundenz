# Political Niche — Operating Guide

The political niche — explicitly partisan content, content naming specific politicians, content using campaign slogans, political commentary and satire — is the highest-risk category in this entire section. It requires explicit Boss approval before any launch and mandatory brand isolation from all other content.

This guide does not prohibit political content — it documents the conditions under which it can be pursued responsibly. Given the risk profile, Boss must make an affirmative decision to enter this niche. The default is **do not enter** until that decision is made.

---

## The honest risk assessment

### What makes this niche high-risk

| Risk | Magnitude | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Platform takedowns (Amazon Merch) | HIGH — automated removal of partisan content; account health penalty per removal | Isolation brand; avoid Amazon for explicitly partisan content |
| Platform takedowns (Redbubble, Etsy) | MODERATE | Isolation brand; conservative content framing |
| Trademark infringement (party marks, campaign slogans) | HIGH if using specific marks | USPTO verification before every slogan; use only non-trademarked political language |
| Right-of-publicity (living politicians) | MODERATE — varies by state, use case | No named/depicted politicians on commercial products |
| Reputational damage to main brand | HIGH if not isolated | Mandatory brand isolation |
| Viral backlash | UNPREDICTABLE | Cannot be fully mitigated; isolation limits blast radius |

### Revenue vs. risk calculus

Explicitly partisan content (MAGA products, anti-Trump products, partisan slogans) has real revenue potential but also:
- Shorter product lifecycle (tied to current news cycle and election cycle)
- Higher takedown rate than faith or patriotic content
- No platform certainty — a product that survives today may be removed tomorrow as policies change
- Attracts vocal opponents as well as buyers

The **civic/constitutional niche** (documented in [[patriotic-niche|patriotic-niche]]) captures much of the same patriotic audience at far lower risk. The incremental revenue from explicitly partisan content rarely justifies the operational overhead and account-health exposure on major platforms.

## If Boss decides to proceed — operating rules

### Mandatory isolation
All political content must operate under a separate platform account from both the main VFXellence brand and the faith/patriotic brand. One political takedown must not damage unrelated products.

**Brand name:** `<ISOLATION-BRAND-TBD>` — a dedicated political brand with its own name, identity, and platform accounts, separate from the faith/patriotic isolation brand. (Brand to be named by Boss.)

### Trademark verification before production
Before producing any product with a political slogan:
1. Run a USPTO TESS search (https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) for the exact phrase in Class 25 (clothing) and Class 16 (printed materials).
2. Document the search date and results.
3. If any live registration exists, do not use that phrase.
4. If registration is pending (not yet registered), treat as if registered — do not use.
5. Re-verify 30 days before any major product push, since registration status can change.

### No named living politicians on products
Use political themes and language rather than specific politicians' names or likenesses. "Freedom," "Liberty," "Resistance," "Constitutional Rights," and civic-values language are all safer than a specific politician's name or face.

### Platform selection
For explicitly partisan political content, the platform hierarchy by risk tolerance is:
1. **Own website / Shopify** (lowest restriction, highest setup cost)
2. **Etsy** (more permissive than Amazon for political commentary)
3. **Redbubble** (moderate)
4. **TeePublic** (moderate)
5. **Amazon Merch** (highest restriction; most likely takedown) — consider whether Amazon is worth the account health risk for political products

### Content selection guidelines for lower risk
Lower-risk political content (within the niche):
- Historical political imagery (Lincoln, historical movements, historical events — all PD)
- Civic themes without specific current politicians (freedom, democracy, rights)
- Satire that is clearly labeled as parody/satire (reduces but does not eliminate right-of-publicity risk)
- Generic political messaging not tied to any specific candidate or current campaign

Higher-risk content to approach with extreme caution or avoid:
- Named current politicians (living, active right-of-publicity risk)
- Current campaign slogans (trademark risk; check USPTO)
- Content that could be read as defamatory (false statements of fact about real people)
- Content that could be read as inciting or threatening

---

## The default recommendation

Unless Boss has made an explicit decision to enter the political niche and accepted the operational requirements above, **do not pursue explicitly partisan political content.** The civic/constitutional patriotic niche captures the reachable demand with far lower operational risk. Build that first.
