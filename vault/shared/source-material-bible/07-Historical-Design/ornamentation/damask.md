# Damask Pattern

> A formal, tone-on-tone foliate repeat — mirrored blossoms and ogee frameworks woven so that the pattern reads through sheen rather than color contrast. The default "luxury wallpaper" look.

## Defining Features
Originally a *weave* (reversible figured cloth from Damascus) whose pattern appears through the contrast of warp-faced and weft-faced satin — i.e., the same color seen at different sheen angles. Visually it became a **tone-on-tone, often single-color, symmetrical foliate repeat**: a central mirrored motif (blossom, urn, pomegranate, pineapple) set within a subtle ogee or lozenge lattice, tiled in a half-drop. The hallmark is restraint of contrast and strict mirror symmetry.

## Visual Vocabulary
- **Central medallion** — a large, bilaterally symmetric floral or foliate motif.
- **Ogee framework** — onion-dome-shaped curved lattice that organizes the repeat.
- **Half-drop repeat** — adjacent columns offset by half a unit.
- **Filler motifs** — small leaves/buds bridging the medallions.
- **Tone-on-tone contrast** — pattern emerges from sheen/shade, not bright color.

## Shapes
Mirrored, vertical-axis symmetry; ogee (S-on-S onion) curves; teardrop and almond floral forms; dense but orderly. Seamless tiling in a half-drop grid.

## Textures
Satin-weave cloth with directional sheen, embossed wallpaper, flocked velvet-on-paper, brocade. In flat design, fake the sheen with a slightly lighter/darker tone of the ground; or render as crisp single-color silhouette for a graphic look.

## Palette(s)
Tone-on-tone is signature: black-on-charcoal, ivory-on-cream, gold-on-champagne, burgundy-on-maroon. For graphic punch: white-on-black, metallic gold on deep jewel ground. Low internal contrast is what makes it read as "damask" rather than a generic floral.

## Modern Remix
- **Brand / packaging:** the go-to "premium / gothic glamour" background pattern.
- **POD:** damask all-over print for home goods, journals, phone cases; skull- or bone-damask for the alt/goth market.
- **Mashup:** swap the floral medallion for skulls, leaves, sea-creatures, or circuitry while keeping the ogee structure — instantly fresh.
- **AI prompt:** *"seamless damask wallpaper pattern, tone-on-tone black on charcoal, mirrored foliate medallion, ogee lattice, half-drop repeat."*

## Legal & Risk
Structure and motifs are ancient and free. Only avoid duplicating a specific living homeware/wallpaper brand's exact damask artwork and colorway. Fresh motifs in the damask *structure* are completely safe.
