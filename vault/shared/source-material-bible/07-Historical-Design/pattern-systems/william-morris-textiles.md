# William Morris Textiles (Arts & Crafts)

> Dense, flat, lovingly-naturalistic foliate repeats — meandering branches, layered flowers, and birds among leaves — by the founder of the Arts & Crafts movement, whose own designs are firmly in the public domain.

## Defining Features
William Morris (1834–1896) built patterns from **honest, flat, hand-drawn nature**: meandering primary stems that structure the field, with layers of secondary and tertiary foliage and flowers filling every gap into a rich, breathing density. He rejected fake 3D shading in favor of flat color and clear outline. His repeats often use a **turnover (mirror) structure** and careful stem "netting" so the eye never finds the seam. Birds (Strawberry Thief), willow, acanthus, and English garden flora recur. The mood is verdant, crafted, anti-industrial.

## Visual Vocabulary
- **Meandering stem** — the structural primary branch.
- **Layered fill** — secondary/tertiary leaves and blossoms in receding planes.
- **Turnover repeat** — mirror-symmetric tiling.
- **Inhabited foliage** — birds (thrushes), small creatures among the leaves.
- **Flat color planes** — no airbrushed gradients; clear flat shapes with outline.

## Shapes
Sinuous organic stems; broad flat leaves (acanthus, willow, oak); rounded blossoms; mirror and half-drop repeats; dense edge-to-edge coverage with intentional rhythm. Curvilinear, never geometric.

## Textures
Block-printed cotton and wool, woven tapestry and carpet, hand-printed wallpaper. Implies flat matte color, visible registration, and a slightly hand-pulled imperfection. Best rendered as flat vector planes with crisp outlines.

## Palette(s)
Morris's natural-dye palette — indigo blue, madder red, weld yellow, woad-green, olive, ochre, on cream or deep blue grounds. Muted, earthy, harmonious. Modern remixes can recolor freely (the art is PD).

## Modern Remix
- **POD / home goods:** Morris repeats on tees, totes, bedding, gift wrap; recolored neon/dark-mode versions for cottagecore and goth-botanical markets.
- **Books:** Morris-style endpapers, chapter borders, and decorative spreads (perfect for fairy tales).
- **Brand:** verdant, crafted backgrounds for artisan/organic brands.
- **AI prompt:** *"William Morris style foliate repeat, meandering stems with layered flat flowers and a thrush, indigo and madder on cream, block-print, seamless turnover repeat."*

## Legal & Risk
Morris died in 1896, so his **own original designs are public domain** — Strawberry Thief, Willow Bough, Acanthus, etc. are free to reproduce and remix. Two cautions: (1) a *living* company's **modern recolorings, high-res digitizations, or photographs** of those designs may carry fresh copyright — source from public-domain originals (museum open-access scans), not a current brand's product files; (2) some pattern *names* may be trademarks for goods. Work from PD originals and you are safe.
