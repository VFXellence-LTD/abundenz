# Golden Age

> A radiant gold sunburst over peacock-teal and lacquer-black geometry, with an elegant quote in art-deco display caps. Jazz-age glamour, fully legal — **never branded 'Gatsby'.** Posters, greeting cards, luxe stationery.

**Resulting concept:** A symmetrical **gold sunburst** radiates from a central point over a **lacquer-black** field, framed by stepped geometric **chevron** bands in **peacock teal**. An elegant deco-display quote sits centered within. Optionally a single stylized **gazelle** leaps across the lower third. High glamour, instantly "1920s" — without ever invoking a protected title.

## Target product

- **Surface:** A2/A3 poster; foldable greeting card.
- **Print method / color space:** CMYK giclée; a black + metallic-gold spot version makes premium foil cards.
- **Layout & technical notes:** strict symmetry; sunburst centered upper-two-thirds, type within the rays; generous deco border. Card: emblem outside, blank inside.

## Target category

- **Niche:** vintage/retro-glam décor (via [[14-Trending-Categories/retro-technology]] deco/streamline adjacency).
- **Adjacent niches:** wedding/celebration stationery, [[14-Trending-Categories/mythology]] (golden-age framing).
