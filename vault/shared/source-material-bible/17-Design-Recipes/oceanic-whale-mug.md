**Resulting concept:** A finely-lined **humpback whale** glides diagonally through a band of stylized, repeating **waves**, with a thin compass-rose detail in a corner. Deep ocean teals and navies on a white ground, with light halftone for depth. A vintage nautical-serif quote about the sea sits along the bottom. Serene and spacious — the kind of mug someone keeps on their desk.

## Target product

- **Surface:** ceramic **mug** (wrap); also A3 poster.
- **Print method / color space:** sublimation for the mug (RGB-bright OK); CMYK for the poster. Ocean blues reproduce well in both.
- **Layout & technical notes:** mug = whale centered on the printable wrap, wave band running around the full circumference, quote opposite the handle. Keep critical detail away from the handle seam.

## Legal guardrails

**Never** copy an animated/branded whale or trace a specific copyrighted map; keep the marine animal a generic real species.
