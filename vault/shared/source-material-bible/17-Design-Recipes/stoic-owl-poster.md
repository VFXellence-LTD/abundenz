# Stoic Owl

> A finely-engraved owl in a laurel wreath above a verified philosophy quote, in ink-black on warm paper. The default "thinking person's wall art." Sells into stoicism, study, and graduation gifting.

**Resulting concept:** A symmetrical engraving-style **owl** faces forward, wings slightly open, framed by an open **laurel wreath**. Below, a verified Stoic wisdom quote is set in a high-contrast didone serif with the attribution in small caps. Pure ink-black linework on a warm cream ground, with fine cross-hatch shading. Reads as an antique frontispiece — equally at home as a study poster, a graduation gift, or a philosophy-podcast merch print.

## Target product

- **Surface:** A2/A3 wall poster (portrait).
- **Print method / color space:** CMYK giclée at 300dpi; effectively 1-color so it screen-prints and risograph-prints cheaply too.
- **Layout & technical notes:** owl + wreath occupy top 60%, quote block bottom 35%, generous margins. Min type size for attribution ≥10pt at final size.

## Target category

- **Niche:** [[14-Trending-Categories/religion-spirituality]] (philosophy/stoicism sub-niche) — study, mindfulness, and gift buyers.
- **Adjacent niches:** [[14-Trending-Categories/musical-instruments]] is unrelated; better adjacents are education/graduation gifting and motivational décor.

## Legal guardrails

**Never** render the owl as a recognizable IP mascot, and **never** ship a quote that isn't on the verified PD list with exact attribution.

- [[line-art-animals]] — real owl only; no Duolingo/Hedwig/mascot owls.
- [[wisdom]] — verified PD lines only; quote translated works from PD translations with translator noted; avoid the misattributed list.
- [[monocrome-ink]] — color is free; keep marks/type original.
