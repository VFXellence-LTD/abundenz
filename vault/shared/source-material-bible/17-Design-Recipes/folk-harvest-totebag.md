**Resulting concept:** A **rising sun** sits at center, ringed by a symmetrical **folk bouquet** of stylized wheat, tulips, and leaves, with a small **rooster** perched among them. Warm terracotta `#A8492A`, ochre, and olive on cream, in a block-print/woodcut texture. A homely proverb arcs beneath in folk-serif lettering. Reads as a hand-printed market tote or kitchen tea-towel.

## Target product

- **Surface:** natural cotton **tote**; cotton/linen **tea-towel**.
- **Print method / color space:** screen/block-print (1–3 earthy inks) on canvas/linen; CMYK if digital. Earth tones are CMYK-safe.
- **Layout & technical notes:** centered ~10" on tote; for tea-towels keep a generous cream border; block-print texture hides minor registration shifts (forgiving for cheap runs).

## Legal guardrails

**Never** market a generic folk pattern as a specific culture's authentic craft; keep motifs generically folk and credit any specific tradition you draw on.

- [[terrafolk-earthen]] — earth tones unownable; cultural-sensitivity note for living traditions.

- [[wisdom]] — verified PD lines only, exact attribution.
