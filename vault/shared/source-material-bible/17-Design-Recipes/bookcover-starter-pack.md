or rendering a specific actor's likeness. Use generic period detective iconography. Original. --ar 2:3

## Legal guardrails (whole pack)
**Never** imitate a known book's trade dress, use character lookalikes on kids' covers, copy Disney/Burton Wonderland designs, title a cover "Sherlock Holmes", or use trademarked fonts. Use verified-PD or original text. Full caveats in each linked recipe and ingredient.
