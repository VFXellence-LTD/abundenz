# Pharaoh of the Cosmos

> A jackal-headed astronaut-god beneath a haloed sun-disc, hieroglyphs reimagined as a star-chart — Ancient Egypt as a voyage between worlds. Gold on deep lapis. For the mythology-meets-space crowd.

**Resulting concept:** A front-facing falcon-headed figure stands within a tall lapis arch, a radiant gold **sun-disc** behind its head doubling as a distant star. Rows of stylized hieroglyphs flank the figure but read as a **star-chart**. Everything is gold linework on deep lapis-blue, with a thin band of geometric Egyptian ornament top and bottom. A short verified wisdom line about eternity sits in geometric gold caps along the lower band. The piece feels like a tomb relief that is secretly a spacecraft schematic.

## Target product

- **Surface:** unisex tee (dark navy or black garment) — gold reads brilliantly on dark.
- **Print method / color space:** DTG or screen-print; a 2-color reduction (metallic gold + white) is ideal for screen economy. Poster variant: full CMYK giclée, 300dpi.
- **Layout & technical notes:** centered vertical emblem, ~10–11" tall on tee; keep gold linework ≥2px; on poster push lapis to a subtle gradient and add halftone texture to the sun-disc.

## Legal guardrails

**Never** reproduce a specific franchise's Egyptian-space design, any Marvel/DC/game version of an Egyptian deity, or any real spacecraft livery/agency logo. Build only from ancient museum references + generic cosmos imagery.

- [[pharaohz-gold-lapis]] — color is free; don't copy a museum photo's exact processed colors or imply museum/state endorsement.
- [[ancient-egypt-x-space]] — avoid Stargate / Moon Knight / game-Anubis designs and trademarked craft livery.
- [[line-art-animals]] — keep the falcon a real bird, never a branded mascot.
- [[wisdom]] — ship only verified PD lines, attribute exactly.
