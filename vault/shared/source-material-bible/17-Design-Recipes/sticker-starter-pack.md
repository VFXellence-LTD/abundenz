# Sticker Starter Pack

> Six ready-to-run sticker recipes, each a small specialization of [[master-formula]] sized for die-cut vinyl. Sell as a themed pack or cherry-pick bestsellers. Shared spec: **die-cut vinyl, ~3", 1/8" bleed, bold silhouette.**

Each sub-recipe below is a catalogued recipe in its own right; they share a file because they share a product surface.

---

### S1 — Eternal Snake (occult)
**Formula:** `[Ouroboros] + [Ink/Gold Palette] + [thin serif word]`
| Slot | Entry | Link |
|------|-------|------|
| Symbol | Ouroboros | [[ouroboros]] |
| Palette | Monocrome Ink + gold | [[monocrome-ink]] |
**Concept:** A clean ouroboros ring in gold-on-black with "∞" or "AS ABOVE" beneath. **Category:** [[14-Trending-Categories/religion-spirituality]].
**Prompt:**
```
A die-cut occult sticker: an ouroboros (serpent biting its own tail) forming a perfect ring, gold #C9A227
line art on black, blackwork style, centered, 1/8 inch bleed. AVOID copying a specific game/film ouroboros;
design an original coil. Original design. --ar 1:1
```
