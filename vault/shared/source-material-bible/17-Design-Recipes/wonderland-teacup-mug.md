**Resulting concept:** A stack of tilting **oversized teacups** spills toward a melting **pocket-watch**, an **original** grinning cat curls atop, a single **mushroom** and a scatter of **playing-card** suits frame the edge. Soft dreamy pastels (or, for the engraving variant, ink-on-cream à la Tenniel). A public-domain Carroll line ("We're all mad here") sits in playful hand-script. Whimsical, surreal, gift-shop-ready — and entirely free of Disney's protected designs.

## Target product

- **Surface:** ceramic **mug** (wrap); die-cut **sticker**.
- **Print method / color space:** sublimation for mug (pastels bright); CMYK for sticker. Ink-engraving variant prints cheaply in one color.
- **Layout & technical notes:** mug = teacup stack on the front face, playing-card suit band around the rim, quote opposite the handle. Sticker = die-cut to the teacup-and-cat silhouette.

## Legal guardrails

**Never** copy a Disney or Tim Burton Wonderland design; build from Carroll's PD text, Tenniel's PD engravings, or original art. A generic blue-pinafore Alice predates Disney and is fine.

- [[alice-wonderland]] — text + Tenniel art PD; Disney/Burton designs restricted.
- [[line-art-animals]] — the grinning cat must be an original design, not a known character.
- Quotes — PD Carroll lines attributed to the work.
