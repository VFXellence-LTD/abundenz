# Gothic Raven

> A stark raven silhouette against a blood-red moon, with a line of public-domain gothic verse in blackletter. Red, black, and bone-white. Halloween-evergreen horror décor and apparel.

**Resulting concept:** A solid black **raven** perches on a bare branch, wings half-spread, silhouetted against a huge **crimson moon** `#C8102E` on a near-black field `#0A0506`. A short line of public-domain gothic verse sits below in tightly-set blackletter, bone-white `#E8DCC8`, with a small splatter texture. Stark, two-and-a-half tones, instantly "horror." Anchors a horror/Halloween line that runs all year.

## Target product

- **Surface:** A2 wall poster; also dark-tee print.
- **Print method / color space:** CMYK giclée (or spot red + black for screen). High contrast handles cheap print well.
- **Layout & technical notes:** raven+moon top 60%, verse block bottom; keep blackletter large enough to read (it's dense). Tee: drop the verse to the back, raven on the front.

## Target category

- **Niche:** horror/gothic décor (via [[14-Trending-Categories/mythology]] dark-folk adjacency and Halloween seasonal spikes).
- **Adjacent niches:** [[14-Trending-Categories/wildlife]] (corvids), literary/Poe fandom.

## Legal guardrails

**Never** imitate a horror franchise's logo or a specific film poster's composition, and **never** render the raven as a branded mascot.

- [[crimzon-noir]] — red+black is free; keep logo/wordmark original.
- [[line-art-animals]] — generic real corvid only.
- [[dracula]] — if referenced, use Stoker's PD text only, never the Lugosi film design or his likeness.
- Quotes — verified PD verse only, exact attribution.
