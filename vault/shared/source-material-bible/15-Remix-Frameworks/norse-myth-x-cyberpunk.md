# Norse Mythology × Cyberpunk

> The gods always knew the world would end — Ragnarok was written into the code. This remix runs the Eddas through neon, fiber, and chrome: runes as code, the Bifrost as bandwidth, and the Allfather watching everything through raven-drones.

## The collision
- **Source A:** Norse mythology — the Eddas, the nine realms, Odin, Thor, Loki, the runes, Yggdrasil, the ravens, the valkyries, Ragnarok. Public domain; ancient/folk.
- **Category B:** Cyberpunk — neon megacities, hackers, megacorps, fiber networks, rogue AI, body augmentation, rain-slick noir-future.
- **Tension (the productive clash):** Norse myth is rooted in ice, wood, and iron — primal and analog; cyberpunk is synthetic, electric, and post-human. Runestone meets neon.
- **Harmony (the unexpected rhyme):** *Both are fatalist worlds running toward an inevitable collapse, full of doomed glory and the hunger for forbidden knowledge.* Odin hangs himself to learn the runes; a hacker sacrifices everything for the data. The nine realms are a network. Fate is the system you can't escape. Ragnarok is the crash. The fusion is almost one-to-one.

## Concept generation rules
Translate each mythic element to a cyberpunk equivalent while keeping its fatalist weight. Runes→glowing code. Bifrost→fiber bridge. Yggdrasil→server network. Ravens→drones. Valkyries→data-couriers of the dead. Ragnarok→system crash. Odin's eye→a sacrifice for access. Keep gods drawn from saga references, never the comic versions.

Concept seeds:
1. Runes glowing like neon code down a rain-slick alley.
2. The Bifrost as a fiber-optic rainbow bridge of pure light.
3. Yggdrasil as the planetary server network linking nine data-realms.
4. Huginn and Muninn as the Allfather's surveillance drones (Thought and Memory).
5. Valkyries as armored couriers who upload the minds of the fallen.
6. Ragnarok as the inevitable system crash no patch can stop.
7. Fenrir as a rogue AI straining at its cryptographic chains.
8. A one-eyed hacker who traded an eye to the well for forbidden knowledge.
9. Jormungandr the world-serpent as the network that encircles the globe.
10. The Norns as the algorithm that writes everyone's fate (past/present/future processes).
11. Mjolnir as a signature/access key only the worthy can wield.
12. Loki as the trickster zero-day exploit walking among the corps.
13. Valhalla as a server-afterlife where warriors respawn nightly.
14. The dwarves as the underground fabricators of legendary hardware.
15. Heimdall as the firewall that sees all packets crossing the bridge.

## Visual generation rules
- **Palette:** "Frost & Neon" — black ice #0A0E14, glacier blue #1FA2C4, rune cyan #35E0E0, blood-rust red #B33A2B, valknut violet #6A3FA0, chrome grey #9AA3AA.
- **Primary motif:** runes and Norse knotwork rendered as glowing circuitry/code; armored figures with chrome and fur; neon against rain and snow.
- **Composition / layout rules:** dark backgrounds, single neon-lit figure, vertical megacity depth, runic borders, knotwork that resolves into circuit traces.
- **Render style cues for AI image prompts:** "Norse runestone and knotwork meets neon cyberpunk, glowing runic code, chrome-and-fur armor, rain-slick neon city, frost blue and rune cyan, saga-accurate gods, no superhero costumes, no real tech logos."
- **What to avoid visually:** comic/film superhero Thor/Loki/Odin designs; real cyberpunk-franchise trade dress; real tech-company logos. Saga references + fictional megacorps only.

## Story generation rules
- **Archetype(s):** the Allfather (corporate god-king), the Trickster (Loki-hacker), the Warrior, the Seer, the Outlaw.
- **Core conflict template:** characters know the system is doomed (Ragnarok is coming) and must choose between doomed glory, escape, or sacrificing themselves for knowledge that might change fate.
- **Setting logic:** the nine realms are network domains; the gods are megacorp executives and AIs; fate is the code; the crash is coming.
- **Story seeds:**
  - A data-courier valkyrie smuggles the mind of a murdered hacker out of a corp's server-Valhalla.
  - A one-eyed runner trades pieces of himself for the keys to stop Ragnarok — and learns it can't be stopped.
  - Fenrir, a chained AI, counts down to the day its bonds fail.
