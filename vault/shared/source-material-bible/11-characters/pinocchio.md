# Pinocchio

## Snapshot

The wooden marionette of Carlo Collodi's 1883 Italian novel *The Adventures of Pinocchio* — carved by the woodcarver Geppetto, brought to life, prone to lying (which makes his nose grow), guided by a talking cricket, and ultimately transformed into a real boy after many misadventures.

## Copyright status (the text/expression)

Fully public domain worldwide. Collodi's *The Adventures of Pinocchio* appeared as a book in 1883 (serialized from 1881). Collodi died in 1890 (life+70 long expired). The novel, Pinocchio, Geppetto, the Talking Cricket, the Blue-Haired Fairy, the Cat and the Fox, the Great Fish/whale, and the original Mazzanti/Chiostri illustrations are all free to use.

## What is RESTRICTED (the modern design / trademark / publicity)

- **Disney's 1940 design is RESTRICTED** (Disney copyright + trademark): the specific Disney Pinocchio — bright red shorts with suspenders, a yellow Tyrolean hat with a red feather, a big blue bow tie, white gloves, the rounded "cute" Disney face. None of this is Collodi; it's Disney's character model.
- **"Jiminy Cricket" is a Disney creation.** Collodi's cricket is simply *the Talking Cricket* (il Grillo Parlante) and is killed early in the book. The dapper, umbrella-carrying, top-hatted **"Jiminy Cricket" — including the name — is a Disney invention and trademark.** Don't use the Jiminy design or name.
- **The Disney Blue Fairy design** and Disney's "When You Wish Upon a Star" are also restricted.
- **Collodi's original look is different and safe:** a rougher, more grotesque peasant puppet, often barefoot, in simple/ragged clothes — far less cute than Disney's.

## Risk level and why

**Caution.** The text is fully free, but "Pinocchio" in the popular mind is the Disney design, and the Jiminy Cricket trap is easy to fall into (people assume the cricket is PD when the *Disney* cricket is not). Work from Collodi and you're safe — and more original.

## Safe-use guidance

- **Do:** Use Collodi's text and characters; design your own (or use the rougher 1883-illustration-style) puppet; call the cricket "the Talking Cricket," not Jiminy. Use the growing nose, Geppetto, the Great Fish, the becoming-real arc.
- **Don't:** Copy the Disney outfit/face or any Disney design. Don't use "Jiminy Cricket" (name or design). Don't use the Disney Blue Fairy or Disney songs.
