# Snow White

## Snapshot

The persecuted princess of the Brothers Grimm tale *Schneewittchen* (1812) — "white as snow, red as blood, black as ebony" — driven into the forest by her jealous stepmother the Queen, sheltered by seven dwarfs, poisoned by an apple, and revived. Among the oldest and most retold European fairy tales.

## Copyright status (the text/expression)

Fully public domain worldwide. The Grimms first published it in *Children's and Household Tales* in 1812; the underlying folk tale is older still. Jacob and Wilhelm Grimm died in 1863 and 1859 (life+70 long expired). Snow White, the Evil Queen/Stepmother, the magic mirror, the poisoned apple, the seven (unnamed) dwarfs, the huntsman, the glass coffin — all free to use.

## What is RESTRICTED (the modern design / trademark / publicity)

- **Disney's 1937 designs are RESTRICTED** (Disney copyright + trademark): the specific Disney Snow White — the puffed blue-and-red bodice with a yellow skirt, the white collar, the red hairbow, the bobbed black hair, the rosy Disney face.
- **THE SEVEN DWARFS' NAMES ARE A DISNEY INVENTION.** Grimm's dwarfs are **unnamed**. **Doc, Grumpy, Happy, Sleepy, Bashful, Sneezy, and Dopey** were created by Disney for the 1937 film. The primary protection is **COPYRIGHT** in that film — which does not expire until **January 1, 2033** (95 years from 1937 publication). Disney also holds trademark registrations covering Snow-White-and-the-Seven-Dwarfs branding/merchandise, but the load-bearing restriction on the *names as a set* is copyright, not (as earlier stated) primarily trademark. Practical effect is the same: this is the key trap — people assume the dwarf names are "fairy tale" — they are not. Do not use them until at least 2033. Their individual designs are also Disney's.
- **Disney's specific Evil Queen / Hag design** (the purple-and-black robed Queen, the witch transformation) is likewise restricted.

## Risk level and why

**Caution.** The Grimm tale is fully free, but the cultural default is the Disney film, and the seven dwarf *names* are a particularly easy accidental infringement. Use Grimm's tale with unnamed (or your own newly-named) dwarfs and you're safe.

## Safe-use guidance

- **Do:** Use the Grimm tale and characters; design your own Snow White; depict the poisoned apple, magic mirror, glass coffin; use the dwarfs **unnamed** or with **your own original names**. Use the "white as snow, red as blood, black as ebony" description (PD).
- **Don't:** Copy Disney's Snow White outfit/face or the Disney Evil Queen design. **Never use the seven Disney dwarf names** (Doc/Grumpy/Happy/Sleepy/Bashful/Sneezy/Dopey) or their designs. Don't market under Disney trademarks.
