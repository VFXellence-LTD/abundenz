# Cinderella

## Snapshot

The mistreated heroine of one of the world's most widespread fairy tales — made to sleep among the cinders by her cruel stepmother and stepsisters, magically sent to the royal ball, and identified by the slipper she leaves behind. Best known from Charles Perrault's *Cendrillon* (1697) and the Grimms' *Aschenputtel* (1812).

## Copyright status (the text/expression)

Fully public domain worldwide. Perrault's version dates to 1697; the Grimms' to 1812; antecedents (e.g. the Greco-Egyptian *Rhodopis*) are ancient. All classic literary tellings are long PD. Cinderella, the stepmother, the stepsisters, the fairy godmother, the prince, the glass slipper, the pumpkin coach, and the midnight deadline are all free to use. (The **glass** slipper specifically is Perrault's, and PD.)

## What is RESTRICTED (the modern design / trademark / publicity)

- **Disney's 1950 designs are RESTRICTED** (Disney copyright + trademark): the specific Disney Cinderella — the shimmering silver-blue off-shoulder ball gown, the up-styled hair with headband and choker, the Disney face.
- **The Disney castle silhouette is a live Disney TRADEMARK/LOGO** (it fronts every Disney film). Cinderella's castle as Disney draws it must not be reproduced as a brand mark.
- **The Disney mice (Jaq and Gus)** and other Disney-original sidekicks are Disney creations/names — not in Perrault or Grimm. Don't use them.
- **Disney's fairy godmother design** ("Bibbidi-Bobbidi-Boo" and her specific look) is restricted.

## Risk level and why

**Caution.** The tale is fully free, but the Disney film dominates the mental image, and two specific traps stand out: the Disney **castle logo** (a trademark, not just a design) and the assumption that the cute **mice** are part of the fairy tale (they are Disney's). Work from Perrault/Grimm with your own designs and you're safe.

## Safe-use guidance

- **Do:** Use the Perrault/Grimm tale and characters; design your own Cinderella and ball gown; use the glass slipper (Perrault, PD), the pumpkin coach, midnight, the fairy godmother concept.
- **Don't:** Copy the Disney silver-blue gown or face; **never reproduce the Disney castle logo silhouette**; don't use the Disney mice (Jaq/Gus) or "Bibbidi-Bobbidi-Boo." Don't market under Disney trademarks.

## Creative opportunities

### Design / POD
Glass-slipper minimalist art, "midnight" clock-strike products, pumpkin-coach silhouettes, ball-gown fashion illustration in an original style, "from the ashes" transformation slogans, a *generic* fairytale castle (clearly not the Disney logo).

### Story / writing
Perrault-faithful or darker Grimm retellings (the Grimm version has the stepsisters mutilate their feet and birds peck their eyes), a gender-swapped "Cinder-lad," the stepsisters' perspective, modern reframes, audio-dramas. Invent Z-named original animal helpers.

### Other applications
Children's-book versions (original art and original animal sidekicks), aspiration/transformation brands, AI-writing rags-to-riches prompt packs, full-range merch with original designs.
