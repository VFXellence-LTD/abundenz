# Minotaur

> Half man, half bull, born of a curse and locked in the heart of an inescapable maze — fed on sacrificed youths until a hero with a thread came for it.

## Who / What
The Minotaur is a monster with the body of a man and the head of a bull, born to Queen Pasiphaë of Crete as the result of a divine curse. King Minos had the inventor Daedalus build the Labyrinth — a maze so complex no one could escape it — to imprison the creature, which was fed a tribute of young Athenians. It was finally slain by the hero Theseus, who navigated the maze using Ariadne's thread. The Minotaur is the archetypal monster-in-the-maze, and a poignant figure of imprisonment and shame.

## Archetype
The Monster / Prisoner / Beast Within / Hybrid. The terrifying creature that is also a tragic captive — born monstrous through no fault of its own, hidden away in shame. A rich symbol for "the beast within" and for things we lock away.

## Visual Motifs (safe to draw)
A powerfully built man's body topped with a bull's head; great curving horns; a snorting muzzle; trapped within the stone corridors of the labyrinth; sometimes chained or crouched in shadow. The double-axe (*labrys*, root of "labyrinth") of Crete.

## Symbols
- **Bull head on a man's body** — the hybrid; instantly recognizable.
- **The Labyrinth** — its prison (see [[theseus]]).
- **Horns** — power and bestiality.
- **Labrys (double axe)** — Cretan emblem tied to the labyrinth.

## Themes
The beast within, monstrousness and imprisonment, shame and hiding, hybrid nature, sacrifice and tribute, the maze as confinement, the monster as victim.

## Reusable Concepts
"The beast within" — the monstrous thing we lock away (a deep psychological metaphor). The monster born innocent and hidden in shame. The creature at the center of the maze (the thing you must face). The tribute that feeds the monster. The hybrid that belongs to neither world.

## Story / Narrative Hooks
The curse on Pasiphaë and the Minotaur's birth. The building of the Labyrinth to contain it. The Athenian tribute of youths. The slaying by Theseus with Ariadne's thread. Reimaginings that give the Minotaur a tragic inner life.
