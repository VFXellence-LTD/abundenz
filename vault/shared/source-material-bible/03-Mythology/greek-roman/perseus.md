# Perseus

> The hero who slew the Gorgon by never looking at her: winged sandals on his feet, a mirror-shield in his hand, and Medusa's head as his ultimate weapon.

## Who / What
Perseus is a Greek hero, son of Zeus and the mortal Danaë, sent on a seemingly impossible quest to fetch the head of the Gorgon Medusa (whose gaze turns flesh to stone). With gifts from the gods — Hermes's winged sandals, Hades's helm of invisibility, Athena's polished shield, and a curved sword (the *harpe*) — he beheads Medusa by looking only at her reflection, then uses her severed head as a petrifying weapon. On his way home he rescues the princess Andromeda from a sea monster. He is the model of the clever, divinely-aided hero.

## Archetype
The Hero / Quester / Rescuer / Favored of the Gods. The hero who wins not by raw strength but by cleverness and the right tools — defeating a foe you cannot look at by using a mirror.

## Visual Motifs (safe to draw)
A young hero holding the severed snake-haired head of Medusa aloft (a classic statuary pose, e.g. Cellini's PD bronze); winged sandals; a curved sword; a polished bronze shield used as a mirror; the helm of invisibility; the bag (*kibisis*) holding the head; rescuing the chained Andromeda from the sea monster.

## Symbols
- **Mirror-shield** — the clever solution to an unlookable foe.
- **Winged sandals** — Hermes's gift; flight.
- **Harpe** — the curved sword that beheads Medusa.
- **Medusa's head** — his trophy and weapon.
- **Helm of invisibility** — Hades's gift.

## Themes
Heroism through cleverness, the quest and the gathering of magical tools, divine favor and aid, rescue and reward, destiny and prophecy, defeating what you cannot face directly.

## Reusable Concepts
Defeating a foe you cannot look at (the mirror solution) — a brilliant problem-solving metaphor. The hero equipped by mentors with exactly the right tools. The rescue of the captive that wins the bride. Turning a monster into a weapon. Prophecy fulfilled despite all precautions.

## Story / Narrative Hooks
His birth from Danaë and the golden rain; the cruel quest set by King Polydectes. The gathering of divine gifts and tricking the Graeae (who share one eye). The beheading of Medusa via her reflection. The rescue of Andromeda. The accidental fulfilment of the prophecy that he would kill his grandfather.
