# Ancient Egyptian Mythology

Ancient Egyptian myth offers one of the most visually distinctive and commercially powerful aesthetics in the world: golden funerary masks, animal-headed gods, hieroglyphs, the Eye of Horus, pyramids, and the iconography of death and the afterlife. The mythology and its art are thousands of years old and entirely in the public domain. Egyptian motifs are perennial bestsellers in jewelry, tattoo, fashion, and esoteric/spiritual markets.

## The traps

Egyptian myth is "dead" religion (no living faith claims it), so the cultural-respect concerns are lighter than for Hindu or Indigenous material — but three cautions apply:

1. **Modern depictions are still restricted.** Marvel's Moon Knight (Khonshu, Ammit), the *Stargate* / *The Mummy* franchises, *Gods of Egypt* (2016), *Assassin's Creed Origins*, Disney, and Smite all have their own protected designs. The ancient iconography is free; their specific looks are not.
2. **The Eye of Horus / Eye of Ra and the Ankh are heavily used in branding** — including by some specific registered logos and by esoteric/occult groups. The base symbols are free, but check trademark in your category and be aware of associations.
3. **Egyptian-style typefaces and "hieroglyph fonts"** can be copyrighted as font software even though hieroglyphs themselves are ancient — use the glyphs, not a specific licensed font file.
