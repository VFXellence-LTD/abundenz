# Odin

> The one-eyed All-Father who gave an eye for a drink from the well of wisdom and hung himself on the World Tree to win the runes: the seeker who pays any price for knowledge.

## Who / What
Odin is the chief god of the Norse pantheon — god of wisdom, war, death, poetry, and magic, and ruler of Asgard. He is relentlessly driven to gather knowledge: he sacrificed one eye at Mímir's well for a single draught of wisdom, and hung himself for nine nights, pierced by his own spear, on the World Tree Yggdrasil to learn the secret of the runes. He wanders the worlds disguised as an old traveler, accompanied by his ravens Huginn ("thought") and Muninn ("memory") and his wolves Geri and Freki. He gathers the bravest of the slain to Valhalla to fight at Ragnarök, where he is fated to be devoured by the wolf Fenrir.

## Archetype
The All-Father / Wise King / Wanderer / Seeker of Knowledge / Sacrificer. The figure who pays terrible prices (an eye, his own hanging) for wisdom — far darker and stranger than a simple "king of the gods."

## Visual Motifs (safe to draw)
A one-eyed, grey-bearded elder in a broad-brimmed hat and travel-worn cloak; two ravens perched on his shoulders; two wolves at his feet; a spear (Gungnir); riding the eight-legged horse Sleipnir; hanging from the World Tree pierced by a spear. Raw iron, wool, runestone art, and a grim, weathered look — never the polished Marvel/film version.

## Symbols
- **The single eye** — the price he paid for wisdom.
- **Two ravens (Huginn & Muninn)** — thought and memory.
- **Two wolves (Geri & Freki)** — his companions.
- **Gungnir** — his never-missing spear.
- **The broad-brimmed hat and cloak** — the wanderer's disguise.
- **The hanged figure on Yggdrasil** — his runic self-sacrifice.

## Themes
The relentless pursuit of wisdom, sacrifice for knowledge, war and death, magic and prophecy, the wandering seeker, kingship as burden, fate accepted even when doom is known.

## Reusable Concepts
"Give an eye for wisdom" — sacrifice for knowledge as a profound, ownable theme. The disguised wanderer who tests mortals. Thought and memory as companions (the two ravens). The king who knows his own doom and acts anyway. Knowledge won through suffering (the hanging for the runes).

## Story / Narrative Hooks
The sacrifice of his eye at Mímir's well. The nine-night hanging on Yggdrasil to win the runes. His ceaseless wandering in disguise. The gathering of einherjar to Valhalla. His foreseen death at Ragnarök in the jaws of Fenrir.

## Legal & Safety
- **Free to use:** the mythological Odin and the Eddas.
- **Avoid:** Marvel's Odin (Hopkins likeness), Sony's God of War Ragnarök Odin, Assassin's Creed Valhalla, the Vikings TV show, Smite.
- **Caution:** Odin-linked symbols (Valknut, certain runes) are sometimes co-opted by hate groups — keep imagery clearly rooted in the rugged myth, never extremist iconography.
