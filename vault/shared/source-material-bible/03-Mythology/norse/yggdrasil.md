# Yggdrasil (the World Tree)

> The immense cosmic ash tree at the center of all existence: its roots and branches hold together the nine worlds, from the realm of the gods to the land of the dead.

## Who / What
Yggdrasil is the World Tree of Norse cosmology — an immense, sacred ash whose trunk stands at the center of the cosmos and whose roots and branches connect and sustain the **nine worlds** (including Asgard of the gods, Midgard of humans, Jötunheim of the giants, and Hel of the dead). It is alive with creatures: an eagle perches at its crown, the dragon Níðhöggr gnaws at its roots, and the squirrel Ratatoskr scurries up and down the trunk carrying insults between them. Three roots reach to three wells, including the Well of Wisdom where Odin sacrificed his eye. Yggdrasil is the axis of existence, the source and structure of the universe, and a powerful, beautiful, family-friendly symbol of interconnection and life.

## Archetype
The Cosmic Axis / Tree of Life / Connector of Worlds / Sacred Tree. The structure that holds everything together — life, connection, and the order of the cosmos in a single living image.

## Symbols
- **The great ash tree** — the cosmic axis and tree of life.
- **The nine worlds** — the connected realms.
- **The eagle and the serpent** — the top and bottom, the cosmic poles.
- **Ratatoskr the squirrel** — the messenger running between (charming, kid-friendly).
- **The three roots and three wells** — the structure of fate and wisdom.
