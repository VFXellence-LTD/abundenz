# Freyr (Frey)

> The golden god of sun, harvest, and prosperity, who owned a ship that folded into a pouch and a boar of pure gold — and who gave up his magic sword for love, leaving him doomed at Ragnarök.

## Who / What
Freyr is one of the most important Norse gods — the god of fertility, prosperity, peace, sunshine, fair weather, and good harvests, and a king of the Vanir. He is the twin brother of Freya. He owns three great treasures: Gullinbursti, a boar of glowing gold that can run through air and water; Skíðblaðnir, a magnificent ship that always has a fair wind and can be folded up to fit in a pouch; and a magical sword that fights on its own. He famously gives away his sword to win the love of the giantess Gerðr — a romantic sacrifice that leaves him weaponless and fated to fall at Ragnarök. He is associated with abundance, joy, and the good life.

## Archetype
The Fertility God / Bringer of Peace / Prosperous King / The Lover Who Gives Up His Sword. The warm, generous god of the good harvest and good times — and a poignant figure of sacrifice for love.

## Visual Motifs (safe to draw)
A handsome, golden-haired god radiating warmth; the glowing golden boar Gullinbursti; the folding ship Skíðblaðnir; fields of ripe grain; sunlight and fair weather; antlers (his weapon after he gives up the sword); abundance and plenty.
