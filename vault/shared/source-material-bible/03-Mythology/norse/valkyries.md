# Valkyries

> The shield-maidens of Odin who ride over the battlefield to choose which warriors die and which live — bearing the bravest of the slain to the halls of Valhalla.

## Who / What
The Valkyries ("choosers of the slain") are Odin's warrior-maidens, who fly or ride over battlefields and decide which warriors will fall and which will be spared, then carry the souls of the worthiest dead to Valhalla, Odin's hall, where they will feast and fight until they are needed at Ragnarök. In Valhalla, the Valkyries serve the fallen heroes mead from great horns. They are fierce, glorious, and solemn — embodiments of valor, fate, and the warrior's honored death. Some, like Brynhildr (Brünnhilde), have famous individual stories of love and tragedy.

## Archetype
The Warrior-Maiden / Chooser of the Slain / Psychopomp / Fierce Feminine. The armored woman who decides fate on the battlefield and guides the honored dead — power, valor, and the sublime feminine.

## Visual Motifs (safe to draw)
Armored warrior-women riding winged horses (or wolves) over a battlefield; bearing spears, shields, and helmets; gathering up the fallen and bearing them aloft; offering a mead-horn to a seated hero in a great hall; ravens accompanying them. Note: avoid the specific Wagner-opera staging and the Marvel design — use a rugged Eddic shield-maiden look.

## Symbols
- **The shield and spear** — the warrior-maiden.
- **The winged horse / the ride** — sweeping over the battle.
- **The mead-horn** — their service to the honored dead in Valhalla.
- **Ravens** — Odin's birds; death and the battlefield.
- **Valhalla** — the destination of the chosen.

## Themes
The choosing of the slain, war and death, fate and glory, the warrior-maiden, the honored death and the afterlife of valor, the fierce sublime feminine.

## Reusable Concepts
The fierce armored warrior-woman (a perennially popular, empowering figure). "Choosing the worthy" — a selection/curation concept (who gets chosen, who is worthy). The guide of the honored dead. Valor and the good death. The mead-horn welcome (hospitality and reward). The flying shield-maiden.

## Story / Narrative Hooks
Their riding over battlefields and choosing of the slain. Their service in Valhalla. The tragic tale of Brynhildr (a Valkyrie punished with mortal sleep, central to the Völsung saga and Wagner's Ring). Their role gathering the army for Ragnarök.

## Legal & Safety
- **Free to use:** mythological valkyries and the Eddas.
- **Avoid:** Marvel's Valkyrie (Tessa Thompson likeness), Wagner-opera staging designs, Sony's God of War Ragnarök valkyries, Smite. "Valkyrie" is a common brand name — check trademark.
