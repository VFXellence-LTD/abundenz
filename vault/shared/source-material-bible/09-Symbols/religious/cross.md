# Christian Cross

> The central living symbol of Christianity — faith, sacrifice, and redemption. Treat with respect.

## Meaning
The cross is the principal symbol of Christianity, representing the crucifixion of Jesus Christ and, through it, sacrifice, redemption, salvation, hope, and resurrection. For roughly a third of the world's population it is a sacred, deeply meaningful emblem of living faith. The crucifix (a cross bearing the body of Christ) is more specifically Catholic/Orthodox; the plain cross is broadly Christian.

## Origin
The cross as a geometric form predates Christianity. Its Christian meaning dates to the 1st century CE and the Roman crucifixion of Jesus; it became the dominant Christian symbol by late antiquity. The form is PD, but it is a **living sacred symbol** in active worship worldwide.

## Visual description
A vertical beam crossed by a shorter horizontal beam set above the midpoint (Latin cross). Many variants exist: Greek cross (equal arms), crucifix (with corpus), Celtic cross (with a ring — note some ring-cross forms are hate-appropriated; see [[../_restricted/hate-symbols-overview]]), Orthodox cross (three bars), Tau cross, and more.

## Emotional tags
Faith, hope, reverence, solace, sacrifice, devotion.

## Design & story applications
- **Respectful faith-based merch**: a legitimate and large market — crosses sold to believers as devotional/faith products (apparel, posters, gifts) paired with scripture, done with sincerity.
- **Editorial/story/video/blog**: depicting Christianity, faith, history, or character belief respectfully.
- **Avoid entirely**: mockery, gag products, desecration, or ironic/edgy uses; these alienate believers and risk severe reputational harm.

## Remix hooks
Respectful faith-based product lines (scripture pairings, stained-glass styling, devotional typography). Historical cross-variants educational set. There is no acceptable "irreverent" remix.
