# Libra (The Scales)

> The cardinal air sign — balance, justice, and the art of harmony.

## Meaning
Libra (September 23 – October 22) is a cardinal air sign ruled by Venus, symbolized by the Scales — the only inanimate zodiac symbol. It signifies balance, justice, harmony, partnership, beauty, and diplomacy. Keywords: fair-minded, charming, relational, aesthetic, sometimes indecisive.

## Origin
Babylonian roots; the Romans formalized the Scales (associated with Astraea/Justitia, goddess of justice). The glyph (a horizontal line with a half-circle resting on it — the setting sun over the horizon, or the beam of a balance) is ancient and PD. Modern illustration sets are their own.

## Visual description
The glyph is a horizontal bar with a flattened half-circle (like the Greek omega or a setting sun) above it. Illustrative versions: a balance/scales, or the scales of justice. Symmetry styling reinforces balance. Crossover with [[../heraldic/scales]].
