# Sagittarius (The Archer)

> The mutable fire sign — the centaur archer aiming at far horizons.

## Meaning
Sagittarius (November 22 – December 21) is a mutable fire sign ruled by Jupiter, symbolized by the Archer (a centaur drawing a bow). It signifies adventure, freedom, philosophy, optimism, expansion, and the quest for meaning. Keywords: adventurous, philosophical, candid, optimistic, restless.

## Origin
Babylonian/Greek zodiac; the centaur archer is often identified with Chiron or Crotus. The glyph (an upward-pointing arrow crossed by a short bar) is ancient and PD. Modern illustration sets are their own.

## Visual description
The glyph is an arrow pointing up-and-right, crossed near its base by a short perpendicular line. Illustrative versions: a centaur drawing a bow, a bow-and-arrow, or the constellation (the "Teapot" asterism). The simple arrow makes a clean minimal motif.
