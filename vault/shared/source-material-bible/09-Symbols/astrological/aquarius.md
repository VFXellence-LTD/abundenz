# Aquarius (The Water-Bearer)

> The fixed air sign — visionary, humanitarian, and gloriously contrarian.

## Meaning
Aquarius (January 20 – February 18) is a fixed **air** sign (despite the water imagery) ruled by Saturn (and modern Uranus), symbolized by the Water-Bearer pouring out the waters of knowledge upon humanity. It signifies innovation, humanitarianism, independence, vision, rebellion, and the collective good. Keywords: original, idealistic, independent, intellectual, unconventional.

## Origin
Babylonian/Greek zodiac; the water-bearer is linked to Ganymede, cupbearer to the gods. The glyph (two parallel horizontal zigzag/wave lines) is ancient and PD. Modern illustration sets are their own.

## Visual description
The glyph is two parallel wavy/zigzag horizontal lines — representing flowing water or waves of energy. Illustrative versions: a figure pouring water from an urn, or the constellation. Futurist and retro "Age of Aquarius" stylings suit its visionary character.
