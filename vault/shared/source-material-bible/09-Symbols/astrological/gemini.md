# Gemini (The Twins)

> The mutable air sign — dual-minded, curious, endlessly communicative.

## Meaning
Gemini (May 21 – June 20) is a mutable air sign ruled by Mercury, symbolized by the Twins (Castor and Pollux). It signifies duality, communication, curiosity, adaptability, wit, and the restless intellect. Keywords: versatile, talkative, clever, changeable, sociable.

## Origin
Babylonian/Greek zodiac; the Twins are Castor and Pollux of Greek myth. The glyph (the Roman numeral II / two pillars joined top and bottom) is ancient and PD. Modern illustration sets are their own.

## Visual description
The glyph resembles the Roman numeral II — two vertical bars joined by horizontal bars at top and bottom. Illustrative versions: two mirrored figures, twin faces, two pillars, or the constellation. Mirror-symmetry styling reinforces the duality.
