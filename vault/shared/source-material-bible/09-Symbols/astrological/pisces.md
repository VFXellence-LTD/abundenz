# Pisces (The Fish)

> The mutable water sign — dreamer, empath, dissolver of boundaries.

## Meaning
Pisces (February 19 – March 20) is a mutable water sign ruled by Jupiter (and modern Neptune), symbolized by two Fish swimming in opposite directions, bound together. It signifies imagination, empathy, dreams, spirituality, compassion, and the dissolution of boundaries (the last sign — endings and the return to source). Keywords: dreamy, compassionate, intuitive, artistic, escapist.

## Origin
Babylonian/Greek zodiac; the two fish are linked to Aphrodite and Eros escaping the monster Typhon. The glyph (two crescent arcs facing away, joined by a horizontal bar) is ancient and PD. Modern illustration sets are their own.

## Visual description
The glyph is two crescent arcs (like ")(") joined across the middle by a horizontal line — two fish tethered, swimming apart. Illustrative versions: two fish swimming in a circle (yin-yang-like), or the constellation. Keep distinct from the Christian Ichthys ([[../religious/ichthys]]) unless intended. Dreamy watercolor styling suits it.
