# Leo (The Lion)

> The fixed fire sign — proud, radiant, born to lead and perform.

## Meaning
Leo (July 23 – August 22) is a fixed fire sign ruled by the Sun, symbolized by the Lion. It signifies pride, leadership, creativity, generosity, warmth, and drama. Keywords: confident, charismatic, theatrical, loyal, attention-loving.

## Origin
Babylonian/Greek zodiac; linked to the Nemean Lion of the Heracles myth. The glyph (a curved loop with a flourishing tail, evoking a lion's mane and tail) is ancient and PD. Modern illustration sets are their own.

## Visual description
The glyph is a rounded loop with a curling tail-like flourish, suggesting a lion's mane and tail. Illustrative versions: a lion's head with full mane, a regal standing lion, or the constellation. Pairs with the [[../celestial/sun]] (ruling body).

Confidence, warmth, pride, theatricality, generosity, regality, vitality.
