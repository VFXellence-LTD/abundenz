# Virgo (The Maiden)

> The mutable earth sign — precise, analytical, devoted to service.

## Meaning
Virgo (August 23 – September 22) is a mutable earth sign ruled by Mercury, symbolized by the Maiden (often holding a sheaf of wheat — the harvest). It signifies precision, service, analysis, health, purity, and practical care. Keywords: meticulous, helpful, analytical, modest, perfectionist.

## Origin
Babylonian/Greek zodiac; the maiden is linked to harvest goddesses (Demeter/Persephone, Astraea). The glyph (a stylized "M" with an inward-crossing loop) is ancient and PD. Modern illustration sets are their own.

## Visual description
The glyph resembles an "M" whose final stroke loops inward and crosses itself. Illustrative versions: a maiden holding [[../botanical/wheat]], or the constellation (with the bright star Spica). Often paired with wheat sheaves.
