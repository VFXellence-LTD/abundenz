# Red Cross / Red Crescent / Red Crystal — DO NOT USE

> A protective emblem under the Geneva Conventions. Misuse is a crime — not merely a trademark issue.

## Why it is NOT free

- **Treaty / international humanitarian law** — the red cross on white (and the Red Crescent and Red Crystal) is a protective emblem under the **Geneva Conventions**, designating medical and humanitarian personnel and facilities in armed conflict. Misuse can endanger protected persons and erode the emblem's protective power.
- **US criminal statute** — **18 U.S.C. § 706** makes unauthorized use of the Greek red cross on a white ground (or any imitation, or the words "Red Cross"/"Geneva Cross") a **criminal misdemeanor** — fine and/or imprisonment up to six months. **18 U.S.C. § 706a** (Geneva Distinctive Emblems Protection Act of 2006) extends this to the **Red Crescent** and **Red Crystal**.

## Doctrine

**Never use** a red cross on white — not for first-aid kits, medical/health branding, pharmacy signage, "medic" costumes, game UI, or decoration. This is one of the very few symbols whose misuse is an actual crime, independent of trademark.

## Safe alternatives for "medical" iconography

- A **green** cross (pharmacy) or other non-red color cross.
- A **heart**, **plus sign in a non-red color**, or the **Rod of Asclepius** (single snake on a staff — see [[../nautical/caduceus]]; note: NOT the caduceus, which is commerce).

## Bottom line

**Recognize and reject. Geneva-protected and criminal to misuse in the US. Use a non-red medical icon instead.**
