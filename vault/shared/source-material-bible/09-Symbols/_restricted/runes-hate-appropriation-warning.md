# Runes — Hate Appropriation Screening

> The Elder Futhark is PD and beautiful — and the single most appropriated symbol system by hate groups. Screen everything against the ADL.

## Why this matters

White-nationalist and neo-Nazi movements have systematically co-opted Norse/Germanic runic imagery to reconstruct a mythic "Aryan" past. The runes themselves are ancient and PD, and most appear in entirely benign pagan, archaeological, scholarly, and decorative contexts. **Context is everything** (per the ADL) — but several specific runes carry heavy extremist associations and must be avoided or handled with great care.

## High-risk runes (verified against ADL)

- **Othala (ᛟ)** — Nazi-era usage included Waffen-SS division insignia; adopted post-war by white supremacists. **Avoid standalone**, especially the "serifed"/"footed" variant used by extremists.
- **Sowilo doubled** — the SS bolts. **Banned hate symbol** — see [[ss-runes-warning]].
- **Algiz / "Lebensrune" (life rune)** — appropriated by Nazis and völkisch groups; inverted = "death rune." **Avoid standalone and inverted** — see [[../runes/algiz]].
- **Tiwaz / Tyr (ᛏ)** — used by extremist and militaristic groups; avoid standalone, especially in militaristic styling.
- **Wolfsangel** and **Odal-with-serifs** — also flagged by the ADL.

## Doctrine

- Use runes only as a **clearly cultural-historical alphabet** (Viking heritage, Norse mythology, fantasy worldbuilding, divination decks) — never as coded extremism.
- **Screen every runic project against the [ADL Hate Symbols Database](https://www.adl.org/resources/hate-symbols/search).**
- Avoid militaristic, Germanic-nationalist, or völkisch aesthetics.
- For the flagged runes above, default to avoid or seek an unambiguous non-runic alternative.

## Bottom line

**Safe as a screened cultural-historical alphabet; specific runes (doubled Sowilo, Othala, Tiwaz, Algiz) require avoidance or great care. Default to avoid on ambiguity.**
