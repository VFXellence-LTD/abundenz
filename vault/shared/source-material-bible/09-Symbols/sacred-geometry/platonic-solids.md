# Platonic Solids

> The five perfect polyhedra — geometry's bridge to the elements and the cosmos.

## Meaning
The five Platonic solids — tetrahedron, cube (hexahedron), octahedron, dodecahedron, icosahedron — are the only regular convex polyhedra (identical faces, edges, and angles). Plato mapped four to the classical elements (tetrahedron = fire, cube = earth, octahedron = air, icosahedron = water) and the dodecahedron to the cosmos/aether. They symbolize cosmic order, perfection, structure, and the geometric foundation of reality.

## Origin
Known to the ancient Greeks (the Pythagoreans and Plato's *Timaeus*; Euclid proved there are exactly five). Mathematical objects, entirely ancient and PD with no restrictions whatsoever.

## Visual description
- **Tetrahedron**: 4 triangular faces (fire).
- **Cube**: 6 square faces (earth).
- **Octahedron**: 8 triangular faces (air).
- **Dodecahedron**: 12 pentagonal faces (cosmos/aether).
- **Icosahedron**: 20 triangular faces (water).
