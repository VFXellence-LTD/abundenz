# Metatron's Cube

> Thirteen circles laced with lines — the geometric container of all Platonic solids.

## Meaning
Metatron's Cube is a figure of thirteen equal circles connected by straight lines, traditionally said to contain all five [[platonic-solids]] within its projection. Named after the archangel Metatron, it symbolizes cosmic order, the divine architecture of reality, balance, and (in esoteric tradition) protection. It is one of the most recognizable "sacred geometry" emblems.

## Origin
The figure derives from the "Fruit of Life" (a 13-circle subset of the [[flower-of-life]]). Its association with the archangel Metatron comes from Kabbalistic and medieval mystical traditions. The geometry is PD; the religious naming is centuries old. Specific modern artworks are their own.

## Visual description
Thirteen circles — one center, an inner ring of six, an outer ring of six — with straight lines connecting every circle's center to every other, forming a dense star-like lattice. Within the lines, the projections of the cube, tetrahedron, octahedron, dodecahedron, and icosahedron can be traced. Drawn as precise gold-on-black line work or geometric tattoo.
