# Wolf (Totem)

> The wild heart of loyalty, instinct, and the untamed pack.

## Meaning
The wolf symbolizes loyalty, the bonds of the pack/family, instinct, freedom, wildness, and leadership (the alpha). The "lone wolf" connotes independence and the outsider, while the pack signifies community and cooperation. In myth the wolf is both guardian and predator — the nurturer (Rome's she-wolf) and the devourer (Norse Fenrir, the Big Bad Wolf) — making it a powerful symbol of the wild and the shadow.

## Origin
A universal symbol across Norse, Roman, Native American, and countless other traditions. Fully PD. **Caution**: avoid appropriating specific Indigenous/First Nations totem-pole or clan wolf designs (these carry cultural ownership). Design original wolf art.

## Visual description
Common forms: a howling wolf in silhouette against a moon, a running pack, a fierce frontal face with piercing eyes, or a geometric/low-poly head. Render in blackwork tattoo, geometric low-poly, watercolor, or realistic illustration.
