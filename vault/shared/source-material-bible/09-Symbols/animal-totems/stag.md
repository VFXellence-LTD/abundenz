# Stag / Deer (Totem)

> The antlered noble of the forest — grace, regeneration, and spiritual guidance.

## Meaning
The stag symbolizes nobility, grace, gentleness, and the spirit of the forest. Its antlers — shed and regrown each year — make it an emblem of regeneration, renewal, and cyclical growth, as well as virility and pride. Across Celtic, Norse, and Christian traditions the stag is a spiritual guide, a messenger between worlds, and a noble quarry of the sacred hunt (the white hart).

## Origin
A universal symbol across Celtic (Cernunnos), Norse, Greek (Artemis), and Christian traditions. Fully PD. Avoid copying a specific live stag logo (whisky/outdoor brands). Design your own.

## Visual description
A majestic stag with large branching antlers, shown in profile, leaping, or frontal with a calm gaze. The antlers alone make a strong motif. Render in geometric/low-poly, woodland-rustic, blackwork, or watercolor.
