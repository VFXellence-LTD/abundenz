# Phoenix (Totem)

> The firebird that rises reborn from its own ashes — resurrection and renewal.

## Meaning
The phoenix is the mythical bird that, at the end of its life, burns to ash and is reborn from the flames — the supreme symbol of rebirth, resurrection, renewal, immortality, and transformation. It embodies resilience and triumph over destruction ("rise from the ashes"), and is associated with the sun and cyclical renewal. The Egyptian Bennu and Chinese Fenghuang are related solar/rebirth birds.

## Origin
Ancient, appearing in Greek, Egyptian (Bennu), and Chinese (Fenghuang) traditions, later adopted into Christian resurrection symbolism. Fully PD. **Caution**: avoid copying a specific franchise's phoenix (film/game) or a live sports/corporate phoenix logo — design your own firebird.

## Visual description
A majestic eagle/peacock-like bird with spread wings, a crested head, and long flowing tail feathers, rising from or wreathed in flames, with fiery red-orange-gold plumage. Render in vibrant fiery gradients, blackwork, geometric, or Art Nouveau styling. The Fenghuang variant is more peacock-like; the Bennu is heron-like.
