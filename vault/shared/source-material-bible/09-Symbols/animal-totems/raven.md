# Raven (Totem)

> The black bird of mystery, prophecy, and transformation.

## Meaning
The raven (and crow) symbolizes mystery, prophecy, intelligence, magic, and the threshold between life and death (death-and-rebirth, the psychopomp). In Norse myth Odin's ravens Huginn (thought) and Muninn (memory) bring him the world's knowledge; in many traditions the raven is a clever trickster and a creator. Its black plumage and carrion habits lend it gothic, ominous, and otherworldly associations — yet also wisdom and prophecy.

## Origin
A universal symbol across Norse, Celtic, Greek, biblical, and many other traditions. Fully PD. **Caution**: avoid appropriating **Pacific Northwest Indigenous Raven formline art** (a culturally owned artistic style). Poe's poem "The Raven" is PD as text, but specific modern illustrations are not. Design original raven art.

## Visual description
A black bird in profile, in flight, or perched with a sharp, knowing eye and heavy beak. Render in blackwork/woodcut, gothic line, geometric, or watercolor. Often paired with skulls, moons, bare branches, or runes.
