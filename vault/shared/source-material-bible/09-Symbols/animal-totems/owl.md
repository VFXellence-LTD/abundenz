# Owl (Totem)

> The wide-eyed watcher of wisdom, knowledge, and the night.

## Meaning
The owl symbolizes wisdom, knowledge, intuition, and insight — most famously as the companion of Athena, Greek goddess of wisdom. Its silent night flight and forward-facing eyes lend it associations with the unseen, mystery, watchfulness, and the ability to "see in the dark" (perceive hidden truths). In some cultures, however, the owl is an omen of death or ill fortune — context shifts the reading.

## Origin
A universal symbol, central to Greek (Athena's owl) and many other traditions. Fully PD. **Note**: education and finance brands frequently use owl logos — design your own; avoid imitating a specific live owl mark. Context-match the "wisdom" vs. "ill omen" meaning.

## Visual description
A front-facing owl with large round eyes, a hooked beak, and feathered "ear" tufts, often perched. Render as a cute geometric nursery owl, a wise scholarly owl with books/glasses, a realistic night owl, or a blackwork/Halloween version.
