# Bear (Totem)

> The grounded force of strength, protection, and quiet power.

## Meaning
The bear symbolizes strength, protection, courage, and grounded power, as well as introspection and renewal (through hibernation — withdrawal and rebirth). The she-bear is a fierce emblem of motherhood and protection ("mama bear"). Across cultures the bear is a guardian, a warrior-totem, and a symbol of the untamed wild and the healing earth.

## Origin
A universal symbol with ancient bear-worship traditions across the Northern Hemisphere (Celtic, Norse/berserkers, Native American, Siberian). Fully PD. **Caution**: avoid appropriating specific Indigenous clan/totem bear designs; avoid copying a live brand bear or a beloved fictional bear (trademarked/copyrighted). Design your own.

## Visual description
A standing/rearing bear, a roaring profile, a bear head, or a geometric mountain-bear silhouette. The sleeping/cub bear suits nurseries. Render in geometric, blackwork, woodcut-cabin, or watercolor styles.

## Emotional tags
Strength, protection, groundedness, ferocity, calm power, the maternal.
