# Koi Fish (Totem)

> The carp that swims upstream to become a dragon — perseverance and transformation.

## Meaning
The koi symbolizes perseverance, ambition, courage, good fortune, and transformation. The famous legend tells of a koi that swam up a waterfall (the Dragon Gate) and was rewarded by becoming a [[dragon]] — making it the emblem of triumphing over adversity through determination. A pair of koi swimming in a circle echoes the [[../religious/yin-yang]] (balance, harmony, love).

## Origin
Rooted in Chinese legend and Japanese culture (koi are prized ornamental carp). Fully PD as a cultural symbol. **Note**: be respectful of East Asian heritage; avoid copying a specific copyrighted irezumi (traditional Japanese tattoo) artwork or a live brand. Design original. Low-friction with respectful framing.

## Visual description
A graceful, colorful carp (white-orange-black patterns) with flowing fins, swimming amid stylized waves, lotus, and cherry blossoms. Often shown swimming upstream or as a circular yin-yang-like pair. Render in watercolor, original-irezumi style, ink-wash, or clean line.
