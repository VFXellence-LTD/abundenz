# Ship's Wheel (Helm)

> The captain's wheel — control, direction, and steering your own course.

## Meaning
The ship's wheel (helm) symbolizes control, direction, leadership, and steering one's own course through life. As the captain's tool for guiding the vessel, it connotes mastery, responsibility, and being at the helm of one's destiny. A popular nautical and tattoo motif signifying "take the wheel" and confident navigation.

## Origin
The steering wheel appeared on ships around the early 1700s, replacing the whipstaff. The spoked helm became an enduring maritime icon. Fully PD. Avoid imitating a specific boating/insurance wheel logo.

## Visual description
A circular wheel with multiple spokes radiating from a central hub, the spokes extending past the rim as turned handles (typically 6, 8, or 10). Often paired with rope. Render in vintage-helm detail, nautical-tattoo style, or clean line.
