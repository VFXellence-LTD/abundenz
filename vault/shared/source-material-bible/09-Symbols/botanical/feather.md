# Feather

> The light plume of freedom, the soul, and truth.

## Meaning
The feather symbolizes freedom, lightness, the soul/spirit, ascension, and communication (the quill = the written word). In Egyptian belief the feather of Ma'at was weighed against the heart to judge truth and righteousness. A falling feather suggests a message from above or a departed spirit. As a quill it represents writing, knowledge, and inspiration.

## Origin
A universal natural symbol across many cultures. Fully PD. **Important caution**: avoid appropriating **Native American sacred feather imagery** — eagle feathers and war bonnets carry profound spiritual and earned significance in many Indigenous cultures, their commercial use is cultural appropriation, and US law restricts eagle-feather possession. Use generic or abstract feathers; do not use culturally specific Indigenous feather symbolism.

## Visual description
A single feather with a central shaft (rachis) and soft barbs spreading symmetrically, often slightly curved. Popular boho versions split the lower barbs into a flock of tiny birds. The quill version is cut to a nib. Render as watercolor, fine line, blackwork, or boho dispersal-into-birds.
