# Rose

> The eternal flower of love, beauty, and bittersweet passion.

## Meaning
The rose is the most symbolically loaded flower in Western culture: love and romance (especially the red rose), beauty, passion, the divine feminine, and — through its thorns — the bittersweet union of pleasure and pain. It also signifies secrecy ("sub rosa," under the rose), mystic perfection (rosa mystica, the Virgin Mary), and the transience of beauty. Colors code distinct meanings: red (love), white (purity), pink (gratitude), yellow (friendship), black (death/farewell).

## Origin
Cultivated and revered since antiquity across Persia, Greece, Rome, and beyond; central to Christian, courtly-love, and Romantic symbolism. Fully PD. **Note**: the Tudor rose and certain national/political roses are specific emblems — design a generic rose; avoid copying a specific artwork or a live rose logo.

## Visual description
A layered, spiraling bloom of overlapping petals, typically with a thorned stem and serrated leaves. Bud, half-open, and full-bloom variants. Render in traditional-tattoo (bold outline, solid red), dark blackwork, watercolor, Victorian-engraving, or minimalist line.
