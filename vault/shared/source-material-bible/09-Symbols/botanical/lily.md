# Lily

> The trumpet bloom of purity, renewal, and graceful devotion.

## Meaning
The lily symbolizes purity, innocence, renewal, and the divine feminine. The white Madonna lily is strongly associated with the Virgin Mary (purity) and with Easter (resurrection, new life). Lilies also carry funerary/sympathy associations (peace and the restored innocence of the soul), so context shifts the meaning between joyful purity and solemn mourning.

## Origin
Revered since antiquity (Minoan, Greek, Roman, then Christian). The stylized lily is the ancestor of the [[../heraldic/fleur-de-lis]]. Fully PD. Context-match the mourning vs. resurrection readings. Avoid copying a specific artwork.

## Visual description
A large trumpet-shaped bloom with six recurved petals and prominent protruding stamens, on a tall leafy stem. The calla lily is a single curved spathe around a yellow spadix. Render in Art Nouveau (whiplash stems), watercolor, line, or engraving.
