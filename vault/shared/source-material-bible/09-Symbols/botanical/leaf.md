# Leaf

> The simple emblem of growth, nature, and living renewal.

## Meaning
The leaf is a broad, simple symbol of growth, nature, life, renewal, and vitality. As foliage it connotes freshness, the seasons (green = spring/growth, orange/red = autumn, falling = transience), and ecological harmony. The single leaf is one of the most flexible natural marks in modern eco and wellness design.

## Origin
A universal natural symbol, fully PD. **Note**: specific national leaf emblems (the Canadian maple leaf) and countless eco-brand leaf logos are protected — design a generic leaf; avoid imitating a national flag or a specific brand mark.

## Visual description
A simple blade with a central midrib and branching veins, in countless shapes (oval, lobed, heart, tropical monstera, fern frond). Render as single-line minimalism, botanical line art, watercolor, or flat eco-green styling.
