# Oak Leaf & Acorn

> The mighty oak and its humble seed — strength, endurance, and great potential.

## Meaning
The oak symbolizes strength, endurance, wisdom, longevity, and steadfastness — "mighty oaks from little acorns grow." The acorn specifically signifies potential, new beginnings, and the great things that arise from small starts. Together they convey grounded power, heritage, and patient growth. The oak was sacred to many traditions (Druidic, Norse/Thor, Greek/Zeus).

## Origin
A universal natural symbol across European and other cultures, sacred in Druidic, Norse, and Greco-Roman traditions. Fully PD. **Note**: the oak/acorn is a common heritage and conservation emblem — avoid copying a specific organization's oak logo; the generic leaf and acorn are free.

## Visual description
The oak leaf has a distinctive lobed/wavy edge; the acorn is an oval nut seated in a textured cap. Render as vintage-botanical engraving, oak-leaf wreath, autumn-foliage, or clean line. Often used as a heritage-crest filler.
