# Ansuz (Elder Futhark Rune)

> The rune of the divine word — wisdom, breath, and inspired speech.

## Meaning
Ansuz (ᚨ, "a") means "a god" (an Æsir) and is strongly associated with Odin, the god of wisdom, poetry, and the runes themselves. It signifies divine breath, inspired communication, wisdom, eloquence, signals/messages, and the power of the word. In rune-lore it counsels listening for divine inspiration and speaking with insight.

## Origin
A core rune of the Elder Futhark (c. 150–800 CE), ancient and PD. As with all Norse runes, screen the broader project against extremist appropriation (see [[../_restricted/runes-hate-appropriation-warning]]); Ansuz individually is low-risk.

A vertical stave with two short arms angling downward to the right (like Fehu's mirror, arms pointing down). Render as carved stone, woodburn, runic typography, or part of a full Futhark row.
