# Gold / Sol (Alchemical Metal)

> The perfect metal — the sun made solid, goal of the Great Work.

## Meaning
Gold (Sol) is the most perfect, incorruptible metal in alchemy — the goal of the Magnum Opus (transmuting base metal into gold, literal and spiritual). It is associated with the [[../celestial/sun]], kingship, enlightenment, perfection, and the soul's purification. It corresponds to the planetary metal of the Sun. Symbolically: attainment, the perfected self, incorruptibility.

## Origin
The seven classical metals were each mapped to a planet (gold/Sun, silver/Moon, etc.) in Greco-Roman and alchemical tradition. The glyph is ancient/PD. Specific artworks are their own.

## Visual description
A circle with a single dot at its center — identical to the common glyph for the Sun and the elemental [[../elemental/spirit-aether]] dotted circle. Often rendered in gold foil. Clean and minimal; pairs with the silver crescent for a Sol-Luna duo.
