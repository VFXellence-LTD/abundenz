# Silver / Luna (Alchemical Metal)

> The moon-metal — intuition, reflection, and the feminine soul.

## Meaning
Silver (Luna) is the metal of the [[../celestial/crescent-moon]] — intuition, the feminine and receptive principle, reflection, purity, and the soul. It is the lunar counterpart to gold's solar perfection; together Sol and Luna form alchemy's primary duality (the "chymical wedding" of king and queen). It corresponds to the planetary metal of the Moon.

## Origin
Part of the seven-metals/seven-planets correspondence in Greco-Roman and alchemical tradition. The glyph is ancient/PD. Specific artworks are their own.

## Visual description
A crescent (waxing or waning), identical to the lunar glyph. Often rendered in silver foil. Pairs with the gold dotted circle for a Sol-Luna duo. Keep distinct from the star-and-crescent religious symbol by avoiding the added star.
