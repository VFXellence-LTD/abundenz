# Salt (Alchemical)

> The fixed body — matter crystallized, third of the three primes.

## Meaning
Salt completes the **tria prima** (with [[alchemy-sulfur]] and [[alchemy-mercury]]), representing the body, matter, the fixed and stable principle, crystallization, and preservation. Where sulfur is soul and mercury is spirit, salt is the physical vessel — the grounding, the result that remains. It connotes permanence, purity (purification by salt), and the material foundation.

## Origin
Paracelsus formalized the three primes in the 16th century, building on earlier alchemical tradition. The glyph is ancient/PD. Specific modern artworks are their own.

## Visual description
A circle bisected by a single horizontal line through its center. Clean and minimal. Distinguish from other bisected-circle glyphs by context (paired with sulfur and mercury). Works as a minimal tattoo-style mark or in vintage apothecary layouts.

## Emotional tags
Solidity, permanence, earthiness, stability, purity, the elemental body.
