# Chevron (Heraldic Ordinary)

> The bold inverted-V — protection, achievement, and structural strength.

## Meaning
The chevron is a heraldic "ordinary" (a basic charge) shaped like an inverted V, said to represent the rafters of a building — and thus protection, faithful service, building/achievement, and reaching a goal. In military insignia, stacked chevrons denote rank. In modern graphic design the chevron reads as momentum, direction, and bold geometric structure.

## Origin
A medieval heraldic ordinary, fully PD. The geometric shape is unprotectable in itself and is ubiquitous in contemporary design. **Note**: a specific famous oil-company logo and particular military rank configurations carry brand/official associations — but the generic chevron is free; just avoid copying a specific brand's exact lockup.

## Visual description
An inverted V (point up) spanning a shield, or a band bent at an angle. Stacked chevrons form a zigzag or rank insignia. Render as a single bold ordinary, a repeating zigzag pattern, or modern geometric bands.
