# Scales of Justice

> The balanced beam of fairness, judgment, and the rule of law.

## Meaning
The scales (balance) symbolize justice, fairness, impartial judgment, law, and equilibrium — weighing evidence and arguments to reach a just verdict. Carried by Lady Justice (often blindfolded, with a sword), they represent the objective, balanced weighing of right and wrong. They are also the emblem of the zodiac sign [[../astrological/libra]].

## Origin
Ancient: the Egyptian goddess Ma'at weighed the heart against a feather; the Greek Themis and Roman Justitia (Lady Justice) carry the scales. Fully PD and entirely generic. **Caution**: a specific law-firm, court, or government justice logo may be trademarked — design generic scales rather than copying a particular emblem.

## Visual description
A central vertical post or a hand holding a horizontal beam, from each end of which hangs a pan/dish, the two suspended in balance. Often paired with a sword (Lady Justice) or a gavel. Render in vintage-engraving, gold-line emblem, or clean modern line.
