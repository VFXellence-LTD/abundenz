# Earth (Classical Element)

> The barred downward triangle of stability, fertility, and grounding.

## Meaning
Earth is the element of stability, material reality, fertility, grounding, endurance, and nourishment. Cold and dry, feminine and receptive, it connotes the body, wealth, the practical, patience, and the foundations beneath everything. Astrologically it rules the earth signs (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn).

## Origin
Greek four-element cosmology, standardized in alchemy as a downward triangle crossed by a horizontal bar (the inverse-with-bar of [[air]]). Earth/mountain imagery is universal. PD.

## Visual description
A downward-pointing equilateral triangle with a horizontal line through the lower portion (▽ with a bar). Illustrative versions use a mountain silhouette, layered soil, roots, or stones. Minimal/tattoo styles favor the barred inverted triangle.
