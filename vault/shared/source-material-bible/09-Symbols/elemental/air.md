# Air (Classical Element)

> The barred triangle of intellect, breath, and freedom.

## Meaning
Air is the element of intellect, communication, breath, freedom, movement, and the mind. Hot and wet, masculine and active, it connotes ideas, speech, travel, and detachment from the material. It is the realm of thought and exchange. Astrologically it rules the air signs (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius).

## Origin
Greek four-element cosmology, standardized in alchemy as an upward triangle crossed by a horizontal bar (distinguishing it from [[fire]]). Wind and breath imagery is universal. PD.

## Visual description
An upward-pointing equilateral triangle with a horizontal line through the upper portion (△ with a bar). Illustrative versions use wind swirls, curling gusts, a feather, or blowing dandelion seeds. Minimal/tattoo styles favor the barred triangle glyph.

## Emotional tags
Lightness, clarity, freedom, restlessness, intellect, detachment, curiosity, mobility.
