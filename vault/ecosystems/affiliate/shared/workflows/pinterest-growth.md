# Pinterest Growth Strategy

Pinterest-specific playbook for building Conduit's traffic engine. Covers account setup, board strategy, pin velocity, content mix, SEO, seasonal patterns, and paid promotion.

---

## Account Setup (Do Once at Activation)

### Business Account

1. Create a new Pinterest account using a dedicated email (not the operator's personal email)
2. Convert to Business account immediately: Settings → Account → Convert to Business
3. Claim your website: Settings → Claimed Accounts → Claim → Website
   - Add the HTML meta tag or upload the HTML verification file to the blog root
   - Claiming enables Rich Pins and website analytics attribution
4. Enable Rich Pins: Visit `https://developers.pinterest.com/tools/url-debugger/` with a product page URL and apply for Rich Pins
   - Rich Pins pull metadata (title, price) directly from the page — increases pin information density

### Account Profile

- Username: brand name for Conduit (not personal name, not "affiliate" in the handle)
- Display name: brand name
- Bio: 160 characters. Describe the value to readers, not "I am an affiliate."
  Example: "Honest product picks across home, kitchen, and lifestyle. No sponsored content, just the gear worth buying."
- Profile image: a clean brand logo or icon, not a face
- Website: Conduit blog URL

### Boards to Create at Activation

## Pin Velocity

### Ramp Schedule

Start slow. Pinterest flags new accounts that post at full velocity on day one.

| Phase | Week | Pins/Day | Total Pins Posted |
|-------|------|----------|-----------------|
| Warm-up | 1 | 5 | 35 |
| Warm-up | 2 | 8 | 91 |
| Ramp | 3 | 12 | 175 |
| Ramp | 4 | 15 | 280 |
| Ramp | 5 | 18 | 406 |
| Ramp | 6 | 20 | 546 |
| Steady state | 7+ | 20–25 | +140–175/week |

**Distribution**: Spread pins across boards. If running 5 boards at 20 pins/day: 4 pins per board per day. Never post all 20 to one board.

See [[agents/03-scheduler]] for optimal time windows. Core principle: post when US audiences are active. Saturday and Sunday evenings are peak.

### Repin Sources (Find at Activation)

For each niche, identify 10–15 accounts worth repinning from:
- Product manufacturers (not competitors — they make original content, not affiliate)
- Interior designers, lifestyle bloggers in the niche
- Home improvement, food, pet, fitness influencers
- Avoid: other obvious affiliate accounts (thin content, stock photos)

Repin from these accounts signals to Pinterest that Conduit is a genuine community participant, not a link-spam account.

## Pinterest SEO

Pinterest is a search engine. Keyword placement matters.

### Keyword Research

Before creating boards and writing pin copy, research keywords:
1. Pinterest search bar autocomplete — type your niche keyword, note all autocomplete suggestions
2. Pinterest Trends (`trends.pinterest.com`) — seasonal and trending search terms
3. Google Keyword Planner — validate search volume for high-intent queries

**Priority keywords** for pin descriptions:
- Intent: buying ("best", "top", "review", "vs", "guide", "worth it")
- Specific: product type + attribute ("best home office chair lumbar support", "air fryer under $100")
- Seasonal: include year where relevant ("2025 picks", "gift ideas 2025")

### Keyword Placement Rules

| Location | Keyword Strategy |
|----------|----------------|
| Board title | Primary niche keyword (e.g. "Home Office Setup Ideas") |
| Board description | Primary keyword in first sentence + 2–3 secondary keywords naturally woven in |
| Pin title | Primary keyword first, then benefit (max 100 chars) |
| Pin description | Primary keyword in first sentence, secondary keywords 2–3 times naturally, CTA, disclosure |
| Image alt text | Describe the image using the keyword phrase (not keyword stuffing) |

**Avoid**: Keyword stuffing. Pinterest's algorithm detects unnatural keyword repetition and treats it as spam.

## Paid Promotion

### When to Introduce Paid

Only promote content organically validated:
- Niche has ≥ 90 days of organic posting history
- At least one pin in the niche has ≥ 1000 organic impressions
- Niche outbound CTR ≥ 0.5% on organic pins

Do not run paid on an unvalidated niche. You are paying to find out what you could learn for free with more time.

### Campaign Structure

**Objective**: Traffic (outbound clicks, not awareness)
**Bid type**: Automatic initially, switch to max CPC bidding once you have data
**Daily budget**: Start $5–10/day per niche. Scale to $20–50/day only if ROAS ≥ 3×.
**Targeting**:
- Keywords: use keyword research list from niche SEO research
- Interests: Pinterest's interest categories for the niche
- Do not use audience retargeting until you have sufficient web traffic for meaningful lists

### What to Promote

Promote pins that already have strong organic performance. Do not create ads-only content — organic signal validates before you spend money.

**Promoted pin checklist**:
- [ ] Pin has ≥ 1000 organic impressions
- [ ] Outbound CTR ≥ 1.0% organic
- [ ] Affiliate link is healthy (Link Manager confirmed)
- [ ] Product has been in stock and available for 30+ days
- [ ] Affiliate disclosure is in pin description

### Monitoring Paid Campaigns

Check every 7 days:
- CPC (cost per click): should be < $0.50 to be viable for Amazon physical products, < $2.00 for high-ticket digital
- ROAS: target ≥ 3×
- If ROAS < 2× at day 14: pause campaign, reevaluate targeting
- If ROAS ≥ 5× at day 14: increase daily budget by 50%

## Shadowban and Account Safety

Pinterest will suppress or ban accounts that exhibit spam patterns. Avoid:

**Hard violations (instant risk)**:
- Posting the same image multiple times to the same board within 14 days
- Copy-pasting identical description text across multiple pins
- Posting dozens of pins in rapid succession (burst behaviour)
- Using link shorteners that hide the affiliate relationship
- Keyword stuffing in descriptions
- Any form of engagement manipulation (buying followers, using bots to save pins)

**Grey area (monitor)**:
- Very high pin volume from a brand-new account (reason for the ramp-up schedule)
- High percentage of outbound-link pins with low engagement (mitigated by repins)
- Very similar pin designs across all pins (vary templates by niche)

**Signs of shadowban**:
- Impressions drop suddenly and dramatically (90%+) with no account warning
- Pins stop appearing in search results for keywords where they previously ranked
- Analytics shows pins are "distributed" but impressions are near zero

**If shadowbanned**:
1. Stop posting immediately for 48 hours
2. Review all recent content for policy violations
3. Remove any pins that violate guidelines
4. Resume at low velocity (5 pins/day) with varied content
5. If account is fully suspended: do not create a new account on the same IP/device. Contact Pinterest business support.

## Pinterest Algorithm Notes (Current Understanding)

Pinterest's SmartFeed algorithm (verify current behaviour at activation, as it changes):

- Fresh pins (new image URLs) get a distribution test window on posting. First 30 days matter most.
- Pins with strong early engagement (saves, clicks) get broader distribution
- Board quality matters: boards with engaged followers distribute new pins better
- Account-level signals: consistent posting, diversity of content types, healthy engagement ratios all contribute to account authority
- Keyword relevance in descriptions directly affects search placement

**Practical implication**: The first 60–90 days of Conduit on Pinterest establishes account authority. Do not skip the ramp-up. Post consistently. Mix in repins. Let the algorithm learn what Conduit posts about before trying to scale.
