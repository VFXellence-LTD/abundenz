# Surge — Content Safeguards

=== READ THIS BEFORE CREATING ANY SURGE CONTENT ===

Surge optimizes for volume and retention. That creates pressure to push boundaries. These safeguards exist to prevent that pressure from producing content that causes harm, violates platform policies, or creates legal exposure.

Above all, the goal is for people to be entertained, engaged, thrilled, and emotionally invested in the stories without crossing into cruelty or genuinely harmful messaging. ‘Not causing distress’ does not mean avoiding tension, fear, danger, trauma, survival, horror, conflict, or emotionally intense material — those elements are often central to compelling storytelling. Stories about heroic survival, impossible odds, historical atrocities, disasters, injustice, resistance, or life-threatening situations are absolutely welcome when handled thoughtfully.

The guiding principle is that we do not punch down. We avoid demeaning marginalized groups or amplifying racist, bigoted, extremist, or hateful viewpoints uncritically. Diverse voices, progressive values, and underrepresented people should be treated with respect and humanity, while oppressive ideologies, cruelty, fascism, racism, and reactionary grievance politics can be framed critically, satirically, or as antagonistic forces within the narrative.

---

## The Standard

Every piece of Surge content must pass this test:

**"Would I be comfortable if someone I respect saw this content and knew I made it?"**

If the answer is no, the content doesn't ship.

## Hard Bans (never, regardless of vertical or trend)

### 1. Protect minors — kid-friendly content allowed, exploitation never

Content that appeals to children under 13 **is allowed** when it follows the kid-friendly rules below. Content that exploits, endangers, or harms minors is never allowed.

**Always banned:**
- No content that sexualizes, ridicules, or endangers minors
- No content featuring identifiable real minors (photos, videos, voice) without parental consent
- No AI-generated depictions of identifiable real children
- No content encouraging children to share personal information, meet strangers, or take dangerous actions
- No predatory engagement tactics targeting children (e.g., "don't tell your parents")

**Kid-friendly verticals (content designed for under-13 audience):**

When a vertical targets children, it must be flagged as `kid-friendly: true` in its README and follow these additional rules:

| Rule | Requirement |
|------|------------|
| Language | No profanity, no sexual innuendo, no drug/alcohol references |
| Violence | No graphic violence. Cartoon/slapstick conflict OK. Fantasy danger OK (dragons, adventure peril). No realistic weapons targeting people. |
| Horror/fear | Light spooky OK (haunted houses, silly monsters). No genuine terror, no jump scares, no psychological horror. |
| Ads/commerce | No direct purchase appeals to children ("ask your parents to buy"). No manipulative scarcity ("only 5 left!"). Affiliate links must target the parent, not the child. |
| Emotional manipulation | No guilt-tripping, shame-based engagement, or content designed to make children feel inadequate |
| Educational claims | Must be factually accurate. No pseudoscience presented as fact. |
| AI disclosure | All AI-generated content labeled per platform requirements |

**Platform-specific compliance for kid-targeted content:**

| Platform | Rules | Action |
|----------|-------|--------|
| YouTube | "Made for Kids" flag required → disables personalized ads, comments, notifications. RPM drops significantly. | Flag content correctly. Budget for lower RPM in kid verticals. |
| TikTok | Minimum user age 13. "Restricted Mode" filters content. Under-13 accounts use TikTok for Younger Users (limited features). | Content should be safe for Restricted Mode. Don't rely on under-13 TikTok users as primary audience — parents and older siblings are the real viewers. |
| Instagram | Minimum user age 13. Teen safety features restrict content visibility. | Same approach as TikTok — content safe for teens, parents are secondary audience. |

**COPPA compliance (US):**
- COPPA restricts data collection from children under 13, not content creation
- If running a website/app that collects data from kids: full COPPA compliance required (parental consent, privacy policy, data minimization)
- Social media platforms handle COPPA at platform level — but "Made for Kids" flags on YouTube trigger COPPA restrictions on your content
- Do not collect email addresses or personal data from minors via lead gen funnels

**Revenue impact:**
- Kid-targeted YouTube content earns lower RPM (no personalized ads)
- Kid-friendly TikTok content can still earn full RPM if not flagged as "for kids" specifically
- Strategy: create content kids enjoy that isn't exclusively "for kids" — family-friendly entertainment that adults also watch (think: satisfying videos, fun facts, cool science, animals)

If a vertical's audience skews young (under 18 but over 13), document additional content restrictions in the vertical brief.

### 2. No hate speech or discrimination
- No content that demeans people based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or national origin
- No "edgy humor" that uses protected groups as the punchline
- Satire is allowed; cruelty is not

### 3. No dangerous misinformation
- No fabricated health claims ("this cures cancer")
- No fabricated financial advice ("guaranteed returns")
- No fabricated news events presented as real
- Historical content clearly labeled as entertainment when dramatized
- Uncertain claims flagged: "allegedly," "according to," "the story goes"

### 4. No violence glorification
- Dramatic storytelling about violence (war, survival, crime) is allowed — it's a core genre
- Glorifying, celebrating, or instructing violence is not
- True crime: focus on the story, not the graphic details. Don't make the perpetrator the hero.

### 5. No real-person impersonation
- AI-generated voices must not impersonate real, identifiable people without explicit permission
- AI-generated visuals must not depict real people in fabricated scenarios
- Public figures can be discussed and quoted; they cannot be deepfaked

### 6. No copyright infringement
- Do not use copyrighted audio (music, podcast clips, movie audio) without license
- Do not use copyrighted footage without fair use justification
- Prefer: public domain content, AI-generated visuals, licensed stock, non-copyright gameplay footage
- When in doubt, generate original content with AI tools

### 7. No financial/investment advice presented as guidance
- "This person made $X doing Y" (reporting) is allowed
- "You should do X to make money" (advice) requires disclaimers
- No income guarantees. No "I made $X, you can too" without honest context.
- Surge content about making money must include "results not typical" framing

## Content Quality Floor

Volume is the strategy, but spam is the failure mode. Every clip must clear these minimums:

| Dimension | Minimum standard |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Hook | First 2 seconds grab attention with a clear promise or question |
| Pacing | No dead zones > 3 seconds. Cuts or new information every 2-3 seconds. |
| Audio | Clean, audible, normalized. No artifacts, clicks, or robot-voice glitches. |
| Captions | Accurate, properly timed, readable at mobile size |
| Visual quality | No obvious AI artifacts (extra fingers, text gibberish). 1080p minimum. |
| Narrative | Clear beginning, middle, end OR clear escalation with payoff |
| Factual accuracy | Claims verifiable to reasonable standard. Uncertain claims flagged. |
| Entertainment value | Viewer learns something, laughs, or is genuinely surprised. Not just clickbait with no payoff. |

**If a clip doesn't clear the quality floor, it doesn't post.** Volume means nothing if the algorithm buries you for low quality.

## Platform Compliance

### AI content disclosure

| Platform | Current policy (as of 2026-05) | Action |
|----------|-------------------------------|--------|
| TikTok | Requires disclosure of AI-generated content | Add AI disclosure label to all posts |
| YouTube | Requires disclosure of "altered or synthetic" content | Use YouTube's AI content label |
| Instagram | Requires AI content labeling on Reels | Use Meta's AI label feature |

=== CHECK PLATFORM POLICIES QUARTERLY — THEY CHANGE ===

Update this table when policies change. The compliance doc at `ecosystems/shared/compliance/ai-content-disclosure.md` tracks cross-platform AI rules.

### Multi-account policy

| Platform | Multi-account policy | Risk level |
|----------|---------------------|------------|
| TikTok | Technically restricted, widely practiced | Medium — maintain distinct content/identity per account |
| YouTube | Allowed (multiple channels per Google account) | Low |
| Instagram | Restricted to 5 accounts per person | Medium — use distinct brands per account |

**Mitigation:** Each account is a distinct brand with distinct content. No identical content across accounts. No obvious pattern that links accounts to one operator.

### Monetization eligibility

| Platform | Requirements | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| TikTok Creator Rewards | 10K followers + 100K views in 30 days + 18+ age | Videos must be 1+ min for RPM payment |
| YouTube Shorts | 1K subs + 10M Shorts views in 90 days (or 4K watch hours) | Shorts Fund being replaced by ad revenue sharing |
| Instagram Reels | Varies by region; invitation-based bonuses | Not guaranteed; treat as supplemental |

## FTC Compliance

- Any content containing affiliate links: FTC disclosure required ("includes paid links" or equivalent)
- Any content sponsored by a brand: clear "paid partnership" or "sponsored" label
- Any content promoting own products: not required to disclose, but should be organic (not infomercial)

---

## Escalation Protocol

If a piece of content is borderline:
1. **Don't post it.** Default to caution.
2. If you think it should post, write a one-line justification: why does this clear the safeguards?
3. If Boss reviews and approves, post with the justification logged.
4. If in doubt, ask Boss.

---

## Vertical-Specific Rules

Each vertical can add stricter rules on top of these safeguards. Verticals CANNOT relax these rules. The vertical README should document any additional content restrictions.

Example: A horror/scary story vertical might add "no gore, no real crime scene imagery, no jump scares targeting photosensitive viewers."
