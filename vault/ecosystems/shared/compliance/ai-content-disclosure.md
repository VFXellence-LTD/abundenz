# AI Content Disclosure — Platform Policies

Cross-platform reference for AI-generated content rules. Every ecosystem that produces AI content must follow these. Updated quarterly.

=== LAST VERIFIED: 2026-05-13 ===

---

## Platform Policies

### TikTok

**Policy:** AI-generated or synthetic content must be labeled using TikTok's AI label feature.
**Applies to:** All content where AI generated the voice, visuals, or substantial parts of the script.
**How to comply:** Enable "AI-generated content" label when posting. Available in advanced settings.
**Penalty for non-compliance:** Content removal, account warning, potential monetization suspension.
**Relevant ecosystems:** Surge (all content), Signal (AI-generated Shorts visuals)

### YouTube

**Policy:** Creators must disclose "altered or synthetic content" that could be mistaken for real.
**Applies to:** Realistic AI-generated people, voices mimicking real people, synthetic events presented as real.
**How to comply:** Use YouTube Studio's "Altered content" disclosure in video details.
**Penalty for non-compliance:** Content removal, channel strike, monetization suspension.
**Relevant ecosystems:** Surge (all AI-voiced content), Signal (AI-generated B-roll if realistic)
**Note:** Clearly fictional/creative content (animation, stylized) may not require disclosure — but err on the side of disclosure.

### Instagram / Facebook (Meta)

**Policy:** AI-generated content must carry Meta's "Made with AI" label.
**Applies to:** Photorealistic AI images, AI-generated video, AI voices.
**How to comply:** Meta auto-detects some AI content and labels it. Manually add label for content that slips through.
**Penalty for non-compliance:** Content labeling by Meta, potential reach reduction.
**Relevant ecosystems:** Surge (all content), Atelier (product images if AI-generated)

### X (Twitter)

**Policy:** Synthetic media that is deceptive may be labeled or removed.
**Applies to:** AI-generated content that could mislead viewers about real events or people.
**How to comply:** Add disclosure in post text: "AI-generated" or "Created with AI."
**Penalty for non-compliance:** Content label, potential removal.
**Relevant ecosystems:** Surge (video posts)

### Pinterest

**Policy:** AI-generated content allowed but must follow community guidelines.
**Applies to:** AI-generated images used in pins.
**How to comply:** No specific label required as of 2026-05, but check quarterly.
**Relevant ecosystems:** Signal (pin images), Atelier (product mockups), Conduit (affiliate pins)

### Amazon KDP

**Policy:** Must disclose AI-generated content during the publishing process.
**Applies to:** AI-generated text, images, or covers.
**How to comply:** Select "AI-generated" in KDP content declaration during upload.
**Penalty for non-compliance:** Listing removal, account warning.
**Relevant ecosystems:** Atelier (coloring books, KDP products)

---

## General Rules

1. **When in doubt, disclose.** Over-disclosure has no penalty. Under-disclosure risks account action.
2. **Check policies quarterly.** These change fast. Calendar reminder in controller.
3. **Platform-specific labels take priority** over generic text disclaimers. Use the platform's built-in tool.
4. **Don't try to hide AI involvement.** Algorithms are getting better at detection. Proactive disclosure is safer.
5. **AI-assisted vs AI-generated:** If AI assisted (e.g., script edited by AI, human recorded voice) — lower disclosure bar. If AI generated (e.g., AI voice, AI visuals) — always disclose.

## Ecosystem-Specific Notes

### Surge
- ALL Surge content is AI-generated. Disclose on EVERY post across EVERY platform.
- Use platform-native disclosure tools. Add text disclosure in caption/description as backup.
- Standard disclosure text: "Created with AI tools" (adapt per platform style)

### Signal
- Primary voice is human (owner) — no voice disclosure needed
- AI-generated thumbnails, B-roll, or Shorts visuals — disclose if realistic
- AI-generated blog content — if Claude writes it, consider disclosure (evolving standard)

### Atelier
- AI-generated product images — disclose per KDP/marketplace requirements
- Product listings — check each marketplace's current AI policy before listing

### Conduit
- If using AI-generated content for affiliate pages — disclose per platform
- FTC affiliate disclosure is SEPARATE from AI disclosure — both may be required simultaneously
